# Questions

Each screen is a moment in one game, in order. The game is played by choosing an action for each of your four creatures (the four at the bottom), then watching the round play out against the machines (at the top). Answer each question from what you can see on that screen (and earlier screens). Keep each answer to one to three sentences, and end each with (confidence 1 to 5).

## Screen 01 (screen-01.png)
1a. Whose decision is it right now, and what are the choices on screen?
1b. In what order will the creatures and machines act this round?
1c. How much health does each machine have, and each of your four creatures?
1d. What will "Crawler 1" do this round? To whom, and how badly?
1e. Of the choices shown, which would do the most good right now, and why?
1f. What do the short striped bars with a number under each choice's name mean?
1g. What does the small red badge with a crossed-swords icon and a "7", under each machine's name, mean?
1h. List everything on this screen you do not understand or had to guess at.
