# Questions

These are two screens from a game on a phone. The game is played by choosing an action for each of your four creatures (the four at the bottom), then watching the round play out against the machines (at the top). Answer from what you can see. Keep each answer to one to three sentences, and end each with (confidence 1 to 5).

## Screen 01 (screen-01.png)
1a. Whose decision is it right now, and what are the choices on screen?
1b. How much health does each machine have, and each of your creatures?
1c. What will "Crawler 1" do this round? To whom, and how badly?
1d. What do the short striped bars with a number under each choice's name mean?
1e. Of the choices shown, which would do the most good right now, and why?
1f. List everything on this screen you do not understand or had to guess at.
