# Questions

These screenshots come from a game where you give orders to four creatures (at the bottom) and then watch a round play out against machines (at the top). "before-the-round.png" is the start of the round. Each "moment-NN" is one beat of the round; its frames are lettered a, b, c and so on, in time order. Moments 01 to 08 are one round; moments 09 and 10 are from a round of a later fight; moment 11 is from a replay of that same later round where different orders were given. Answer from what you can see. Keep each answer to one to three sentences, and end each with (confidence 1 to 5).

For each moment 01 to 11, answer:
- (i) What happened? Who acted, on whom, and what was the result?
- (ii) Did it work, or was something stopped, missed or changed? Why?

Then, for these moments, also answer:
- M03 (iii): What will the label that appeared on the horse-like creature do to it later?
- M05 (iii): Did Crawler 2 attack in this moment? How can you tell?
- M08 (iii): Why did the creature that acted hit the machine it hit?
- M09 (iii): Why did the large machine act here?
- M11 (iii): What stopped the large machine's attack?

At the end:
- E1. Which moments were hard to follow, and why?
- E2. Did any animation, mark or label mean something different from what you first thought?
