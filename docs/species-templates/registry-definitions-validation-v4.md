# Registry definitions draft v4 — blind validation

Fresh pass, no earlier validation report consulted. Method: read the draft, the ratified design doc, the data-structure doc, the ability-grammar draft, skill sections 2/4/5, RULINGS.md, and the validator script; then assigned bodyPlan, composition, covering, diet, and lifespan for all 29 species using only the legacy description, the artwork, and the draft's own definitions and selection rules. Mass is the legacy weight, as the draft instructs.

**Verdict: NOT READY.** Four blocking items, all in coverage: the `bare`/`covering` default does not cover plant, mineral, metal, or gas bodies; the `photosynthetic` fallback contradicts canon for the one species it fires on; the biped/quadruped art test misreads at least four bodies; and the lifespan armored-adjustment interacts with the mass cuts in a way that is order-dependent and undefined for `gas`.

| 1 | GAP | covering / `bare` | The `bare` definition names its default scope as "slime, energy, and spectral bodies, and for a flesh body with no stated surface." It says nothing about `plant`, `mineral`, `metal`, or `gas` bodies with no stated surface. Xylum (plant, a mass of roots, no surface word in the description or art) has no reachable default; the selection rule's own fallback sentence ("otherwise take `bare`") sits inside the hide-vs-bare comparison and is not stated to govern a body that is neither. Then make the selection rule's fallback global: "When no source sentence and no visible feature in the art names a surface, the covering is `bare` and the field is listed under Authored fields." |
| 2 | CONTRADICTS | diet / `photosynthetic` | The fallback says "take `photosynthetic` for a plant body." The only plant body with no feeding sentence is Xylum, whose description states it "lives mostly underground as this is where it absorbs its power." A creature that lives underground cannot feed on light, so the fallback assigns a value the source contradicts. It also collides with the `photosynthetic` definition itself ("feeds on light directly") and with its own gate two sentences earlier ("requires a `plant` composition **or** a source sentence naming light as its food") — the gate is disjunctive, so composition alone licenses a value the description denies. | Tighten both. Definition gate: "`photosynthetic` requires a `plant` composition **and** no source sentence placing the body away from light." Fallback: "take `photosynthetic` for a plant body the sources place in light, `energy-feeder` for a plant or mineral body a source sentence shows drawing power from its surroundings, `omnivore` for a flesh body, and `none` for a spectral or energy body." Xylum then reads `energy-feeder` from "absorbs its power," which is decidable. |
| 3 | AMBIGUOUS | bodyPlan / `biped` vs `quadruped` | The art test is: "a creature drawn reared, leaping, or upright with no stated leg count is `biped` only when its forelimbs end in hands, fists, or held tools in the art, and `quadruped` otherwise." It misfires on at least four bodies. Codazzo's art is a fox-like creature standing squarely upright on two legs with small clawed forelimbs held free — not reared, not leaping, so the "drawn reared" branch does not apply, and the "drawn on all fours" branch does not either; the rule has no branch for a body simply drawn standing on two legs with non-hand forelimbs. Akinza is the same shape. Scalatto is drawn upright with small clawed forelimbs. Yetimoth's art is a bipedal ape stance whose forelimbs end in fists (so `biped`), while the description calls it an ape with fists and says nothing about leg count — a second agent reading "hulking ape" may take `quadruped`. Imprit's art is a four-limbed crouch on hands and feet with monkey anatomy, so the rule gives `quadruped`, but the description says it swings by its tail and was designed as a tinkerer that fixes equipment, which implies free forelimbs. | Add the missing branch and make the forelimb test about use, not shape: "When the description gives no leg count, the art decides: a body drawn with all limbs on the ground is `quadruped`; a body drawn standing, reared, leaping, or crouched with its forelimbs off the ground is `biped` when the forelimbs are free for other work (hands, fists, claws that grasp, or a held object) and `quadruped` when they are weight-bearing feet or hooves. A description that names work the forelimbs do, such as handling tools, carrying, or gripping, settles `biped` and overrides the pose." |
| 4 | AMBIGUOUS | lifespan / cut 3 | The mass bands and the one adjustment are order-dependent in a way the text does not fix. ## Non-coverage checks

**Value sets.** All six sets match the ratified sets exactly. `corporeality` (2), `composition` (8), `bodyPlan` (9), `covering` (9), `diet` (6), `communication` (5), phases (3), `senses.special` (6), `lifespan` (6), `genome.chirality` (2), `capabilities` (7), `actions` (16) — each checked against the design doc, skill section 5.5, and the validator's `ENUMS` and `CAPABILITIES` constants. No value is added, dropped, or renamed. The anatomy count (34) and channel count (7) are cited correctly and correctly deferred to section 5.6.

**Ratified rulings.** No contradiction found with the 2026-09-02 one-surface-key ruling, the artwork-as-source ruling, the conjured-familiar ruling (the `swarm` bullet and the Tetrahive disambiguation both match RULINGS.md), or the trait-model ruling (the draft correctly writes `armored` and `phasing` at 100 rather than as guaranteed traits). The Graviclaw amphibious and `hide`-removed rulings are consistent with what the draft's covering rule produces (`chitin`, and `shell` rather than `hide`).

**Anatomy registry and allowed-actions matrix.** The draft's restated standing rules (functional not material, external only, one surface key, a crab's claws are `pincers`) match section 5.6 verbatim in substance.
