# Xylum: v5 ability audit

Review date: 2026-09-21. Status: frozen in `generation-0.6.0-1`; game consumers still use v4. Sources: `../xylum.json`, `../xylum.md`, `../art/xylum.png`, canonical teaser and home-planet record. The teaser remains verbatim.

The fixed passive regrows its own torn root/tentacle body through gradual direct restoration. Ground power uptake explains that self process, not a general mana meter or theft of vitality from an enemy. A separately guaranteed ongoing Root Hold applies source-bound `restrained`; stopping the hold ends the sustained condition. Ordinary domains add root wrapping, compression and spine piercing, all from the rooted anatomy. Its strong burrowing and `tremorsense` are physiological rather than combat move slots.

No blanket ally healing, water cannon, underground area trap or generic drain was inferred from plant identity or old action pools. Temperament/output ratings were reviewed in the [cross-roster calibration](../../design/creature-v5-calibration.md). Ordinary moves are constructed from mechanism domains, not a prewritten list.

Compilation and seeded cohort generation verify four distinct actions plus the guaranteed self-regrowth and hold; this audit records source permission separately.

## Derived acts, 2026-09-22

**Channels:** none. Communication is vibration, the sight band starts at zero so the gaze predicate fails, and no source shows a mind, aura or secretion acting on another creature.

**Conduits:** none. This audit excludes a water cannon, a generic drain and blanket ally healing from plant identity or old action pools; no guaranteed or authored ability carries the plant element, and the v4 record declared none.

**Mechanisms removed as redundant:** `root-compression` (derived `roots/strike` compression, band preserved at 45 to 70) and `serrated-spine` (derived `spines/strike` piercing, band preserved at 32 to 58).

**Mechanisms kept:** `root-wrap`. Its `restrained` is sustained and bound to the source, matching the guaranteed Root Hold's maintained grip, where the derived `roots/snare` is a lingering brief status.

**Exclusions:** `spines/hurl`, because the serrations are edges along the tentacles rather than detachable spines, and launching them contradicts the described anatomy. `roots/drain` and `tendrils/drain` were removed on 2026-09-22, when physical `drain` became medium-only: Xylum declares no conduit, so neither row derives a drain. The ruling they recorded stands, that this audit rules out a generic drain and grounds the self-restore in ground power uptake rather than theft from an enemy.
