# Canonical v5 species definitions

The redesigned model and release adapter are implemented and the complete roster has passed source, creative-coverage, calibration, compilation, scale and frozen-release checks. Powerworks and Reclamation use the v5 canonical release; the legacy public generator remains v4. Do not feed this directory to the v4 bundler or change another game's imports as part of ability authoring.

Current staged species: all 32 canonical species. Each has a same-key `*.ability-audit.md` recording sources, included/excluded mechanism families, essential guaranteed capabilities, deferred mechanics and verification. The roster-wide representation audit is [here](../../design/creature-roster-audit.md); the cross-roster rating pass is [here](../../design/creature-v5-calibration.md). The current frozen canonical v5 release is `generation-0.8.0-1` on schema 5.1.0, with earlier archives replayable. Powerworks and Reclamation consume the v5 roster; the legacy public generator remains v4. Fathomaw is a draft proposal and audit, not yet part of that roster.

Schema 5.1 records mass as `massKg` and at least one applicable overall `heightCm`, `lengthCm` or `widthCm`. Additional overall dimensions are optional when they clarify the body. All declared measurements on an individual resolve from one size percentile. Frackworm's 900 to 1500 cm body extent is now length rather than height. Existing frozen records keep their original fields and replay under their archived schemas.

The existing descriptions stay verbatim. The fluid-development v5 templates deliberately remove traits, archetypes, corporeality and complete-move pools. Since 2026-09-22 the ordinary act space is derived from the body rather than authored: registry tables in `packages/content/src/creature/acts.ts` turn a species' anatomy, its declared `channels` and its declared `conduits` into acts, `acts.exclude` removes only those contradicted by evidence, and `acts.output` re-bands one. An authored `mechanisms` entry is an extension for justified behavior those tables cannot express, such as Avilily's paralyzing saliva or Graviclaw's pull toward the performer. New and edited species require a logically exhaustive review of all derived acts, exclusions and possible extensions; compactness is not an exclusion reason. The contract is [docs/design/creature-derived-acts.md](../../design/creature-derived-acts.md). The generator compiles the full space once, then constructs four distinct actions per individual without per-creature review or retry. Four actions are not a species-level cap.

The v5 creature catalog adds `fins` to its anatomy/instrument vocabulary without altering the v4 `registries.json` bundle, which is pinned by the current legacy release. The v5 derived-act table supplies the corresponding strike and shove permissions. A future release can reconcile the published registry vocabularies without rewriting any frozen archive.

Check source files with `npm run check:creature-model -- docs/species-templates/v5/<key>.json`. The package test `creatureCanonicalPilot.test.ts` additionally exercises seeded generation and guaranteed identity for all 32 species. The current archive is frozen; adding a species requires a new release, and each game's adoption remains separately scoped.
