# Graviclaw: v5 ability audit

Review date: 2026-09-21. Status: frozen in `generation-0.6.0-1`; game consumers still use v4. Sources: `../graviclaw.json`, `../graviclaw.md`, `../art/graviclaw.png`, canonical teaser and Grimedes record. The teaser remains verbatim.

All three indispensable source mechanisms are guaranteed: gravity-amplified pincer compression is the fixed signature, a gravity field draws a selected target toward the performer, and Ground Anchor applies a temporary `protected` status scoped specifically to displacement. The self anchor does not imply blanket harm immunity. The field's toward direction is relative to the performer, consistent with the ratified displace model. Ordinary domains vary gravity pincer force, single-target attraction and shell impact.

The lore's “miniature black holes” expresses its gravitational effect; no persistent summoned black-hole object, infinite reach, selective area or spacetime destruction is created. The shell does not automatically protect from every attack. Temperament and output bands were reviewed in the [cross-roster calibration](../../design/creature-v5-calibration.md); old dark-element neighboring powers are excluded. Permissions remain factored mechanisms, not complete moves.

Compilation and seeded cohort generation verify four distinct actions and all three guaranteed functions; this audit records source permission separately.

## Derived acts, 2026-09-22

Channels declared: `aura`. Justification: the guaranteed gravity field uses it, and the description has the Graviclaw intensifying gravitational waves around itself to generate miniature black holes in the water, which is a field around the body and not a part of it. Conduits `pincers: dark` and `aura: dark`. The pincer pair is carried from v4 and confirmed by the lore's gravity-amplified claws; the aura pair is the same gravitational effect, and dark is gravity under the ratified element fantasy, so both are the species element leaving through an evidenced outlet.

Mechanisms removed as redundant: `gravity-pincer` (pincers strike, compression) and `shell-crash` (shell strike, impact). Kept: `gravity-draw`, because every derived displacement pushes away and this one pulls toward the performer, which the tables cannot say. The authored pincer band, well above the derived default and the source of the fixed signature, is preserved as `acts.output`.

Exclusions:

- `*/drain` - the dark medium offers a drain; the audit above excludes old dark-element neighboring powers, and nothing has the Graviclaw taking vitality rather than crushing.
- `aura/mend` - there is no restorative process anywhere in the source; the gravitational field draws and anchors, it does not repair.
