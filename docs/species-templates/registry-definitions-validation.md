# Registry definitions: blind validation (2026-09-02)

Validator did not write the draft. Judged as an implementer and as a lore reader, against the ratified design doc, the data-structure doc, the ability-grammar draft, the migrate-species skill (sections 2, 4, 5) and its validator script, RULINGS.md, all 29 legacy `species.json` descriptions, and sixteen of the twenty-nine artwork renders (Neph, Ectoghoul, Xylum, Thirstaserp, Bioflim, Smokat, Avilily, Figzy, Crystorn, Venemist, Terragoyle, Kosanos, Voltish, Codazzo, Luceras, Scalatto, Hippochamp, Foromeer, Chromocat, Newtapede).

## 1. Value sets: PASS

Mechanically diffed every enum in the draft against the ratified sets in the design doc, skill section 5.5, and the `ENUMS`/`ACTIONS`/`CAPABILITIES` constants in `docs/species-templates/tools/validate-template.js`. All thirteen enums match exactly, including declaration order: corporeality (2), composition (8), bodyPlan (9), covering (9), diet (6), communication (5), phases (3), senses.special (6), lifespan (6), chirality (2), descriptionStatus (2), capabilities (7), actions (16). Nothing added, dropped, or renamed. The counts in the headings are correct.

## 2. Findings

| # | Severity | Enum and value | Problem | Proposed wording |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | CONTRADICTS | `corporeality: non-corporeal` | The draft says non-corporeal "Forces `phasing` at 100 and a composition of `spectral` or `energy`." The validator script accepts a third composition: `if (P.corporeality === 'non-corporeal' && P.composition && !['spectral','energy','gas'].includes(...)) warn(...)`. `gas` is allowed and the check is a WARN, not a FAIL. The draft's list is narrower than the enforced rule, so a compliant Smokat (feline that "atomize[s] into a cloud of smoke") is blocked from `gas` by the definition but permitted by the script. `phasing` at 100 is correct and is a genuine FAIL in the script. | "`non-corporeal`: has no persistent physical body; matter passes through it and it through matter. Forces `phasing` at 100, and a composition of `spectral`, `energy`, or `gas`." |
| 2 | CONTRADICTS | `covering: chitin`, `covering: plating` | "Demands `armored` at 100 and the `shell` anatomy key." The script raises only a WARN that must be answered in the walkthrough (`traits.armored`), never a FAIL, and it raises no `shell` requirement at all. It checks the converse: `hide` plus an armored covering warns. "Demands" reads as a hard invariant an implementer would encode as a gate; two agents will disagree on whether a chitin species may argue its way out. Also note Scalatto's source says "scaly exoskeleton," where `scales` and `chitin` both read. | "`chitin`: a hard exoskeleton or carapace grown by the body. Normally carries `armored` at 100 and the `shell` anatomy key; a species that departs from either must justify it in the walkthrough." Same shape for `plating`. |
| 3 | CONTRADICTS | `bodyPlan: swarm` | "many bodies acting as one creature, whether the units are its own flesh, split-off pieces, or **conjured projections held by a central mind**." This collides with RULINGS.md 2026-09-02 on Tetrahive, which rules the exact opposite: the creature "is a controller, **not itself the swarm**," and the conjured-familiar ruling assigns the familiar swarm to the `swarm` *channel* "regardless of the creature's own body plan." Skill section 5.6 repeats this. As written the draft would push Tetrahive to `bodyPlan: swarm`, contradicting a dated ruling on the one species the ruling was written for. | "`swarm`: many bodies acting as one creature, where the units are the creature's own flesh or split-off pieces. | 4 | CONTRADICTS | `bodyPlan: avian` | "the wings are the defining locomotion whether the creature is bird-like, bat-like, or **insect-like**," and the disambiguation says "a bat is `avian`." Applied to the sources this misfires twice. Terragoyle is a winged horned humanoid that crouches on two legs and "rise[s] high into the sky" (art: bat wings on a bipedal body) so `avian` and `biped` both fit under this rule. Tetrahive's central body is bat-like and winged, so the definition drags it to `avian` while the ruling calls it a controller with wings. Making wings alone decisive strips `biped`/`quadruped` from every winged legged creature, which is not what the enum was for. | "`avian`: a winged body whose primary locomotion is flight, that perches or lands between flights rather than walking as its normal gait; bird-like, bat-like, or insect-like alike. A creature that stands and walks on legs as its normal gait is `biped`, `quadruped`, or `multiped` even when it flies well, with `capabilities.flight` carrying the flight." |
| 5 | CONTRADICTS | `ambush` (action) | "a strike delivered from **concealment** or with a sudden closing rush." The ratified nature in the grammar draft is "a burst of closing speed ending in a hit" (delivery: melee). Concealment is `stealthy`, a trait, and the redesign explicitly rules that traits amplify while anatomy gates. Folding concealment into the action verb duplicates a trait inside the ability grammar and invites `ambush` on any stealthy species regardless of how it closes. | "`ambush`: a burst of closing speed that ends in a hit." |
| 6 | AMBIGUOUS | `bodyPlan: floating` vs `avian` | "movement is slow and directionless compared with `avian`" is a relative comparison with no anchor. Ectoghoul is non-corporeal, has no wings, and "zip[s] across the Dreadscape" (fast, directed) with a legacy `canFly: true`, so neither half of the `floating` definition holds and `avian` is barred for want of wings. The definition leaves a fast wingless flier with no key. | Replace the relative clause with a structural test: "`floating`: a body that hangs or drifts in its medium with no wings or fins, held up by gas, field, or the ghost register. The absence of a wing or fin surface is what selects this key, not the speed of the drift." |
| 7 | AMBIGUOUS | `capabilities.flight` vs `bodyPlan: avian` / `floating` | The draft never states the relationship, and an implementer must guess. The script's `flight.means` warn already encodes one: flight above 0 needs `wings` in anatomy, `bodyPlan: floating`, or a non-corporeal body. That rule is not in the draft, so an agent can write `flight: [60,80]` on a legged wingless body and only discover the objection when the script runs. | Add to the `capabilities` preamble: "`flight` is the outcome; `bodyPlan` is the presentation. A band above 0 requires `wings` in anatomy, `bodyPlan: floating`, or a non-corporeal body, and any other means must be justified in the walkthrough." |
| 8 | AMBIGUOUS | `diet: energy-feeder` vs `none` | "`energy-feeder`: feeds on a non-food energy: heat, charge, radiation, gravity, minds" against "`none`: does not feed; sustained by its nature (spectral, mineral, or Generator-sustained bodies)." Both name the same populations. Voltish "can store electrical energy from its surroundings," which is `energy-feeder` by the letter and arguably `none` by "sustained by its nature." Xylum "lives mostly underground as this is where it absorbs its power" reads as either. "Generator-sustained" is an escape hatch that applies to all 29 species, since every Xalian is printed by a Generator. | Give the pair a discriminator: "`energy-feeder`: takes in an external non-food energy (heat, charge, radiation, gravity, minds) and would fail without a supply of it. `none`: takes in nothing at all; the body neither eats nor draws on an external supply. 'Generator-sustained' is not a reason on its own, since every Xalian is generated." |
| 9 | AMBIGUOUS | `covering: crystal` | "Demands `armored` at 100 **only when the description says it is a defense**." Every other covering states a fixed consequence; this one is conditional on a reading of prose, so two agents split on Crystorn, whose gems "transmit powerful light energy" with no defensive claim. It also sits oddly beside `chitin`/`plating`, which demand `armored` unconditionally. | "`crystal`: a surface of crystal growth or facets. Carries `armored` at 100 when the crystal encloses or shields the body; a crystal growth that is an emitter rather than a surface is anatomy (`spines`, `core`, `crest`) and leaves `covering` to the actual surface." |
| 10 | GAP | `covering` (all values) | The draft never says what to do when a body carries two coverings, and the sources produce this repeatedly: Imprit has "fire-retardant fur" and a "scythe-like" tail; Bioflim is "acid slime" under "a thick rocky exoskeleton"; Dromeus is "partially feathered ... with lizard features"; Yetimoth is white-furred but "cover[s] themselves in a near-impenetrable armor" of ice. `covering` is a single value in the template contract, so a selection rule is mandatory. | Add to the section preamble: "One value. When a body shows more than one surface, name the one that covers the greatest area of the body at rest; a covering the creature forms or assumes in a fight is an ability, not the `covering`." |
| 11 | GAP | `composition` secondary | The header says "secondary optional and different" but the body gives no test for when a secondary is warranted. The design doc's explicit-none principle classes `composition.secondary` as an additive extra, omitted when absent, and cites Voltish as flesh/metal and Bioflim as slime/mineral. Without a stated threshold, agents will differ on whether Crystorn (crystal horns on a flesh body) or Imprit (constant immolation on a fur body) earns one. | Add: "Declare a secondary only when a second substance makes up a structural part of the body, not a coating, an emission, or an effect. Voltish is `flesh` with `metal` secondary because its bones and claws are alloy; a creature wreathed in flame is `flesh` with no secondary, because the flame is not the body." |
| 12 | AMBIGUOUS | `lifespan` bands | The bands mix three axes (duration, body class, role) and collide. `fleeting` names "swarms, small hot-running fliers, disposable field units"; `short` names "small, fast, hard-working flesh." Avilily is a small, fast, hard-working flier that swarms, so both fit. `long` names "large, slow, cold, or armored bodies" while `enduring` names "mineral, metal, crystal, and the largest slow bodies," so Bioflim (417 kg, slime over a rocky exoskeleton) and Foromeer (metallic exoskeleton on flesh limbs) split. | Make composition the first cut and mass the second: "Apply in order. A spectral or energy body is `ageless`. A body whose primary composition is mineral or metal is `enduring`. Otherwise band by mass and metabolic intensity within flesh and plant bodies: `fleeting` under a few kilograms or hot-running, `short` small and fast, `standard` mid-sized, `long` large, slow, cold, or armored." |
| 13 | AMBIGUOUS | `composition: flesh` vs `plant` | "`flesh`: living tissue, muscle and organ, whether or not it is covered in chitin, scale, or fur" against "`plant`: living vegetable tissue: wood, fiber, sap-carrying stem, leaf." Xylum, "a giant organism of thick, intertwined roots that act as tentacles," is a plant-element Florian whose art is a rooted mass with no head; both keys are defensible because roots are named in neither definition. Avilily is a plant-element bird made of bird. | Add roots to the `plant` gloss and state that the element never decides composition: "`plant`: living vegetable tissue: wood, fiber, root, sap-carrying stem, leaf. | 14 | GAP | `bodyPlan` for legless anchored bodies | Xylum has no locomotion at all in the source ("lives mostly underground"); the art shows a rooted trunk with tentacle-arms. `serpentine` needs undulation, `amorphous` needs a body without fixed shape, `multiped` needs limbs it stands on. Voice and form (check 5): PASS. No em-dashes, no British spellings, no invented lore, no year figures, no game-mechanics vocabulary in the definitions themselves. One incidental use of "a mechanical fact" in the `covering` preamble describes the trait system rather than importing game vocabulary and is acceptable.

Clean assignments: 6 of 29 (Kosanos, Akinza, Thirstaserp, Graviclaw, Hypnopet, Drilltail). Twenty-three species split on at least one of the five enums, and one (Xylum) has a genuine coverage gap in `bodyPlan`.

The two dominant causes are structural, not per-species. First, `covering` has no rule for a body with two surfaces, which alone splits Dromeus, Bioflim, Crystorn, Foromeer, Scalatto, Yetimoth, Imprit, Neph, Hippochamp, Codazzo, Newtapede, and Venemist. Second, `diet` is simply unstated in eleven of the source descriptions, and the `energy-feeder`/`none` pair overlaps, so the agent is guessing on Tizzie, Luceras, Figzy, Foromeer, Imprit, Crystorn, Yetimoth, Hippochamp, Codazzo, Scalatto, and Akinza. Neither is a wording defect in a single value; both need a rule added to the section preamble. A third, smaller cause is that `composition` gives no threshold for a secondary, which splits Voltish, Crystorn, Chromocat, Neph, Smokat, and Terragoyle.

## 4. Verdict

**NOT READY.**

Blocking items, in order:

1. Finding 3 (`swarm` covering conjured projections) directly contradicts the Tetrahive ruling of 2026-09-02 and the conjured-familiar rule in skill section 2. A migration agent following the draft will assign Tetrahive a body plan the orchestrator has already ruled against.
2. Finding 4 (`avian` decided by wings alone) reassigns every winged legged creature, splitting Terragoyle and Tetrahive and colliding with finding 3.
3. Finding 1 (`non-corporeal` forces spectral or energy) is narrower than the validator, which also accepts `gas`. The definition and the script must agree or Smokat and Ectoghoul are decided by whichever the agent read last.
4. Finding 2 (chitin and plating "demand" armored at 100 and `shell`) states as an invariant what the script raises only as an answerable warning, and adds a `shell` requirement the script does not check at all.
5. Finding 10 (no rule for a body with two coverings) and finding 8 (`energy-feeder` versus `none` overlap) are the two causes of most of the coverage splits. Neither is fixable per value; each needs a sentence in its section preamble.
6. Finding 5 (`ambush` includes concealment) puts a trait concept inside an action definition and departs from the ratified nature of the verb.

Findings 6, 7, 9, 11 through 16 should be fixed in the same pass; 17 through 20 are cosmetic and can ride along. Finding 14 (no body plan for a rooted body) needs Nick, not a wording change, because the nine body plans are ratified and Xylum falls outside them.

The value sets themselves are correct and need no change. The defect is entirely in the definitions, which is what this draft exists to supply.
