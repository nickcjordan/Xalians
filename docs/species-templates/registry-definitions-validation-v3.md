# Registry definitions draft v3: blind validation

Validator: independent agent, no access to the draft's authoring session and no earlier validation report read. Sources used: the ratified design doc and its dated rulings, `xalian-creature-data-structure.md`, `xalian-ability-grammar-draft.md`, migrate-species SKILL sections 2, 4, 5, `RULINGS.md`, `tools/validate-template.js`, all 29 descriptions in `lambda/src/json/species.json`, and all 29 PNGs in `art/`.

Method: for each of the 29 species I assigned bodyPlan, composition, covering, diet, and lifespan using only the description, the art, and the draft's own definitions and selection rules, deliberately refusing to reach for outside judgment where the draft was meant to decide. CLEAN means a rule in the draft named the value directly. FALLBACK means only a stated default produced it (and the field lands under Authored fields). SPLIT means two values stay equally defensible after every rule in the draft has been applied.

**Verdict: NOT READY.** Four blocking items, all in the covering and lifespan sections. Everything else is either clean or a nit.

## Verdict

**NOT READY.** The draft is close and its structure is right, but four items would produce divergent templates across two agents on the current text.

Blocking items:

1. **Finding 1** (CONTRADICTS): the `flight` sentence claims `swarm` suppresses a script warn that it does not suppress. This is the only outright contradiction with an enforced rule in the file and must be fixed before the text can be cited as authoritative.
2. **Finding 2** (AMBIGUOUS): the covering mass clause reads a mass that does not exist at decision time, and the design doc explicitly demotes the only number available. Seven covering SPLITs trace here or to finding 10.
3. **Findings 4 and 5** (AMBIGUOUS): the lifespan mass cuts have the same undefined-mass problem plus an "armored at any mass" clause whose strict and loose readings give different bands for five species.
4. **Finding 7** (AMBIGUOUS): three bodyPlan SPLITs from a single missing tie-breaker for action-pose art.

Findings 3, 6, 8, 9, and 10 are not individually blocking but 10 produces a SPLIT on a real species (Ectoghoul) and should be fixed in the same pass. Findings 11 through 16 are nits.
