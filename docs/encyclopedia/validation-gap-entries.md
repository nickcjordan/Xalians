| "which converted their Xalian miners and forge-workers into corporate raiders" | SUPPORTED (minor actor drift — see note) | Magmuth, para 6: "The Vallerii began using Xalian miners and forge-workers for combat, converting them into corporate raiders in the truest sense of the word." Source names **the Vallerii** as the converting actor, not "the corporations" directly, though the corporations are Vallerii-owned; treat as a loose paraphrase rather than a contradiction. |
| "in disputes over the planet's natural resources" | SUPPORTED | Magmuth, para 6: "Vying for control over ever larger shares of the planet's natural resources, Magmuth's corporations grew increasingly greedy and hostile toward one another." |
| "went on in limited fashion for decades until the Magmuth Massacre" | SUPPORTED | Magmuth, para 6: "These 'company wars' went on in limited fashion for decades until the Magmuth Massacre, when a neutral mining territory with a working population of over a hundred thousand Xalians was surprise attacked and raided for resources, its Xalian laborers slain to the last creature." |
| "the use of Xalians in warfare that they represented is what the APEX Accords were signed to end" | **UNSUPPORTED / SCOPE INFLATION** | No sentence in Magmuth's history, or anywhere else in `planets.json`, links the Company Wars specifically to the signing of the APEX Accords. This is a causal link the source does not assert — the Company Wars are never cited as the trigger or rationale for the Accords. |

**Verdict: FAIL** — failing clause: "the use of Xalians in warfare that they represented is what the APEX Accords were signed to end" (unsupported causal/scope claim).

Capitalizing it as an encyclopedia entry title ("Ghost Fleet") is a reasonable editorial promotion of an attested phrase, not a fabrication — flagged for awareness only.
