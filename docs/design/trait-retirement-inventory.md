# Trait retirement — migration inventory

2026-09-17. The user approved retiring independently rolled traits, preserving unique information in explicit creature facts/capabilities, and deriving summaries outside the authoritative record. Essential capabilities remain guaranteed; optional passives may vary through authored compatible possibilities. This supersedes the earlier guaranteed-only passive restriction.

The mappings below are proposals for source-based migration, not automatic edits or newly ratified species powers. A label cannot by itself establish mechanism, scope, strength, immunity, or a new trigger. Existing archives remain unchanged.

## Remaining decisions before migration

### Commanding / Vespersyn — scope ratified, 2026-09-21

Vespersyn explicitly summons flying toothed familiars, controls them mentally, and attacks/defends as one unit. Current abilities already use the registered swarm instrument; current signature expresses harm through that instrument. User ratified preserving swarm as a source mechanism for generated attack/defense outcomes under the existing effect vocabulary, without requiring a universal summon effect or independently generated familiar records. Games may realize the mechanism with their own entities, but the creature contract does not invent companion health, turns, count, or lifespan. This is not ordinary social coordination. The commanding trait cannot by itself imply mind control of unrelated creatures. Exact signature and defense effects still need source-based authoring.

### Behavioral labels versus conditional power — review finding, 2026-09-19

The legacy registry gives territorial, pack-bonded, and solitary explicit grows-stronger meanings (defending claimed ground, coordinating with others, operating alone). These are not equivalent to habitat defense, social preference, or living alone. For example, Avilily's source describes defending forests and living in swarms, but does not establish a physiological strength boost from occupying territory or coordinating. Akinza's source establishes night activity and night vision; these are separate facts, not a generic nighttime combat bonus.

Ratified: retire conditional bonuses supplied only by those legacy trait definitions. Preserve supported habits in lore and existing temperament/senses fields where their meanings actually match; do not derive night activity solely from sight or group membership solely from sociability. Author an actual conditional enhancement only when independently supported by species mechanism, and surface any missing bounded trigger/condition semantics for review rather than introduce them silently. Do not add home-territory, ally-count, or day/night activation predicates solely to preserve legacy trait wording. Structured activity/social representation remains a separate open question.

Sensory follow-up ratified: Akinza explicitly has night vision and night activity. Current senses have general sight/hearing/smell ratings and special keys echolocation, tremorsense, electroreception, psychic, heat-sense, void-sense, but no explicit low-light vision. Add lowlight to physiology.senses.special's registry. A high sight rating cannot safely substitute for low-light adaptation, and heat-sense is a different mechanism. Night activity remains a separate behavioral fact; lowlight must not imply night-only activity, sight in zero light, or a second general acuity multiplier.

Luminous follow-up resolved: the legacy definition bundles bodily light emission with revealing nearby concealed things. Chromocat's light blades support a visible light source, whereas Crystorn's gems transmitting light when provoked do not alone establish continuous emission. User approved retiring luminous without adding the proposed physiology.emission field. Keep ordinary glow descriptive and light-producing ability mechanisms with their existing explicit effects. Structured illumination gameplay is deferred; do not infer revealed or a universally usable light source from glow or light identity.

Foresighted follow-up finding: the registry defines this as reading moments before they happen. Its only current ratified-species trait-pool occurrences are optional Figzy (10) and Tizzie (5). Their reviewed lore describes psychic intervention and hypnotic mental attack, respectively, without establishing literal future perception. Recommendation pending discussion: do not infer precognition from psychic identity, intelligence, instinct, or these old trait rolls. Defer a new prediction sense/effect rather than silently mapping foresighted to psychic sensing or a generic combat bonus. This does not forbid future authored precognition; its information and gameplay boundaries would require explicit review.

### Phasing review — distinction ratified, 2026-09-19

Latest decision supersedes the field-retention proposals below: retire corporeality entirely. Material description stays in composition, resistance/immunity in explicit protections, and actual obstacle-traversal powers must receive a bounded representation. Smokat and Ectoghoul retain their ratified material-smoke versus through-surface distinction without the corporeality field. Production migration is pending.

Both Ectoghoul and Smokat currently declare physiology.corporeality = non-corporeal and phasing = 100. The shared non-corporeal definition says matter passes through the body and the body through matter. Ectoghoul's lore explicitly supports passing through surfaces. Smokat's lore supports atomizing into smoke for evasion or sneak attacks, but does not explicitly establish passage through sealed solid surfaces. The present broad category therefore risks granting Smokat unsupported wall traversal and conflates spectral intangibility with a dispersed material body.

Recommendation for discussion: distinguish persistent spectral intangibility from smoke dispersion. Do not infer wall traversal or universal physical-harm immunity from ghost elemental identity or gas composition. Ectoghoul's persistent body property and Smokat's deliberate dispersal process need separate faithful representations. Exact corporeality vocabulary and the latter capability's effects remain unratified; do not solve by adding an unrestricted transformation engine or silently deleting the species mechanism.

User ratified this distinction: Smokat is material smoke that can disperse, while Ectoghoul can pass through solid surfaces. Ectoghoul's persistent intangibility belongs in physiology; Smokat's deliberate dispersal belongs in an action. No automatic universal damage immunity is conferred. The paragraphs above document the pre-migration inconsistency, not the desired classification.

Follow-up source inspection: Smokat's existing signature, The Scattering Pounce, describes atomization but contains only one target-directed impact harm effect. Its defining dispersal therefore has no structured outcome in the signature. Migration must explicitly represent the supported evasion/concealment outcome rather than merely remove phasing. Recommendation: make deliberate smoke dispersal the guaranteed signature, with pounce/attack variations in the ordinary authored pool. Exact concealment/protection payloads and lifetime remain species-authoring decisions; no blanket physical immunity or new generic transformation effect is approved.

Boundary ratified 2026-09-17: physiology owns intrinsic bodily properties, including explicit protection; actions own deliberate processes; passives own automatic processes. Protection is not inferred solely from composition. A passive rebuilding armor is distinct from the protection provided by that armor. Applied resistance statuses do not replace innate resistance. The concrete physiology protection structure remains open.

1. Decide how intrinsic protection and activity/social facts are represented without recreating an unrestricted trait bag.
2. Review conditional-strength and absolute-immunity wording against source intent. Removing the old field must neither silently delete real powers nor turn descriptive/game shorthand into new biology.
3. Resolve direct processes versus applied statuses and the missing effect/trigger vocabulary needed by evidenced existing powers, such as reflection and dormancy.
4. Author compatible optional passive choices while preserving all defining species capabilities. Choose generation policy after the contents are reconciled; no independent trait roll or runtime dependency chain.
5. Define summaries through shared deterministic rules. Cached summaries remain rebuildable, versioned indexes rather than independent creature facts.
