## Source inventory

Proposed usable sources: whole body including its blunt scoop brow, claws on the forehands, weight-bearing feet, blunt plate-bearing jaws, and flexible insulating hide. The brow is an integrated head surface, not separately declared horns. Proposed senses are ordinary sight, hearing, smell, and touch. No special sense, elemental outlet, secretion weapon, signal attack, or projecting channel is established. Lung respiration alone is not evidence of an offensive breath. Hind webbing belongs to the feet, not a separate fin pair.

The guaranteed identity candidate is contacted scooping displacement. The action should not depend on ice or environmental supply. Essential ordinary capabilities include standing, walking, swimming with air access, grazing, and shelter excavation. Storm suspension is proposed ecology, not a passive or an action.
