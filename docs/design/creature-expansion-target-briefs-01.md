# Creature Expansion Target Briefs 01

## Status and scope

These are proposed design targets, not creature concepts and not ratified species. They identify what a future creature should accomplish before it receives a name, complete anatomy, appearance, measurements, prose, or a finalized ability.

The ranking applies the [expansion framework](./xalian-creature-expansion-framework.md) to the current [coverage ledger](./CREATURE-EXPANSION-COVERAGE.md). It favors a coherent cluster of needs over the raw number of empty registry cells.

## Recommended sequence

| Rank | Target | Primary reason | Coherent coverage gain | Main risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Veridium / Metal | Metal has only one species | Primary metal body, enduring lifespan, restorative identity, high manipulation | Turning material into automatic armor or making a machine instead of a creature |
| 2 | Zolton / Electric | Electric has only one species | Non-storage electrical identity, group contrast, possible canyon-navigation sense | Repeating Voltish with a different silhouette or forcing unused vocabulary |
| 3 | Poseidas / Water | The deep-ocean lane is absent | Piscine body plan, echolocation, pressure specialization, unused crush signature | Becoming a generic Earth fish or duplicating Newtapede's underwater control |

This order is not a power ranking. It is the order in which the target briefs currently provide the most information and coverage per new species.

## Target 1: Veridium metallurgical restorer

### Primary deficit

Metal and Veridium have only one species, Foromeer. Foromeer is a flesh-bodied, plated biped centered on drilling, ramming, and direct forward damage. It does not represent Veridium's other explicit fauna lanes: metallurgical manipulation and self-repairing frame architectures.

### Proposed target identity

A Veridium organism whose defining act is restoring, rejoining, or reconfiguring metal rather than breaking through it. Its identity must remain legible as an organism performing a bodily act, not as a robot using a repair tool.

### Proposed constraints

- **World and element:** Veridium; Metal.
- **Composition:** primary `metal`. This establishes the registry's first primary-metal species; Voltish currently uses metal only as a secondary composition.
- **Environmental fit:** sustained activity must stay inside Veridium's -10 to 80 C habitable band and acknowledge forge heat, molten pours, discharge arcs, and machinery that ignores proximity.
- **Capabilities:** manipulation and climb should be genuine strengths. Burrow should not repeat Foromeer's mining profile, and flight conflicts with the report's crane, cable, and thermal-plume density unless a compelling exception is developed.
- **Trait foundation:** `regenerative` is strongly supported as mechanical self-repair. Metal composition must not automatically imply `armored`; armor requires a covering or body behavior that actually provides it.
- **Lifespan candidate:** `enduring`, currently unused, is coherent with a self-repairing metal body but must follow the lifespan rubric rather than being selected merely to fill the gap.
- **Signature direction:** `mend`, currently unused by signatures, is the preferred baseline action if the defining act restores the user or another body. The final instrument must come from visible anatomy and pass the instrument/action matrix.
- **Archetype direction:** favor intelligence, willpower, vitality, resilience, or manipulation-compatible readings over Foromeer's strength-first berserker/juggernaut identity. Do not force `balanced` simply because it is unused.

### Contrast set

- **Foromeer:** breaks and bores; the target restores and reconfigures. Foromeer is flesh under plating; the target is materially metal.
- **Voltish:** stores and discharges electricity through alloy anatomy; the target's metal is its body and its identity is metallurgical restoration.
- **Crystorn:** focuses stored light through crystal horns; the target must not become another stationary mineral emitter.

### Supporting deficits this target may close

- Primary metal composition.
- Enduring lifespan.
- Mend signature action.
- An unused emitter such as `core` only if the anatomy and act independently demand it. `Core` does not qualify merely because the target is metallic.

### Do not force

- Armor, a humanoid body, a core, `balanced`, `inspiring`, or any unused instrument.
- A fabricated-machine aesthetic that erases animal life, appetite, behavior, or ecological presence.
- Healing as vague magic. The restored material and bodily mechanism must be visible and bounded.

### Success test

Remove its name and element label. A reader should still distinguish it from Foromeer as Veridium's restorative, metal-bodied manipulator rather than its breaker.

## Target 2: Zolton discharge router

### Primary deficit

Electric and Zolton have only one species, Voltish. Voltish already occupies the minority storage lineage named by the planet report: it absorbs environmental charge and drives the stored charge through alloy claws in a contact strike.

### Proposed target identity

A Zolton organism centered on the world's universal conduction-and-redirection adaptation and discharge-cycle timing, without storing charge as its defining behavior. It should manage where a strike or surface current goes rather than serving as another battery.

### Proposed constraints

- **World and element:** Zolton; Electric.
- **Environmental fit:** sustained activity must stay inside Zolton's -55 to 35 C habitable band and account for continuous lightning, cryogenic canyon gas, black-lightning dead zones, and cyclical surface-current rivers.
- **Trait foundation:** `insulated` is required by the report's universal adaptation. Other electrical traits must describe this organism specifically rather than restate its element.
- **Mobility:** climb, timed sprint, or storm-shelter burrow are supported. Flight is inefficient and swim is unsupported; neither should be used to manufacture novelty.
- **Body contrast:** it should not be another biped with grasping claws. The exact body plan remains open until its current-routing mechanism and silhouette are chosen.
- **Signature direction:** deflection, redirection, or controlled displacement is preferred over another `strike`. `ward` or `shove` are stronger baseline candidates than forcing the currently unused `lash`, `crush`, or `mend` actions.
- **Social direction:** the discontinued relay program provides a legitimate paired-lineage lane. If used, synchronized joint action must be central enough to support `pack-bonded`; mere proximity is insufficient.
- **Sense candidate:** echolocation is currently unused and coherent with canyon navigation through dense gas, but it remains optional because the world report does not require every canyon organism to use it.

### Contrast set

- **Voltish:** stores charge, feeds on energy, and attacks through alloy claws. The target redirects current and must not depend on storage or contact claws for its identity.
- **Crystorn:** releases stored energy as a focused line. The target must remain about routing active environmental current rather than charging and firing an emitter.
- **Luceras:** already occupies aerial lightning behavior. The target stays grounded in Zolton's spires, canyons, or current rivers rather than becoming another storm flier.

### Supporting deficits this target may close

- Echolocation, if canyon navigation becomes defining.
- A non-biped electrical silhouette.
- A cooperative electrical profile contrasting Voltish's solitary weighting.
- An unused instrument such as antennae only if it is physically necessary for sensing or routing current.

### Do not force

- An unused signature action, `balanced`, `inspiring`, antennae, or echolocation solely for ledger improvement.
- Electrical storage, claw delivery, humanoid posture, or generic lightning projection.
- Flight through a report that explicitly makes Zolton's air corridors hazardous.

### Success test

Its defining act must still work as a distinct baseline if all electrical spectacle is described plainly as current entering, moving through, and leaving the body.

## Target 3: Poseidas deep-pressure navigator

### Primary deficit

Poseidas has two species, but neither occupies the planet report's pressure-adapted deep-form lane. Hippochamp is a quadrupedal settlement responder with a sustained water jet; Newtapede is a multiped grappler that coils around swimmers. The roster has no `piscine` body plan and no echolocating species.

### Proposed target identity

A native of Poseidas's stable deep layer whose defining competence is navigating and acting under crush depth. Its form should read as purpose-built for open three-dimensional water rather than as a land animal that can swim.

### Proposed constraints

- **World and element:** Poseidas; Water.
- **Body plan:** `piscine`. This is the brief's clearest morphological requirement, not a suggestion to copy an Earth fish.
- **Environmental fit:** breathes and tolerates liquid; sustained activity stays inside Poseidas's -2 to 40 C habitable band. Pressure is expressed through lore, `hardened`, and the central act because pressure itself is not a separate record field.
- **Capabilities:** swim must dominate. Sprint and climb should remain genuinely inapplicable or weak rather than being raised to make the template broadly capable.
- **Sense:** echolocation is the preferred special sense for deep navigation. Its visible or behavioral mechanism must be established without importing an Earth-animal silhouette wholesale.
- **Trait foundation:** `hardened` is required if the species normally operates at crush depth. `perceptive` is not automatically required merely because echolocation is present.
- **Signature direction:** `crush`, currently unused by signatures, is preferred if the central act turns pressure into direct compression. It must remain distinct from Newtapede's `snare`: crushing is the effect, not prolonged restraint.
- **Instrument direction:** body, jaws, or tail can support `crush` in the existing matrix. The anatomy must decide among them; the action must not decide the anatomy backward.

### Contrast set

- **Hippochamp:** patrols settlements and projects a high-pressure stream. The target belongs to the deep layer and does not operate as a surface or settlement responder.
- **Newtapede:** controls a swimmer by wrapping and holding. The target navigates open depth and compresses rather than grapples.
- **Neph:** already supplies a floating, broad-volume body in air. The target must be shaped by liquid locomotion and pressure, not simply translated from atmospheric suspension.

### Supporting deficits this target may close

- Piscine body plan.
- Echolocation.
- Crush signature action.
- A second use of scales, currently a singleton covering, only if the final surface design calls for scales rather than bare skin, hide, or another coherent covering.

### Do not force

- A recognizable shark, whale, ray, or ordinary fish with an elemental effect attached.
- Grasping anatomy or high manipulation without a visible means.
- `perceptive`, scales, carnivory, or solitary behavior as assumed consequences of deep-ocean life.
- Mend simply because Poseidas has healing associations; this brief is about deep pressure, not Algael processing.

### Success test

The template should remain unmistakably a deep-water navigator when its signature is temporarily removed, and the signature should remain a crush rather than a disguised beam, spray, or snare.

## Reserve briefs, not yet recommended

### Floria ecosystem supporter

Floria could coherently combine the unused photosynthetic diet with the unused `inspiring` trait through the planet's coordinated ecosystem response. It remains behind the top three because Plant already has three species, and a presence that bolsters allies needs a concrete behavior rather than a convenient trait assignment.

### Luminax energy-bodied mutation

Luminax's ION-9 blast zones could support the unused energy composition and possibly heat-sense. It remains reserved because blast-zone records are explicitly provisional and because an energy body requires careful corporeality, diet, lifespan, and anatomy decisions. It should not be promoted merely to clear a registry zero.

## Decision point after these briefs

The recommended first full design pass is the Veridium target. The Zolton and Poseidas briefs should remain visible as controls: if the Veridium design starts absorbing unrelated electrical, sensory, aquatic, or social gaps, those features belong to another brief and should be removed.
