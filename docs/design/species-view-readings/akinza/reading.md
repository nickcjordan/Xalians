## What the drawing establishes

Akinza is a slender, fur-covered upright feline biped with two very large ears held broadly sideways. Their uneven outlines represent shaggy hair, not jagged or damaged ear anatomy. Its head has two large abstract white eye shapes, two white inner-ear markings, and a small white nose-and-mouth design. Nick clarified that the eye squiggles are an abstraction: render big, distinctive eyes in those positions rather than literal jagged shapes. Their exact design is open to the pilot. The white features are not holes through the head.

Both hands rest at the hips, with elbows out. The stance is deliberately uneven: Akinza's right leg, on the viewer's left in front, stays closer beneath the hip; its left leg extends diagonally outward. The hands, elbows, knees, feet, and shoulders are not mirror copies. Preserve each side's actual position. White hand and toe strokes describe the drawn divisions; do not add a conventional five-finger hand or extra toe marks.

Three long, broad, pointed plume shapes sweep to Akinza's left, the viewer's right in front. The upper plume rises highest, the middle plume reaches farthest sideways, and the lower plume curves downward before turning outward. Preserve their jagged edges, unequal shapes, and white separations. There are two arms, two legs, two ears, and these three plume shapes, with no props or accessories.

## Approved depth interpretation for the pilot

Nick confirmed the attachment on the rear centerline at the base of the spine, not the hip, flank or either buttock. His latest correction specifies three distinct tails fused together and into the body at that compact shared base. They separate immediately at the body and sweep left and slightly backward; there is no extended shared stalk before they branch. Each tail has full rounded cattail-like volume, meaningful depth and a sustained taper to a pointed tip. In front, the hip hides the central fusion; the point where the tails become visible beside the body is not their anatomical origin. The drawing establishes the three shapes; Nick's direction resolves their hidden attachment and volume.

Each tail must have a continuous, visible connection into the shared body-level fusion. Do not let two tails appear to originate in empty space behind a third foreground tail. The earlier tree-trunk analogy established continuity, but Nick has now rejected a trident-like common stalk with a delayed split. The three distinct tails and body blend together directly at the base. Preserve smooth organic transitions without a long neck, disconnected roots or crossing stems hiding the joins.

Reduce the prominence of the buttock forms. The rear pelvis should be modest, flatter, and softly fur-covered, without a pronounced buttock cleft, rounded cheek highlights, or exaggerated hourglass shaping. Preserve the established stance and hands-on-hips pose while reducing that emphasis.

Give the head a short, modest muzzle; keep the ears broad, thin, and fur-covered, held in the drawing's sideways attitude. Give the slender torso, arms, legs, hands, and feet enough rounded depth to read as a body. Do not enlarge the chest, paws, muzzle, or ear thickness into a different animal. The back has continuous fur, without invented stripes, ridges, or ornaments. These unseen surfaces are restrained proposals, not facts established by the front drawing.

## Rules shared by every view

Only the camera moves. Do not level the shoulders, straighten the bent legs, turn the feet into a neutral stance, raise the ears, or rearrange the plumes between views. All views use the same scale, ground row, and heights for corresponding head, eye, shoulder, hip, knee, and sole landmarks. Hidden features are recorded as hidden with a named occluder, never guessed as visible measurements.

Use neutral mid-gray shaded form with soft even light, a transparent or pure white background, no cast shadows, and no perspective distortion. White source markings remain recognizable features on the shaded front and in its derived feature mask. No image generation begins until Nick approves this reading in his own words.
