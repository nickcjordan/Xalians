# Hypnopet CP2 round 4: head critique

Sources: cp2-round4.png, cp2-round4-body.png, cp2-R2-extra.png, targets/flesh-front.png, targets/flesh-side.png. Head and face only. Ears, horn and body are excluded from this review.

## Verdict

**No.** All three options read as a goggle-eyed alien or gremlin, not the sheet's soft, round-cheeked bunny. Heavy raised rims around the eyes, a lower face pinched narrower than the skull, and a small fanged mouth replace the sheet's wide open smile and full cheeks.

## Head defects, worst first

1. **Raised rims around the eyes (front and three-quarter, all of the brow and orbit).** Every option has a thick raised rim around each eye, with a ridge over the brow. From the front this looks like a pair of goggles, and in three-quarter it throws a dark shadow under the eye. The sheet has no rim at all. The eyeball meets the face with only a thin soft lid crease, and the forehead runs smoothly into the top of the eye. Fix: flatten the rim until the surface around the eye sits flush with the cheek and forehead, leaving only a lid lip about 5 percent of the eye width. **Real shape defect.** Fur on the finished model would soften it a little but would still show it as a mask.

2. **Lower face pinched narrower than the skull (front, cheeks and jaw).** In the sheet, the outline stays about as wide as the eye line down to the mouth corners, with round cheek pads, and then curves into a round chin. In the renders, the outline steps sharply inward just below the eyes. The muzzle reads as a narrow block hanging off a wide cranium, about 70 percent of the width at eye level where it should be about 90 percent. Fix: fill the cheeks outward and downward so the width at mouth-corner height is about 0.85 to 0.9 of the width at eye level, and round the jaw into the chin. **Real shape defect.** The sheet's cheek fluff would hide some of it, but the bare-flesh sheet (flesh-front) is plainly full-cheeked too.

3. **Mouth too small, too short, with fang points (front, three-quarter and side).** On the sheet, the mouth is wide open in a D-shaped smile about 1.4 eye widths across. Its corners curl up into the cheeks, the lower lip is a deep U, and the tongue shows. The render mouth is about 1.0 eye width across and opens only about half as far. The two outer points of the upper lip come to sharp tips that read as fangs, which turns the smile into a snarl. Fix: widen the mouth about 40 percent, drop the lower jaw so the opening is about 0.7 eye diameter tall, round off the upper lip corners, and lift the corners up and back into the cheeks. **Real shape defect.**

4. **Lower face too short, nose too close to the eyes (front).** From the eye center to the chin measures about 2.0 eye diameters on the sheet and about 1.6 in the renders. On the sheet the nose sits about 1.1 eye diameters below the eye center. In the renders it sits about 0.75 below. Fix: move the nose and mouth down about a third of an eye diameter, and lengthen the chin and jaw to match. **Real shape defect.**

5. **Snout shaped like a pointed beak (side).** On the sheet, the snout is a short, soft, rounded muzzle ending in a round button nose that sits slightly above the muzzle line. In the render side view, the snout tapers to a point like a beak, the nose tip comes to a sharp upturned point, and there is a hollow under it before the mouth. Fix: blunt the snout so its front reads as a round arc of about half an eye diameter, and add a separate round nose pad at the tip. The nose should not project further forward than it does now. Thicken the upper lip below it so the upper lip hangs down over the mouth opening. **Real shape defect.** Fur would not hide the pointed tip.

6. **Nose ridge between the eyes (front).** A raised ridge runs from between the eyes down to the nose, ending in a triangular wedge. The sheet's bridge between the eyes is flat to slightly hollow, and the nose is a small separate Y-shaped pad with the split lip below it. Fix: flatten the ridge, and shrink the nose to a small rounded pad about 0.3 eye widths across with a short vertical groove under it. **Real shape defect.**

7. **Weak chin, and jaw that runs straight into the throat (side and three-quarter).** On the sheet, a rounded lower jaw and chin sit under the open mouth, and the underside of the jaw curves back to meet a slim throat with a clear notch. In the render, the chin recedes, and the line from the lower lip falls nearly straight into a thick, upright neck, so the head looks like a cap set on a post. Fix: bring the chin forward about a quarter of an eye width, add a horizontal underside to the jaw that runs back about one eye diameter before it meets the throat, and slim the top of the neck just below the head. **Mostly a real shape defect.** The sheet's chest ruff would hide some of the neck thickness, but it would not hide the missing jaw underside.

8. **Eyes set too far out to the sides (three-quarter and top).** In three-quarter, the far eye bulges past the face outline like a bug eye. In the top view, the orange of both eyes shows outside the skull outline at the front. The sheet's eyes face forward and sit inside the head's surface, with the far eye only just touching the outline in three-quarter. Fix: turn each eye about 10 to 15 degrees toward the front and sink it in by about 10 percent of its diameter, so that from above no part of the eye shows past the skull. **Real shape defect.**

Not a shape defect, but it affects likeness: the eye material shows a wide orange ring around a small pupil, which looks startled. The sheet's eye is almost entirely a dark iris with a big catchlight. Even in clay, the material should match: an iris about 85 percent of the visible eye, and a pupil about 60 percent of the iris.

## Construction defects

- **Three-quarter, far eye:** pokes out past the cheek and brow outline, so its edge reads as stuck on rather than set in.
- **Top view, front of the face:** the eyeballs show past the skull surface on both sides, which means the eye spheres stick out laterally through the head.
- **Front view, mouth:** the corner points of the upper lip are sharp spikes, like a cut or clip edge rather than a modeled lip. The lower edge of the mouth cavity looks hard-cut, with a small dark notch at the center of the lower lip and chin, which may be what is left after the chin clip fix.
- **Side view, below and behind the eye:** a shallow crease runs from the back of the mouth toward the bottom of the eye rim, where the rim and cheek pieces meet.
- **Side view, under the snout:** a pinched hollow sits between the nose tip and the upper lip, which reads as a pinch where two pieces were joined.
- **Top view, forehead:** a faint lengthwise groove runs from the base of the horn forward to the brow. The sheet's forehead is a smooth dome.
- **Back of head and nape (cp2-R2-extra, first panel):** the head meets the neck with a soft step and a slight horizontal band rather than one continuous curve. This is minor, but visible against the light.

## Which eye size

**R2 is closest.** From the front, its eye diameter is about 0.28 of the head width at eye level, against about 0.29 on the sheet. R1 falls short, and R3 crowds the head outline and makes the alien read worse. R3 matches the side view's big eye a little better, but none of the three can be judged fairly until the raised rims (defect 1) are removed, because the rims make every option look smaller and set deeper than it is.
