# Xalian Creature System: Hardening Pass (September 2026)

## Context

The creature system design (`xalian-creature-system-redesign.md`) is ratified, all 29 species records are migrated and validated (`docs/species-templates/`, `RATIFIED.json`), all 14 element catalogs plus the neutral pools are consolidated (`docs/ability-catalog/`, about 21,600 element names and 1,100 neutral names), the registry vocabularies ship as data (`docs/species-templates/registries.json`), and a first runnable generator exists (`apps/web/src/gameplay/generator/`, version 0.1.0, built 2026-09-03 so Reclamation could play real creatures). This pass closes the gaps between that shipped state and what the design doc still promises, without reopening any ratified lever on taste.

What this pass found missing, in dependency order:

1. **Documentation debt from the trait-model change (2026-09-02).** The independent-percent trait model was ratified but applied only to the ratifying paragraphs. `trait-model-audit.md` lists 26 findings: the design doc, the handoff doc, the sample record, two walkthroughs and the migration skill still teach the retired `guaranteed`/`rolledCount` model, and two rulings were left open (how rarity reads trait outcomes; what the record stores). The handoff doc also drifted from the design doc on record shape (`traits.keys` versus a flat array) and on the build math.
2. **The seed is not the 128-bit genome the design promises.** `prng.js` folds any seed string into a 32-bit hash before mulberry32, so at most about four billion distinct creatures exist per species and two Scrambler Tokens collide at birthday scale (about 65,000 mints). Fine for ephemeral Reclamation rosters, wrong for anything persisted.
3. **No catalog coverage checker.** The grammar doc requires an automated floor check (at least 6 valid names per reachable instrument by action by medium cell, at least 30 reachable names per species) and says coverage claims come only from that checker. It was never built; the bundler only counts.
4. **No intensity-weighted naming.** The ratified intensity-naming rule (heavy names to heavy rolls) has no data behind it; the generator draws names uniformly.
5. **No simulated-batch report.** Affinity odds, favored-attribute skew, tilt strength, trait expected counts and catalog floors were all ratified as "tuned in the simulated-batch eyeball session before first mint". No tool produces that report.
6. **No derived rarity grade.** Section 10 ratifies a computed PSA-style grade; nothing computes it.
7. **Frackworm is on the catalog branch only.** Its record is validated and its silhouette shipped (PR #102), but its migration was reverted on main on 2026-09-02 and it is not in `RATIFIED.json`.

## Results (2026-09-07)

- **WP1** applied: every audit finding dispositioned in `trait-model-audit.md`; the validator also now accepts the encyclopedia's `pronunciation` block (every ratified record had been failing `enc.extra` since that field shipped).
- **WP2** applied: `scripts/checkCatalogCoverage.js` and `docs/ability-catalog/COVERAGE.md`. 972 distinct reachable cells across the roster, zero thin cells (thinnest 19 names), every species between about 1,900 and 4,600 reachable names against the 30 floor. The floors are safety nets that the catalog clears by two orders of magnitude.
- **WP3** applied: generator 0.2.0 (cyrb128 into xoshiro128**, heft-weighted naming, Reclamation save guard). Heft 1 is scarce (552 of 21,447 entries), so the low-intensity target mostly falls through to heft 2; measured mean heft rises from 1.94 below intensity 34 to 2.13 above 66. Making low rolls feel small is a heft-rule lever (Decision 9), not a weight lever.
- **WP4** applied: `simulateGenerator.js`, `grade.js`, `BATCH-REPORT.md`, `gradeCalibration.json`. Secondary share 25.3 percent, half of them at strength 50 or more; finish counts on their odds; favored attributes sit 17 band-points above unfavored. Trait load is the one number to look at in the tuning session: mean landed count is about 3.7 roster-wide, a quarter of creatures carry five or more traits and only 18 percent carry two or fewer, because the migrated pools were authored with expected counts near four where the retired model capped at three. Two tilts are visible at N=200 (Kosanos pack-bonded 20 authored, 7 observed; Tetrahive slippery 45 authored, 57 observed).
- **WP5 withdrawn.** The dedupe ledger (`DEDUPE-LEDGER-2026-09-07.md`) found 470 names sitting in more than one cell and resolved each under the one-cell-per-name rule; Nick struck that rule on 2026-09-07 (overlap is fine wherever each placement is logically sound) and none of the removals were kept. What stayed: the bundler now strips any parenthetical, drops split-note tails and slash shorthand (garbage such as `Toxic Blast (same flag)` and `see below)` had been shipping as ability names), and the rock and ice shorthand tokens were expanded into real names. The ledger is kept as reference for names that sit in a cell their definition does not support (Avalanche in ice/burst, Deluge and Geyser in water/burst, Torrent in water/lash, Nimbus and Aureole in light/ward, Streak in light/lash, the Blaze family in light).
