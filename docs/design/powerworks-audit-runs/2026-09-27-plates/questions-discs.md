# Questions

Each screen is a moment in one game. The game is played by choosing an action for each of your four creatures (the four at the bottom), then watching the round play out against the machines (at the top). Answer each question from what you can see on that screen (and earlier screens). Keep each answer to one to three sentences, and end each with (confidence 1 to 5).

## Screen 01 (screen-01.png): the scorpion-like creature on the right has been selected.
1a. What are the things that appeared above the creatures, and whose are they?
1b. For each of them, say what that choice would do this round.
1c. Which of them would you pick right now, and why?
1d. Some of them carry a short word besides their name ("once", "rests 1"). What does each of those words tell you?
1e. What does "no effect" mean on "Ground Anchor"?
1f. List everything on this screen you do not understand or had to guess at.

## Screen 02 (screen-02.png): the horse-like creature is selected, and the pointer is resting on its choice "Water Sweep".
2a. What would "Water Sweep" do if chosen: to which machines, and how much?
2b. What does the small hourglass mark on "Crushing Kick" tell you?
2c. Compare "Water Sweep" with "Emergency Water Cannon". Which is better right now, and why?
2d. List everything on this screen you do not understand or had to guess at.

## Screen 03 (screen-03.png): a later fight; the bird is selected.
3a. Can the bird use every one of its choices right now? If not, which one, and why not?
3b. Which choice would you pick for the bird right now, and why?
3c. List everything on this screen you do not understand or had to guess at.

## Screen 04 (screen-04.png): the same game on a phone; the bird is selected.
4a. For each of the bird's choices, say what it would do this round.
4b. List everything on this screen you do not understand or had to guess at.

## At the end
5a. Do the choices differ in color or outline? If so, what do you think that means?
5b. Overall, what was hardest to understand about the creatures' choices?
