There are no W, CT or M grades. Every P falls in one of three places:
- 5a, for all six readers: no one tied the outline colors to element or kind of move.

- 2a for p1-r1: "stops 2" read as a delay, not damage prevented.

## What differs between the designs

The only question that separates the designs is 2b. All four plate readers read the written "lands next round / rests 2" correctly; on the discs, the hourglass left both readers guessing "delayed or winding up", and one thought the greyed disc might not be usable yet. On 2a, p3's "stops 2 dmg" led both readers to damage being prevented, while one p1 reader took "stops 2" as a delay. p3 readers still asked whose damage it protects, and "across 2" still left one unsure whether it means 8 each or 8 split. Question 5a was equally weak everywhere: all six readers took gold to mean the once-only move, but none linked blue or steel outlines to element or kind of move (blue was read as the hover highlight). The same unexplained words ("stops", "STRONG", "next hit", "no effect", which machine is targeted) top the confusion lists in all three packets.

## Confusions named by both readers in every packet

- Which machine each move targets, and how the target is picked.
- What "stops N" stops, and for whom.
- What "next hit 7" means.
- What Ground Anchor does, and why "no effect".
- "STRONG" on a machine.
- "Charging" and "Slowed 2", and the hourglass on the Guardian's plate.
- Why the bird's pecks do only 1 damage.
