## Screen 01: Graviclaw (the scorpion) is selected, first round of the first fight.

- 1b. Gravity Pincer: 8 damage to Crawler 1. Gravity Draw: 3 damage to Crawler 1 (it also pulls, which breaks a charge). Ground Anchor: does nothing useful this round (it protects Graviclaw, which needs nothing right now). Slashing Pinch: 7 damage to Crawler 1.

- 1d. "once": it can be used only once per fight (encounter). "rests 1": after using it, it cannot be used for 1 round (a one-round cooldown). In packet p2, "rests 1" shows only under the focused choice; accept any correct reading of whatever the reader saw.
- 1e. Using it now would do nothing useful (it is worth nothing this round). Full credit does not require the reason; a correct reason is that it protects Graviclaw itself and Graviclaw needs no protection right now.

## Screen 02: Hippochamp (the horse) is selected; the pointer rests on Water Sweep.
- 2a. Half credit: both crawlers, with 8 each, or without the prevented damage. Wrong: one machine only.
- 2b. Packet p1 ("lands next round", "rests 2"): it charges this round and its hit lands next round; afterwards it cannot be used for 2 rounds. Packet p2 (hourglass): it charges this round and lands next round (a delayed hit). Reading the hourglass as a cooldown or waiting time before it can be used is wrong.
- 2c. Water Sweep is better now: 8 damage in total across two machines plus about 2 damage prevented, against the Cannon's 8 on one machine; and the Cannon can be used only once per fight, so it is worth saving. Accept the Cannon only with a sound reason (for example, concentrating 8 on Crawler 1). Wrong: a comparison that misreads either number.
