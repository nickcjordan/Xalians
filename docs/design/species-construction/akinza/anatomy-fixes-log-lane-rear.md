## 6. Tail base

- **Base:** assembled-3199 (`recipe-neck-v3.json`). **Output:** assembled-3223, recipe `recipe-tailbase-v1.json` (chain ... P-neck3, P-tailbase1; step `art/species-construction/tailbase_v1_post.py` with `tailbase_v1_field.py`, spec `art/species-construction/specs/tailbase-v1.json`). Earlier builds of the same step, superseded and moved to the scratch archive: assembled-3220 (tail-to-tail fillet .02) and assembled-3221 (committed in 552865b2; it lowered the crotch's arch apex by .022 and left a pocket there, see below).
- **Cause, measured:** beyond the root every tail is still the exact round-cone sweep of tails v7 (skin radius equals the sweep radius to 1e-4 from arc length .14 on), so the shafts were never the problem. At the root, tails v7 joined the tails to each other with a cubic smooth union (tailUnionK .035), which swells past both tubes: that is the round mounds on top of the root and the collar round the lowest tail. The lowest tail (tail 2) starts on the midline at the bottom of the root and runs back and out over the top of the +x buttock; against the body under it its surface sat up to .010 inside the buttock at arc length .09 to .14, and pelvis v1/v2's tail guard froze that skin, so the buttock never formed round it (the channel and its creases). The hanging tail is the same tail 2. A second cause, found while building: tail 2's root tube starts lower than the crotch's underside (tube bottom z -.2395, arch apex z -.228); tails v7 hid that with its keepY band, so any rebuild of the root has to keep that tube and its fillet off the arch.
- **What changed:** (1) the body under the tails is rebuilt: every cell within .035 of an old or new tail, except the crotch's arch, is refilled by a biharmonic fill of the skin's field from its surroundings (a harmonic fill was tried first and sank a flat disc with a ring round its edge into the convex rump, variants a to c). Air round a tail is given its distance to the body, not to the tail, so the fill cannot pull a surface into the air. (2) The three tails are joined to each other with a .03 circular fillet and to the body with a .045 circular fillet (both concave by construction: no swelling past either surface). The body fillet eases to half its radius above z -.09, so the top tail's flare does not raise the back above the root, and falls to zero at the crotch's arch. (3) Tail 2 is offset .015 back and toward the midline over arc length .03 to .11, then swung 5 degrees outward (+x, about the y axis through its s .14 point, ramped in over s .14 to .30); its free shaft is carried rigidly, so it keeps its exact sweep; the start of its centreline inside the body is lifted .02 so the tube stays inside, above the arch. (4) A small blur of the body field under tail 1 on the +x buttock, and a tangential relax at the two crotches between tails and at the top of the cleft, where the old fused skin otherwise folded (20 folded faces in variant c, 0 now).
- **Numbers:** tail 2 clearance from the body under it (centreline distance to the body minus radius) at arc length .09 / .115 / .14 / .16: before -.0004 / -.0097 / -.013 / -.018 (sunk into the buttock; harmonic body, variant b2, old path); after +.009 at s .08, +.022 at .11, +.026 at .13, +.029 at .15.
