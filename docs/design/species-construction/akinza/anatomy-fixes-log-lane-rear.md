## 6. Tail base

- **Base:** assembled-3199 (`recipe-neck-v3.json`). **Output:** assembled-3221, recipe `recipe-tailbase-v1.json` (chain ... - **Cause, measured:** beyond the root every tail is still the exact round-cone sweep of tails v7 (skin radius equals the sweep radius to 1e-4 from arc length .14 on), so the shafts were never the problem. At the root, tails v7 joined the tails to each other with a cubic smooth union (tailUnionK .035), which swells past both tubes: that is the round mounds on top of the root and the collar round the lowest tail. The lowest tail (tail 2) starts on the midline at the bottom of the root and runs back and out over the top of the +x buttock; against the body under it (see below) its surface sat up to .010 inside the buttock at arc length .09 to .14, and pelvis v1/v2's tail guard froze that skin, so the buttock never formed round it (the channel and its creases). The hanging tail is the same tail 2.
- **What changed:** (1) the body under the tails is rebuilt: every cell within .035 of an old or new tail is refilled by a biharmonic fill of the skin's field from its surroundings (a harmonic fill was tried first and sank a flat disc with a ring round its edge into the convex rump, variants a to c). Air round a tail is given its distance to the body, not to the tail, so the fill cannot pull a surface out into the air. (2) The three tails are joined to each other with a .03 circular fillet and to the body with a .045 circular fillet (both concave by construction: no swelling past either surface), the body fillet easing to half its radius above z -.09 so the top tail's flare does not raise the back above the root. (3) Tail 2 is offset .015 back and toward the midline over arc length .03 to .11, then swung 5 degrees outward (+x, about the y axis through its s .14 point, ramped in over s .14 to .30); its free shaft is carried rigidly, so it keeps its exact sweep. (4) Two small blurs of the body field under the tails (the top of the cleft beside tail 2's root on -x; the +x buttock under tail 1), and a tangential relax at the two crotches between tails, where the old fused skin otherwise folded (20 folded faces in variant c, 0 now).
