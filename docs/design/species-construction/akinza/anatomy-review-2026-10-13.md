# Akinza anatomy review, assembled-3195 (2026-10-13)

Independent review of the bare clay model at assembled-3195 (recipe `recipe-pelvis-v2.json`). The model was not changed. The rule applied: this is the shaved animal, so bare anatomy is judged and fur is ignored. Approved items were not re-judged (eyes, nose, mouth and muzzle, ear shape, round head, body B widths, foot width, three separate tails from one base). Where a finding touches an approved item, it is about the join or the depth, not the approved shape, and it says so.

Images are in `untracked/species-construction/akinza/surface/anatomy-review/` (untracked, one view per image, list at the end). Positions below are given as image name plus a place in that image (for example "upper left", "center, two thirds down"). Image left and right are as seen on screen. In front views, image right is the figure's left (+x). In back views, image right is the figure's right (-x). Side-left views look at the figure's left side (+x), with the face on image left.

## Notes by region

**Head and neck.** Face, eyes, muzzle, ears and head shape are approved and were not judged. Everything wrong here is the neck and the joins (findings 2 and 10). The mesh is symmetric across the head and neck (mirror distance under .004 everywhere outside the tails and rump).

**Shoulders and arms.** Findings 3 and 5. Using the joints recorded in the body (shoulder z .30, elbow .10, wrist -.09, fingertip -.21), the segment lengths are fine: upper arm .21, forearm .21, hand .14. The problem is mass, not length.

**Hands.** Finding 8. The wrist does narrow (to .063), so the wrist joint itself is not the problem.

**Torso.** Finding 1. Shoulder-to-hip widths are body B and were not judged. The depth profile and the flat front are a separate issue from those widths.

**Hips and legs.** Findings 9 and 12 cover the hips. The thigh to knee taper reads well in the front views. The legs are straight in a wide A stance, with the knee on the hip-to-ankle line, which is fine as a rig rest pose.

**Knees.** Finding 11.

**Ankles and feet.** Findings 4, 7 and 13. Toe lumps and claw placement on the hind feet read well from the front and three-quarter views.

**Tail base and tails.** Finding 6. The tail shafts themselves are clean tapered tubes. Shadow bands on the tails in the shaded side views are cast shadows, not geometry (checked in the `ns-` no-shadow renders).

**Rig pipeline (aside, not a model fault).** `rig_core.derive_joints` puts `neck_base` at z .185 and `clavicle` at z .135 on this body, both below the shoulder joints (z .30). The derived neck bone therefore starts in the upper chest. Any rig built from the derived joints will bend the chest when the head nods. The arm joints come from the body record and are correct.

## Method

- Renders: Cycles CPU, 32 samples, denoised, with the fixed three-point rig from `render_surface.py` (`setup_world`, `make_rig`, `place`). The skin is in the diagnosis clay material (gray .35, roughness .7, smooth shading), from `assembled-3195/akinza.blend`. The script is a copy of `head-redesign/pelvis-v1/work/clayviews.py` that reads its camera list from a JSON file and can turn shadows off. Images prefixed `ns-` are rendered with shadows off, which separates surface form from cast shadows. Orthographic cameras.
- Measures: the skin vertices (547,455) were dumped and cut into sections every .01 to .03 in z (arm and leg widths and depths, trunk midline front and back, neck profile), plus a mirror test (each vertex mirrored in x against the nearest vertex, tails and rump behind y .06 excluded). The only asymmetry above .004 is on the rump beside the tail root.
- Deformation: `loop_tools.py posed assembled-3195 --pose art/species-construction/rig/akinza-sheet-pose.json`, output to `anatomy-review/posed/` (sheet pose, hands on hips), re-rendered in clay in `posed/clay/`. The weights are the automatic distance-falloff weights, so the collapse seen there is a worst case, not a measure of a painted rig.
