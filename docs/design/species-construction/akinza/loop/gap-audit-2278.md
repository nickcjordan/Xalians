Target: the preferred first sheet (packet `m01`, `evidence/identity-run-0001.png`) with the accepted crops r01 (eyes), r02 (paws), r03 (tail and back study) and r04 (head and coat masses). **How the numbers were taken.** Equal-height comparisons were made by cropping each sheet view out of m01 (figure 53 to 651 px, H = 598 px) and the matching full render (figure 51 to 748 px, H = 697 px), scaling the render by 598/697 and enlarging both crops 2x or 3x side by side (front, left, back, front-left, and a 6x crop of the profile eye). Widths are fractions of figure height (H), read at a height fraction from the top of the fan (0) to the floor (1). Forepaw widths were read row by row from the render mask. Values marked as estimates are read by eye from those crops. Numbers from `measured.json` and `posed/posed-fit.json` are copied, not re-judged.

**What changed since gap-audit-0458.** The fan's back is no longer a crumpled-paper bowl: each wing is now covered by rows of pointed locks flowing outward and down from a crown tuft, the pale continuous lower rim is gone, and no smooth area on the wings is larger than one lock. That was 0458's rank 1, and it is now a smaller gap (rank 6 below). The profile eye is now about as tall as the sheet's (about 0.3 of head height in both, estimate), so the gap there is its shape and iris, not its size. The side upper chest depth now passes (R06.11, 0.128 / 0.125), so the trunk profile gap has moved to the waist. Unchanged since 0458: the front of the fan, the ear seen from the side, the forepaws, the hind paws, the legs, the face from the front and the bare body.

## 1. First glance

From the front, the fan still reads as a crown of stiff leaves and thorns: flat pointed slabs and serrated chevrons around white faceted blades in the cups, a dark slot behind the image-right tuft, and a row of small thorn spikes standing up along the top edge and across the crown. The sheet shows one soft shaggy mass with fluffy pale inner tufts. The face is a flat-topped block with two round disc eyes and small pupils dead centre, so it stares; in three-quarter view the pupils shrink to dots under a pinched brow and the creature looks startled. From the side, the head is a crumpled column of shard slabs with no pale cup showing, and the eye is a wedge that tapers to a point below, with a thin sliver of iris on its front edge. The sheet's profile is a round fur mop with a large pale ear cup and a clean round eye looking forward. From behind, the fan now reads close to the sheet's two wings of locks; the head in its centre is a smooth shield framed by a crease. Below the neck the model is still a bare smooth figure: a plank of a trunk in profile, square shoulders with deltoid and triceps relief and a ledge across the shoulder blades, an hourglass from the front, small closed fists with needle claws, and flat bead-toed slipper feet. The sheet's creature is slim, lithe and furred all over. The silhouette matches; the head's surfaces and the body's build do not.

## 4. Proposed new criteria

Each one fails on assembled-2278.

- **R02 (visual):** in the true side view (`m02 left` at full figure scale), the eye's visible outline is a closed oval whose width at mid height is at least half its height and does not taper to a point; the iris fills at least the front third of it.
- **R02 (visual):** in `m02 front-left`, the iris is at least half the eye's width and offset toward the gaze, and no brow ridge pinches the upper lid.
- **R03 (visual, repeated from 0458, not yet adopted):** in `m02 left` and `m04 head-side`, the pale inner cup is visible from the side over at least a tenth of the ear's area, and the ear reads as a rounded shaggy mass, not a column of slabs.
- **R01 (visual):** in `m04 head-front` and `m02 front`, nothing rises above the crown outline between the ear roots taller than 0.01H except soft lock tips; no thorn or spike row.
- **R04 (visual):** in `m04 head-back`, no crease, ledge or line traces the head dome outline, and the lower edge of each wing ends in lock-sized points, not a fringe of needle tips.
- **R07 (visual):** in `m07 forepaw-front`, four separate digit lobes each end in their own curved claw, spread across the paw's width, not in a single column on one corner.
- **R09 (visual, repeated from 0458):** in `m09 hindpaw-profile`, the top line rises from the toes to the ankle as a dome, with no vertical face at the heel.

## 5. Structural versus local

The rear rebuild worked: the fan's back went from a crumpled bowl to wings of locks in one pass without losing the outline. The same move is now the largest gap on the front.

**Structural:**
- **Fan front and ear side (ranks 1 and 2).** Rebuild the front of the fan and each ear cup the way the rear was rebuilt: soft pointed lock masses inside the fitted envelope, with the cup opening forward and outward so its pale fluffy tuft shows from the side. No slabs, blades, chevrons, thorns or dark slots. Keep the outline; it is right.
- **Eye set in profile (rank 3).** Reshape the socket so the eyeball's visible outline in profile is a full oval with the iris forward, while the brow and nose still stand in front of it (R02.6).
- **Trunk waist in profile (rank 4, with 16).** Thin the waist front to back and deepen the lumbar sway, keeping the chest depth and the front outline, which both pass.
- **Smaller structural items:** the dome seam from behind (rank 6), the forepaw digits and claws (rank 7), crown height and jaw section (rank 9), the hind paw instep, heel and toe arch (rank 11), and the rest stance (rank 17).

**Surface:** the body coat masses and one lock idiom (rank 5, after the fan front rebuild); the athletic relief, shoulder ledge and dimples (rank 8); iris size, gaze and lid weight from the front (rank 10); the knee groove and shin dip (rank 12); the upper arm slab (rank 13); the cheek plates and crust (rank 14); and the neck kink (rank 15).

The scores for R01 and R02 (8.1 and 8.3) still run well ahead of how the head reads, because their passing criteria are outline metrics and close-ups. The front of the fan, the ear from the side and the eye from the side carry most of the remaining identity loss, and none of them shows up in an outline metric.
