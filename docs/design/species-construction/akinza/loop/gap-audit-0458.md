# Akinza gap audit, assembled-0458

Independent gap audit, 2026-10-01. Target: the preferred first sheet (packet `m01`, `evidence/identity-run-0001.png`) with the accepted crops r01 (eyes), r02 (paws) and r04 (head and coat masses). Model: packet `untracked/species-construction/akinza/loop/packets/assembled-0458` (m02 to m11, `posed/`) and the full renders in `untracked/species-construction/akinza/assembled-0458/render/`. R10 Tails and R11 Tail root and pelvis are on hold by Nick's direction and are not ranked; where a tail hides a body part, the gap below says so.

**How the numbers were taken.** The m11 overlay and `posed/posed-fit.png` were split back into masks (grey plus blue is the model, grey plus orange plus the red outline is the sheet). Both figures are 497 to 499 px tall in m11, so widths and heights are fractions of figure height (H), read at the same height fraction from the top of the fan (0) to the floor (1). The equal-height visual comparisons were made by cropping each view out of m01 and m02 and scaling both to 900 px of figure height. Where a value is read by eye from those crops it is marked as an estimate. Numbers from `measured.json` are copied, not re-judged.

**What changed since gap-audit-0226.** Several of the old ranked gaps are now closed and are not repeated: a head dome now shows in the back view (R01.6 back width at .18 is 1.03x, was 1.43x), the ankles taper (front ankle 0.033 / 0.033, was 0.075 / 0.039), the shin and ankle-to-thigh ratios pass, the side head depth at .22 passes (1.05x), the chest now stands ahead of the chin (R06.8 passes), the hind paws are a broad fan with an underside pad set, and a pale inner tuft sits in each ear cup. The fan outline matches the sheet within a few percent at every column, in front and back, including the thickness near the tips (back view at 95 percent of the half-span: 0.056 / 0.062 and 0.056 / 0.066). What is left is mostly the inside of that outline, the side view of the trunk and head, and the paws at the end of the arms.

## 1. First glance

From the front, the fan reads as a wreath of crumpled paper or a pile of thorny leaves: hard-edged shards, serrated sawtooth spikes and flat planes, with dark slots behind the pale inner tufts, where the sheet shows one soft, thick, shaggy mass. The face below it is a broad block with a flat forehead running straight across between the ear roots, and two round discs for eyes, each with a small centred pupil, so it stares. From the side, the head is a crumpled column of shards with a row of teeth-like spikes down its back edge, and the eye is a narrow slanted slit pinched by a cheek ridge, so the creature looks angry or squinting. The sheet's profile is a round fur mop with a large pale ear cup and a huge round eye. Below the neck the model is a smooth bare vinyl figure: a straight plank of a trunk in profile, square shoulders with a ledge from behind, small closed fists for forepaws, and flat slipper hind paws. The sheet's creature is a soft, furred, slender animal with a big gentle face and a fur-mop head. The model's silhouette is close to the sheet. Its surfaces are not.

## 4. Proposed new criteria

Each one fails on assembled-0458.

- **R02 (measured or estimated):** in the true side view (`m02 left`), visible eye height over head height (crown to chin, ears excluded) at least 0.25 (sheet about 0.33; model about 0.15).
- **R02 (visual):** judge the eye's shape and gaze from `m02 front` at full figure scale, not only from the `m05` close-up. The eye height over width is at least 1.35 there, and the iris is offset from centre.
- **R03 (visual):** in `m04 head-side` and `m02 left`, the pale inner cup is visible from the side, and no row of small regular spikes runs along any fan edge.
- **R04 (visual):** in `m02 back`, no continuous rim runs along the fan's lower edge, and no wing ends in a flat cut plane.
- **R06 (measured):** side chest depth at .36 at least 0.9x the sheet's 0.168H (model 0.131H).
- **R07 (measured):** front forepaw width at least 0.85x the sheet's paw width at equal figure height, read from a posed front render with the paw on the hip (sheet about 0.055H; model about 0.033H).
- **R09 (visual):** in `m09 hindpaw-profile`, the top line rises from the toes to the ankle as a dome, with no vertical face at the heel.

## 5. Structural versus local

The loop has done what it set out to do with outlines. The fan, neck, shoulders, ankles and head base now sit on the sheet's silhouette. The gaps that remain split into one construction problem, three structural problems and a list of surface work.

**Structural:**
- **Fan construction (ranks 1, 2, 6).** This is the largest remaining gap, and it cannot be fixed by sculpting the current shell. The fan has to be rebuilt as a volume of soft pointed lock masses inside the fitted envelope. Each ear cup opens forward and outward so it shows from the side, and is filled with a fur tuft rather than blades. There are no stuck-on shards, sawteeth or end planes. Keep the outline as the target; it is right.
- **Eye set in profile (rank 3).** Bring the eyeball forward and remove the cheek plate that wraps over it, so the eye shows large in the side view while the brow and nose still stand in front of it (R02.6).
- **Trunk side section (rank 5, with 15).** Deepen the rib cage forward and pinch the waist in profile, keeping the front outline, which already passes.
- **Forepaws (rank 7).** A larger open paw with separate digits and claws from the tips, about 1.5x the wrist width.
- **Smaller structural items:** crown height and jaw section (rank 8), the hind paw instep and heel (rank 11), and the rest stance (rank 16).

**Surface:** the body coat masses and a single lock idiom (rank 4, after the fan rebuild); iris size, gaze and lid weight (rank 9); the shoulder ledge, collar ring and corner knobs (rank 10); the double calf swell and knee knob (rank 12); the upper arm facet and box shoulder cap (rank 13); and the cheek crust and plate (rank 14).

The pattern to break this time is the reverse of 0226. The outline criteria now pass across the board, so the region scores for the head and fan (8.1, 8.3, 6.4) run well ahead of how the model reads. The fan and the side view of the face carry most of the remaining identity loss, and neither shows up in an outline metric.
