## 0. What the re-read changed

The method entry asked for the stations to be re-read from `sheet.json` because the old spec's side depth (peak .129) and the gap audit (.168 at .36) disagreed by 30 percent. The re-read settles it, and it moves the target:

1. **The audit's .168 is the akimbo upper arm, not the chest.** At .32 to .38 the left-view run in `sheet.json` ends at +.043, +.057, +.070, +.079. Those ends lie on the back edge of the upper arm, which runs from the shoulder (+.007, .303) back to the elbow (+.086, .405) (panel A, red crosses; checked on a 6x contrast crop). The trunk's own back edge is hidden there; it shows again in the white gap below the arm, at +.017 at .385. The sheet's trunk depth at .32 to .38 is therefore about .117 to .134, not .126 to .176 (panel G).
2. **The model's trunk already follows the sheet's side profile closely.** Sectioned without the arms, `body-0448` has front -.0822, -.0920, -.0978 at .32, .34, .36 against the sheet's -.082, -.092, -.097, and a back edge of +.0012 and -.0048 at .42 and .44 against the sheet's -.005 and -.013. The trunk is not a plank. It is .008 too deep at the waist (.0927 against .082) and .004 to .006 shallow over the upper back.
3. **The plank in `m02 left` is the hanging arm.** With the arms down, the arm's back edge sits at +.012, +.014, +.008 at .42, .44, .46, which is .011 to .019 behind the trunk's back (panel B, red). It fills the lumbar hollow in the silhouette. R06.6 and R06.7 read that run, so they measure the arm, not the waist (section 5, friction 1).
4. **What still reads as a tube is the cross section.** The native sections fit a superellipse front exponent of 1.7 (a rounded keel) and a back exponent of 1.6 to 1.8 (a ridge) from .36 to .40, with a shelf at |x| about .06 on the back at .36 (panel F). From the front that light falls as a cylinder, with no chest plane and no rib cage volume. This is the R06.4 gap, and it is the main thing the loft changes.

So the loft has three jobs: change the section shape over the rib cage and chest (structural), take .007 out of the waist depth and add .004 to .006 to the upper back (structural, small), and replace the surface between .30 and .56 with a clean loft so the old band at .48 to .50 goes (local, but it falls out of the rebuild). It holds the front-view flank widths, which round 10 already fitted.

### How to build it

`author_trunk_sections_field.py --body <shape.glb> --fairing <fairing.json> --spec trunk-sections.json --out <dir>`, inserted after B-20 (`body-0369`) and before B-21. Sections measured on `body-0369` and `body-0426` equal `body-0448` within .002 at every trunk row, so the "now" column above is also the loft's input. B-23 (the arm rebuild) runs after it and re-fillets the arm root; acceptance is read on the final assembly.

1. **Sections.** At each z row (voxel .0025), a two-half superellipse in the (x, side) plane: widest line at `d_w = front + split (back - front)`; front half |x / a|^nF + |(d - d_w) / (d_w - front)|^nF = 1, back half the same with `back - d_w` and nB; a = halfWidth. Every parameter is interpolated over y with a monotone cubic (PCHIP), never per-slice linear maps.
2. **Field.** The loft's signed distance is blended into the native field: SDF = (1 - w) SDF_native + w SDF_loft, then one meshing.
3. **Weight** w = w_y x w_lat x w_arm x w_tail:
   - w_y: smoothstep from 0 at .26 to 1 at .32; 1 to .50; smoothstep from 1 at .50 to 0 at .58 (the hip joints are at .577, so the R08 thigh and the crotch are untouched; R05 owns everything above .26).
   - w_lat: for rows up to .36, 1 for |x| up to .06 (fit, front view) and smoothstep to 0 at .10, so the shoulders and arm root keep the native field; from .40 down, 1 over the whole trunk; the band edges move linearly between .36 and .40.
   - w_arm: 0 within r + .01 world of the shoulder, elbow and wrist polyline of each arm (`fairing.json` `armJoints`, mirrored; r is the `upperRod` radius in `arm-field.json`, about .04), smoothstep to 1 at r + .07.
   - w_tail: 0 within .09 world of the first point of each of the three `tailControls` entries (xyz is the first three numbers; the roots sit at world z -.09 to -.14, y .08 to .09), smoothstep to 1 at .14.
   - Fades: every ramp spans at least ten times the relief change it carries. The largest change (.0072 fit, .0134 world, at .44) sits inside full weight; the top ramp carries at most .0056 fit (.0104 world) over .06 fit (.11 world).
4. **Rows .28 to .34** (arm root): the half width is not authored; the loft section there uses a chest-core half width of .085, .095, .100, .104 at .28, .30, .32, .34 so that its surface inside the lateral band lies near the native, and w_lat keeps it off the shoulders.
5. **Rows .52 to .56** (tail root and thigh roots): fit nF, nB and split to the native section outside the tail-root mask by least squares, and check that front, half width and buttock-strip back hold within .002.
6. **Record.** Write `trunkSections` into `fairing.json`: per station the target and achieved front, back, depth and half width, the fitted native exponents, and the weight; plus the deviation check in section 3.

## 4. What it must not look like

From the history card:

- **Round 9, reverted (R06 4 to 3.5, tail dent R10 4.2 to 3.3).** Three field passes driven by ray-cast rows. Geometric reasons: the y pass moved the back surface at .48 to .56 across the whole trunk width, tail roots included, which dented the top tail where it leaves the body; and deepening the lumbar hollow failed R06.2, whose sheet value is the depth at .40, not the waist (friction 2). Here: w_tail keeps the tail roots native, w_y is 0 below .58, and R06.2 is handled before the build (section 5).
- **Round 10, kept.** The flank-only x pass fitted the front-view flank to the sheet's at .42 to .56 and removed the pelvis shelf. The loft must reproduce those widths within .002 and must not bring the shelf back.
- **Round 15, reverted (verdict same).** A dorsal swell of +.008 at .32 to .36, the chest front .003 forward, a hip flare of +.0065 and a z blur. The deltas pointed the right way, but the critic saw nothing because what reads wrong is elsewhere: the hanging arm covers the waist in `m02 left`, the section stayed a keel at the front and a ridge at the back, and resampling the old surface kept its band. This spec changes the section exponents and rebuilds the surface, and does not count on edge deltas alone.

From the method entry and the earlier rounds:

- **Belt lines** (round 3: four new bands): per-slice linear y maps whose scale changes from slice to slice leave slope breaks. The loft interpolates its parameters with PCHIP and replaces the surface, and every weight ramp is at least ten times longer than its relief change.
- **A lathe-turned vase:** one exponent all round, split .5, or exponents that change faster than about .1 per .02 make the trunk a turned column. Keep the front and back halves separate (split .55 to .59, front and back exponents different), as the table gives.
- **A crater, notch or shelf at the armpit, deltoid or thigh root:** a mask boundary inside a region the loft changes. The arm and tail masks fade over .05 to .06 world, and the half widths next to the arm are held.
- **A pigeon breast or a box chest:** the midline front more than .005 ahead of now anywhere, or a front exponent above 2.5. The chest plane comes from the section's fullness at the front corners, not from the midline.
- **A wasp waist:** depth under .080 or half width under .062 at .44.
- **A dorsal hump:** the back edge above +.040 at .32 to .36.
- **Hips off the posed front band:** do not widen the trunk to chase the posed front drawing, which places the trunk .023 further to the character's right than its back drawing (friction 4).
