## 3. Cross-sections and surface

- **Sections are near round below the knee.** The sheet's left view shows both legs, staggered, overlapping down to .88. Below .88 they separate, and each ankle measures .036 deep at .88, .033 and .035 at .90, and .036 and .037 at .92 (panel C, blue and red bars), against a front width of .034 to .036. Above .88 one leg's depth was estimated as the two-leg run minus the stagger, which grows evenly from about .005 at .74 to .040 at .88: .080 at .74, .072 at .80, .052 at .84, .043 at .86 (about plus or minus .008). Depth over front width: about 1.2 at the knee, 1.0 at the calf, 0.85 to 1.0 at the shin, 1.0 to 1.1 at the ankle.
- **Correction to the gap audit.** Its "ankle front width over side depth: model 1.2, sheet 0.5" read the sheet's side run as one leg. It is two legs. The single sheet ankle is about 1.0 (round), so do not build a flat-sided, deep ankle; build a slim round one with a tendon behind. The model's ankle (1.3) is still wider than deep and must change.
- **Convex and concave.** Lateral edge (front and back views): one convex curve over the thigh, a gentle concave turn above the knee, then one straight or faintly convex taper to the ankle. Medial edge: straight down the thigh taper, one convex swell (the calf) from .77 to .87, then straight and vertical to the ankle. Profile: front edge convex at the knee plane, straight down the shin; back edge convex over the calf, concave into the tendon from .84 to .90, then convex again into the heel.
- **Surface.** Smooth short fur over soft muscle: no ridges, facets, creases or tendon cords standing out, no fur locks or tufts on the leg. Field blends only, no Boolean seams, so the calf and kneecap read as soft masses inside one skin.

## 4. What it must not look like

There is no history card for R08 under this checklist. The baseline critique (assembled-0226) and the gap audit name these looks; the geometric reason for each:

- **Column leg, ankle as wide as the calf** (R08.4, R08.9 fail): the analytic ankle capsule's half-width .0685 and the native shin left at .082 to .088. The leg holds .070 to .074 from .88 to .94, so nothing reads as an ankle.
- **Wavy outline, double bulge** (R08.1 fail): the calf swell sits on the lateral edge at .80 to .83, then the outline steps out again at .90 to .91 where the native shin morphs into the wider capsule. Fix: one medial swell, and a capsule narrower than the native leg above it.
- **Knee as a knob or bump** (R08.2 partial): the thigh stays a cylinder to .73 (medial .056 against .033) and then pinches to .074, with the lateral calf bulge below it, so the knee is a knob between two masses. Fix: the medial thigh taper from .66 to .76, which makes the knee a smooth waist.
- **Knee as a groove** (overcorrection to avoid): do not carve a crease above or below the kneecap to make it read. The knee waist is .061 wide and one smooth curve.
- **Rabbit thigh** (overcorrection to avoid, R08.3 passes today): slimming the shin while widening or swelling the thigh. Keep the thigh at .110 plus or minus .006 at .66 and do not add depth behind it.
- **Needle shin** (overcorrection to avoid, I06): the calf peak must stay at least .068 wide at .80 to .82, and the thigh full. The sheet's leg is slender below the calf, not thin overall.
- **Calf too high.** The critique's suggested fix put the calf peak near .74. The sheet's calf peaks at .81 in both the front and back views; .74 is the lower thigh.

Friction for the orchestrator:

1. **R08.10 and R08.11 cannot pass for any single 3D leg.** The sheet's front and back figures disagree on where the right leg is: relative to their fan centers, the front leg sits .036 further out at .66, .029 at .76 and .027 at .91 (the neck is also offset .011 between the two drawings). Mirroring the sheet's back figure onto its front scores only thigh .735 and shin .356, so the two views are not one object. A simulation that replaced the posed model's leg with the sheet's own widths at the best compromise placement gave a mean of thigh .834 to .856 and shin .693 to .712 (left view as now), at every compromise from front-exact to back-exact. Slimming the leg without placing it exactly even lowers the front shin IoU (.554 to .543), because a .035 ankle off by .011 overlaps less than a wide one. The same spec leg, aligned by one horizontal shift per view (-.016 front, -.018 back), scores thigh .902 and shin .854. Proposed smallest change: compute the posed thigh and shin bands after a best horizontal shift of the leg within plus or minus .02 per view, so they measure leg shape (which the two drawings agree on within .008), and leave placement to a separate stance criterion; or lower the thresholds to .84 (thigh) and .70 (shin). The row criteria R08.5 to R08.8 are unaffected and can all pass.
2. **I06 ("the hind legs stay fuller") against the measured rows.** The measured targets need a lower leg about half as wide as now below .85. This spec reads I06 as the thigh and calf masses, which stay at the sheet's full widths (.110 at .66, .072 at .81). If Nick's request meant a fuller shin and ankle too, R08.5 to R08.8 contradict it and one of them should move.
3. **The gap audit's proposed "ankle width over side depth at most 0.7"** (not adopted in the rubric) rests on the two-leg reading in section 3. If it is ever added, the sheet's single-leg value is about 1.0, so the bound would be about 0.9 to 1.15.
