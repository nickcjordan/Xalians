## 4. What it must not look like

There is no history card for R05 under this checklist. The baseline critique (assembled-0320, unchanged since 0305) and the gap audit name these looks; the geometric cause of each, with its letter in `R05.png`:

- **(a) Lit collar ring at the head base from behind** (R05.4, R05.6; audit rank 1, "the neck rises from a disc"). In `neck-rear` the centerline brightness peaks at y .2315 (132, against 90 in the nape shadow at .222 and 127 just below at .235): an upward-facing lip at world z .465 to .477. In the vertices the back of the stub bulges to +.028 world at z .47, behind both the nape above (+.017 at z .49) and the neck below (+.026 at .45). The heights match `neck_warp.HEAD`'s depth ramp exactly (the warp ran in head-0158 and body-0173; that later heads and bodies carry it is inferred from their geometry, not from a stage record) (factor .66 below z .463, 1.0 at .477, sigma .004): the stub below was compressed toward cy -.0095 and the head above was not, so the transition makes a lip. The width ramp (.74 to 1.0 over z .470 to .500) is the same kind of seam in front.
- **(h) Nape step in profile** (R05.4; audit rank 8). Same cause plus the neck's position: the back edge goes .017 (.22), .021 (.228), .022 (.23 to .25), a .005 step out within .008 of height, because the body's neck sits .007 behind the head's nape.
- **(j) Throat notch** (R05.4). The front of the neck is .008 behind the sheet at .24 to .245 (-.023 against -.031) under a sloping chin underside: the throat reads as a notch under the jaw.
- **(g) Jaw crease, "stem pinch" in front** (R05.4). The silhouette is within .005 of the sheet, but the head stub's width ramp turns the jaw into the neck over .03 world of height, which shades as a crease, and the trumpet web (f) shortens the straight column to about .013 of height (the sheet's is .025). The column must be straight from .228 to .248.
- **(f) Trumpet web at the neck base** (R05.5; audit rank 10, "the arm grows out of a long sloped web"). The neck flares from .245 at a steady slope to .28, .009 to .010 too wide at .25 to .255 and .011 to .013 too narrow at .265 to .27. Likely cause: the seam smoothing (90 iterations over z .395 to .477) rounds the concave neck-base corner into one long curve.
- **(e) Square yoke ramp and (d) corner wall** (R05.5; audit rank 10, "broad flat shoulder plate"). The top line rises at a constant .003 per .01 to d .15 and drops vertically from .30 to .35. In `neck_warp.BODY` the x factor jumps from .70 at z .40 to 1.0 at .41 (sigma .006), right at the shoulder's top rows (the rear half width jumps from .066 at y .268 to .086 at .272), and the cap rows z .30 to .38 are narrowed by a near-constant .78 to .86, which flattens the top and squares the corner.
- **(b) Dark pockets behind the caps and (i) the cap spike in profile** (R05.5, R05.6; audit ranks 10 and 18). The `BODY` depth factor (.71 to .84 at the shoulder rows) acts fully only inside |x| .22 world and is gone by .32 (`windowX`), so the back was pulled in and the caps, at |x| .20 to .28, were not: they stand .03 to .04 behind the back, the pocket forms between, and in profile the cap pokes .004 past the back edge at .30 (+.039 against R06's .032), with a point where it meets the back.
- **(c) Ledge across the back of the shoulders** (R05.6). The back slab's top dips to z .374 at |x| .10 to .12 and rises to .382 over the cap bulge.
- **Overcorrection: bottle shoulders.** The top line must not fall faster than .012 per .01 of d inside d .12, and must stay above y .300 at d .13; otherwise the shoulders read as a wine bottle with no cap.
- **Overcorrection: a thick neck or a ruff.** Do not widen the column past .029 half width at .235 to .245 (R05.1 fails at 1.10), and do not hide the seam under a fur collar; the sheet has none.
