# R05 spec: neck and shoulders

Region R05, rewritten 2026-10-01 against baseline packet `assembled-0458` (head `head-0455`, body `body-0448`) for the method in `methods.md`: assembly join parameters at step J, then a no-cut convex rounding of the yoke with `shape_neck_shoulders_field.py` after B-19. It replaces the 0320 spec, whose neck fix went through the head stub warp and a body shift that a join order cannot reach. Governing reference: the first sheet `evidence/identity-run-0001.png` (m01), front, back and left figures, read through the shared measurement `loop/sheet.json` (outline polylines and station tables, tolerance .002). The other references do not show this region at a usable scale. Annotated image: `specs/R05.png` (A, B, C the sheet's front, back and left figures with the sheet.json outline as red dots, the target neck rows as orange squares and the masses root to tip in green; D the model's `neck-rear` detail with the ring and the cut heights; F the model's `neck-profile` with the sheet edges in red, the model now in blue and the J setting in orange; E the model's back render with the yoke faults and the round block; G and H the profile edges and the neck half width, sheet against now, J at offset .038 and J at the method's .020; I and J sagittal sections of the shoulder at |x| .20 and .24, now and after the rounding).

**Frame.** Loop fit units as in `sheet.json`: `x` across in figure heights, `y` down from the crown, 1 at the floor. World z = -.957 + 1.8605 (1 - y); front and back world x = 1.8605 x. In the left view negative x is forward and **world y = 1.8605 x - .011**, calibrated on this packet (model `sheet_measure.py model assembled-0458` station edges against mesh sections at y .22 to .34: front offset .0097, back .0120). Front and back half widths are measured about the neck's own midline (sheet front -.0105, sheet back +.001; the model's neck is centered on world x 0). Model values come from plane sections of `assembled-0458/akinza.glb`, `head-0455/shape.glb` (placed at scale .5, offset (0, -.02, .635)) and `body-0448/shape.glb`. ## 0. What the method can and cannot reach (read first)

1. **The collar ring is a lip on the head stub.** The neck's back edge has a local maximum at z .465 to .477 (y .230 to .236): rear +.0284 world at z .47 against +.0258 at z .45 below it and +.0218 at z .48 above it (fit .0212 against .0196 and .0176). Its underside faces down from z .4625 to .47, which is the dark line in `m06 neck-rear`: the centerline brightness peaks at 131.9 at z .4737 and dips to 127.5 at z .467 before rising again to 137.9 at z .4526. The lip sits in the head mesh above the current head cut (z .452 = .500 - .048), so body orders never touched it.
2. **No head cut above the lip leaves a neck section.** At every z from .477 up, the head's horizontal section is the jaw underside: front -.085 to -.134 world, half width .058 to .068 (fit front -.040 to -.066, half .031 to .036). A loft to that ring fills the throat under the chin while the back stays where it is. At the method's offset .020 (cut z .480) the replica gives **R05.1 1.17 to 1.36 and R05.2 1.27 to 1.52 for every tangent limit from 0 to 30**: both measured criteria, which pass now, would fail, and the neck would read as a thick tapered column with no throat (panels G and H, grey). 3. **So the join setting is offset .038 plus a fusion window that covers the lip.** The cut at z .462 replaces the head stub below the lip with a loft whose front comes .003 to .005 forward toward the sheet, which leaves the lip .0011 fit high instead of .0016 and keeps R05.2 near 1.08, with room under the 1.15 limit. Offsets .037 and .036 cut the lip further (.0009, .0007) but put the expected R05.2 at 1.11 and 1.13, too close to the limit once the smoothing adds depth; they are a fallback only if R05.2 measures low. The method's "fusion windowed below z .44" is not used: it was meant to stop a trumpet flare at the neck base, which is gone in 0458 (neck half widths within .0015 of the sheet from y .2475 to .2575), and a window below .44 would leave both the lip and the new cut at z .462 unsmoothed, a new seam.
4. **`bridge-rings`, `tangent-limit` and `min-neck-length` are not levers here.** Thirty-one rings over a .039 span are .0013 apart, finer than the .0028 remesh voxel; at offsets .036 to .038 the tangent limit moves R05.1 by .013 to .024 between 1 and 3 and not at all above 3; the minimum length is only a guard (span .039 against .015). Keep the defaults.
5. **What stays after this round.** The neck's back edge sits .007 fit behind the sheet over the whole column (model .0196 to .0214 against .0133 to .0175), in both the body stub and the head stub, and the head's own nape flares back from .0146 at y .2223 to .0211 at y .2325 above the cut. Those are the nape step and most of the profile stem read. Neither a join parameter nor a no-cut body round can move them (friction 2). Expect R05.4 to reach partial at best; R05.5 and the ledge half of R05.6 are fully in reach.

## 7. Friction for the orchestrator

1. **The method's offset .020 is refuted by measurement.** Every head section above the lip is the jaw underside, so a cut there fills the throat and fails R05.1 and R05.2 for any tangent limit (section 0). This spec uses offset .038 and a fusion window raised over the lip instead of one windowed below z .44. `methods.md` R05 should read so.
2. **The neck's back sits .007 fit (.013 world) behind the sheet over the whole column**, in the body stub (rear .0288 to .0326 world at z .425 to .455) and in the head stub (.0255 to .0284), and the head's nape flares back above the cut. That position error is what makes the nape step and the remaining throat notch, and it caps R05.4 at partial. The join cannot move either ring, and B-19r is a no-cut round by method. Moving it needs either a head step tagged R05 (the stub warp of the 0320 spec: a forward shift of about -.014 world at z .45 to .475 fading out by z .50) together with a body cut at B-19 that brings the body stub's rear to about +.016 world, or the method's stall route, a neck-and-yoke generator on the joined skin after assembly. Moving only one of the two makes a new lip where they meet.
3. **The stem pinch above the cut is R01's.** The head base is .002 to .0047 fit wider than the sheet from y .2223 to .2325; a narrower jaw underside is an R01 lever.
4. **R05.1 and R05.2 read one station row (.24)**, so a lip, notch or bulge between stations is invisible to them. The back edge rule and the brightness rule in section 3 are the measurable checks for R05.4; they are worth adding to the rubric as measured guards.
5. **sheet.json's neck is narrower than the 0320 spec's finer trace** (front .0525 at station .24, half widths .0265 to .0278 from the polylines, against .0546 to .0556 before). This spec uses sheet.json as the loop requires; the 0320 spec's half width targets of .028 are retired.
6. **The rounding predictions are 2D.** They ignore blur along x; in 3D the cap corner rounds more and the cap's outer face shrinks a little, which is why R05.3 has a floor and `dilate` a fallback.
