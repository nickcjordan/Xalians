## Coordinates

All positions are loop fit units from `loop_tools.py canonical()`: `y` down from the top of the figure (0 at the top, 1 at the floor), `x` across from the centerline in figure heights, positive to the viewer's right in the front view. In the left (profile) view, `x_side` is positive toward the rear, so the face is at negative `x_side`.

Conversions for this assembly (head transform scale .5, translation (0, -.02, .635)):

- Front: world x = 1.8605 x; head-local x = 3.721 x.
- Height: world z = .9035 - 1.8605 y; head-local z = .537 - 3.721 y.
- Depth: on assembled-0265 the profile fit frame reads x_side = world y / 1.8605 + .0102 (measured on 16 rows from y .08 to .23 against the mesh, spread ±.001). So world y = 1.8605 (x_side - .0102) and head-local y = 3.721 (x_side - .0102) + .04.

## 4. What it must not look like

No earlier attempts are recorded for this region under the checklist. These are the failed looks in the `assembled-0265` critique and the gap audit, each with the geometry that causes it, plus the overshoots the fixes invite.

- **Disc eye at the front line (R02.1, R02.6 fail).** The globes sit with the iris lens at world y -.1864, level with the bridge, and the globe edge stands about .01 world proud of the skin at the top, bottom and inner side (top: globe -.162 against skin -.152; bottom -.142 against -.135). `refine_reconstructed_eyes.py` keeps the globe placement unchanged, so no pass has ever set the eye back. The fix is structural: new globes and a rebuilt socket rim, moved back .017 world at the apex.
- **Googly eyes.** The aperture is .0614 wide and .079 tall (height over width 1.29), about 1.25 times the sheet's linear size, with gap over width .77. A round, oversized disc reads as a sticker regardless of depth.
- **Uniform raised ring (R02.2, R02.6 fail).** The lid is a round bevel tube (bevel .0028 head-local, radius scaled 1 to 2.05 on the upper half only) laid .001 in front of the globe surface. Its round section catches a highlight along the top, so the thicker upper lid renders no darker than the bottom, and in profile it shows as a raised band. Measured in `eyes-front`, the ring reads 2 to 2.7 percent of the eye width all round.
- **Stuck-on bead or flat tab nose (R02.3 partial).** `head_nose_finish` is a separate shell .014 world thick that stands .003 to .004 world proud of the skin with a free lower edge, which reads as a tab with a lip from below and as a bead in profile.
- **Upper lip ahead of the nose (R02.4 partial).** The whisker-pad lobes reach world y -.1974 at |x| .01 to .02, ahead of the nose pad's front at -.1939, and the nose tip is .008 behind the sheet's.
- **Low relief cheeks and a crust (R02.5 partial).** The R01 round smoothed the outline to the right widths but left the cheek as a smooth arc with faint bulges, plus small scale-like lumps from the earlier cheek coat groups (`refine_reconstructed_cheek.py`) around the jaw.
- **Overshoot: sunken holes.** Setting the globes back without rebuilding the rim leaves a dark gap or a ring of old socket around the eye. The globe edge must always meet the rim within .003 world.
- **Overshoot: squint or frown.** A lid overhang larger than .006 world down, or a band wider than 8 percent at the top, closes the eye and reads as a scowl.
- **Overshoot: pig snout or ball nose.** Moving the nose forward as a flat front face or a sphere. The tip is a rounded point with sloping top and underside faces, .020 wide.
- **Overshoot: thorn beard or mutton chops.** Tufts longer than about 1.8 times their root width, with a tip angle under 25 degrees, or tips more than .005 beyond the outline table. They must read as broad soft coat masses.

## Round 26 addendum (2026-10-06): a bold outline that stays in the skin

Three rounds in a row (22, 25, 26) the blind readers preferred a face with a heavier, darker eye outline and the critic reverted it, each time for the same two faults, confirmed side by side against the baseline by the paired re-grade in round 26:

- **The outline stood proud as a disc.** The thick dark band was built as relief around the globe, so in `eyes-front` it read as a separate ring sitting on the face and in `nose-profile` as a side wall along the lower and outer edge (R02.1 and R02.2 lost). The readers like the darkness; the critic fails the relief.
- **The irises turned inward.** Round 26's first candidate set the iris offset to -.034 head-local (about .009 fit units toward the nose, three times the spec), a convergent stare that broke invariant I01 (forward gaze).

What every face variant must hold from now on:

1. **Darkness, not relief.** Make the outline bold through the band's darkness and the widths in the mass 5 table (heaviest at the upper outer corner, 6.5 to 7.3 percent of the eye width; thinnest at the lower inner corner), never through height. The band is raised at most .0005 world everywhere, and the profile shows no rim band (R02.2 target row). A variant that thickens the outline must also show, in its own builder check, the rim-to-globe depth at the 12 angles inside .0005 to .003 world.
2. **Iris offset capped.** Toward the nose by .003 fit units at most (mass 7, 8), which is about .011 head-local (fit units are world over 1.86, head-local is world times 2); down by .002 fit units. Anything larger breaks I01.
3. **Keep the bold look the readers asked for** within those two limits: a darker band (vertex color or conformal strip), a slightly larger iris (up to .55 of the eye width) and the upper-lid shadow band from mass 5 are all allowed.
