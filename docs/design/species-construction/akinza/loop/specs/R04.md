## Coordinates

Loop fit units from `loop_tools.py canonical()`: `y` down from the top of the figure (0 top, 1 floor). **Every x in this spec is a back-view x** (as seen in `back.png`, positive to the viewer's right), because the back view is where R04 is judged. The character's left (the higher ear, `L`) is **-x** in the back view and +x in the front view, so front-view x = -(back-view x). `u` = |x|. Depth `df` = world y / 1.8605, positive toward the rear. In the left (profile) view x_side = df + .011 (R01 spec), positive toward the rear; the left camera sees the L ear.

Conversions (head transform scale .5, translation (0, -.02, .635), unchanged in assembled-0305): world x = -1.8605 x_back; world z = -.957 + 1.8605 (1 - y). Head-local x = -3.721 x_back, head-local z = .537 - 3.721 y, head-local y (Blender, front is -y) = 3.721 df + .04. Lengths, widths and thicknesses in head-local units are 3.721 times the figure values. Examples: LB1-06 tip (-.213, .126, df .038) is head-local (.793, .068, .181); LT-02 tip (-.209, .002, df .032) is (.778, .530, .159); C3 tip (-.003, .014, df .021) is (.011, .485, .118).

The sheet's back figure is centered on the fan in its fit frame; its head sits about .005 to the viewer's right and its crown tuft about .012 to the right. Wing positions are used as measured (the model's back outline already matches them: head band IoU .921). The crown tuft tips were shifted -.012 so the tuft is centered on the model's head.

## 2. Structure

Counted in the first sheet's back view, with r03 for the idiom. **Each wing's back carries four rows of locks radiating from the ear-root crease (R01 masses 3 and 4), fanning outward, rising slightly along the top and turning down toward the lower edge**: T (4 locks along the top edge), B1 (14 on L, 13 on R; their tips are the measured tip and lower-edge outline tips), B2 (14 middle locks) and B3 (9 on L, 10 on R, at the root). The head dome carries D (26 small locks flowing down and converging into the neck), and the crown carries C (6 spikes from the whorl). That is 41 locks per wing, 26 on the dome and 6 in the tuft. Count check against the sheet: B1 takes the 14 and 13 tip and lower-edge tips; the top-edge tips (L at x -.249, -.209, -.142, -.105; R at .126, .217, .242, plus .172, added so the pitch stays under .05) are the T tips; the 6 crown tips are C.

Columns: root and tip are back-view (x, y); length is the arc length; width is the full width at the root and at the widest point (t .4); thickness is at the widest point; Dir is the back-view direction at the root and at the tip, in degrees below the horizontal pointing away from the centerline (negative points up; for the dome and crown, above 90 points down and toward the centerline). The axis is a cubic curve from root to tip with those end tangents, so tip minus root is the curl. Depth is the df of the lock's outer (rear-facing) surface at the root and at the tip; roots are buried under the next row, tips lie on the outer surface. Lock shape in the back view is R03's: width rises from the root width to the widest at t .4, then falls as (1 - (t - .4)/.6)^1.4 to a sharp point. Lengths, positions and widths of B2, B3 and D carry a deterministic jitter (length ±12 percent, position ±.003, width ±10 percent) that is part of the spec: do not re-grid them.
