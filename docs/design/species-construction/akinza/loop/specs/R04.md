# R04 spec: ear fan rear and profile (clump volume)

Region R04, written 2026-10-01 against baseline packet `assembled-0458` (head `head-0455`, body `body-0448`). It replaces the earlier R04 spec (the 114-row lock table over a plate and dome cut, written against `assembled-0305`): that construction is what parked the region. Method (`methods.md`, R04): `author_fan_clumps_field.py --part rear`, which strips the old rear shell, plate and lock rows in field space, then grows clumps from the crown line and the rear root band that flow outward and down into two tapering wings, with pointed crown tuft clumps and the head dome kept as a soft center. The rear step runs before the R03 front step.

Governing reference: the first sheet `evidence/identity-run-0001.png` (m01), back and left figures, read from the shared measurement `loop/sheet.json` and the same figure masks in the `canonical()` frame. Idiom references: `evidence/back-study-0018.png` (r03) for long wing locks flowing out and down from a crown tuft, and `evidence/head-clay-study-0021.png` (r04) for the curved shell of coat masses around the ear cup in profile. Gap audit rows closed (`gap-audit-0458.md`): rank 1 (shard bowl from behind, continuous pale lower rim, flat end planes, dome behind seams), the R04 proposed criterion of section 4, and the R04 share of rank 6 (the ear in profile). Annotated image: `specs/R04.png`.

## Coordinates

Fit units from `loop_tools.py canonical()`: `y` down from the top of the figure (0 top, 1 floor). **Every x in this spec is a back-view x** (positive to the viewer's right in `back.png`). The character's left wing (L, the higher ear, the one the left camera sees) is -x in the back view and +x in the front view, so front-view x = -(back-view x). `u` = |x|. Depth `df` = world y / 1.8605, positive toward the rear. In the left view x_side = df + .011.

Conversions (head transform scale .5, translation (0, -.02, .635), unchanged in assembled-0458): world x = -1.8605 x_back, world z = -.957 + 1.8605 (1 - y), world y = 1.8605 df. Head-local (Blender, front is -y): x = -3.721 x_back, z = .537 - 3.721 y, y = 3.721 df + .04. Lengths and widths in head-local units are 3.721 times the fit values. Checked against `head-0455/shape.glb`: its top vertex is at y .0035, its dome crest at x 0 is df .087 (y .08) and .085 (y .10), which is the profile rear x_side .096 to .097 in `left.png`.

## 2. Structure

Counted in the first sheet's back view (tips and flow above), with r03 for the idiom. Each wing is a volume of thick tapering clumps (31 on L, 32 on R) in five rows that radiate from the root band and overlap three to five neighbors each, plus a shared crown tuft of 6. Rows, from the one on top (seen from behind) to the one underneath:

- **K, crown line (2 per side).** Rooted on the crown parting at |x| .006, y .038 and .046, on the top-rear of the crown. They run outward almost level (-12 to -1 degrees) to |x| .064 and fill the crown's top-rear corner that the profile lacks.
- **I, root band (6 per side).** Rooted .004 outside the crease (R01 masses 3 and 4) at y .050 to .140, every .018, buried under the K and dome edge. They flow outward along the field: nearly level at the top, +30 to +44 degrees low on the band.
- **M, middle (5 L, 6 R).** Rooted .050 outside the crease at y .047 to .130 under the I tips, offset half a pitch, flowing outward along the field. The top three are the long wing clumps (.100).
- **T, top edge (4 per side).** Rising slightly outward (-31 to +6 degrees at the tip) along the top dome, .014 to .016 inside the top outline until the last fifth, ending at the top-edge tips: three sheet tips on L and two on R, plus one added on L (-.100, .018) and two on R (.170, .023) (.125, .026) so the pitch stays under .05.
- **E, edge row (14 per side).** One per sheet outline tip on the tip and lower edges, traced back along the field: .100 long at the wing tip (u above .24), .080 at u .18 to .24, .060 at u .13 to .18 and .048 to .028 at the notch. Their tips are the back silhouette's serration, and they end on the fan mid-surface, so the lower edge and the tip are thin.
- **C, crown tuft (6, shared).** Round pointed spikes from the whorl at (0, .036, df .031), the sheet's tuft tips shifted -.012 so the tuft centers on the model's head (the sheet's back figure has its crown .012 to the right of its fan center).

**Head dome: no clumps.** Inside the crease minus .004 (and below y .055) nothing is added. The dome stays R01's soft convex mass, and the valley at the crease is made by the I roots burying .006 below the envelope next to it.

**Columns.** Positions are (x, y, df) of the clump axis (the center of its section) at the root, at one third and two thirds of the arc length, and at the tip; pass them as the 4 `path` control points through which a Catmull-Rom or cubic B-spline runs. Length is the arc length. Width is the full width in the back view at the root and at the widest point (t .4). Thick is the depth thickness at t .4. Dir is the back-view direction at the root and at the tip, in degrees below the horizontal pointing away from the centerline (negative is up); for C it is the angle from straight up, positive toward the viewer's right. Layer: higher is on top seen from behind and unions later. Every clump: `twistDeg` 0 to 8 (deterministic jitter allowed), `taperExp` 1.3, `material` "Head clay"; `roundness` 1.3 (I, M), 1.2 (T, E), 1.0 (K, C); `tipRadius` .0015 (T, E), .002 (I, M, K), .0012 (C).

## Addendum 2026-10-08: soft masses, fur from texture (Nick's ruling)
