# R03 spec: ear fan front (coat clump volume)

Region R03, written 2026-10-01 against baseline packet `assembled-0458` (head `head-0455`, body `body-0448`). This replaces the earlier plate-and-lens R03 spec. Method (`methods.md`, R03): a coat clump volume built by the new `author_fan_clumps_field.py --part front`. Guide curves grow from the cup rim and the ear-root crease into the sheet's fan envelope. Each one is swept as a thick, tapering clump with a rounded section, and the clumps are unioned as level sets over a small inset core. The cup is carved to open forward and outward and filled with pale tuft clumps. Governing reference: the first sheet `evidence/identity-run-0001.png` (m01), front and left figures. Idiom reference: `evidence/head-clay-study-0021.png` (r04). Shared measurement: `loop/sheet.json`. Gap audit rows closed (`gap-audit-0458.md`): rank 2 (fan front construction) and rank 6 (ear in profile). Annotated image: `specs/R03.png`.

All numbers below were measured or checked in Python against the sheet masks (`loop_tools.reference_figure`, `canonical` at 1000 px per figure height), the baseline render masks (`assembled-0458/render`), and the baseline head mesh (`head-0455/shape.glb`, 939k vertices). The clump table was splatted back into the fit frame and the predicted front footprint, serration, cup coverage and side visibility were measured from that splat (section 6).

## Coordinates

Fit frame of `loop_tools.py canonical()` and `sheet.json`: `y` down from the top of the figure (0 top, 1 floor), `x` across in figure heights, positive to the viewer's right in the front view. **Every x in this spec is a fit-frame front-view x.** `L` is the character's left (+x, the higher ear); `R` is the character's right (-x). Depth `df` is world y / 1.8605, positive toward the rear, so the "front" of a clump is its most negative df face.

- **The model head is centered at x +.0046 in the fit frame, not at 0.** The fit centers the model on the species centerline (world x -.00859, `species.json` frame.centerLine.front), and the head is symmetric about world x 0. Measured: the baseline's L tip vertex (head-local x 1.036) lands at fit .283 in the render mask, and 1.036 / 3.721 + .0046 = .283. Use `u` = |x - .0046| for distances from the head's own center line.
- **Silhouette points (outline, edge tips) are the sheet's own fit-frame values, used as they are.** The model's fan outline already sits on the sheet's (R03.1 1.002, R03.2 .056).
- **Face-tied points (cup polygon, tuft roots, cup floor) are carried from the sheet by +.020.** The sheet's face center sits at x -.015 (eye midpoint -.0155, nose -.0196, chin -.0096) and the model's at +.0046. After the shift, the sheet's tuft base lands on the model's skull side line within .003 on both sides (L sheet tuft base x .081 to .091 becomes .101 to .111, model skull side .105; R -.108 to -.114 becomes -.088 to -.094, model -.095).
- **Profile:** in the left view x_side = df + .0067 (`centerLine.left` -.0125 world). Checked on the nose: df -.112 gives x_side -.105, and the render mask's front-most head column is -.107.
- **Head-local (Blender, head transform scale .5, translation (0, -.02, .635), unchanged):** x_h = 3.721 (x - .0046); z_h = .537 - 3.721 y; y_h = 3.721 df + .04 (front is -y). In `shape.glb` (Y up) that is (X, Y, Z) = (x_h, z_h, -y_h). Lengths, widths and thicknesses in head-local units are 3.721 times the figure values. Examples: LP5 tip (+.276, .033, +.018) is head-local (1.010, .414, .107); LT1 root (+.110, .080, -.028) is (.392, .239, -.064); RP4 root (-.169, .070, +.013) is (-.646, .277, .088).

## 2. Structure

Counted in the first sheet's front view, with r04 for the idiom. Every coat clump grows from the cup rim (or, for the drape, the skull side) outward to a tip on the envelope, and they radiate from the ear root like r04's fan. Per side:

- **Primaries P1 to P9 (layer 2):** the large shaggy pointed locks R03.4 counts. P1 to P4 make the top edge, P5 the wing tip, P6 to P9 the lower edge. On L, P8 and P9 are shorter because the band under the cup is only .03 to .04 deep there (they keep a length at least 1.6 times the width at the middle); on R all nine are large.
- **Secondaries (layer 1, behind the primaries):** S6 to S8 between the top primaries, and S1 (L only) to S5 between the tip and lower primaries. Their tips are the remaining measured serration points, so the edge reads as one run of points.
- **Drape D1 (layer 1):** one short clump from the skull side under the cup toward the cheek (R03.6).
- **Tuft T1 to T5 (layer 3, pale):** fluffy pointed clumps fanning from the skull side line inside the cup toward its upper outer tip.

That is 18 coat clumps on L (9 P, 8 S, 1 D) and 17 on R (9 P, 7 S, 1 D), plus 5 tuft clumps per side. Tips on the tip and lower edges are the measured serration points of section 1 (LS4 is placed midway between LP8 and LP9, where the sheet's points are only .013 apart) pushed .010 outward along the chord, the drape .003. Tips on the top edge lie on the section 1 top edge table, and they and the wing tips are not pushed. The top tips are held at y .006 or below the baseline skin top (y .0035, head-local z .524) so `skinTopZ` cannot rise.

**Columns.** Root and tip are (x, y, df), with df the clump's **front face** at that point. Length is the chord. Dir is the chord's direction in the front view, in degrees (0 points straight out, away from the face; 90 points up; negative points down). Curl is the extra turn of the tip tangent away from the chord (positive turns the tip upward, negative turns it outward and down); the root tangent turns the same amount the other way. Width is the full front-view width at the root and at the widest point (t .4). Thick is the front-to-back thickness at t .4. Taper is the exponent of the width fall after t .4. "Root on" gives the rim edge and the arc-length fraction s around the cup polygon from its inner top (s 0) to its inner bottom (s 1); each root sits .010 inside the rim, so the cup carve trims it.

**Guide curve (the `path` of each clump).** In the front view the path is a quadratic Bezier from root to tip whose control point is the chord midpoint moved (length / 2) tan(curl) to the outward-right of the chord (looking from root to tip with "outward" as +x' and "up" as +y'), so the tip tangent leaves the chord by `curl` degrees. The top clumps therefore rise steeply off the upper rim and lay their tips outward along the top edge, so their upper flanks form that edge. In depth the front face runs linearly from the root df to the tip df, with a forward camber of .004 at the middle: df_front(t) = df_root + (df_tip - df_root) t - .016 t (1 - t). The clump's centerline lies at df_front(t) + thick(t) / 2.

**Overlap order (front to back at any point).** Tuft (in the cup only), then P, then S and D, then the core. Among neighboring primaries the one nearer P5 lies .002 in front, so the edge shingles fan from the wing tip. S clumps lie .004 behind the front of the two primaries beside them. The rear step (R04) runs first and this step unions last, so these clumps own the visible edge in the front view.

**Roots.** All coat roots lie on the cup rim except D1 (skull side, under the cup's inner bottom). No root lies on the face, the cheek, the crown tuft (|u| below .06) or the forehead. If R01 or R02 move the skull side line, move the cup polygon, the tuft roots and every root by the same x and keep the tips.

**Strip and core (the tool's `strip` and core).** Strip the old fan in front of the fan's mid-surface (the mean of today's front and rear faces per .01 cell in u and y), inside the fan zone (u at least .100 for y .045 to .195; u at least .085 above y .045), outside R01's skull ellipsoids plus .006, and outside `graft_face_field.py`'s face window. Replace it with a core: the sheet's front outline closed with a .010 disk (padded so the top rows are not eroded), inset **.006** in the front view and **.012** from the plan's front surface in depth. The .006 outline inset is a measured choice, not the method's .010 to .015: with the same clumps, a .010 inset predicts a front band missing of .069 and a .012 inset .073, both of which fail R03.2.

## 3. Cross-sections and surface

**Clump section.** A rounded superellipse, exponent 2.2 across and in depth (fuller than an ellipse, no flank edge). Width over thickness at t .4 (`roundness`) is 1.4 to 1.8 for every clump (table range 1.38 to 1.80). The old locks were 2.4 to 2.6 with a flat back. The back is not flat: each clump is a full volume, and the union with its neighbors and the core makes the back.

**Profiles along t (t = distance from the root over the length).**

- Width: w(t) = w_root + (w_mid - w_root) S(t / .4) for t up to .4, where S is smoothstep; then w_mid ((1 - t) / .6)^taper, never below .004 (twice `tipRadius` .002). Width at t .9 is .07 to .11 of w_mid: a sharp point, but not a needle.
- Thickness: th(t) = thick (.75 + .25 S(t / .4)) for t up to .4; then thick ((1 - t) / .6)^(.8 taper), never below .002. Clamp th(t) to at most rear(x, y) - df_front(t) - .004, where rear is the R04 rear surface in place when this step runs. On the baseline rear this clamp binds near the tips of LP5, LP6, LS1 to LS3, RP5 to RP7 and RS2, near the tip of the top clump LS7, and on the lower fringe (LS4, LS5, LD1, RP9, RD1), by .001 to .025.
- Tip lift: the tip may stand at most .004 off the envelope surface under it. No tip points at the front camera: the tip tangent stays within 15 degrees of the local fan surface.
- Twist under 10 degrees.

**Union and blend (`blend`).** Core with layer 1: smooth-union radius .006. Within layer 1, and within layer 2: .004. Layer 2 onto layer 1: .003. Light per-clump field blur at most .0015 voxel-equivalent. No global blur and no closing over the fan. The grooves between neighbors stay .004 to .008 deep; the notches at the edge reach the core and are at most .010 deep from the closed envelope.

**Depth plan (front surface).** Panel E of `R03.png` draws the y .09 and y .03 sections against the baseline mesh.

- **Cup floor** (inside the cup polygon): floor(u) = min(-.016 + .70 (u - .100), +.036), plus up to .008 more depth toward the polygon's middle (a concave section of radius .03 to .05). The .70 slope is a 35 degree yaw: the cup's opening normal points 35 degrees outward from straight forward and 5 degrees up. Values: u .10 -.016, .13 +.005, .16 +.026, .174 and beyond +.036.
- **Rims:** upper rim front = floor - .022; lower rim front = floor - .018; lateral rim front = floor - .004, so the cup is open toward the side. The upper tip of the rim is a sharp corner, interior angle 50 to 65 degrees (L 54, R 65 in the polygon). The skull side has no rim: a concave fillet of radius at least .006 into the skull.
- **Coat fronts** run root to tip as in the tables. The top-edge tips sit at the profile crest (P1 -.016 near u .085, rising through P2 +.004 and P3 +.020 to P4 +.024), so the top band is a dome that rolls back over the top, not a lip. The wing tip trio sits at +.018 to +.022, the lateral wing at +.022 to +.026, and the lower edge at +.016 near the wing, coming forward to +.002 and the drape to -.014 toward the cheek.
- **Wing beyond the cup:** for rows y .05 to .15 and u .19 to .25 the coat front stays at +.018 or further back. The front of the tuft must be the nearest surface to the side camera, so nothing lateral to it may stand in front of it.
- Against the baseline: at y .03 the top band moves back .010 to .025 (baseline -.040 at u .10, -.022 at .13, -.016 at .16, +.005 at .19). At y .09 the tuft front sits at -.028 to -.005 over u .10 to .15 (baseline pale -.045 to -.022), and the outer cup floor at u .16 to .18 drops to +.026 to +.036 (baseline -.022). The wall behind the floor stays at least .016 (baseline rear .052 to .055 at u .16 to .19).

**Cup carve (`cup`).** After the coat union, remove everything inside the cup polygon in front of the floor surface. Fillet the rim: radius .005 on the upper and lower rims, .003 at the upper tip (so the tip stays sharp), .006 on the lateral rim. The rim is one continuous edge from the inner top round the upper tip to the inner bottom. Because every root runs .010 inside it, the carve, not a row of root ends, makes the edge. The floor is never more than .024 behind the rim front, and nothing opens through to the rear.

**Tuft (`tuft`).** T1 to T5 lie on the floor and fan from the skull side line toward the upper outer tip; T1's long upper edge is the tuft's top line. Front face: from the table root df (floor - thick) to the table tip df (floor - .004), with a .004 forward camber. The tuft front stays at least .004 behind the rim front at the same (u, y). T1's tip ends about .028 short of the cup's upper tip and .009 below the upper rim, so the tip of the hollow stays a dark point. Neighboring tuft clumps blend at .004 over their inner half and stay separate points over their outer .3 of length. They join the floor by a smooth union of at most .002, within .010 of their roots only. A dark ring of floor .004 to .012 wide stays visible between the tuft and the rim on the upper and lower sides. That ring is R03.5's hollow.

**Pale material.** Assign `Pale inner-ear coat` (base color value .58; head clay .38) to every face within .0015 figure units (.0056 head-local) of a tuft clump's field after meshing. The skin stays one closed mesh (I08); only the material index changes.

## 4. What it must not look like

From the history card (rounds 12, 13, 15 and 16 all retuned the plate-plus-lens system) and the methods' failure list, each with the geometric reason and the rule here that prevents it.

- **Shards, flat leaf facets and scales (gap rank 2; all four rounds).** Each lock was a lens with a flat back pressed on a plate, width over thickness 2.4 to 2.6, so every lock was a lit plane with a hard flank edge, and the plate showed between them. Rounds 13 to 16 changed the sizes, counts and tips, but not that section. Rule: full rounded volumes, roundness 1.4 to 1.8, smooth-unioned grooves .004 to .008 deep, no plate.
- **Serrated thorn edges and sawteeth (rank 2; round 15's sub-point forks).** Forks and narrow tips (`tip_min` .0015 with `width_scale` 1.45 on narrow locks), each lifted up to .008 off the plate, gave a row of small, equal, sharp teeth. Rule: no forks; width at t .9 at least .07 of w_mid; tip lift at most .004; tips at the measured, irregular serration points, never regularized.
- **White faceted blades and dark slots in the cup (rank 2; rounds 13 and 16).** The tuft was three to five flat lens blades on a cut ramp, and the cut left a deep pit behind the cup's upper tip with nothing in it. Rule: rounded tuft clumps (roundness 1.4 to 1.8) blended over their inner half; a concave floor no more than .024 behind the rim; the dark ring, not a slot.
- **Ear in profile as a ragged column with teeth down its back edge (rank 6).** The cup faced 25 to 32 degrees outward with the tuft at x_side -.037 to +.013, so from the side the pale faces were seen edge-on, in front of the eye-side fur, and the plate's edge locks showed as a picket. Rule: the 35 degree floor, the swept-back wing (front at +.018 or behind next to the cup), top tips on the profile crest, no tip lift above .004.
- **Sea urchin or porcupine.** Thin clumps of equal length radiating from one point with tips sticking out. Rule: widths at t .4 of .027 to .051 (fringe .014 to .025), every coat clump at least 1.6 times as long as w_mid, roots spread around the rim (s .02 to .93), not at the pivot.
- **Spaghetti or sausages.** Round tubes of constant width. Rule: the width profile above (widest at t .4, tapering to a point), roundness not below 1.38, and the overlap order so neighbors merge into one coat.
- **Core showing through gaps.** Rule: predicted coat cover of the fan front outside the cup .80 (L) and .83 (R); the rest is notch bottoms and root shadows, no exposed core patch wider than .020.
- **Crinkled root seams.** Root ends showing as a row of lumps along the rim. Rule: roots .010 inside the rim, trimmed by the carve, with a filleted rim.
- **Blurred blob.** Rule: the blend radii above; no closing or global blur over the fan.
- **Outline shrinking or tips leaving the envelope.** Rule: the core inset .006; tips at the measured points (edge tips pushed .010 out, top and wing tips not pushed); no point above y .0035.
- **A cup that faces only forward.** Rule: the 35 degree floor and the open lateral rim.
- **Rolled top lip and dish (the old spec's failure, still in the baseline at y .03).** Rule: top tips on the crest, so the top band rolls back .010 to .025.

## Addendum 2026-10-08: soft masses, fur from texture (Nick's ruling)

Nick ruled the ear fan's fur idiom: tone the spikiness down a little and rely on the fur texture added later, instead of building fur out of modeled shapes. The fan keeps its outline, V tilt, cups, pale inner tufts, drape and profile sweep as soft masses with a few gently pointed tips; there is no new lock geometry, no lock-sweep or post-assembly lock tool, and no per-lock count target. Orders retune the existing steps (H33 front, H34 rear): wider blends, larger tip minimums, lower ogive and curl, less edge serration, a wider skin blend at the dome. The rubric lines R03.4, R03.7, R04.3, R04.4, R04.5 and R04.7 were rewritten to match; where this spec's tables call for separate locks, layers or tip standoff, this addendum wins.
