## Coordinates

All positions are loop fit units from `loop_tools.py canonical()`: `y` down from the top of the figure (0 at the top, 1 at the floor), `x` across from the fan centerline in figure heights, positive to the viewer's right. In the back view that means x is mirrored against the front view: the character's left side is +x in front and -x in back. In the left (profile) view x is positive toward the rear.

Conversions for this assembly (head transform scale .5, translation (0, -.02, .635)):

- world x = 1.8605 x (front view; negate for back-view x). Head-local x = 3.721 x.
- world z = -.957 + 1.8605 (1 - y). Head-local z = .537 - 3.721 y (y .1443 is head-local z 0).
- Depth: `df` = world y / 1.8605 (world y positive toward the rear). Head-local y = 3.721 df + .04. On this assembly the profile fit frame reads x_side = df + .011 (measured from the mesh against the left fit frame at y .10 and .22).

Key points converted: notch (±.085, .175) is world (±.158, z .578), head-local (±.316, z -.114). Shelf end (±.14, .172) is world (±.260, z .583), head-local (±.521, z -.103). Back crease at y .10, |x| .068 is world (±.127, z .717), head-local (±.253, z .165).

**Rules the table encodes.**

- **The dome is narrower than the wings and has its own contour.** In the back view, across rows y .06 to .14, the rear surface must have a valley on the crease line (|x| about .068 to .080): crest at x 0 minus valley at least .018, and the wing root at |x| .12 at least .003 above the valley. Baseline: crest minus crease is .011 to .015 and the wing root sits .002 to .006 below the crease, so there is no valley, only a slope. At y .10 the target profile is .083 (x 0), .080 (.03), .075 (.05), .068 (.065), .064 (.08), .066 (.095), .068 (.115 to .16) (panel E). The valley is soft: concave, with a section radius of at least .008 (world .015) and a full width of about .02 to .025. It fades out above y .05 toward the crown and runs into the silhouette notch at y .175.
- **The crown stands in front of the ear roots.** For rows y .04 to .09 in the front view, the fan's front surface at |x| .10 lies at least .015 behind the skull's surface at |x| .07. Baseline gaps: .015, .014, .004, .001, .006 at y .04 to .08, so the deficit is at y .06 to .08. The crown must read by this contour and its own convexity, never by lowering the fan's top edge.
- **Nothing below the shelf except the head.** For y .175 to .235 there is no mesh outside the head base widths at any depth: the web at (±.10, .18) goes.
- **Keep the rear crest.** The side silhouette at y .04 to .18 already matches (rear .084 to .096), and it is set by the crest at x 0. Make the dome stand proud by moving the wing roots forward and by cutting the valley, not by pushing the crest back.

## 3. Cross-sections and surface

- **Skull section (horizontal, y .08 to .14):** a rounded egg, depth about .17 (forehead -.09 to crest .083) by visible width .12 between the creases. Convex everywhere except the two valleys where the wing roots join. No flat back, no plateau running across into the wings.
- **Wing root section (vertical, at |x| .10):** front face set back from the dome (the cup opens forward and outward, R03). Rear face convex, at df .066 to .070, bowing back slightly from the valley. Underside level to |x| .14.
- **Head base section (horizontal, y .20 to .22):** front width .128, .103, .070, with depth from chin to nape -.093 to .032, -.080 to .024, -.072 to .021. In profile the underside of the head curves smoothly from the back-facing occiput (y .16) through a down-facing surface under the ear rim to the nape. There is no horizontal plane: the rear surface slope dy/dz stays below 1.5 everywhere between y .17 and .23, and no row holds a flat disc wider than .04.
- **Surface idiom:** the dome is a smooth, rounded mass under fine coat. The wings carry the locks (R03 and R04 own that idiom). At the crease the wing's root locks overlap onto the dome edge by no more than .01, so the valley stays readable. The crease is a soft valley in the coat, not a cut, ridge or rim.

## 4. What it must not look like

- **Goblet or bowl (rank 1; R01.4 and R01.8 fail).** The geometry behind it: `rear_smooth` with half_width .8 local (.215 fig, the whole fan) and sigma .045 over 2 passes blurred head and wings into one field. Skull 1's x radius of .38 local (.102 fig) is wider than the visible dome (.058 to .063), so its smooth union (blend .05 local) filled the junction. `wing_recess` at .01 local (.003 fig) was too small to show, and the notch web closes the cup from below. The fix: limit any rear blur to |x| below .05 fig (.19 local), or run it only on the wings outside the crease. Narrow skull 1 to an x radius of about .31 local (.083 fig), with blend at most .03 local. Rebuild the wing roots as their own volumes joined at the crease, then delete the web.
- **Separate disc or rim line (the first build of round 1).** The old shingle disc and its arc rim were a convex ridge. The valley here must be concave and soft (radius of at least .008), with no convex lip on either side.
- **Lit lens or collar disc at the nape.** The head's underside is a flat, down-facing plane at y .19 to .21 with a vertical wall below (rear df .035 to .033 from .20 to .22). It comes from skull 2 (head-local center z 0, z radius .30, which reaches y .225), flattened by `rear_low_pull` rather than tapered. Shape the occiput so it is already shallow at its base: its rear at y .20 to .22 should come out near df .021 to .010 before any warp. Do not raise the old `nape_pull`: it squashes the neck stem (head-0221). `nape_pull_gentle` is acceptable if every slope stays under 1.
- **Sunk roof.** Making the crown rise by lowering the fan's top edge beside it (`inner_rim_lower`, `inner_rim_lower_left`) left the top edge .008 to .019 low; matching the sheet's top edge alone raises front head IoU from .874 to .911. Set both to 0 and lift the rim to the edge table instead.
- **Window-edge creases.** Any windowed morph needs a fade of at least 10 times its relief change (history card), so a .02 lift of the rim needs a fade of at least .2 local.
- **Square jaw or pinched stem (R01.5).** A jaw .120 wide at .21 and .088 at .22 that then drops to a .062 neck makes both a square chin and a pinch. Taper the jaw as in the head base table; then the neck needs no change.
- **Crown nubs.** Rounded knobs on the crown (removed in round 1 by `top_smooth`). If the crown tuft comes back, it is pointed locks with sharp tips, not knobs.

## 5. Gap audit rows: kind and order (most visible first)

1. **Rank 1, back of head as a goblet: structural.** Rebuild the skull-to-wing junction: a narrower skull ellipsoid, wing roots joined at a soft valley, the notch web removed, and the shelf. The outline envelope stays as the target; the construction inside it changes. Serves R01.4, R01.6 and R01.8, and also R04.1 and R04.6.
2. **Rank 8, head base and nape in profile: structural (proportion lever on the occiput).** Remove .012 to .023 of depth behind the nape at y .20 to .23 and let the ear-disc rim overhang. Serves R01.7 and R01.2, and R05.4 at the nape.
3. **R01.3 crown in front: structural, the same junction rebuild seen from the front.** Set the ear roots back .010 to .014 at y .06 to .08 so the dome's contour shows.
4. **Fan rim sag between crown and tip: proportion lever on the envelope.** Undo `inner_rim_lower` and `inner_rim_lower_left` and lift the top edge .008 to .019 at |x| .06 to .20 per the edge table. The outer underside hangs .006 to .03 lower beyond |x| .14. Shared with R03 and R04 (it improves R03.2, R04.1 and R03.6), but R01.1 needs it. The head base alone predicts only .892 front.
5. **R01.5 chin taper: local surface fix.** Narrow the jaw outline .008 to .009 per side at y .21 to .22, round the chin bottom at .226, and pull the chin front back .008 at .22.

Friction for the orchestrator:

1. **R01.1 cannot reach .90 on the head alone.** About half of the front band's missing area is the fan's top rim (rows 0 to .04), which R03 and R04 also touch. This spec includes the rim lift, so whichever region builds first should carry it. The R03 and R04 outline metrics move the same way.
2. **R01.4 and R01.8 pull in different directions as worded.** R01.4 says "blends into it, with no seam", and the round 1 builder read that as one blurred surface. R01.8 needs the dome to stand apart. Both references show a soft dark valley at |x| about .08. Proposed wording for R01.4: "...blends into it through a soft concave valley, with no convex rim line, ridge or separate disc".
3. **Profile nape versus neck posture.** The R01 nape target stops .004 to .006 short of the sheet because the model's neck (R05 and R06) sits .010 behind it. When R05 moves the neck forward, re-set the nape to the sheet values in section 1.
