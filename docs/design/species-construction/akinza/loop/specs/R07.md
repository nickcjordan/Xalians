# R07 spec: arms and forepaws

Region R07, rewritten 2026-10-01 against baseline packet `assembled-0458` (body `body-0448`, built by B-23 `rebuild_arms_field.py` with `art/species-construction/specs/arms-r07-v10.json`). Method (methods.md, R07): the analytic arm and forepaw rebuild at B-23, with the paw at about 1.5 wrist widths, four separately grooved digit lobes, claws leaving each tip at about a third of the digit length, and the deltoid cap authored from the ARM dict instead of the kept native shoulder. This spec replaces the assembled-0291 version.

Sources: the shared sheet measurement `loop/sheet.json` (outlines, station runs and landmarks; no re-tracing of the sheet), the paws study `evidence/paws-study-0020.png` (r02), the packet renders and `posed/` (vertex dump, weights, joints, pose), and the body's `arm-field.json`. Every number below was measured with Python on those files; the scripts are in the session scratchpad and are not needed to build.

Coordinates: fit units, `x` across from the centerline (positive to the viewer's right), `y` down from the crown (0) to the floor (1). For the model, world z = -.957 + 1.8605 (1 - y) and world x = 1.8605 x in the front view. Construction values are world units in the script's side frame (x' outward, y forward negative, z up) and the paw frame of `rebuild_arms_field.py` (s along the forearm axis a2 from W, t along u2 toward the dorsum, r along v2 toward the back). The measured arm is the character's right arm (image left in front, image right in back).

Annotated image `specs/R07.png`: A to C the sheet's right arm in front, left and back with the traced outline (red) and masses 1, 3, 5 root to tip (blue); D and E the r02 forepaw with outline, digit lobes 6 to 9 and claws 10 to 13; F and G the model's arms-down front and left renders with the joints, the target deltoid (orange), the forearm width bars (dark now, green target) and the flat-slab rows; H the target forepaw in its own frame; I the posed right-side outer edge, sheet front against the mirrored sheet back and the model.

Two new keys (None keeps every earlier run exact):

- `lobeTipWidthRadius` (.0095): the lobe capsule's width radius at B; A keeps `lobeWidthRadius`.
- `pawRollDeg` (25; acceptable 15 to 25): turn the paw frame about a2 so the dorsum faces outward and forward: u2' = cos(r) u2 - sin(r) v2, v2' = sin(r) u2 + cos(r) v2. Apply it to the lobe and claw frame (sW, tW, rW and the claw base, forward and curl), and to the rod's (qu, qv) weighted by smooth((t_axis - wristS + .02)/.04), so the roll fades in across the wrist where the section is near round and no twist shows.

## 3. Cross-sections and surface

- **Deltoid and upper arm.** Horizontal slices of the outer face at every row .29 to .36 stay within .003 world of their outermost point over at most .032 world of depth (a round arm of this size). Now .053 to .084 at y .30 to .33, the kept native cap, and .030 to .033 below .34. In profile the cap is one convex curve from the biceps front at about y .36 over the top to the triceps; no straight run longer than .02 fit, no corner where cap meets shaft. The cap meets the trapezius slope through a concave fillet with no step.
- **Forearm.** One convex swell on the outer and back side centered about E + .05, then one straight taper to the wrist; no lit ledge at the top of the swell (the swell's blend into the elbow stays at least .020).
- **Paw.** Dorsum gently domed across, palm flat to slightly cupped. Grooves: on sections perpendicular to a2, the dip between neighboring lobe crests is 0 at the knuckle line (s .065), .0025 to .004 fit at mid lobe and opens into a notch at the tips at least .005 fit deep, so each tip is its own round in the dorsum view. A groove's depth never decreases toward the tip by more than .001 world, which is what keeps it from closing into a pit. The raw saddle between lobes before blending is .005 world (width .0165 on .0183 spacing); the v10 lobes (.0135 on .0205) leave .012.
- **Claws.** Pale, glossy, tapered cones, base width about half the lobe tip width; each leaves the end of its lobe, at least .008 fit of it beyond the fur.
- **Surface idiom.** Smooth short fur over soft muscle, the same as the legs (R08): field blends only, no rings, creases, ridges or Boolean seams. Claws are the only hard parts.

## 4. What it must not look like

Each failed look and its geometric cause:

- **Flat slab and box corner at the top of the upper arm** (rounds 11 and 16, R07.1 partial, audit rank 13): `removeZ` .235 to .285 with `fusionBlend` "native" keeps the native cap above y .33, whose outer face is flat over .053 to .084 world of depth at y .30 to .33.
- **Pointed cap corner, dark pockets from behind, wider shoulders** (round 5, the first authored deltoid): radii .050, .058, .050 offset .015 back stood about .008 proud of the arm and reached world y +.020, .029 behind the arm's back line, into the concave junction with the back, under a .09 fillet; R05.3 rose from 1.003 to 1.052. This spec's cap is .006 proud at row .32, stops .061 world (.033 fit) short of the back silhouette and uses a .06 fillet.
- **Pits between the lobes** (round 11): tall narrow lobes (thickness .0335 against width .0135) leave a saddle .012 world deep, closed at its root by the rod end, so the blend leaves pockets. Wider roots on a tighter spacing and grooves that only deepen toward the tip prevent them.
- **Small closed fist** (audit rank 7): the paw (.048) is narrower than the forearm swell (.057), the wrist (.033) is thick, the lobes are parallel with a blunt square end and the rod covers all but the last .02 to .04 of them. Thinning the forearm and wrist, tapering the lobe tips into an arch and ending the rod at s .065 change this; the dorsum width itself already matches the sheet (posed front paw .042 against the sheet's .042).
- **Claws in one column in front, read as one serrated hook** (round 16, R07.5): the paw is exactly edge-on in the front view, so all four claw bases share one lateral position; tilts of 0 to 12 degrees spread them by only about .005 fit. The 25 degree roll spreads them by .014.
- **Straight needle claws in profile** (rounds 11 and 16, R07.3): the claws curl palmward, which is along the forepaw-profile camera's line of sight (+x), so the curve cannot show; bend .8 also folds most of the claw toward the camera. With the roll and bend .5, the tip tangent turns about 20 degrees in that view.
- **Overcorrections.** A human hand (free digits longer than .03 fit, splayed or separate fingers, a thumb or dewclaw); a mitten past the hip outline in the posed front; a needle forearm (below .044 at E + .04) or an elbow without a point; a dorsum wider than .050 fit; the claws on the front face of the lobes instead of out of the tips; a ledge at the new deltoid.
- **Refit noise taken for a result** (round 11): the drop from .874 to .8445 came from refitting the pose. Keep the joints, and the body's pose cache, unchanged.

## Friction for the orchestrator

1. **R07.6 cannot reach .90, by any arm.** The sheet's front and back figures disagree on the right side's position in their own canonical frames. In the arm band the sheet front against the mirrored sheet back overlaps at IoU .740 unshifted and .971 when shifted .028 (panel I). Any 3D change to the right arm moves the front and back silhouettes together, mirrored, so it trades one view for the other: on the 0458 posed masks, a uniform outward shift of the arm band gives front .751, .798, .828, .871 and back .927, .892, .859, .806 at .000, .006, .010 and .016, a mean of .867 to .871 at best. Of the front view's 2526 missing pixels, 898 lie in rows .47 to .58, where the edge is the hip, which the audit already flagged as the sheet's weight shift. Smallest change: register each sheet view on its own trunk center (the midpoint of the central run at y .40 to .46) before scoring the posed bands, or score front and back after one shared lateral offset. Failing that, set R07.6's minimum to .87 so it measures the arm and not the frame.
2. **The audit's paw-size numbers compare different views.** Its .033 against .055 sets the arms-down model paw seen edge-on against the sheet's akimbo paw seen from the dorsum. Measured like for like, the posed front paw is .042 against the sheet's .042 (.047 with claws). If the proposed R07 measured criterion is adopted, read both in the posed front: the model already passes .85 of .047. The real gap is the ratio to the wrist and forearm, which this spec sets.
3. **Ownership of the cap.** The method puts the deltoid in R07's ARM dict, while R05 owns the shoulder outline and the back-view ledge and knob criteria (R05.3, R05.5, R05.6). This spec bounds the cap by R05's numbers. If the opening at `removeZ` .335 to .385 rounds off the trapezius corner and R05.3 or the back view regresses, the builder falls back to `removeZ` [.320, .360] and raises the deltoid's vertical radius to .056.
