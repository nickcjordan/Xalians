## Coordinates used here

All positions are loop fit units (`loop_tools.py canonical()`, figure height H, y down from the crown, 1 at the floor), but for a paw the useful origin is the ankle, so every point is given relative to it:

- **Front and back views:** `U` is the offset from the ankle run's center at y .92, positive **lateral** (away from the body midline), for the character's right leg (the leftmost run in front, the rightmost run in back, as `row_measures.py` picks them). Ankle centers at .92: sheet front x -.087, sheet back x .059, model arms-down front x -.178 (|x|; world |x| .331).
- **Side (left) view:** `V` is the offset from the ankle run's center at y .90, **negative forward** (the left view faces image-left). Ankle centers at .90: sheet paw 1 (the far leg, the one drawn in clean profile) x .040; model x .0245.
- **World and the builder's paw frame:** world width = 1.8605 x fit width; world z = -.957 + 1.8605 (1 - y). The paw frame is the `HIND` frame of `rebuild_body_field.py`: `U = side*(X-ax)`, `V = Y-ay`, with `(ax, ay)` from `section(side, -.805)`. Calibrated on `body-0303`: the rendered ankle centers (front at .92, side at .90) lie on that origin within .001 fit, so fit = paw-frame world / 1.8605 with no offset.
- **Sheet paw 1 heights:** the sheet draws the far foot of the left view .018 higher than the near foot (its sole sits at y .982). Section 1's side points are shifted down by .018 so the sole is at 1.0; they are heights above the paw's own sole.

Every number below was measured with Python on the canonical masks (`loop_tools.reference_figure`, `row_measures.mask_frame`, `rig_fit.reference`) or computed with the same `smin`, `ellipsoid` and `capsule` functions the build script uses; none is read by eye.

## 3. Cross-sections and surface

- **Toe lobes:** each a soft elongated dome, longer than wide (length over width 1.2 to 1.36), rounder in section than the current beads only in that the top is the high point, not the sides. Grooves between lobes are shallow folds (smin .006, at most .005 world deep on top) that fade back into the body by V -.09 world; seen from the front the top line is one gently scalloped arch, highest over toes 2 and 3 (h .068 to .070), lower over toe 4 (.065) and lowest over toe 1 (.053). From the side the front fifth of the paw domes down from the crown (.961) to the tip (.982 to .990): a quarter-ellipse about .025 [fit] long and .022 tall, never a vertical face.
- **Dorsum and instep:** convex in both directions, one continuous surface from the toe crowns to the ankle; the profile line rises in one smooth curve from the crown at V -.045 to the ankle front at V -.019, with no concave corner tighter than radius .010 [fit] where it meets the leg. Laterally the instep keeps the top high (.952 to .957) out to U .066 and then rolls down to the tip.
- **Sole:** flat contact patch from the heel (V +.012 fit) to the toe bases (V -.075 fit), mean width about .10 fit; each toe touches the floor along the middle of its underside, and the toe tips lift (underside at h .007 to .015 at the very front). The sole meets the sides through the floor blend (.004 world), a small soft radius, not a sharp rim.
- **Heel:** a round heel ball, its back .002 to .004 [fit] behind the leg's back line and rounding into the sole; not a vertical wall.
- **Claws:** pale, glossy, sheathed at the root by the toe's own surface (the lobe overlaps the claw root by about .010 world), emerging from the top of the toe tip and curving down along the front face, tips .008 to .010 fit above the floor and at most .002 fit ahead of the lobe face below them. Middle claws .040 world long along the curve, outer .034: long against the toe tip, short against the paw.
- **Surface idiom:** the same smooth short fur as the leg; no fur locks, ridges, knuckle creases or pad seams on the top. All joins are field blends.

## 4. What it must not look like

No earlier attempts are recorded for this region. The baseline critique (assembled-0305) and the gap audit name these looks; the geometric reason for each:

- **Four equal beads in a straight row** (R09.1 fail): all four toes share one radius set (.025, .035, .035) and one height (z -.922 to -.924), and their tips spread only .020 world in V, so from the front they read as four identical balls on one line. Fix: graded sizes, middle tips .014 to .032 ahead of the outer ones, scalloped arch.
- **Claws as white spikes stuck on the toe fronts, standing clear** (R09.2 partial): the current claws start at mid-height inside the lobe, .012 behind its front, point straight forward (`forward` [0, -1, 0]) and are .044 long, so they project .015 fit horizontally beyond a vertical toe face. Fix: root at the top of the tip, 42 degrees down, ending on the face.
- **Low slipper, flat instep, vertical heel wall** (R09.3 partial): the old dorsum capsule is narrow (r .047 to .030) and centered medially, so the paw's lateral half is only the toe beads (h .036), and the heel ellipsoid's back is the straight ankle line. Fix: instep mass and round heel.
- **Paw too small against the leg** (R09.4 partial): front width .099 against .113, top .011 low over the lateral half. Note R08 already slimmed the ankle, so the ratio is 2.9 against the sheet's 3.4, and the fix is the paw, not the ankle.
- **Overcorrections to avoid.** A long slipper: do not lengthen the paw to pass R09.6 (friction 1); the side length is already the sheet's. Clown feet: the paw width is .113 fit and no more; do not let the instep bulge above .952. Sausage toes: deep gaps between toes (blend below .004) or toes longer than .075 world make fingers. Talons: claws reaching the floor or hanging below the sole line, or longer than .045 world. A paw turned out in the mesh (see friction 3).

## Friction for the orchestrator

1. **R09.6 and R09.7 measure two paws in the sheet against one in the model.** In the sheet's left view the far leg (paw 1, in profile) and the near leg (paw 2, turned toward the viewer) are staggered: at .89 to .94 they are two separate runs, and at .99 two separate soles (panel C). The R09.6 run (-.051 to +.104) is paw 1's claw tip to paw 2's heel, and the R09.7 run (.015 to .109) is both ankles. In the arms-down render both model feet coincide. The single sheet paw is about .116 long with claws (the model now: .135 with claws, .120 without) and about .045 deep at .93 (model now .061). Passing R09.6 as written would need a .145 paw, 25 percent longer than the sheet's; R09.7 cannot be moved by the paw at all. This is the same two-leg reading R08 corrected for the ankle depth. Proposed smallest change: replace R09.7 with "front and back paw width at .96 (pick left and right), model over sheet, mean at least .85" (sheet .1003 front, .0896 back; model now .0603 and .0618, so .60 and .69; this spec predicts .083, so .83 and .93, mean .88), which measures exactly the low lateral half the critic sees; and replace R09.6 with a single-paw length against a sheet constant of .116 plus or minus 7 percent (left view, full run at .975, arms-down, where both model feet coincide), with the constant's provenance recorded in the rubric, or drop it.
2. **R09.9 is bound by the pose, not the paw.** The shared pose (`posed/pose.json`) rolls each foot 15 degrees about y and pitches it 3 to 9 degrees (`foot.L` [3, 15, 25], `foot.R` [9, 15, 15]); the posed rows .99 to .995 show the sole lifted on one edge, and the posed ankle at .92 reads .064 wide in front against .034 arms-down. Pasting the sheet's own paws at the posed model's ankle centers scores front .59 and back .76; the best horizontal shift of the current posed paws gives front .76 and .74 per paw, back .72 and .73, left .82. Proposed: after the rebuild, refit the foot bones (`rig_fit`, `foot` limits) with the roll held to plus or minus 5 degrees so the sole stays flat, and score the foot band after a per-paw shift within plus or minus .02, as R08's friction 1 proposed for the leg bands; or lower the bar to .78.
3. **The sheet's paws are turned out; the spec builds them straight.** In every sheet view the claws sit at U .04 to .09 lateral of the ankle, and the lateral toe's claw shows from behind, which means a turnout of about 20 to 30 degrees. R09.1 and R09.8 are judged on the arms-down close-ups (`m09`), where a paw turned in the mesh would read as "the medial toe leads", and a rig wants feet built forward. So the lateral reach is built into the toe layout (toe 4 splay and the instep) and the turnout is left to the pose, which already turns the feet out 15 and 25 degrees. With the wider paw, the posed views will then show a little more lateral reach than the sheet; the foot refit in friction 2 absorbs it.
4. **R08 coupling.** The model's ankle is .051 deep at .90 against the sheet's .032 (R08's depth stations were not reached). The toe reach is given from the ankle center and the heel from the leg's back line, so the paw stays right if R08 later slims the depth; until then the dorsum meets the leg .004 to .008 higher than the sheet in profile (panel H), which is expected and not a paw fault.
5. **"Short" (R09.2) against "long curved claws" (R09.8).** Read together as: long against the toe tip (a fifth of the toe lobe's length along the curve, .040 world on the middle toes), short against the paw, never standing clear of the toe. That is what both references show.
