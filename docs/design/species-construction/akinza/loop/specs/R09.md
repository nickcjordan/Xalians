## Coordinates used here

Fit units (`loop_tools.py canonical()`): figure height H, y down from the crown, 1 at the floor. For a paw the origin is the ankle, so points are given relative to it, as heights `h` above the paw's own sole:

- **Side:** `V` is the forward offset from the ankle center, negative forward (toward the toes). In the sheet's left view the ankle center of paw 1 (the far leg, drawn in clean profile) is x .040 at y .90 and its sole is y .9812; the model's ankle center is the section of the leg at world z -.805.
- **Front and back:** `U` is the offset from the ankle center at y .92, positive lateral. Sheet ankle centers: front right paw x -.087 (U = -.087 - x), back right paw x .059 (U = x - .059).
- **Model frames.** The builder's paw frame is the `HIND` frame of `rebuild_hind_paws_field.py`: U = side (X - ax), V = Y - ay, origin from `section(side, -.805)` of the script's **input**, which is `body-0369` for B-21: (ax, ay) = (.32887, .02554). B-22 later moves the leg, so the final model (`body-0448`) has its ankle center at ay .02291. Hence **V_final = V_B21 + .0014 fit** (U unchanged within .0003). All fit targets below are in the final frame (what the packet renders show); all world parameters in section 2 are in the B-21 frame. World = 1.8605 x fit; world z = -.957 + 1.8605 h.

Every number below was measured with Python: sheet values from `sheet.json` outlines, the model from the `body-0448` mesh, and the proposal from a numpy rebuild of the field with the script's own `smin`, `smax` and ellipsoid functions, blended in the morph band with a slice-wise signed distance of the real B-21 input (`body-0369`). That simulation reproduces the v3 result (`body-0400`) within .0036 fit at every side row, so the proposed numbers carry about that uncertainty.

### Field (what the builder adds to `rebuild_hind_paws_field.py`)

1. **`topLine` dome.** On the 2D (U, V) plane of the box (the script's sparse grids broadcast to (nx, ny, 1)): `h, uc, w = interp(V, table)`; `t = min(|U - uc| / w, 1)`; `top = FLOOR + h (1 - a t^n)` with `n` 3 and `a` .3, so the cross-section is a broad flat top that falls to 70 percent of the mid-line height at the plan edge; `dtop = (Z - top) / sqrt(1 + |grad top|^2)` with `np.gradient` of `top` over the two plane axes at voxel spacing; `dside = |U - uc| - w`; `dome = smax(dtop, dside, k)`, then `smax(dome, max(V0 - V, V - V1), k)` with `k` = `topLine.blend` .012 and V0, V1 the first and last nodes. Do not use a superellipse that falls to zero height at the edge: its slope is unbounded there and the field streaks (panel E was first built that way).
2. **Null masses.** `body`, `instep` and `dorsum` set to `null` in the spec; the script skips a mass whose entry is `None` (the `merge` function already writes `None` over the default).
3. **`driftStopZ`** -.835: the column center uses `max(Z, driftStopZ)` in its drift terms, so below the band top the column stands upright instead of leaning forward into a ramp and outward.
4. **`columnFlare`** `{"z": [-.835, -.885], "medialU": [0, -.028]}`: the column's medial edge moves medially by a linear amount from 0 at the first z to -.028 world at the second (center += m / 2, half width -= m / 2), so the lateral edge stays put. This fills the medial pinch.
5. **Order:** `mass = smin(column, heel, paw)`, `mass = smin(mass, dome, paw)`, `paw = smin(mass, toes, toeToPad)`, then the floor cut, as now.

Blends: `toeToToe` .005, `toeToPad` .012, `paw` .024, `floor` .004. Column: `columnZ` -.830 (as now), `columnDepthScale` .92, `columnBottomZ` -.900, `columnBottomBlend` .02. Morph band **[-.830, -.850]** (v3 used [-.83, -.89], which kept 40 to 75 percent of the old ramp in the field from h .055 to .065; the column at z -.830 is measured from the native leg, so the shorter band adds no step; the simulation shows at most one voxel, .0013 fit, of bulge at h .0625).

## 3. Cross-sections and surface

- **Side top line:** convex from the claw roots (toe front at h .019) to the crest (h .040 at V -.065), level to V -.047 (h .0415), then a concave turn into an upright ankle front by h .05. Over the toes the line is the toe lobes' own crowns; behind them it is the dome. No crease, step or saddle anywhere on the line (the dome rises under the toes and meets their tops at about V -.105 world, and `toeToPad` .012 merges them).
- **Dome cross-section (front view):** flat across the middle, falling to 70 percent of the mid-line height at the plan edge and then rounding (blend .012 world) into a near-vertical side wall. This gives the sheet's lateral shoulder (h .046 at U .064, .039 at .075) and the near-vertical lateral edge from h .008 to .024 at U .089.
- **Toes:** each lobe longer than wide (length over width 1.4 for the middle toes, 1.5 for the outer), the middle two tall eggs whose crowns reach the paw top, the outer two smaller and lower, set back .023 to .030 world. Seen from the front the four lobes are separate down to the floor: the gaps between them at the floor are about .02 world wide and recessed .03 to .04 world behind the toe tips (the dome ends at V -.138, behind them). Above the equator the grooves are shallow folds (`toeToToe` .005, a fillet of about .0025 fit) that fade into the dome by about V -.09.
- **Sole:** flat contact from the toe pads (each toe touches along the middle of its underside, its tip lifted .012 fit at the front) through the dome's broad plan (panel G) to V -.008 world, then the heel rounds up. The sole meets the sides through the floor blend .004.
- **Heel:** a round heel, its back leaning forward going down (V +.024 at h .05 to +.016 at h .022) and rounding into the sole by about V +.005; no vertical face and nothing standing behind the leg's back line.
- **Claws:** pale, flattened sideways (flat .85), sheathed at the root by the toe (the root is .008 to .010 world inside the surface, so no root ring or cap can show), leaving the lower front of the toe tip and hooking down onto the floor line. From the side they read as hooks on the toe tips; from the front, as short tapered points under the toes that are never wider than the claw itself where they leave the toe.
- **Surface idiom:** the same smooth short fur as the leg; no locks, ridges, knuckle creases or pad seams on the top. All joins are field blends.

## 4. What it must not look like

- **Low slipper, straight ramp, no domed instep** (round 6 and round 12, current R09.3 and R09.8): the ankle column was measured at z -.83 and kept its forward drift (.29 world of V per unit of z) all the way to z -.925, so its front leaned forward to V -.043 fit at h .041; with the dorsum capsule and the instep ellipsoid on top of it this made one straight ramp from the toe crowns to the leg, and the morph band [-.83, -.89] kept up to 75 percent of the older ramp. Fix: drift frozen below -.835, dome in place of dorsum and instep, short band.
- **Flat level slab under the toes** (round 6, current gap audit): the pad mass (`body`) ran across the full width ahead of the outer toes and down to the floor, and the toes sat on the floor along most of their length, so their flattened bottoms merged into one flat band. Fix: no pad; the dome ends behind the toe tips; middle toe centers .033 world above the floor, so each toe's contact is a short chord and the gaps open to the floor.
- **Beads in a row / balls on a shelf:** toes much lower than the mass behind them read as beads in front of a rim. The first dome try here had its front rising to full height within .014 world and showed exactly that rim (panel F was rebuilt). Fix: the dome stays below the toe crowns over the front half of the toes and only meets their tops behind them.
- **Pearl-headed cones on the toe faces** (round 12, current R09.2): claw root radius .012 world sunk only .003 inside a face that curves away, so the root's circular end stood out larger than the shaft. Fix: radius .0062 to .0072, root .008 to .010 inside, exit below the toe's equator.
- **Pits and knots where toes part** (round 12): two lobes that barely touch with a .006 blend leave a deep narrow hole. Fix: middle toes overlap .008 at the equator, toe 1 to toe 2 gap closed by the .005 blend, and the dome fills the groove floors behind.
- **Ankle step or heel bulb** (round 6): a morph band whose ends disagree, or a heel ellipsoid standing behind the leg's back line. Fix: the band starts where the column is measured; the heel's back is at least .002 fit inside the leg line above it.
- **Overcorrections:** a clog or club foot (lateral shoulder above .048 or the paw wider than .113 fit); toes as long sausages (length over .095 world) or deep slots (blend below .004); talons (claws reaching the floor or more than .013 fit visible); a sole lifting off the floor (the dome's floor footprint must stay flat from V -.12 to -.01 world); a paw yawed in the mesh to copy the sheet's turnout (leave turnout to the pose, as before).

## Friction for the orchestrator

1. **R08's ankle sets where the instep can turn up.** At the band top (h .068) the model's ankle front is at V -.030 against the sheet's -.016, its depth is .055 against .036, and its medial edge pinches to -.008 to -.012 at h .055 to .065 against the sheet's -.018. The paw pass owns only z below -.830, so its concave turn into the ankle spans .015 fit of V where the sheet's spans .030, and the crest band is shorter than the sheet's. The paw is not wrong for that; the leg is. Smallest change: R08's depth stations at y .90 to .94 toward the sheet (.031 at .90, .036 at .92) and its medial edge at .935 to .94 toward -.018. The v4 dome attaches to the column, so R09 needs no change when R08 moves; only rerun B-21 before B-22 as the recipe does.
2. **R09.9 is still bound by the pose.** Posed foot band per view: front .682 (extra .287), left .707, back .550 (extra .411); arms-down .372.
