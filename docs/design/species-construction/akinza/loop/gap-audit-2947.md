Row widths are copied from `measurements.json`; values marked as estimates are read by eye from the crops. Numbers from `measured.json` and `fit.json` are copied, not re-judged.

The front of the fan is no longer slabs, chevrons and thorn rows: each wing is now a broad rounded pad. But the pads carry a beaded rim of knobs, scattered bumps on their faces and a peg fringe along the lower edge from behind, and the softening widened and flattened the outline, so four outline measures that passed in 2751 now fail (R01.1 .874, R01.6 1.329, R03.1 1.043, R04.1 .870). Unchanged since 2751: the eye in profile, the front stare, the bare body and its athletic relief, the box cap on the upper arm, the knee, the neck stem and the stance.

## 1. First glance

From the front, the head reads as a flat-topped mushroom cap: two rounded pads with lumpy, beaded rims, level across the top instead of sweeping up into a V, with rounded ends where the sheet has up-flared points, and stiff white leaf blades standing in each cup. The sheet shows one soft shaggy V of fur with pale fluffy tufts. In three-quarter the far ear is a ring with a toothed rim around a hole, like a wreath. From the side the ear is a tall shell with a slit lined with beads, a clam or flytrap, behind a wedge-shaped eye; the sheet shows a round fur mop with a big pale cup and a clean round eye looking forward. From behind, the fan is a lumpy tray with knobs and a fringe of pegs along its lower edge, and the head is a smooth disc cut into it by two straight vertical lines; the fan's inner lower edge comes down to the neck, so the head base no longer narrows into the neck as its own dome. Below the neck the model is the same bare smooth figure as in 2751: square shoulders with deltoid caps over a pinched waist, a rounded upper-back disc with dark notches at the shoulder corners, a flat pale cap on the upper arm in profile, and flat four-bead hind paws. The trunk profile still matches the sheet.

## 4. Proposed new criteria

Each one fails on assembled-2947.

- **R03 (measured, new):** front view full width at .02 at most 1.3x the sheet's (.280): the top edge dips at the crown and only the two tips reach the top row. Now .501, 1.79x.
- **R03 (visual, repeated from 0458, 2278 and 2751):** in `m02 left` and `m04 head-side`, the pale inner cup is visible from the side over at least a tenth of the ear's area, and the ear reads as a rounded soft mass, with no slit, lip or row of beads.
- **R03 (visual, new):** in `m02 front-left` and `m05 head-three-quarter`, the far ear reads as a solid soft mass with a cup in it, not a ring around a hole.
- **R01 and R04 (visual, new):** in `m04 head-back` and `m02 back`, no straight line, cut or seam runs vertically through the fan beside the head dome, and the head dome narrows into the neck below the fan's lower edge.
- **R02 (visual, repeated from 2278 and 2751):** in the true side view (`m02 left` at full figure scale), the eye's visible outline is a closed oval whose width at mid height is at least half its height and does not taper to a point; the iris fills at least the front third of it.
- **R02 (visual, repeated from 2278 and 2751):** in `m02 front-left`, the iris is at least half the eye's width and offset toward the gaze, and the far eye's lid line has no kink or pinch.
- **R04 (visual, repeated from 2751):** in `m04 head-top`, each cup shows forward depth (a hollow bounded by a rim), and the fan does not read as a thin flat plate.
- **R05 (visual, repeated from 2751):** in `m02 back`, no dark notch or groove outlines the upper back at the shoulder corners.
- **R09 (visual, repeated from 0458 and 2751):** in `m09 hindpaw-profile`, the top line rises from the toes to the ankle as a dome, with no vertical face at the heel.

## 5. Structural versus local

The new pads are the right direction for the fan front (no more slabs, chevrons or thorn rows), but softening by inflation cost the outline and left the knobs. The largest identity loss is still the head, and now part of it is in a silhouette that used to pass.

**Structural:**
- **Fan outline from the front (rank 3).** Restore the V: lower the crown and inner top edge about .025 below the tips, taper each tip to an up-flared point, raise the outer lower edge, and trim the span to within 3 percent. This is the measured loss from Nick's pick and the cheapest structural gain.
- **Fan front and ear side (ranks 1 and 2).** Build each cup as a soft hollow inside a solid fur mass, opening forward and outward with real depth so the pale tuft shows from the side and from three-quarter; no ring around a hole, no slit, no bead rows. Make the inner tuft a soft pale mass covering at least half the cup instead of blades.
- **Eye set in profile (rank 4).** Reshape the socket so the eyeball's visible outline in profile is a full oval with the iris forward, while the brow and nose still stand in front of it (R02.6).
- **Dome and wings from behind (rank 5).** Remove the two vertical cuts by merging the dome into the wings through a soft valley, and raise the fan's inner lower edge so the head narrows into the neck below it (R01.6).
- **Smaller structural items:** crown height and jaw section (rank 8), the hind paw toe arch, heel and instep (rank 11), and the rest stance (rank 15).

**Surface:** the knobs, beads, lumps, peg fringe and cup seams across the fan (part of ranks 1 and 5, under Nick's fur ruling); the coherence read once the fan is smooth (rank 6); iris size, gaze and lid weight from the front (rank 7); the athletic relief and shoulder-corner notches (rank 9); the upper-arm cap and wrist ring (rank 10); the knee crease (rank 12); the cheek lumps and crust (rank 13); and the neck stem (rank 14).

R02 (8.3) is still scored far above how the face reads, because its passing criteria are judged from close-ups. The fan front, the ear from the side, the fan outline and the eye from the side carry most of the remaining identity loss.
