## 1. First glance

From the front, the fan still reads as a crown of stiff leaves and thorns: flat pointed slabs and serrated chevrons, white faceted blades in the cups, a dark slot behind the image-right tuft, and thorn spikes along the top edge and across a lumpy crown. The sheet shows one soft shaggy mass with pale fluffy inner tufts. The face is two round white discs with small pupils dead centre, so it stares; in three-quarter the pupils shrink to dots under a pinched brow and it looks startled. From the side the head is a column of crumpled shard slabs behind a wedge-shaped eye, with no pale ear cup; the sheet shows a round fur mop with a big pale cup and a clean round eye looking forward. From above (`m04 head-top`) the fan is a thin flat tray with a ragged front edge. From behind, the wings read as locks, but the head in the centre is a tall smooth shield with a ragged outline set into them. Below the neck the model is a bare smooth figure: square shoulders with deltoid caps over a strongly pinched waist from the front, a rounded upper-back disc with dark notches at the shoulder corners from behind, small curled paws, and flat bead-toed slipper feet. The side trunk now has the sheet's chest, waist and lumbar curve. The silhouette matches; the head's surfaces and the body's finish do not.

## 4. Proposed new criteria

Each one fails on assembled-2751.

- **R02 (visual, repeated from 2278):** in the true side view (`m02 left` at full figure scale), the eye's visible outline is a closed oval whose width at mid height is at least half its height and does not taper to a point; the iris fills at least the front third of it.
- **R02 (visual, repeated from 2278):** in `m02 front-left`, the iris is at least half the eye's width and offset toward the gaze, and no brow ridge pinches the upper lid.
- **R03 (visual, repeated from 0458 and 2278):** in `m02 left` and `m04 head-side`, the pale inner cup is visible from the side over at least a tenth of the ear's area, and the ear reads as a rounded shaggy mass, not a column of slabs.
- **R04 (visual):** in `m04 head-top`, each cup shows forward depth (a hollow bounded by a rim), and the fan does not read as a thin flat plate.
- **R01 (visual, repeated from 2278):** in `m04 head-front` and `m02 front`, nothing rises above the crown outline between the ear roots taller than 0.01H except soft lock tips; no thorn or spike row.
- **R07 (visual, replaces the 2278 wording):** in `m07 forepaw-front` and `m02 front`, the forepaw is longer than it is wide, its digits point down rather than curling under, and its claw tips spread across at least two thirds of the paw width.
- **R05 (visual):** in `m02 back`, no dark notch or groove outlines the upper back at the shoulder corners; the back flows into the arms through a rounded cap.
- **R09 (visual, repeated from 0458):** in `m09 hindpaw-profile`, the top line rises from the toes to the ankle as a dome, with no vertical face at the heel.

## 5. Structural versus local

The trunk profile and the forepaw digit chains show the loop can fix a structural gap without losing the outline. The largest remaining identity loss is still in the head, inside a silhouette that already passes.

**Structural:**
- **Fan front and ear side (ranks 1 and 2).** Rebuild the front of the fan and each ear cup the way the rear was rebuilt: soft pointed lock masses inside the fitted envelope, the cup opening forward and outward with real depth, so its pale fluffy tuft shows from the side and the fan is not a flat plate from above. No slabs, blades, chevrons, thorns or dark slots. Keep the outline.
- **Eye set in profile (rank 3).** Reshape the socket so the eyeball's visible outline in profile is a full oval with the iris forward, while the brow and nose still stand in front of it (R02.6).
- **Dome seam from behind (rank 5).** Merge the head dome into the wings through a valley of locks instead of a ledge.
- **Forepaw digits (rank 6).** Uncurl the digits so they point down along the paw, lengthen the paw, and spread the claws across its width.
- **Smaller structural items:** crown height and jaw section (rank 8), the hind paw instep, heel and toe arch (rank 10), and the rest stance (rank 15).

**Surface:** the body coat masses and one lock idiom (rank 4, after rank 1); the athletic relief, the shoulder-corner notches and the box shoulder cap (ranks 7 and 12); iris size, gaze and lid weight from the front (rank 9); the knee crease (rank 11); the cheek plates and crust (rank 13); and the neck kink (rank 14).

R01 and R02 (8.1 and 8.3) are still scored well above how the head reads, because their passing criteria are outline metrics and close-ups. The front of the fan, the ear from the side and the eye from the side carry most of the remaining identity loss, and no outline metric catches any of them.
