- `spec:voxel` (default 0.001): fine voxel (head-local). .001 builds F-parts in about 1.1 min and adds about 0.55M vertices; .0008 about 2.5 min; .0006 for a final. NOTE the assembly voxel-remeshes the whole creature at .0028 world (.0056 head-local), so skin edits finer than about .01 head-local alias at the assembly (see notes).
- `spec:window.x / z / yCut` (default 0.33 / [-0.29, 0.215] / -0.05): rectangle of the old face skin that is replaced (head-local); outside it nothing moves. Keep it inside the face: the old fan front starts at |x| about .34, and tufts and their blend (.03) must end inside z -0.29 plus margin plus fade.
- `spec:window.margin / fade` (default 0.006 / 0.025): the new skin equals the old one at margin from the window edge and takes over fully by margin plus fade
- `spec:window.faceSmooth` (default 3): cells (.002) of box smoothing, 2 passes, applied to the donor face height used for the dome front, the orbit floor and the fold
- `spec:eye.aperture.center` (default [0.1887, 0.0291]): aperture centre of the left eye (x outward, z up); the right eye mirrors. R02.md target centre is (.1767, .0123) for a smaller eye
- `spec:eye.aperture.semi` (default [0.105, 0.1433]): aperture semi axes. R02.md asks .0930 by .1265 (height over width 1.36, gap over width .90)
- `spec:eye.globe.kind` (default conformal): conformal = donor face surface plus the r10e dome profile (edge a little behind the rim, apex proud); ellipsoid = analytic ellipsoid fitted to the aperture (yaw and pitch from the face plane, depth semi, curvature from a quadratic fit). The ellipsoid leaves rim depth errors of +-.03 on this face, use it only with a lateral opening or a smaller eye
- `spec:eye.globe.apexProud / edgeBehindTopBottom` (default 0.013 / [0.0045, 0.0028]): conformal dome profile: apex height proud of the rim plane and the edge depth behind the skin (top, bottom). R02.md wants the apex .012 to .016 proud and the frontmost eye point at least .010 (fit) behind the brow to nose line
- `spec:eye.globe.depthSemi / margin / yawDeg / pitchDeg / mode` (default 0.06 / 1.0 / auto / auto / edge): ellipsoid kind only
- `spec:eye.orbit.shear` (default 0.1): vertical shear of the opening with depth: the upper rim overhangs the globe (lid fold overhang)
- `spec:eye.orbit.lateralShear` (default 0.0): the opening moves outward with depth by this much per unit depth (lateral wall opening); about .4 opens the lateral wall
- `spec:eye.orbit.floorBehind / floorBlend / rimBlend` (default 0.0025 / 0.008 / 0.012): floor depth behind the globe and the two smooth radii; rimBlend .012 and floorBlend .008 are larger than the r10e values (.005, .004) because the assembly remesh aliases a sharper rim into notches
- `spec:eye.orbit.extraCutters` (default []): list of {center [x,y,z canonical], semi, yawDeg, blend} ellipsoids subtracted from the skin: the orbit mask that opens the cheek or brow wall for the profile oval
- `spec:eye.lid.bandPercent` (default r10e table): angle table of the dark band width as percent of eye width (a material zone on the globe)
- `spec:eye.lid.fold.height / width / center / blend` (default [] (off)): swept lens on the aperture curve, half buried in the skin: angle tables [[deg, value]] of its height above the face plane and width; a raised lid fold
- `spec:eye.iris.offset / semi / pupil / lensFront` (default [-0.0111,-0.0055] / [0.0555,0.094] / [0.0305,0.0414] / 0.005): gaze offset toward the nose and down, iris and pupil size, lens thickness
- `spec:tufts.list` (default two per side (r10e)): one entry per tuft per side (mirrored): root [x,z], tip [x,z], length, widthRoot, thickRoot, lift; append entries for the three tufts of R02.md
- `spec:tufts.blend / bury / taperPower / tipRadius / taperExponent / thicknessExponent` (default 0.03 / 0.003 / 1.15 / 0.0035 / 0.85 / 0.9): tuft union radius and lens section; blend .03 is the r10e value and is what makes the plates read wide and flat
- `spec:nosePad.topZ / apexZ / halfWidth / cornerRadius` (default -0.11 / -0.148 / 0.043 / 0.006): outline of the rounded triangle in x, z (the inlay footprint); R02.md asks .020 wide (half .0372) top .179 apex .191 fit
- `spec:nosePad.mode / recessDepth / recessBlend / shrink / topLift / bury` (default inlay / 0.004 / 0.0025 / 0.0005 / 0.0003 / 0.0015): inlay (v2 default): a recess is carved in the skin under the outline and a closed pad with vertical walls (top = skin plus topLift, walls buried recessDepth plus bury) fills it, so the visible footprint is the rounded outline at any remesh; recessBlend softens the lip. mode shell is the v1 sloped shell (flat hard-edged triangle after the assembly remesh, rejected by readers); wrapUnder, lift, dome, sinkRim, buryStart, feather apply to shell only
- `spec:maxIsland` (default 3000): vertices: free fragments up to this size are removed and recorded in face-parts.json (islandsRemoved)
