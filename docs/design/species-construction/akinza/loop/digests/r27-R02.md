- `spec:voxel` (default 0.0008): fine voxel, head-local. The assembly no longer resamples it. .0008 builds in about 7.7 min alone (20 to 30 min when four builds share the machine) and adds about 1.08M skin vertices (709k to 1.79M); .0006 for a final; .001 is about a third faster.
- `spec:window.x / z / yCut / margin / fade` (default 0.33 / [-0.29, 0.215] / -0.05 / 0.006 / 0.025): the rectangle of assembled skin that is replaced (head-local); outside it nothing moves. Keep it inside the face (the fan front starts at |x| about .34).
- `spec:eye.aperture.center / semi` (default [0.1887, 0.0291] / [0.105, 0.1433]): left eye aperture centre (x outward, z up) and semi axes, head-local; the right eye mirrors. The starter eye is .0564 wide in fit units (height over width 1.365, gap over width .797); R02.md asks center [0.1767, 0.0123] and semi [0.0930, 0.1265] (.050 wide, gap .90). Verified to move: that setting gave width .0500 and gap .90.
- `spec:eye.orbit.rimBlend / floorBlend` (default 0.005 / 0.004): rounding of the orbit rim and floor, head-local. The r10e values are restored because the post step is not remeshed afterwards; .0025 gives a sharper rim (rim uniformity std .0126 to .0124 fit).
- `spec:eye.orbit.lateralShear / shear / extraCutters` (default 0.0 / 0.1 / []): open the lateral wall of the orbit (so a globe edge no longer reads as a bug eye), vertical shear, extra opening ellipsoids; unchanged from v2.
- `spec:eye.globe.kind / apexProud / edgeBehindTopBottom / depthSemi` (default conformal / 0.013 / [0.0045, 0.0028] / 0.06): globe shape (v2 meaning). R02.md wants the apex .012 to .016 proud and the eye front at least .010 behind the brow to nose line; the starter reads -.0136 fit on that measure, so the eye still sits ahead of the line and the builder must set it.
- `spec:eye.lid.bandScale` (default 1.5): multiplier on the bandPercent table widths for the strip. The skin rim rounds over the outer part of the band, so at 1.0 the visible band is about two thirds of the table; 1.5 reads close to the table (heavy upper outer, thin lower inner).
- `spec:eye.lid.bandPercent` (default [[0,6.5],[30,7.3],[60,6.6],[90,4.2],[120,3.5],[150,2.8],[180,2.3],[-150,1.5],[-120,1.4],[-90,2.9],[-60,3.7],[-30,5.0]]): band width as percent of eye width by angle from the top (R02.md table).
- `spec:eye.lid.strip.mode / thickness / bury / radial / inset` (default shell / 0.001 / 0.0003 / 6 / 0.0): the lid band as its own thin closed shell on the aperture curve (thickness .001 head-local = .0005 world in front of the globe surface, bury behind it); any other mode puts the band back as a material zone of the globe.
- `spec:eye.iris.offset / semi / pupil` (default [-0.0111, -0.0055] / [0.0555, 0.094] / [0.0305, 0.0414]): iris lens: gaze offset from the aperture centre, semi axes and pupil; unchanged from v2.
- `spec:tufts.list / blend / thicknessExponent / taperExponent` (default 2 per side, blend 0.03): cheek tuft roots, tips, lengths, widths and thicknesses (head-local) and the union blend. R02.md has three per side; the starter has two. The starter tufts read as creased facets in profile, so blend and thickness are the first things to tune.
- `spec:nosePad.*` (default mode inlay, topZ -0.11, apexZ -0.148, halfWidth 0.043, recessDepth 0.004): nose pad inlay outline and recess (v2 meaning). The starter pad has a straight top edge that reads as a ledge in the front view: tune cornerRadius, topLift and recessBlend.
Notes: A post step is cached under its own key (a spec edit rebuilds only A-face, 8 to 20 min) but each output is a whole assembled-NNNN with the 1.8M vertex skin. The starter reaches the baseline face but is not tuned: the eye is wider than R02.md and still ahead of the brow to nose line, the tufts are two per side and read creased, the nose pad has a flat top. The first order tunes those parameters. The recipe is the baseline recipe plus F-bulk and A-face; tools/R02-notes.md (round 26, blocked) is superseded.
