# Akinza surface backlog

Things the reference shows that belong to the surface phase (textures, painting, normal maps or a fur shader), not to construction geometry. The construction loop does not model these and must not be judged on them (see the Modeling scope section of the loop briefs). When the surface phase starts, it begins here.

| Item | Where | What the reference shows | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fur strands and softness | ear fan front and back, cheek tufts, chest, forearms, calves, tails | fine strands and fluffy edges on every coat mass | the geometry carries the clump shapes and tips; strands, hair cards or a fur shader go on top |
| Pale inner coat | ear cups, inner fan, chest | pale cream fur inside the cups and on the chest | the geometry already carries a pale material split for the cups |
| Coat color and markings | whole figure | base coat color, darker tips and the markings on the sheet | none modeled |
| Eye color and highlights | eyes | iris color, the large dark iris, catch lights | geometry owns the eye shape, size and the lids |
| Nose and pad color | nose, paw pads | dark nose leather and pads | a separate nose material exists |
| Fine creases | paws, face | toe creases, lip line detail | only creases that change the outline are geometry |

## Fur stage log

**Step 1, white coat between stripes (2026-10-14).** New palette T5 in `art/species-construction/surface/akinza-palettes.json`: T4 with the blue tipping removed (`tip` #7DB8E8, `tipFrom` .025, `tipTo` .045, `tipEnds` [.75, 1]); every other value, the stripes and the ear-cup blue included, is T4's. T5 is the default for the current look (`stripe_swatch.py` default too); T4 stays as the color-pass record. Measured on the renders (mean sRGB, blue minus red): ear backs 16.9 to 4.7, crown 8.9 to 4.2, tail between rings 9.8 to 6.7, against 4.9 on the white of the back, so the blue cast is gone. Brightness: at the old light-path limits (max 4, diffuse 2, glossy 2, transmission 2) the long fur read gray (ear backs 145, crown 146 against the back's 204) because dense white fur lost its light; forcing the hair diffuse share to .95 moved the ears only 3 levels. `render_surface.py` now renders with max 64, diffuse 16, glossy 48, transmission 48, transparent 64 (32/8/16/16/32 reached ears 197). Same render, luminance: ear backs 204 and crown top 194 against the back's 228, nape 195, tails between rings 236; the fold where the ears meet the skull stays at 164 (form shadow). About 1.4 times the render time. Also fixed: `apply_palette`'s `diffuseShare` set input 2 of every float mix, which overwrote the lock-roughness mix (coat roughness .45 to .5); it now sets only the mix that feeds the hair/diffuse shader mix. Side effects: the stripes keep their color and placement but read a little brighter with the extra bounces, and white fur in shade picks up a cool blue cast bounced from the stripes and the ear-cup patch. Checks: `untracked/species-construction/akinza/surface/fur-steps/01-white-coat-{turnaround,ears,crown,tails}.png`.

**Step 2, ears (2026-10-14).** Cup blue widened (`cupInner` t0 .22 to .86, width .14) so it fills the cup; ear guides split by face (`splitFacing`: front and back of the thin ear get their own guide sets, 160 each per ear), which removed the clumps and dark pits on the backs (strands had interpolated guides rooted on the other face); long tufts along the rim from a new `rim` option (graph distance to where the faces meet, length .085, width .02), tips longer (length .025 to .1, power 2.2). Still off: a few long inner-rim strands lie across the blue cup. Checks: `fur-steps/02-ears-{front,back,rim}.png`.
