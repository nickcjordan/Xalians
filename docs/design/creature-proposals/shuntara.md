# Shuntara: Zolton current-router proposal

## Ratification boundary

Shuntara was ratified as species 00032 on 2026-09-15 after the identity, record, naming, portable-moveset, and concept-art reviews were completed. This proposal remains as the decision record behind the canonical template.

The proposal implements the approved distinction for Zolton: Voltish represents the world's minority storage lineage, Shuntara represents active conduction and redirection, and entangled pairs remain a rare specialized lineage for a later creature rather than a theme imposed on Electric fauna generally.

## Identity

The Shuntara is a low, broad, eight-legged organism with thick insulating hide and three terminal spinneret openings at the tip of its raised abdomen. It produces conductive filament internally from ordinary food and mineral salts. Its own metabolism supplies the working current that stiffens, tensions, and powers the filament after extrusion.

Its defining act is a physical sequence:

1. sense stable routes and dangerous current through its feet;
2. brace eight wide foot pads against the available surface;
3. raise the abdomen and cast filament from its visible terminal spinnerets;
4. tension the strands into a closed lattice;
5. distribute an impact or electrical load across many paths and return it through the insulated body.

On Zolton, this mechanism lets the creature bridge broken conductors and route much larger environmental currents while repairs proceed. Away from Zolton, neither a storm nor conductive terrain is needed: the filament reserve, working current, insulation, feet, and full physical sequence all belong to the creature.

## Signature

**Arcweave** uses `spinnerets / ward / electric` at intensity 35 to 80.

Plain behavior: the Shuntara casts a charged filament lattice around itself or another creature. Taut strands catch an incoming force and divide its load across many paths before closing the route through the Shuntara's insulated body.

The protection applies to impacts and other threats, not only electricity. Electricity is what gives the freshly cast strands their rapid tension and responsive routing; it is not a restriction on what the lattice may catch. A Zolton storm can feed more current into the same arrangement and increase its scale, but it does not unlock the move.

`spinnerets` directly permits `ward` in the physical instrument/action registry, so the signature needs neither an exception nor a conduit declaration.

## Ecological and cross-game integrity

The design preserves a meaningful home ecology without requiring a game to invent support equipment:

- Eight load-bearing legs support the body and provide ordinary ground movement.
- Gas respiration and the stated temperature band cover normal encounter conditions as well as Zolton's habitable region.
- The creature carries a normal-encounter reserve of internally produced filament.
- Metabolism provides the current needed for every move; storms and live conductors are amplifiers only.
- Closed lattice paths prevent the signature from requiring conductive ground.
- It can replenish filament through later feeding, but no external resource must appear during a normal encounter.

This does not make the Shuntara equally comfortable everywhere. Long-term health still depends on food with enough mineral content, Zolton's structures remain its best climbing and working environment, and extreme worlds can exceed its temperature or respiration tolerances. The design only ensures that placing it in an ordinary arena does not visibly contradict its body or erase its moveset.

## Quantitative shape

The range profile is intentionally defensive without becoming slow or universally durable:

- Endurance, resilience, instinct, and reflex carry the highest ceilings because maintaining tension and reacting before impact are the core tasks.
- Strength and vitality are solid but not dominant; eight legs distribute force rather than turning the creature into a bruiser.
- Climb is its only exceptional movement. Sprint remains viable, while leap, swim, and burrow cannot substitute for the main silhouette.
- Intelligence supports route choice without approaching Sonalloy's repair-specialist ceiling.
- Manipulation stops at 40 because the terminal spinnerets can place the creature's own filament but are not general-purpose grasping anatomy.
- Charisma is modest because protecting nearby beings is an engineered instinct, not leadership or social command.

At 50 to 75 centimeters tall and 55 to 105 kilograms, it reads as low and substantial rather than tiny, fragile, or monstrous. The broad stance is credible under Zolton's 1.59 Earth gravity while still allowing the creature to appear in ordinary encounter spaces.

Two traits are universal. The rolled portion totals exactly 100, creating one expected additional trait and an expected total of 3.0 traits before exclusions.

## Contrast audit

### Against Voltish

Voltish is a bipedal flesh-and-metal energy-feeder that stores environmental charge in alloy bones and releases it offensively through claws. Shuntara is an eight-legged flesh organism that metabolically generates working current for immediate use, emits filament through abdomen-tip spinnerets, and protects by dividing force across a temporary structure. Storage, claws, striking, metallic body composition, and energy-feeding are absent.

### Against Sonalloy

Sonalloy repairs material after a break by transferring living alloy and shaping it with pincers and tendrils. Shuntara does not mend a target or permanently replace lost structure. It lays a temporary bypass or shield that preserves function while danger is redirected. Its work is tension and routing, not restoration.

### Against Newtapede

Newtapede uses body coils to snare in an aquatic ecology. Shuntara uses emitted spinneret filament to ward in gas and on solid surfaces. Removing the names and elements still leaves constricting hunter versus anchored protector.

### Against the reserved entangled-pair lineage

Shuntaras may occupy the same damaged route, but they do not share identity, distance state, sensation, charge, or a required partner. Each powers a separate lattice and retains its complete behavior alone. This keeps entanglement rare and preserves room for a later species whose paired condition is genuinely defining.

`Shuntara` is pronounced **shun-TAR-uh**, three syllables. Its root identifies the creature's most distinctive purpose: creating a safe alternate route around a damaged or threatened path. The ending softens the technical root without adding a second false meaning.

The spelling has one expected pronunciation and does not collide with a current species. Its opening sound and `-ara` ending form a roster silhouette not currently used by another creature.

### Signature-title candidates

Candidates considered were `Nothing Reaches the Center`, `Thunderloom`, `Arc Bastion`, `Shuntweb`, `Livewire Aegis`, and `Arcweave`.

`Arcweave` is selected. The compact compound names the visible action and its charge without turning a descriptive sentence into a title. It also avoids implying that a storm is required: the `arc` comes from the Shuntara's internally generated working current.

## Review judgments after art

The proposal recommends these decisions as a package:

1. **Name:** `Shuntara`, built from `shunt`, pronounced **shun-TAR-uh**.
2. **Signature title:** `Arcweave`.
3. **Silhouette:** low shield-shaped octoped with eight separate jointed legs, rounded gripping pads, a raised pointed abdomen, three terminal spinneret openings, and a compact three-lobed crest. A recognizable spider association is welcome, while the shield body, crest, current markings, and current-routing purpose keep that association from becoming the complete design.
4. **Universal traits:** insulation and protection are species-defining; anchoring, perception, and hardening vary.
5. **Electroreception:** retained as a practical current-routing sense, not as entanglement or storm dependence.
6. **No conduit declaration:** the legal physical signature is narrower and more coherent without one.
7. **Portable baseline:** internal filament, self-generated current, closed paths, gas respiration, and load-bearing legs keep the full creature usable away from Zolton.

These judgments held through the concept-art and art-to-record consistency passes. The canonical record and image now describe the same body and filament path.

## Art consistency

The approved concept and record describe the same creature. The body is supported by exactly eight independently traceable legs, each ending in one unnotched rounded gripping pad. White ventral pigmentation remains subordinate to the black leg silhouettes so it cannot imply crossed or fused limbs. The matte black raised abdomen carries two broad angular current paths that converge visually toward three terminal spinneret openings, and exactly three filament strands establish the emission source. The white face, simple eyes, and compact three-lobed swept crest provide character at small scale without adding fur, ears, horns, antennae, or a special visual sense.

The spinnerets moved from the underside of the early prose sketch to the raised abdomen tip during holistic visual review. This was an intentional anatomy decision rather than prose being rewritten to excuse an accidental render: the terminal placement makes the filament source visible, reachable, and compositionally coherent while preserving the approved mechanism and registry instrument.
