## Structural targeting queue

These are prompts for the next design brief, not instructions to force a creature into every empty cell. A target should close two to four coherent gaps without contradicting its world, ecology, silhouette, or central ability.

1. **Roster shortages:** elements/worlds below two species: electric (1), metal (1).
2. **Unused reusable species vocabulary:** composition (primary or secondary): energy; body plan: piscine; covering: crystal; diet: photosynthetic; lifespan: fleeting; lifespan: enduring; special senses: echolocation; special senses: heat-sense; archetype: balanced; trait pool: inspiring. These are prompts, not quotas.
3. **Singleton reusable species vocabulary:** composition (primary or secondary): plant; composition (primary or secondary): metal; composition (primary or secondary): slime; composition (primary or secondary): gas; composition (primary or secondary): spectral; body plan: avian; body plan: amorphous; body plan: swarm; covering: scales; diet: herbivore; ambient media: vacuum; special senses: electroreception; special senses: void-sense; trait pool: dormant; trait pool: commanding; trait pool: reflective. Confirm whether rarity is intentional before targeting it.
4. **Unused signature actions:** lash, crush, mend. These are identity opportunities, not required slots.
5. **Available but unused instruments/channels:** antlers, tongue, stinger, spinnerets, light-organs, core, antennae, breath, aura. Select only when the body or ability demands one.
6. **Intentional non-target:** `vacuum` is valid ambient tolerance but not a breathing target; a creature that does not breathe uses an empty list.
7. **Distribution checks:** review low archetype exposure, trait landed share, capability centers, and attribute centers through generated batches before calling any of them balance problems.
8. **Collision checks:** every proposed signature must add a distinct instrument/action/medium identity and retain adequate catalog-name reachability.
