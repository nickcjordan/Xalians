## Structural targeting queue

These are prompts for the next design brief, not instructions to force a creature into every empty cell. A target should close two to four coherent gaps without contradicting its world, ecology, silhouette, or central ability.

1. **Roster shortages:** elements/worlds below two species: none.
2. **Unused reusable species vocabulary:** composition (primary or secondary): energy; covering: crystal; diet: photosynthetic; lifespan: fleeting; special senses: heat-sense. These are prompts, not quotas.
3. **Singleton reusable species vocabulary:** composition (primary or secondary): plant; composition (primary or secondary): slime; composition (primary or secondary): gas; composition (primary or secondary): spectral; body plan: avian; body plan: piscine; body plan: amorphous; body plan: swarm; covering: scales; diet: herbivore; ambient media: vacuum; lifespan: enduring; special senses: echolocation; special senses: void-sense; special senses: lowlight. Confirm whether rarity is intentional before targeting it.
4. **Available but unused instruments/channels:** tongue, stinger, light-organs, core, breath. Select only when the body or ability demands one.
5. **Intentional non-target:** `vacuum` is valid ambient tolerance but not a breathing target; a creature that does not breathe uses an empty list.
6. **Distribution checks:** review capability and attribute centers through generated batches (`npm run simulate:creatures`) before calling any of them balance problems.
7. **Act-space checks:** every proposed species must clear the derived-act audit (`docs/species-templates/ABILITY-AUDIT.md`) and `npm run check:creature-model`.
