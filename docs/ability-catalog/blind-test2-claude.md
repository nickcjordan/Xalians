# Blind Record Analysis — blind-test-record-v2.json (species: graviclaw)

## 1. The picture

This is a large, armored, crab-or-lobster-like creature — a "multiped" body plan with "pincers" and "walking-legs" as its only two named anatomical features, covered in chitin rather than skin, fur, or scales. At 198 cm tall and 372 kg, it is dense and heavy for its height — bulk consistent with thick armor plating and load-bearing limbs rather than a lean predator build. It is amphibious in a specific sense: it breathes both gas and liquid and tolerates both as ambient media, and its capability profile (swim 52, burrow 31, climb 18, sprint 12, leap 4) reads as a creature built for moving through water, mud, or loose substrate rather than open ground — it is slow and a poor jumper on land (sprint 12, leap 4) but a competent digger and swimmer. It likely inhabits cold, wet, low-visibility terrain: the tolerance band tops out at 20°C and bottoms at -15°C, and its senses lean on smell (47) and especially a special sense, tremorsense, over hearing (28) — it "feels" prey through vibration in substrate or water rather than seeing or hearing it (sight 55 is middling-high, so it isn't blind, but tremorsense plus vibration-based communication suggests a creature equally or more at home when it can't see).

Temperamentally it is bold (81), aggressive (66), and not very social (22) or energetic (25) — a solitary ambush predator that doesn't flee but also doesn't burn a lot of activity; it waits, and when it commits, it commits hard. Its attribute spread confirms this: resilience 93 and willpower 78 dwarf agility (14) and charisma (19) — this is a tank, not a skirmisher, and its own archetype tag agrees explicitly ("stalwart," favoring "resilience" and "willpower"). Strength (74) and instinct (71) are also high, intelligence is middling (42), so it is a powerful, instinct-driven brute rather than a tactician.

Its signature capability is unambiguous and vivid: it manipulates localized gravity through its claws. The signature ability, "Point of No Return," is described in prose — the only ability with a description — as collapsing gravitational waves into a point inside an open claw to drag prey into the closing pincer: a miniature, bespoke black hole used as a hunting mechanism. The other three abilities (undescribed, so partly inferred from name/action/medium) extend that same theme: "Wraith Vise" crushes with a ghost-flavored grip (a claw attack that ignores or bypasses something physical, given the ghost medium); "Null Grasp" is a mind-instrument, dark-medium snare — a psychic or supernatural pin rather than a physical one, meaning this creature can immobilize prey without touching it; "Event Horizon" is a dark, pincer-based "ward" — almost certainly a defensive gravity-field or damage-shield effect, continuing the black-hole metaphor into a protective use. All three non-signature abilities are meaningfully weaker in intensity (48, 33, 41) than the signature (74), consistent with the signature being its headline, most-honed trick.

Its two guaranteed traits, "armored" and "anchored," reinforce the tank archetype — armored plainly reflects the chitin/high-resilience build, and anchored (paired with sprint 12 / leap 4 and burrow 31) suggests it plants itself and is hard to displace or knock back, a natural complement to a creature that pulls things toward itself rather than chasing them. "Stealthy" is a rolled (i.e., randomly assigned, non-guaranteed) trait, which sits oddly against boldness 81 and aggression 66 — see Section 4. It has a dextro genome chirality (mirror-handedness of its biochemistry — presumably meaningful only in comparison to other creatures with the opposite chirality) and an "enduring" lifespan, i.e., long-lived. Its element is dark (primary, 100 affinity) with a secondary ghost affinity (44) — consistent with three of four abilities being dark-medium and one being ghost-medium. Appearance is reduced to a single word, "gleam" (a finish/sheen on its chitin, likely dark and glossy given the element). It originates from a place called "grimedes," which the record treats as an opaque origin tag with no further description.

Where I am guessing rather than reading: that it is aquatic/mud-dwelling specifically (inferred from swim+burrow+breathes+temperature, not stated); that "anchored" means resistance to displacement (the trait has no definition in the record, only a name); that ghost-medium abilities "bypass" physicality (inferred from the word "ghost" and genre convention, not defined); and that ambush-predator behavior follows from the temperament numbers (a reasonable but not certain read of boldness+aggression+low energy+low sociability).

## 2. Uninterpretable fields

- **`provenance.origin: "grimedes"`** — a bare string with no gazetteer. Could be a planet, a region, a biome, or a lab/batch designation. Nothing in the record says which, or what "grimedes" implies environmentally (though the temperature/media tolerances happen to be consistent with a dark, cold, wet reading of the name). Disambiguator: a lookup table or document defining known origin values and their properties.
- **`provenance.seed` / `generatorVersion` / `schemaVersion` / `serial`** — clearly plumbing for a procedural generation pipeline, but it's a guess that "seed" drives randomization and "serial" is a sequence/batch index rather than, say, an in-world identifier. Disambiguator: generator source code or a spec describing the provenance block's semantics.
- **`archetype.key: "stalwart"` / `favors`** — is "favors" descriptive (a post-hoc summary of the rolled attributes) or prescriptive (a template that weighted the attribute roll)? The record doesn't say whether archetype causes the attribute spread or merely labels it. Disambiguator: whether other "stalwart" records show consistent favors and attribute patterns, or a generator spec.
- **`covering: "chitin"` vs `composition.primary: "flesh"`** — two separate fields for material composition with no defined relationship. Does covering sit "on top of" flesh as an exoskeleton, or are they alternate classification axes (e.g., composition = what it fundamentally is, covering = surface texture) that could in principle contradict each other for some other record? Disambiguator: an enum/schema definition, or a record where the two fields are in obvious tension.
- **`appearance.finish: "gleam"`** — one word standing in for the entirety of visual presentation. Is this a color, a texture, a material sheen, or a categorical enum value from a small fixed list (like "matte"/"gleam"/"dull")? No color, pattern, or shape information exists anywhere. Disambiguator: a controlled vocabulary for `finish`, or an accompanying color/pattern field on another record.
- **`temperament.energy: 25`** — energy could mean metabolic activity level, willingness to initiate combat, or literal stamina/endurance-in-combat (which would double up with `attributes.endurance`). Low energy plus high boldness is a plausible "ambush predator" read, but the field could equally be tracking something like "playfulness" in a more sanitized taxonomy. Disambiguator: a second record with an obviously mismatched energy/boldness pair that forces a single consistent definition.
- **`abilities[].instrument: "mind"`** (Null Grasp) — for a creature whose only named anatomy is pincers and walking-legs, an ability channeled through "mind" is uninterpretable as physical action. Is this telepathy, a psychic organ not listed in `anatomy`, or is "mind" simply the schema's placeholder for "no physical instrument, treat as an innate/passive effect"? Disambiguator: an anatomy entry for a sensory/psychic organ, or a schema note on what "mind" as an instrument means.
- **`action: "ward"`** (Event Horizon) — unlike "snare" and "crush," which read as offensive verbs matching the description style of the signature ability, "ward" is ambiguous between "creates a protective barrier" and "wards off/repels" as an attack. No description field is present to disambiguate, and the other three abilities all lack descriptions too, so this is representative of a broader problem (see 3).
- **`genome.chirality: "dextro"`** — presumably meaningful in a system where chirality affects compatibility (breeding, disease, digestion of other creatures) but nothing in this single record shows what dextro vs. some implied "levo" opposite actually does.

## 3. Missing aspects

- **Ability descriptions for 3 of 4 abilities.** Only the signature ability has prose; "Wraith Vise," "Null Grasp," and "Event Horizon" are reduced to name + instrument + action + medium + intensity, which is not enough to actually stage or narrate them (what does a "ward" via pincers look/feel like? what happens when Null Grasp hits?). A comprehensive record should either describe all abilities or make clear that non-signature abilities are deliberately terse by design.
- **Diet detail beyond "carnivore."** No indication of prey size, hunting strategy (ambush vs. pursuit — though inferable), or whether it's a solitary or pack hunter (sociability 22 suggests solitary, but that's an attribute value doing double duty for a behavioral fact that isn't stated outright).
- **Reproduction/lifecycle.** "Enduring" lifespan and "dextro" chirality gesture at biology, but there's nothing on how it reproduces, whether it has distinct life stages/molts (odd for a chitin-covered creature, where molting is often a defining trait), or juvenile vs. adult form.
- **Social structure beyond a single sociability number.** Solitary vs. territorial vs. hierarchical pack behavior isn't captured — just a 0-100 scalar.
- **Relationship to other creatures / ecological role.** No predators-of / prey-of, no rivalry or symbiosis data, nothing situating it in a food web or narrative context.
- **Voice/vocalization detail.** `communication: ["vibration"]` tells us the channel but not the content — is it just prey-detection (which tremorsense already covers) or actual signaling to conspecifics? Given sociability of 22, it's unclear this creature communicates with others at all, which makes listing "communication" as a capability slightly puzzling.
- **Any visual/color information beyond one word.** No color, pattern, markings, or size-relative-to-species-average context. "Gleam" cannot support illustration on its own.
- **Combat role/weaknesses.** Type effectiveness, elemental resistances/weaknesses, or explicit strengths-vs-weaknesses framing is entirely absent — we only have raw affinity numbers (dark 100, ghost 44) with no stated implication for what beats or is beaten by this creature.
- **Naming/identity beyond a UUID-like id and species string.** No individual name, no indication of whether "graviclaw" specimens are meaningfully distinct from each other beyond stat rolls, no rarity/tier marker.
- **History/origin story.** "Grimedes" as an origin tag carries none of the narrative weight a "comprehensive codification" might promise — no sense of how this creature came to exist, evolved, or was created (natural species vs. engineered).

## 4. Internal tensions

- **"Stealthy" (rolled trait) vs. boldness 81, aggression 66, and a chitin "gleam" finish.** A bold, aggressive creature with a glossy/reflective exterior is a poor fit for stealth in the conventional sense. This could be reconciled (ambush stealth is compatible with boldness once committed — the tension is about approach, not the strike itself), but the record gives no ability or behavior that actually uses stealth (no ability has a "conceal" or "ambush" action tag), so the trait floats disconnected from everything else in the sheet.
- **`communication: ["vibration"]` vs. `temperament.sociability: 22`.** A creature that communicates is generally understood to be communicating with something — conspecifics, presumably. A sociability score this low sits awkwardly next to having a dedicated communication channel; if vibration communication is purely for hunting (detecting/luring prey) rather than social signaling, the field is arguably mislabeled, or the two fields are tracking unrelated things that happen to look related.
- **`capabilities.manipulation: 44` (middling) vs. an ability set that is almost entirely pincer/claw-based fine action** (snaring, crushing, closing around a point of collapsed gravity). Manipulation at 44 out of what appears to be a 0-100 scale is unremarkable, yet the signature ability specifically requires collapsing gravity waves "inside its open claw" and orchestrating a precise closing motion — a feat that reads as requiring high dexterity/control, not average manipulation.
- **`attributes.agility: 14` vs. `capabilities.leap: 4` and `sprint: 12` all being very low, but `capabilities.swim: 52` being comparatively high.** This is internally consistent as "clumsy on land, capable in water," but the record never states this creature is aquatic or amphibious as a headline fact — it must be reconstructed from five separate numeric fields, and a careless reader could easily average agility down against swim and conclude a merely "sluggish" creature rather than a specifically land-slow/water-capable one.
- **Two elemental affinities (dark 100, ghost 44) but abilities span three mediums total if you count precisely** — three abilities are dark-medium, one is ghost-medium, and none are any other medium, so the affinities line up fine numerically. But "Wraith Vise" (ghost medium, the creature's weaker secondary affinity) is listed second among four abilities and has no signature flag, while intensity ordering otherwise roughly tracks dark-affinity abilities being strongest (74, 41, 33) except that Wraith Vise (48, ghost) actually outranks two of the dark abilities in intensity despite ghost being the weaker affinity (44 vs. 100 for dark). If higher affinity is supposed to predict higher ability intensity, this ability breaks that pattern.
- **High willpower (78) and instinct (71) alongside only middling intelligence (42).** Not necessarily contradictory, but it creates tension with "Point of No Return"'s description, which implies a fairly sophisticated, precise application of gravitational manipulation ("collapses gravitational waves into a point") — a feat that reads as requiring more calculation/understanding than a 42 intelligence score (roughly average-to-below on a 0-100 scale where other stats reach 90+) would suggest. This may be intentional (instinctive mastery rather than intellectual understanding of its own power) but the record doesn't say so.
