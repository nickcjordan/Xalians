# Flags (Section 8)

**Dual-element candidates (GHOST — decay/miasma register):** Rot Drain, Rot Sap, Decay Drain, Decay Sap, Miasma Drain, Miasma Siphon, Putrid Drain, Festering Drain, Festering Sap, Draining Miasma (drain cell); Miasma Ambush [rejected, listed for visibility] (ambush cell); Miasma Beam / Vapor Beam [both rejected as definition-dishonest, listed for visibility] (beam cell); Rot, Decay, Necrotic, Gangrenous rows generally across all four actions carry the standing GHOST dual-candidate flag per Step 0 even where individual cells rejected.

**Dual-element candidates (WATER — liquid-medium/phase):** Solvent Drain, Toxic Undertow, Sludge Undertow, Caustic Undertow, Acid Undertow (drain cell); Solvent Ray (beam cell); Toxic Wave [rejected — crosses into water's own vocabulary, see beam/hurl notes] (hurl cell); Toxic Scald (beam cell, also FIRE-adjacent).

**Dual-element candidates (FIRE — burn-sense words used in chemical scope):** Toxic Sear, Acid Sear, Corrosive Sear, Toxic Scald, Toxic Flare, Acid Flare — all scoped per Step 0 to the chemical-burn/reaction sense, not literal flame; flagged for cross-check against the FIRE composer's own cells to avoid true duplication of the flame-sense meaning.

**Dual-element candidates (ROCK — mineral/corrosion-byproduct):** Rust Ray (beam), Scale Hurl, Crust Toss (hurl) — scoped to corrosion-byproduct sense per Step 0, not literal mineral/geological sense.

**Borrowed-name suspicions (flagged, kept):**
- Acid Rain (hurl) — real-world environmental-science term, not a franchise move; kept per brief's borrowed-name rule (franchise moves only), flagged for designer awareness since it is an extremely recognizable phrase.
- Toxic Shock (ambush) — recognizable real-world medical syndrome name (Toxic Shock Syndrome); flagged prominently, kept since it is not a franchise game-move name.
- Toxic Spike (beam) — adjacent to the well-known Pokemon move "Toxic Spikes" (plural/hazard-tile form); the singular non-hazard beam-sense usage is a different collocation and mechanic, kept but flagged prominently for designer review.
- Acid Rush (ambush) — flagged for a possible modern-idiom overtone ("adrenaline rush" register bleed); kept, not a franchise move, definitionally sound.
- Toxic Missile / Venom Missile (hurl) — flagged per harvest source's own borderline caution on "Missile"; kept since harvest explicitly sanctions pre-gunpowder senses and these are not exact franchise-move matches.

**Signature-register names (moved out of cells, released to signature pool):**
- Alembic Drain
- Crucible Drain
- Essence Distillate (drain — flagged signature-adjacent in standalone section, kept plain in final list but noted here for designer visibility as borderline)

**Cross-cell definition tensions (flagged, both placements kept):**
- Venom Bound (ambush) — reads ambiguously against a snare/restraint sense ("bound by venom"); kept in ambush under the springing-leap definition, flagged.
- Venom Bolt appears in both the AMBUSH cell (closing-speed dash sense) and the HURL cell (thrown-projectile sense) — same string, genuinely different definitions per action; both kept, not a true duplicate per brief's per-action cell scoping.
- Acid Spear / Acid Shaft (beam) carry tension with the hurl field's own thrown-spear vocabulary; kept in beam under the directed-continuous-line definition, cross-referenced.
- Acid Skewer / Toxic Skewer / Venom Skewer (beam) carry tension toward strike/rake (piercing-thrust sense); kept in beam per harvest-action-beam.md's own explicit inclusion of Skewer in its verb list, definition-honest as a piercing line.

- Toxic Bolt / Venom Bolt (beam candidates considered, ultimately not placed in beam cell — see beam cell's cross-referenced rejection of "Acid Train" and general avoidance of "Bolt" in beam to prevent triple-collision across ambush/hurl/beam; Bolt was reserved for ambush's closing-speed sense and hurl's thrown-quarrel sense per canon note that Bolt/projectile verbs "home in hurl" per the standing ruling in the brief).
