## 5. Excluded as other-domain / borrowed

**Other-domain exclusions**
- Combustion, Flame, Blaze, Scorch (literal-flame sense), Ignite, Inferno, Ember, Char, Cinder — fire-domain words; only the reaction-word sense of combustion was kept as a chemistry-process noun ("combustion" listed in nouns strictly as a chemical reaction term, not as a flame-based ability name)
- Solvent, Solution, Dissolve when used loosely as "watery" — kept here because their true chemical definition is a lab-process term, not a water-element term; flagged for cross-check against the WATER field to avoid duplication
- Deluge, Torrent, Flood, Tide, Wave, Current, Splash (as a pure water-impact term) — excluded, these are water-domain
- Steam, Boil (as pure heat/water-vapor terms) — excluded to fire/water; kept "Simmering," "Seething," "Bubbling" only in the chemical-reaction sense (effervescence), not as heat-of-water terms
- Contagion, Plague, Epidemic, Pandemic, Curse, Hex, Jinx — excluded as disease/curse register that reads supernatural rather than chemical-biological; kept "Infect," "Blight," "Wither," "Rot," "Decompose," "Putrefy" because these describe chemical-biological decay processes rather than curse-like affliction
- Spore, Mold, Mildew, Infest — borderline biological/fungal-domain words; included narrowly only where they double as toxin-delivery or decay vocabulary consistent with venom/rot; flag for review against a possible PLANT or GHOST/decay field
- Necrotic, Gangrenous, Septic — borderline medical/decay register; kept because they describe literal tissue-chemical breakdown (necrosis is a biochemical process), not curse-flavored death magic
