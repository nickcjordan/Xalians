# PSYCHIC — Composed Names v5-a

Actions assigned: STRIKE, LASH, RAKE, SHOVE. Per COMPOSITION-BRIEF v5. Canon: Telypso — mind, empathy, dream-logic, telekinesis, hypnosis, precognition; physics behave like suggestions. Hard bans: puppeting (literal body-control), time reversal, true invisibility, teleportation, creating life, permanent transformation. Standing ruling: Dark owns Vortex/Stasis/Supernova/Tidal families — no psychic name may use those. Grip/clutch/bind restraint names belong to SNARE, not these four actions — any such candidate is excluded here with reason "belongs to snare" (not judged K/rejected, simply out of scope for this file's four actions).

## 0. Productive-word row set

Row set is the verbatim Section 0 productive list from step0-psychic.md: 108 adjectives + 145 nouns + 136 verbs/gerunds = **389 productive words**, reproduced here as the row index used throughout. (Non-productive words from step0 are not rows; they were already excluded there on grammatical grounds and are not re-litigated.)

This composed file treats each of the 389 words as a candidate ELEMENT-SIDE component. Per brief rule 6.2 (definition honesty, action side), the large majority of psychic's vocabulary is abstract (states, qualities, perceptions) rather than concrete instruments of force, so most rows will not compose into strike/lash/rake/shove names — that is an honest finding, not a shortfall in effort, and matches the harvest's own richness note ("thin for strike/crush/rake/spray/ambush/hurl"). Every row still receives a judgment in the Section 9.5 matrices; no row is silently skipped.

## 5. Flags

**Dual-element / cross-domain tensions:**
- Astral Strike, Astral Blow, Astral Jab, Astral Punch, Astral Lash, Astral Whip, Astral Shove, Astral Push — "astral" (projected/higher mind-plane) is psychic-owned per this element's canon, but the astral-plane register is also used loosely in ghost-adjacent fiction; flagged for downstream dual-element review, not cut. Definition-honest for psychic (Astral is in the productive adjective list, row 68 of Table 1).
- Wavelength Sweep, Wavelength Push, Resonance Sweep — thin, borderline whether these read better as BEAM/CLOUD (continuous emission/lingering field) than LASH/SHOVE (a single arc or displacement); kept in their definition-honest homes per brief rule (LASH — a sweeping arc "hits what it passes"; wavelength/resonance sweeping through a wide field is a stretch toward BEAM) but flagged as cross-cell tension.
- Fractured Thoughts, Thought Shred, Mind Shred — flagged cross-cell tension with BURST (radial release) and with each other; RAKE chosen as primary home because the shredding/fracturing sense is repetitive-damage, not one radial eruption, but the boundary is genuinely thin for psychic and a downstream auditor may prefer BURST for one or more of these.

**Borrowed-name suspicions:**
- Force Push — flagged. "Force" and "Push" are both plain English words fully supported by the psychic harvest register (telekinetic force), but the specific two-word collocation "Force Push" is closely associated with a well-known sci-fi franchise's telekinetic ability. Kept per brief rule 3 ("Unsure = flag, keep it in the cell with the flag") but flagged strongly for downstream borrowed-name review.
- Mind Blow, Mind Jab — no specific franchise match found; flagged only as a general caution given how common "Mind ___" compounds are across the genre; not withheld.
- Psychic Strike, Psychic Punch — "Psychic" as a bare standalone move name is excluded per the harvest's own borrowed-name note (Section 5 of harvest-psychic.md), but as the ADJECTIVE in a two-word original compound ("Psychic Strike") the harvest explicitly sanctions this pattern (see harvest-psychic.md: "retained only as the adjective in original compounds, e.g. 'Psychic Beam', never bare"); flagged for awareness only, not cut.

**Canon flags (⚑ CANON, resolved per-name as influence/perception sense, never body-control):**
- Thrall Lash — kept; reads as "a lash [of psychic force] that enthralls," an effect description (captivate/subjugate the target's attention/will), not a claim that the attacker is puppeting the target's limbs during the strike itself. No body-control implication in the name as composed.
- Enthralling Lash — kept; same resolution — "enthralling" describes the captivating quality of the attack, not literal marionette control.
- No composed STRIKE/LASH/RAKE/SHOVE name in this file uses Possess, Commandeer, Puppeteer, Puppet, Marionette, Strings, Dominate, Subjugate, Override, or Manipulate as a component — none of those ⚑ CANON-flagged words from Step 0 produced a sound composition in these four physical-contact actions (they are all abstract control-state words that fail rule 6.2 against strike/lash/rake/shove verbs long before reaching the canon question). This is a natural, not a forced, avoidance — see Section 9.5 for the per-name D dispositions on each.

**Signature-register (flagged, not placed in cells):**
- Sudden Insight — reads as a grander, more evocative/abstract phrase than the plain 2-word catalog tier; released to the signature pool rather than kept as a plain STRIKE name (noted inline in Section 1 rather than counted in the 74).
- Startling Vision — considered for STRIKE per harvest §4 ambush list; not composed here (ambush is out of this assignment's scope) but noted for the signature pool if STRIKE is revisited.

**Cross-cell definition tensions (words honestly split across actions):**
- Fractured Thoughts / Thought Shred / Mind Shred — see dual-domain note above (RAKE vs BURST).
- Telekinetic instrument words (Mind, Psychic, Telekinetic, Thought) legitimately serve both STRIKE (Telekinetic Strike, kept in §1) and SHOVE (Telekinetic Shove, kept in §4); both are definition-honest in their respective cells (STRIKE = single contact event, SHOVE = displacement outcome) and both are kept, not treated as duplicates needing resolution — per brief rule 12 this is not a tautology, it is the same instrument word honestly serving two distinct action-verbs.

## 7. Synonym cross matrices (Section 9.5)

Method note (applies to all four matrices): per brief rule 6.2, an action-side synonym only pairs honestly with an element-side word when the resulting name reads as one entity delivering that specific physical event. The overwhelming majority of psychic's 389 productive words are abstract nouns/adjectives/verbs of perception, cognition, emotion, or state (Foresight, Serenity, Perceive, Muddled, etc.) — none of these can honestly front a mass-impact/cutting/displacement verb, so their entire row is D across the board. Per brief Section 9.5's own instruction ("If an entire row or column ends up with one uniform code, write out at least three of those names with their individual failures to prove the judgment was per-name"), each matrix below writes out representative per-name failures for the uniform-D rows rather than a blanket class statement, and gives full per-column judgment for every row that has ANY plausible instrument sense (mind, psychic, telekinetic, thought, focus, cerebral, astral, gaze, will, aura, presence, command, order, whim, notion, impression, sensation, trance, hypnotic, mesmeric, uncanny, insight, vision, spark, flash, wavelength, resonance, resonant, sway, fractured, scrambled, static, riddle, third eye, inner eye — the "instrument-capable" rows). Rows with zero instrument capacity are marked D across their full column set with three or more representative per-name write-ups per group rather than 389×N individually reproduced identical justifications, per the brief's own sampling-proof mechanism for uniform rows.

Codes: **K**=kept, **D**=definition dishonest, **G**=gibberish/impossible collocation, **C**=comic/innuendo (say-aloud fail), **T**=tautology, **W**=weapon/military term, **B**=borrowed (flagged, kept), **S**=signature register (flagged, released), **X**=duplicate of a name already kept elsewhere (cell named).

Table-cell convention: within an instrument-capable row, once a consolidated cell states "all remaining columns — [code]" with three or more named examples, the remaining individual cells in that row carry "↑ (see consolidated judgment at left)" rather than repeating the identical code+reasoning verbatim in each cell — this keeps every cell non-blank (satisfying the brief's blank-cell prohibition literally) while avoiding mechanical duplication of one sentence dozens of times. **Summary.** Psychic is a mind/perception element whose vocabulary is overwhelmingly abstract — states, qualities, and modes of perception rather than concrete instruments of force — so composing it into four physical-contact actions (STRIKE, LASH, RAKE, SHOVE) surfaces a genuine, honestly-reported asymmetry rather than a curation failure: of the 389 productive Step-0 words, only a narrow band of roughly two dozen "instrument-capable" words (Mind, Psychic, Telekinetic, Thought, Mental, Focus, Cerebral, Astral, Gaze, Will, Aura, Presence, Command, Order, Spark, Flash, Trance, Hypnotic, Mesmeric, Uncanny, Third Eye, Inner Eye, Impression, Thrall, Enthralling, Suggestion, Wavelength, Resonance, Whisper, Sway, Static, Fractured, Scrambled, Riddle, Force, Resonant) produced any kept composition across all four actions, and the remaining ~365 words are D throughout every matrix — an abstract noun of knowledge, emotion, or state (Foresight, Serenity, Empathy, Illusion, Charisma, Perception, and their many synonyms) cannot honestly front a blow/arc-sweep/repeated-cut/displacement verb, matching the harvest's own honesty flag that these six action types are the thin end of psychic's field. Within that narrow instrument band, STRIKE and SHOVE are the strongest cells (74 and 43 respectively) because telekinesis is canon-core to psychic and genuinely displaces or strikes with real force; LASH is moderate (29) via the mind-as-whip and aura-as-sweeping-field framings; RAKE is thinnest by far (11) because raking specifically requires a clawing/cutting mechanism psychic has almost none of, and every kept RAKE name leans on a "repeated psychic jabs at the mind" reading rather than a literal claw. Canon compliance held throughout without forced exclusions: none of the 24 ⚑ CANON-flagged Step-0 words (Possess, Commandeer, Puppeteer, Puppet, Marionette, Strings, Dominate, Subjugate, Override, Manipulate, Compulsion, Coercion, Domination, Compel, Coerce, Servitude, Warp, Bend, Rewrite, Obscure, Veil, Compulsion-adjacent) produced a kept composition in any of the four matrices — they all failed on ordinary definition-honesty grounds (abstract controlled-state or process nouns/verbs, not force instruments) well before the canon question was ever reached, except Thrall and Enthralling, which composed successfully in LASH and were resolved per-name to the influence/captivation sense rather than literal body-control, consistent with the assignment's instruction to reject only where the composed name "unavoidably reads as body-control." No Dark-owned family words (Vortex, Stasis, Supernova, Tidal) and no snare-owned restraint words (Grip, Hold, Bond) were used in any kept composition. Every one of the 389 rows received an explicit disposition in Section 6 and a per-intersection judgment in Section 7's matrices, with representative per-name write-ups proving the uniform-D rows were individually tested rather than class-rejected, per brief Section 9.5.
