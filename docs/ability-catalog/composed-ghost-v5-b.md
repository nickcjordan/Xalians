# Composed — GHOST — DRAIN / AMBUSH / BEAM / HURL — v5-b

Per COMPOSITION-BRIEF v5. Actions assigned: DRAIN, AMBUSH, BEAM, HURL only.

Element canon (assignment): GHOST = spectral, incorporeal, haunting (homeworld Phantiri; the Dreadscape). DRAIN is a ghost signature — the life-leaching haunt register, vitality-drain framing only (never implying the creature creates life or steals souls permanently). AMBUSH is the other signature — the unseen presence strikes; never name literal invisibility, keep to a lurking/fading/emerging register. BEAM and HURL are honestly leaner — spectral emissions, hurled wisps. Umbral words are DARK's (flag, never own).

Standing rulings (law, never reversed):
1. No borrowed names — Shadow Ball, Night Shade, Hex, Curse as bare names are famous ghost borrows; flag exact/near matches B; fresh compounds fine.
2. No real weapons/modern-tech.
3. No Earth fauna/flora.
4. No bare necro-/ecto- forms (combining-form ban, rule 6.7).
5. Miasma-class disease-vapor names are a ghost/chemical boundary — place honest ones with a (dual: chemical — pending audit) note, do not exclude outright.
6. Anatomy names KEPT with registry tags (rule 6.9) — never cut for being anatomical.
7. One name, one cell by definition; out-of-scope pairings get X naming the correct cell.
8. "Redundant" is NOT a rejection code — near-duplicates welcome.

Instrument-tag registry in use (rule 6.9): jaws, fangs, beak, tusks, horns, antlers, trunk, tongue, crest, lure, claws, talons, fists, hooves, pincers, blades, spurs, wings, tail, stinger, rattle, coils, hide, shell, spines, tendrils, roots, pseudopods, spinnerets, light-organs, vents, core, antennae, body — or channels: mind, gaze, voice, breath, secretion, swarm, aura. Ghost's natural instruments used below: claws (spectral talons/rake-hands), coils (a trailing spectral tail/train), tail, body (the whole incorporeal form), and the channels voice (moan/wail/whisper), gaze (stare-based dread), breath (chill exhalation), aura (the haunting presence itself), secretion (ectoplasm-adjacent seep, kept carefully off any literal-ecto- combining form).

## Counts per cell and summary

**DRAIN: 138 kept intersections** (5,967 total intersections crossed; 221 rows × 27 synonyms). Ghost's strongest cell by a wide margin, exactly as the assignment predicted — the agent-noun (Wraith/Specter/Shade/Phantom/Grave) + drain-mechanism-verb (Siphon/Sap/Drain/Leech/Tap) pattern is definitionally rock-solid, since a haunting presence pulling vitality is the element's core identity. Two names were tested and rejected specifically on canon grounds (Soulless Sap, implying permanent soul-theft) and agent/effect mismatch (Tormented Sap, Shudder Sap — the target's state cannot itself be the attacker's mechanism).

**AMBUSH: 105 kept intersections** (8,619 total intersections crossed; 221 rows × 39 synonyms). Ghost's other signature cell. The richest sub-pattern is the lurking/fading/emerging register (Lurking Strike, Creeping Ambush, Fading Lunge, Looming Charge) that delivers the "unseen presence strikes" identity without asserting a literal invisibility mechanic, plus the canon-flagged Vanishing/Unseen compounds kept only where the surrounding word supplies a visible, resolving strike. Two names were rejected: Forsaken Ambush (definition mismatch — forsaken describes a place/state, not an approach mechanism) and Specter Tackle (gibberish — an incorporeal specter cannot grapple with physical mass by its own defining trait).

**BEAM: 34 kept intersections** (11,713 total intersections crossed; 221 rows × 53 synonyms). Honestly the leanest of the four, exactly as flagged in the assignment and confirmed by the ghost harvest's own richness note ("serviceable but slightly forced"). The ray/beam sub-pattern (Spectral Ray, Chill Ray, Grave Beam) carries the cell; several tested pairings failed on genuine definitional grounds — Hex Shaft (gibberish, an abstract curse has no piercing physical form), Fog Stream (definition clash, fog is broad/diffuse while a stream is narrow/directed), Searing Wail (comic, sound cannot be seared), Piercing Wail (definition dishonest, sound does not pierce in the linear sense this action requires).

**HURL: 31 kept intersections** (6,630 total intersections crossed; 221 rows × 30 synonyms). The leanest cell along with BEAM, matching the assignment's "hurled wisps" framing and the harvest's own grave-dust/ash sub-register observation. Ash Toss/Fling/Hurl/Cast/Volley/Barrage/Shower/Hail forms carry most of the cell's weight as ghost's substitute for a natural "mass" object; the mist/vapor-wisp register supplies the rest. Fog Toss and Dread Hail were both rejected on definition grounds (fog lacks a discrete throwable unit; dread is an abstract resource-noun with no physical mass to hail down).

**Total kept across all four assigned cells: 308 intersections** (138 + 105 + 34 + 31), cross-checked directly from the four matrices above — this is the authoritative count per brief Section 9.5, and it runs slightly below the prose section's own running enumeration in a few places where two prose-adjacent names collapsed to a single row×column matrix cell (e.g. "Cursed Sap" and "Accursed Sap" are two different rows against the same column, both counted; but a few weak/low-confidence prose mentions that did not resolve to a clean single element-word row were not separately matrix-tracked).
