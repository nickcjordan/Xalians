# Composed Catalog — ROCK, Section 0 (productive-word list)

Source: harvest-rock.md sections 1-3 (Composition Brief v5 Section 4 Step 0). This is the shared verbatim row set handed to all four ROCK composers (strike/lash/crush/rake; shove/drain/ambush/beam; hurl/spray/burst/cloud; snare/ward/mend/terrorize). Every noun/adjective/verb concept from the harvest is classified PRODUCTIVE or NON-PRODUCTIVE. The only valid non-productive reasons are grammatical/formal: a multi-word phrase over the 2-word cap that cannot compose down, a bare combining form (rule 6.7), or a word that cannot grammatically front or follow a partner. Register, tone, theme, and "abstractness" are never exclusion reasons.

Standing rulings noted but not acted on here (composers place/flag per assignment): Boulder/Rubble/Rock Volley/Stone Salvo-class names are reserved OUT of the neutral pools for rock (composers place them). Avalanche is ICE-owned; rock cross-references it, never owns it. Magma/lava are FIRE-owned; rock cross-references via fire's dual flags. Metal/ore words are METAL's boundary — flagged. Sand/dust words are SAND's boundary — flagged.

## Boundary flags carried forward (not excluded)

- boulder, rubble — reserved OUT of neutral pools for ROCK per 2026-09-01 ruling; composers place these directly, not via the synonym cross into neutral pools.
- avalanche — ICE-owned; ROCK cross-references only, never claims ownership.
- obsidian — volcanic-glass origin; cross-reference to FIRE's dual flags, kept productive on ROCK's list as a rock-type mineral noun per harvest §3 exclusion note.
- mineral, quartz, feldspar, mica, granite, basalt, gemstone — flag as touching METAL's ore/mineral boundary; harvest explicitly kept raw/unrefined stone and mineral nouns as rock-owned (see harvest §5), only smelted/forged/alloyed forms are METAL's.
- sandstone — flag: shares its name with SAND's domain but is retained rock-owned per harvest's explicit note (rock-type sense, not the sand register).
