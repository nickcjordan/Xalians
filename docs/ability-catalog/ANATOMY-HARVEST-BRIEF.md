# Anatomy Harvest Brief (shared instructions for all harvest agents)

You are harvesting candidate vocabulary for the `anatomy` field of a creature-record schema in a creature-collection game. The field lists a creature's **external functional parts relevant to action** — the body parts a creature acts WITH or defends WITH. Never internal organs (no hearts, lungs, gills, stomachs), never generic body regions (no "torso", "head" alone), never abstract capacities (no "mind" — that is handled elsewhere).

## What a good entry looks like

Each candidate is a registry key: short kebab-case, plain-language (the most universally known word), singular concept (the key names the part type, e.g. `pincers`, `tendrils`, `tusks` — plural spelling is fine where the part conventionally comes in multiples). For each candidate, record:

- **key** — kebab-case
- **what it is** — one line, concrete
- **actions it enables** — which of these 16 action verbs the part could plausibly deliver: strike, lash, crush, rake, shove, drain, ambush, beam, hurl, spray, burst, cloud, snare, ward, mend, terrorize
- **example creatures** (real animals or well-known fictional creature types; NO names borrowed from existing creature-collection games)
- **notes** — overlaps with sibling parts, whether it is a variant of another key (e.g. talons vs claws), edge cases

## Method — exhaustive-then-curate

Harvest EXHAUSTIVELY from your knowledge of real animal anatomy and broadly-known creature fiction FIRST. Do not stop at the obvious dozen. Sweep systematically through your assigned domain (see your individual assignment) and list everything action-relevant, including parts that later curation might merge or cut. Duplicates across agents are expected and fine — consolidation happens centrally afterward. Prefer being over-inclusive; a cut is cheap, a miss is not.

## Hard constraints

- American English.
- No Earth-species proper names as keys (no "python-coil"); generic anatomy words only.
- No internal organs, no sensory-only organs as primary entries (eyes/ears are senses, not instruments) — EXCEPT parts that both sense and act (antennae, whiskers, barbels: include, note the dual role).
- No game-mechanics language in descriptions (no damage, stats, cooldowns).
- Write your complete results to your assigned log file. All research must land in the log — nothing may exist only in your reply. End your reply with a one-paragraph summary and the count of candidates.
