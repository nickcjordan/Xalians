## Flags

**Canon flags (puppeting-adjacent, resolved per-name; kept only in influence/suggestion/perception senses):**
- Thrall Grip (CRUSH/WARD) — kept as a squeeze/defense exerted on a mind's will, never a body; ⚑ if consolidation wants a harder line, this is the single closest-to-the-boundary kept name.
- Coercion Ward, Compulsion Ward, Suggestion Ward, Influence Ward (WARD) — kept because the *ward* defends against coercion/compulsion, i.e. the psychic subject of the name is the protection, not the act of coercing; flagged for downstream double-check that "X Ward" never reads as "a ward performed via coercion."
