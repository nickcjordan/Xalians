# Step 0 — Productive-Word List — METAL (Composition Brief v5, Section 4 Step 0)

Source: harvest-metal.md, Sections 1-3 (Adjectives, Nouns, Verbs/gerunds). Every noun/adjective/verb concept classified PRODUCTIVE or NON-PRODUCTIVE. Only grammatical/formal reasons justify non-productive status (multi-word phrase that cannot compose to 2 words, a bare combining form per rule 6.7, or a word that cannot grammatically front/follow a partner). Register, tone, theme, and "abstractness" are never valid exclusion reasons. Same-root duplicates (e.g. an adjective and its gerund/verb sharing a root, or a noun and its verbed form) are collapsed into one row with all surface forms noted. Boundary flags are noted without excluding the word — composers retain and may use these words; the note is advisory for the auditor.

Element canon: METAL = alloys, blades-as-material, magnetism-as-metal, machined mass (homeworld Veridium, an all-metal factory world, possibly an ancient worldship). Standing rulings noted per assignment, not acted on here: Steel Trap / Iron Grip / Iron Guard reserved OUT of neutral pools FOR metal (composers place them — "Steel" and "Iron" as bare roots do not appear in the metal harvest lexicon itself, so no Section 0 row is affected; noted for composer awareness only). Leaden Blow flagged TO metal from dark's audit (root "Leaden" already present in harvest, marked below). Magnetism words border ELECTRIC — flagged shared-register. Rust/corrosion words border CHEMICAL — flagged where present. Forge/kiln words are FIRE's (fire owns Forge/Furnace compounds) — flagged, metal does not exclude the root but does not claim the compound. Modern-machine idiom (engine, piston, servo, etc.) needs prominent flags per the modern-tech rule; Veridium's factory-world lore gives metal wider machine latitude than other elements, but the flag stands for the auditor's judgment.

- **Table 1 (Adjectives):** 55 productive, 6 non-productive (Case-hardened, Cold-forged, Ferrous, Tungsten-tipped — grammatical/multi-word/combining-form reasons; plus 2 root-collapses folded into other rows are not separately counted as non-productive).

- Boundary flags raised (not exclusions): forge/kiln register bordering FIRE (Forged, Brazier, Crucible, Foundry, Smelting); magnetism bordering ELECTRIC (Magnetized); shared surface form with ELECTRIC (Coil — metal-spring sense); galvanic root shared with ELECTRIC (Galvanized); Leaden confirmed flagged TO metal per dark's audit ruling; modern-machine idiom cluster flagged prominently per the modern-tech rule with Veridium latitude noted (Piston, Servo, Bearing, Chassis, Cog, Cylinder, Gear, Grille, Hinge, Linkage, Machined, Ratchet, Retractable, Rotor, Sprocket, Strut).
