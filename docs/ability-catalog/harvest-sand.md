## 5. Excluded as other-domain / borrowed

**Other-domain exclusions:**
- Boulder — solid-rock word (excluded per boundary rule)
- Bedrock — solid-rock word (excluded per boundary rule)
- Landslip — rock/earth geology domain, not sand-specific
- Cairn — a stacked-stone marker, rock domain
- Mesa — landform defined by rock strata, geology/rock domain
- Pediment — geological rock-erosion landform term, rock domain
- Scarp — rock/cliff landform, rock domain
- Undertow — water domain (current beneath waves)
- Mire — leans swamp/mud/water domain more than sand; kept only as a flagged cautious inclusion under snare, otherwise treat as excluded
- Gale, Cyclone — wind-force words, excluded per boundary rule (pure air-domain, not sand events)

- Sands of Time — excluded, time domain
- Time-worn — kept as adjective for erosion-by-ages sense but flagged; if a stricter reading is wanted, exclude
- Grain-fall / Sand-fall (hourglass pouring imagery) — flagged as time-adjacent, kept only as a vocabulary curiosity, not recommended as a candidate ability name

## 6. Notes on richness

Adjectives and nouns are very rich (dozens of dry/eroded/granular/buried registers). Verbs are rich, especially in the erosion/burial/concealment clusters. Ability-name candidates are strong for strike, lash, crush, rake, shove, hurl, spray, burst, cloud, snare, and ward — these map naturally onto grit, dunes, and burial imagery. Thin/forced categories: **drain** (sand has no natural vitality-leech metaphor beyond desiccation, which is serviceable but not vivid), **beam** (sand cannot naturally form a focused line — this action type barely supports the field; only one weak forced candidate offered), and **mend** (restoration is not a natural sand concept; candidates lean on "sealing over" or "settling back," which are serviceable but not strong). **Terrorize** is moderately rich via burial/suffocation dread imagery but a couple of candidates are forced. Dual-tag flags for air are concentrated in the wind-driven sand-event words (sandstorm, haboob, sirocco, simoom) as instructed.
