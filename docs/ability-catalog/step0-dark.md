# Step 0 — Productive-word list — DARK (gravity/void/singularity/time-dilation)

Source: harvest-dark.md sections 1-3. Element canon: DARK = gravity, void, singularity, bounded time-dilation (black-hole physics conceit). NOT evil/shadow/spookiness (that register belongs to ghost). Format follows composed-water-v4.md Section 0.

Every noun/adjective/verb concept from harvest-dark.md is classified PRODUCTIVE or NON-PRODUCTIVE. Only grammatical/formal reasons justify non-productive status (multi-word phrase that cannot compose to 2 words, a bare combining form, or a word that cannot grammatically front/follow a partner). Register/tone/theme are never valid exclusion reasons. Obvious same-root duplicates are collapsed to one row with a note. Standing rulings and canon flags are noted without excluding the word (unless the ruling itself is a grammatical exclusion).

## Standing rulings and canon flags noted (not exclusions)

- **"Chrono"**: does not appear as its own headword in harvest-dark.md §1-3 (only "chronic," a distinct word, appears and is productive). Noted here per instruction: "Chrono" is a bare combining form and is a standing ratified exclusion for dark (see harvest-dark.md consolidated snare-cell note, "Chrono Snare (chrono not standalone English)" — cut and ratified). If "Chrono" resurfaces in any downstream sweep, it is non-productive as a bare combining form (brief rule 6.7) and the standing cut is not to be reversed.
- **Time-reversal canon ban**: no word in this harvest denotes reversal/rewinding. Words touching on time-slowing (dilated, delayed, decelerated, slow, decelerate, lag, delay) and time-speeding (accelerating, accelerate, hasten, quicken) are flagged above as canon-sanctioned (dilation/bounded time effects), not banned — bounded slowing and speeding are fine per canon; only true rewind/reversal is banned, and no such word appears in this productive list. "Irreversible" explicitly affirms the no-reversal constraint and is productive.
- **Multi-word source phrases** (black hole, event horizon, accretion disk, gravity well, heat death, neutron star, white dwarf, stellar remnant, dark star, point of no return, sands of time, tidal force) are non-productive as Step 0 compositional rows for the grammatical reason given (already at or past the 2-word cap with no room for a partner), per brief rule 6.7/Section 4 Step 0. Several of these (Event Horizon, Gravity Well, Dark Star, Point of No Return) are separately usable as standalone Step A names or signature-register names per the harvest's own consolidated snare-cell precedent — that is a downstream cell/flags decision, not a Step 0 reclassification.
- **Same-root collapses**: collapsing/collapsed, boundless/unbounded, degenerate/degenerative, compressive/compressed (kept distinct — see row note) were collapsed to single rows per the "collapse obvious same-root duplicates" instruction; all other near-synonyms (e.g., unending/ceaseless, pull/tug/drag/draw) were kept as separate rows since the brief explicitly welcomes near-duplicates and they are not the same root.
