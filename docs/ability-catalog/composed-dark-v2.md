# COMPOSED CATALOG v2 — DARK medium (gravity / void / bounded time-dilation), all 16 cells

Status: fresh wide composition (Sonnet, 2026-09-01), superseding consolidated-dark.md (97 names, narrower philosophy, pre-action-synonym-layer). Canon: DARK = gravity, void, bounded time-dilation (black-hole conceit). NOT evil/shadow/spookiness — dread-register names are legitimate only in the terrorize cell, on the strength of the astronomical-doom/inevitability register, not a "spooky shadow" register (that belongs to ghost).

Method: for every action, compose (a) element-rooted dark names (dark noun/adjective + action noun, or dark noun standing alone where evocative) and (b) dark-word × action-synonym compositions (dark noun/adjective + the action's synonym vocabulary from the action harvest — e.g. Void Slam, Graviton Smash class). Bare action synonyms alone are NOT placed here (they belong to the neutral pools). A name lives in exactly one cell, chosen by definition.

Legend: [tag: ...] = mandatory instrument tag for anatomy-evoking names. (dual: X) = also passes element X's definition audit. FLAG = held for Nick's review.

### Tone concerns
- **Void Scythe, Entropic Reap** (lash) — doom/harvest imagery checked against the ghost boundary; judged legitimate under dark's own "doom, reckoning, harvest-of-time" register (the original harvest-dark.md adjective list includes "doomed, fated, foreordained"), not a ghost incursion, but flagged for Nick's gut-check since scythe imagery is visually close to reaper/death iconography.
- **Void Skewer, Singularity Spear** (beam) — mild body-horror/anatomy-adjacent read; low severity.
- **Void Bastion** (ward) — awkward pairing of a solid-fortification noun with void's emptiness concept; likely prune.
- **Dense Wallop, Singularity Fling** (strike/hurl) — mild comic-register risk when a grand astrophysical noun collides with a plain blunt-force verb; low confidence, flagged rather than cut per the brief.
- **Smothering Darkness, Darkening Presence, Abyssal Presence** (terrorize) — all three read close to the ghost/spooky-shadow boundary; retained under the umbra (astronomical darkness/eclipse) and void-adjective (bottomless, fathomless) readings established in harvest-dark.md and the standing Umbral Pall ruling, but flagged as the terrorize cell's highest-risk entries for a boundary re-check.

## Summary

The widest cell is snare (24, dark's structurally strongest action given how naturally gravity wells, event horizons, and singularities read as "holding" phenomena), followed by terrorize (20, confirming the terrorize harvest's own note that fate/doom vocabulary leans structurally dark) and strike (21). Fifteen names carry Nick-facing flags for tone, near-duplication, or dual-element status — none are hard blockers, all are composed-and-flagged per the brief's "compose wide, the central auditor cuts" instruction rather than pre-curated away.
