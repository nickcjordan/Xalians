# PLANT Field Harvest

Semantic field: growth & territory — spread, root, entangle, regenerate ground. Registers used: botany, forestry, vines/thorns/brambles, spores and pollen, overgrowth.

Boundaries applied: excluded poison/venom/toxin words (owned by a different element); excluded water words; excluded proper/regional Earth plant species (kudzu, ivy-as-proper-species, oak, etc.) in favor of generic botanical nouns (thorn, vine, canopy, root, bramble as a generic mass-noun is kept since it names a growth form, not a species).

## 5. Excluded as other-domain / borrowed

**Excluded as other-domain:**
- Kudzu — proper/regional Earth species name, excluded per boundary rule
- Ivy — proper Earth species name commonly used as a genus reference, excluded in favor of generic "vine"
- Oak — proper Earth species name, excluded in favor of generic "bough"/"branch"
- Toxin, Venom, Poison, Toxic, Venomous — excluded, belongs to a different element (poison/venom domain)
- Nettle sting / Sting — stinging-poison connotation, excluded as leaning toxin domain
- Reed — borderline water-plant; excluded as leaning water/wetland domain rather than pure growth/territory
- Fen, Bog, Marsh growth — excluded as water-adjacent domain
- Algae, Kelp, Seaweed — excluded, water domain
- Nightshade — proper Earth species name AND leans poison domain, doubly excluded
- Mangrove — proper/regional Earth species name, excluded
- Willow — proper Earth species name, excluded
- Fungus/Mushroom/Toadstool — excluded as a separate fungal domain (adjacent but distinct from plant per typical element taxonomies; also toadstool often poison-coded)
- Spore Rot / Blight — leans decay/poison domain, excluded
- Sunlight, Photosynthesis (as a beam concept) — excluded, leans Light element domain for any beam-style ability

**Excluded as borrowed (recognized existing-game move names):**
- Vine Whip — recognized existing creature-game move name, excluded
- Leaf Blade — recognized existing creature-game move name, excluded
- Petal Dance — recognized existing creature-game move name, excluded
- Solar Beam — recognized existing creature-game move name, excluded (also other-domain, Light)
- Razor Leaf — recognized existing creature-game move name, excluded
- Giga Drain — recognized existing creature-game move name, excluded
- Leech Seed — recognized existing creature-game move name, excluded
- Spore (as standalone ability name identical to well-known move) — the noun "spore" itself is retained as vocabulary, but not offered standalone as a candidate ability name due to overlap with a recognized existing move
- Wood Hammer — recognized existing creature-game move name, excluded
- Thorn Whip — recognized existing creature-game move name, excluded (parallel construction to Vine Whip)
