# PLANT — Section 0 Productive-Word List

Source: harvest-plant.md sections 1-3. Per COMPOSITION-BRIEF v5 Section 4 Step 0: every noun/adjective/verb concept classified PRODUCTIVE or NON-PRODUCTIVE. Only grammatical/formal reasons justify non-productive status (multi-word phrase that cannot compose to 2 words, a bare combining form, or a word that cannot grammatically front/follow a partner). Register/tone/theme are never valid exclusion reasons. Earth-flora-boundary words are classified PRODUCTIVE and flagged per the standing rule (generic flora vocabulary is plant's core; proper Earth species names are flagged for the composers/auditor to weigh, not excluded here). Fungal/spore words are plant's. Toxin-adjacent words are flagged as chemical's boundary but not excluded (composers/auditor decide placement).
