# Composed Catalog — ICE, Actions: CRUSH, WARD, MEND, TERRORIZE (Composition Brief v5)

Element: ICE. Canon: cold, frost, frozen water, glacial mass. Homeworld Krystos (frozen former resort world, now prison world). Crush is strong (glacial weight, pack-ice pressure, the closing floe). Ward is strong (ice walls, frozen shells). Mend is the soothing-numbness/preservation register — cold numbs pain, preserves, reduces swelling — composed fully, with no implication of time-stopping (Stasis is dark's alone). Terrorize is the whiteout, the deathly still cold, the groan of the glacier. Liquid-water words are WATER's and are flagged, never claimed here.

Standing rulings applied silently throughout: the Stasis family (Stasis, Stasis Ward, Stasis Heal/Mend/Restore) is DARK-owned — never placed as a name; ward/mend cells carry cross-reference notes instead. Avalanche may be claimed by ice per the material-word ruling, noted with (dual: rock — pending audit) in its honest cell.
