# Harvest: ACTION field — CLOUD ("a lingering emitted area")

Scope: miasmas, palls, shrouds, veils, hazes, mists, and other obscuring or ambient fields that a creature emits and that persist in a place after emission — including "field"/"zone" style words for elements that express this action as a persistent effect-area rather than a literal airborne cloud.

## 1. Nouns

- Miasma — a noxious, often supernatural-feeling airborne emanation, historically believed to carry disease
- Pall — a heavy, spreading covering of gloom, smoke, or darkness over an area
- Shroud — a covering that wraps or obscures something, often with a funereal connotation
- Veil — a thin, diaphanous covering that partially obscures without fully concealing
- Haze — a light, diffuse obscuring of the air, softer and thinner than fog
- Mist — fine airborne droplets or particles hanging low over an area
- Fog — a dense, low-lying, disorienting airborne cover
- Smog — a thick, murky, often toxic-feeling airborne cover (chemical/industrial register)
- Fume — a visible, often acrid exhalation of gas or vapor
- Vapor — a diffuse gaseous emanation, more neutral/scientific register
- Reek — a strong, foul-smelling emanation (registers as noxious cloud)
- Fetor — a formal/archaic word for a strong, offensive lingering smell-cloud
- Effluvium — an invisible or faintly visible emanation, often unpleasant, given off by a substance
- Exhalation — something breathed or given off into the surrounding air
- Emanation — a general word for something that flows or radiates outward and lingers
- Miasm — archaic variant/shortening of miasma
- Murk — a thick, dim, obscuring darkness or haziness in the air
- Gloom — a spreading dimness or shadow that settles over an area
- Nimbus — a luminous or atmospheric cloud/halo surrounding something
- Aura — a subtle, pervasive emanation or field surrounding a source
- Penumbra — the shadowed fringe area around a central obscured zone
- Cloud — the plain generic noun itself, usable for a persistent airborne mass
- Overcast — a covering layer that dims or obscures from above
- Canopy — an overhead spreading cover (works for a cloud that hangs above an area)
- Cere-cloth-like Shroud-work — (rejected, see exclusions; kept here as note only) — REMOVE, not a real standalone word
- Blanket — a thick, all-covering layer draped over an area
- Curtain — a hanging, obscuring sheet-like field
- Screen — a diffuse barrier that obscures sight or passage
- Scrim — a thin, gauzy veil-like covering, theatrical/textile origin but works as a visual metaphor
- Smother — a thick, choking, all-covering mass (noun use, as in "a smother of smoke")
- Billow — a great swelling, rolling mass of cloud or smoke
- Plume — a rising, trailing column of emitted matter that spreads and lingers
- Wreath — a curling, encircling band of mist or smoke around something
- Drift — a slow-moving, settled mass of airborne matter
- Residue — matter left behind that lingers in an area
- Film — a thin lingering layer coating a surface or space
- Sheen — a faint lingering coating, often with a visual shimmer
- Slick — a lingering surface-coating film (works better for chemical/oil-adjacent zones)
- Cloak — an enveloping cover that conceals
- Mantle — a covering layer that spreads over and drapes an area
- Fog bank — a large, defined mass of standing fog
- Vaporcloud — REMOVE, not a real standalone word (combining form)
- Zone — a bounded area defined by a persistent condition or effect
- Field — a bounded area pervaded by a persistent influence or condition
- Aura-field — REMOVE, not a real standalone word (hyphenated combining form, not natural English)
- Domain — a bounded area under a pervasive influence (more abstract/formal register)
- Radius — the bounded extent of an emitted effect (technical/geometric register)
- Presence — a felt, pervasive, lingering influence occupying a space
- Fallout — matter that settles and lingers over an area after being released
- Wake — a trailing, lingering disturbance left behind after passage
- Trail — a lingering line or path of emitted matter
- Ambience / Ambiance — the pervasive, lingering quality that fills a space
- Envelope — a surrounding, enclosing layer or field
- Sheath — a close-fitting enveloping layer

## 2. Verbs / Gerunds

- Shroud (v.) — to wrap or cover something in an obscuring layer
- Veil (v.) — to cover thinly, partially concealing
- Pall (v., rare) — to cast a heavy, gloomy covering over
- Miasmatize — NOT a real standalone word; excluded
- Fume (v.) — to give off visible smoke or vapor
- Reek (v.) — to give off a strong, offensive smell persistently
- Exhale — to breathe or give off matter into the surrounding air
- Emanate — to flow outward from a source and spread through an area
- Waft — to drift gently and persistently through the air
- Billow — to swell and roll outward in large masses
- Diffuse — to spread out gradually and thinly through a space
- Permeate — to spread through and fill every part of a space
- Pervade — to spread through and be present throughout a space
- Suffuse — to spread through or over something gradually, especially with a quality of color or light
- Blanket (v.) — to cover an entire area thickly and uniformly
- Cloak (v.) — to cover so as to conceal
- Mantle (v.) — to cover as with a cloak or layer
- Envelop — to completely surround and cover
- Steep — to soak or saturate an area thoroughly with an emanation
- Hang (v., of air) — to remain suspended and lingering in place
- Linger (v.) — to remain in a place for longer than expected
- Settle (v.) — to sink and come to rest, remaining in place
- Coalesce — to come together and thicken into a persistent mass
- Thicken — to grow denser and more concentrated over time
- Smolder — to give off a low, lingering haze without open flame (note: leans fire; kept here as general verb, see elemental leanings)
- Seep — to spread slowly and pervasively, often used of gas or smell
- Ooze — to spread slowly and persistently, viscous connotation
- Congeal — to thicken and settle into a persistent mass
- Cling — to remain adhered and lingering in an area
- Choke — to fill an area densely enough to obstruct breathing or sight
- Obscure (v.) — to make an area dim, unclear, or hidden
- Dim (v.) — to lessen visibility or brightness over an area
- Overcast (v., rare) — to cover over, darkening
- Fester — to linger and worsen in an unwholesome way (fits miasma/noxious register)
- Stagnate — to remain motionless and lingering, without dissipating

## 4. Anatomy / mechanism-implying words

| Word | Implied anatomy / mechanism |
|---|---|
| Exhale / Exhalation | lungs, breath sacs, or a respiratory vent |
| Fume / Fuming | glands or pores that vent gas or smoke |
| Reek / Reeking | scent glands or a foul secretory organ |
| Ooze / Oozing | a soft, secretory, glandular body surface |
| Seep / Seeping | porous skin or a weeping wound-like membrane |
| Wreath (self-encircling) | a coiling or serpentine body that curls matter around itself |
| Plume (rising column) | a vent, chimney-like spiracle, or upward-facing emitter |
| Cloak / Cloaking | a wing-like membrane, cape of fur/feathers, or draping body covering |
| Wake (trailing) | a tail or trailing appendage that drags residue behind it |
| Sheath / Sheathing | an outer shell or carapace that a field clings to |
| Suffuse | a porous, permeable hide that a substance seeps through evenly |
| Nimbus / Aura | a halo-producing organ or bioluminescent/energetic emission ring |

## 5. Element-leaning candidates

- **Fire:** Smoldering Pall, Smoke Shroud, Ash Veil, Smoldering Haze, Cinder Fog, Ember Wreath
- **Chemical:** Toxic Miasma, Noxious Fume, Fetid Reek, Acrid Fume, Corrosive Haze, Fallout Field (leans chemical/radiological)
- **Water/Ice:** Standing Fog, Fog Bank, Rime Mist, Frost Veil, Drifting Mist
- **Ghost:** Spectral Shroud, Wraith Veil, Grave Pall, Haunting Miasma, Dreadful Gloom
- **Psychic:** Suffusing Presence, Pervading Aura, Domain of Dread (leans terrorize-adjacent, flagged)
- **Plant:** Pollen Haze, Spore Cloud, Bloom Miasma
- **Air:** Drifting Haze, Windborne Fume (borderline; "windborne" reads as air-specific)
- **Light:** Luminous Nimbus, Radiant Aura
- **Dark:** Umbral Pall, Shadow Shroud, Encroaching Murk

## 6. Honest richness assessment

The CLOUD field is very rich in the "noxious/obscuring emanation" register (miasma, pall, fume, reek, effluvium, fetor) and in the "visual obscuring" register (haze, mist, fog, veil, murk, gloom). It is comparatively thin in truly element-neutral field/zone vocabulary that doesn't smuggle in a visual or chemical implication — words like "zone," "field," "domain," "presence," and "radius" are the most reliably neutral because they describe the boundedness of an effect rather than its texture, but they are also somewhat abstract/dry as ability names and read best paired with a descriptor (e.g. "Pervading Field," "Presence Field"). The verb inventory splits cleanly into emission verbs (exhale, emanate, fume, waft) and persistence verbs (linger, settle, stagnate, cling, hang), both well populated. Anatomy-implying words are moderately rich for glandular/respiratory creatures (exhale, fume, reek, ooze, seep) but thinner for purely energy-based emitters (nimbus/aura cover this but don't imply concrete anatomy beyond "a halo organ"). Element-leaning candidates were easiest to generate for fire (smoke/ash/ember register), chemical (toxic/noxious register), and ghost (spectral/grave register) since those domains have long-established literary associations with lingering clouds; water/ice, plant, and psychic were serviceable but required more construction (compounding a neutral noun with an element-flavored modifier) rather than pulling from a deep native vocabulary of their own.
