# SPRAY — Action Harvest (matter expelled in a spreading arc)

Field scope: breath weapons, jets, gouts, torrents, sprays, mists ejected from the body in a cone or fan. Distinguished from BEAM (focused line), BURST (radial release from self), CLOUD (lingering emitted area), and HURL (a single launched mass). SPRAY is about the *expulsion pattern* — matter leaving the body under pressure and fanning outward — not the substance itself (that's element-specific).

## 6. Honest richness assessment

The field is very rich overall. Pure expulsion-pattern vocabulary (spray, spew, gush, spout, jet, gout, torrent, discharge, expel, emit) is deep and gives many strong element-neutral compounds without straining. Breath-weapon-specific words (breath, exhale, vent, disgorge, vomit) are also plentiful and read naturally as ability names ("Breath Spray," "Vented Torrent"). The "sudden/violent single burst" sub-concept (spurt, squirt, gout, geyser) is strong. The "wide continuous outpouring" sub-concept (deluge, cascade, torrent, wash, flume, sluice) is strong. The weakest sub-concept is dry/solid-matter spray (spraying particulate rather than liquid or gas) — words like grit, sand wash, and ash spew work but lean heavily element-specific rather than staying neutral, since most spray vocabulary in English assumes a liquid or gaseous medium; I did not force neutral dry-matter spray names beyond what's listed. Foam/froth vocabulary is decent but thin (foam, froth, lather) and mostly useful for aerated or viscous substances rather than a distinct action concept of its own. No significant overlap concerns: spray stayed cleanly separated from burst (self-centered radial release, no directional cone), cloud (lingering area effect vs. an expelled jet), and beam (a focused line rather than a spreading fan) throughout.

## Excluded as other-domain / borrowed

- Hydro Pump — borrowed from an existing creature game
- Ember (as standalone ability name) — too close to a known existing game's move name; used only as a modifier here
- Sludge Bomb — borrowed, and "bomb" belongs to burst/hurl, not spray
- Spit Up — too informal/modern-idiomatic register
- Puke — too informal/modern-idiomatic register (used "vomit" instead, which is the clinical/literary register)
- Firehose — modern tech-adjacent idiom, excluded
- Power Wash — modern product-name idiom, excluded
- Sneeze — comedic/informal register, excluded despite technically being an expulsion
- Squirt Gun anything — modern object reference, excluded
- Nozzle (as a noun candidate) — reads as a manufactured mechanical part, not organic anatomy; excluded from anatomy table
- Sprinkle, Sprinkler — too gentle/domestic register, doesn't fit forceful expulsion; excluded
- Drool, Drooling — too passive/comedic, excluded (lacks the forceful expulsion quality)
