# Light Element — Section 0: Productive-Word List

Source: harvest-light.md sections 1-3 (Adjectives, Nouns, Verbs/gerunds). Classification follows COMPOSITION-BRIEF v5 Section 4 Step 0: every noun/adjective/verb concept is PRODUCTIVE unless a grammatical/formal reason applies (multi-word phrase that cannot compose to 2 words, a bare combining form per rule 6.7, or a word that cannot grammatically front/follow a partner). Register, tone, theme, and abstractness are never valid exclusion reasons. Same-root duplicates are collapsed into one row with a note; nothing is silently dropped.

Standing rulings acknowledged, not acted on here (cell placement is a later step): Supernova is DARK-owned (light cross-references only, never owns it). Eclipse-family dual precedent exists (dark+light duals are real — `eclipse`, `occultation`, `umbra`, `penumbra`, `antumbra`, `transit` below may be dual candidates for the composer to weigh). Fire owns heat-register blaze/glow words at the flare/glow boundary — several words below are flagged fire-boundary rather than reclassified. `Radiant` is flagged as a known pending-question word per assignment — its productive status is recorded here but its resolution is explicitly not attempted.

## Notes for downstream composers

- **Radiant** (adjective) and **radiate** (verb): flagged pending-question word per assignment. Both recorded PRODUCTIVE here; resolution deliberately not attempted in this step.
- **Fire-boundary words** flagged, not excluded: `blazing`/`blaze` (adj/noun/verb), `searing`/`sear` (adj/verb), `white-hot` (non-productive on hyphenation grounds independent of the fire question), `ignite`, `incandesce`/`incandescent`, `kindle`. These stay PRODUCTIVE for light; the flag exists so the composer weighs the Fire/Light boundary per-name rather than this step pre-deciding it.
- **Eclipse-family dual candidates** flagged per standing dark+light dual precedent: `eclipse`, `occultation`, `transit`, `umbra`, `penumbra`, `antumbra`. All PRODUCTIVE; dual-ownership resolution left to the composer, consistent with Supernova remaining DARK-owned and light only cross-referencing it.
- **Psychic-boundary words**: `insight`, `vision`, `clairvoyance` retained PRODUCTIVE only in their literal-eyesight/optical-clarity senses per harvest §5; their foresight/mental senses are excluded to Psychic and are not part of this productive list's scope.
- Every NON-PRODUCTIVE row above is grammatical/formal only (hyphenated compounds already at the naming cap, phrases exceeding 2 words, or fixed idioms whose meaningful root word is separately listed as productive). No word was marked non-productive for register, tone, theme, or abstractness.
