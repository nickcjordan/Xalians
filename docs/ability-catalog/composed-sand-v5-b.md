## DRAIN (signature-strength: desiccation/moisture-sapping)

Sand's drain is desiccation — pulling moisture and vitality out the way arid ground pulls water from anything that touches it, and the way sand silently swallows/settles/consumes what is buried in it over time (Sink, Settle, Swallow senses reused as slow-consumption).

## BEAM (honestly thin — sand has no natural line-emission mechanism)

Per the assignment, sand does not naturally form a directed line of continuous emission the way light, electric, or water do. The harvest source explicitly flags this field as thin/forced. The only honest sand-side beam concepts are: (1) a vitrified/fused glass line from abrasive friction-heat (glass is sand's own per canon, though no Step-0 "Glass" row exists to compose from — noted honestly, no invented row), (2) a scouring/abrasive line of driven grit (a "sandblast" register, plain and physical rather than energy-beam), and (3) figurative "shaft/spire/column" imagery applied to a rising dune-formation or a directed jet of grit. This cell is kept honestly thin per the assignment's explicit instruction not to pad it.
