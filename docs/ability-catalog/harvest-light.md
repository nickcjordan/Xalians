## 5. Excluded as other-domain / borrowed

**Excluded as other-domain (fire/heat)**
- burn, scorch, flame, char, singe, roast, smolder, immolate, incinerate, combust — heat/fire domain, not optical light
- Note: "blaze," "flare," "sear," "kindle," "ignite," "incandesce," "white-hot" were retained above only in strictly optical/intensity senses; if any downstream curator judges them too heat-coded, they should move here. Flagging explicitly: **sear, white-hot, searing** lean hardest toward Fire and are the most likely to be re-cut in curation.

**Excluded as other-domain (electric/lightning)**
- bolt (lightning sense), spark, static, charge (electrical sense), voltage, current, jolt, shock, thunderbolt, arc (electrical sense), electrify — these belong to the Electric field. "Bolt" and "arc" are kept above ONLY in explicitly optical senses (shaft of light; arc of a beam's sweep); the electrical senses are excluded here.

**Excluded as other-domain (weather/water)**
- mist, fog — primarily weather/water domain; "Blinding Mist" listed above is flagged borderline and should be reviewed by the curator, not treated as settled.

**Excluded as other-domain (psychic)**
- clairvoyance, insight, vision (as "foresight") — where these mean literal eyesight/optical clarity they were kept above; where they mean mental foresight they belong to Psychic and are excluded.

**Excluded as borrowed (recognized existing game move names or too close)**
- Solar Beam (Pokemon) — used "Solar Beam Lance" instead as a distinct compound
- Flash Cannon (Pokemon)
- Dazzling Gleam (Pokemon)
- Light Screen (Pokemon) — used "Light Shield" / "Prism Barrier" instead
- Moonlight (Pokemon move name) — kept "moonlight" as plain vocabulary noun/adjective only, not as a standalone ability name
- Aurora Beam (Pokemon)
- Signal Beam (Pokemon) — used "Guiding Light" / "Signal Beam" avoided as ability name, kept only as vocabulary
- Judgment (Pokemon, light-type)
- Photon Geyser (Pokemon)
- Prismatic Laser (Pokemon)

**Excluded as borderline holy/divine-smiting (kept abstract only, flagged for curator judgment)**
- smite, smiting, divine wrath, holy light, sacred fire, judgment beam — "smite" leans borrowed-genre/divine-combat cliche per brief; excluded as a standalone candidate name. Abstract restrained forms (hallow, consecrate, seraphic, benediction, grace) were kept per brief's explicit allowance for abstract religious register, but "Benediction" and "Grace" themselves were not proposed as ability names to avoid duplicating the brief's own examples — flagged for the curator to consider directly if desired.

## Summary

Sections delivered: Adjectives (~85), Nouns (~140), Verbs/gerunds (~75, each with 5-10 word definitions for non-everyday terms), Candidate ability names across all 16 action types, and Excluded lists (other-domain: fire/heat, electric, weather/water, psychic; borrowed: 9 Pokemon-recognizable names; borderline holy/divine flagged separately per brief instruction).

Richest sub-fields: optics/refraction vocabulary (prism, lens, focus, converge, diffract) and solar/dawn imagery (sunrise, corona, aureole, zenith) were extremely rich — these carried the beam, burst, ward, and mend action types easily.

Thinnest action type: **crush** — light has no natural grip/compress mechanic in English idiom; only produced 3 weak candidates via a "focal convergence squeezing" metaphor, flagged honestly rather than forced. **snare** and **cloud** were moderately thin and leaned on "glare/dazzle" overload framing rather than true light vocabulary, since light does not naturally linger or bind.

Boundary calls flagged for curator: sear/white-hot/searing (fire-adjacent), Blinding Mist (weather-adjacent), and the holy/divine-smiting register (kept abstract per brief, "smite" itself excluded).
