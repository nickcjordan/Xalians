# Anatomy Harvest — Exotic & Non-Standard Bodies

Domain: serpentine, amorphous, plant, mineral/crystal, metal/construct, gas/mist, energy, spectral, floating/levitating, and swarm bodies. Goal: give non-animal creatures real, generic anatomy keys so they are not locked out of the instrument system.

---

## Serpentine bodies (coils as weapons)

### `coils`
- **What it is:** The muscular length of a serpentine body, capable of wrapping and squeezing.
- **Actions:** crush, snare, shove, ward
- **Example creatures:** constrictor snakes, sea serpents, wyrms, naga, lindworms
- **Notes:** Distinct from `tail` (a discrete appendage) — coils are the whole trunk acting as an instrument. Overlaps with `tendrils` in function (wrap/snare) but coils imply much greater mass and crushing force.

### `tail` (whip-tail / serpent-tail variant)
- **What it is:** A long trailing appendage capable of sweeping strikes or a stinging tip.
- **Actions:** strike, lash, sweep(shove), crush
- **Example creatures:** sea serpents, dragons, scorpion-tailed chimeras, stingrays
- **Notes:** Already a likely core-domain key; noted here because serpentine bodies rely on it heavily. Variant with venom tip overlaps `stinger`.

### `fangs`
- **What it is:** Long piercing teeth, often hollow or grooved for venom delivery.
- **Actions:** strike, drain
- **Example creatures:** vipers, cobras, fanged sea serpents
- **Notes:** Core-domain key likely covers this; included because serpentine bodies define the archetype. Pairs with venom-drain function.

### `hood` (flared neck/hood)
- **What it is:** A flexible flare of skin or ribs around the neck that expands to threaten or intimidate.
- **Actions:** terrorize, ward
- **Example creatures:** cobras, frilled lizards, hooded serpents of myth
- **Notes:** Purely display/defense; not a strike instrument. Overlaps thematically with `frill`/`crest` in the reptile domain.

### `constricting-body`
- **What it is:** Alternate framing of the whole serpentine trunk as a single wrap-and-crush instrument (see `coils`).
- **Actions:** crush, snare
- **Example creatures:** anacondas, giant serpents, world-serpents of myth
- **Notes:** Likely redundant with `coils` — flag for merge during curation; kept as a distinct candidate phrase in case "coils" reads too specifically ophidian for non-snake serpentine creatures (eel-bodied, ribbon-bodied).

## Amorphous bodies (slime, ooze, engulfing mass)

### `pseudopods`
- **What it is:** Temporary limb-like projections a soft body extends from its mass to grip, punch, or drag.
- **Actions:** strike, shove, crush, snare, drain
- **Example creatures:** amoebas (microscopic analogue), slimes, oozes, blobs of fiction, giant amoeba monsters
- **Notes:** The signature amorphous-body key — functions as an all-purpose limb substitute. Should be flagged as broadly action-capable so amorphous creatures aren't anatomy-starved.

### `engulfing-mass`
- **What it is:** The creature's entire body used to surround, smother, and dissolve a target.
- **Actions:** crush, snare, drain, terrorize
- **Example creatures:** oozes, blobs, tar pits given life, carnivorous slime molds
- **Notes:** Distinct from pseudopods (a limb) — this is the whole-body engulf action. Overlaps with `coils` in the "whole body as weapon" sense but implies smothering/dissolving rather than squeezing.

### `acidic-membrane` / `corrosive-surface`
- **What it is:** An outer surface that secretes or carries corrosive/dissolving fluid, damaging on contact.
- **Actions:** drain, ward, spray
- **Example creatures:** acid slimes, ooze monsters, digestive-fluid blobs
- **Notes:** Dual-purpose — passive ward (touch it, get burned) and active spray if the creature can eject fluid. Consider splitting into a passive-only `corrosive-surface` and an active `spray` capability.

### `tendril` (slime variant)
- **What it is:** A thin extruded strand of gelatinous body-matter, used to lash or ensnare.
- **Actions:** lash, snare, strike
- **Example creatures:** slimes, oozes, gelatinous cubes, tar creatures
- **Notes:** Overlaps heavily with the plant-domain `tendrils`/`vines` key and the general core-domain `tendrils` — likely a single shared key across domains, just re-used by amorphous creatures.

### `absorptive-body`
- **What it is:** A body surface that can engulf and incorporate small objects or weapons thrown at it, or split into smaller units.
- **Actions:** ward, drain
- **Example creatures:** oozes that absorb weapons, self-splitting slimes
- **Notes:** More of a defensive trait than a strike instrument; borderline — may belong in a "traits" field rather than anatomy. Flagging as edge case for curators.

### `spore-bladder` / `bursting-sac`
- **What it is:** A blister or sac within the amorphous mass that ruptures, releasing caustic or noxious matter outward.
- **Actions:** burst, cloud, spray
- **Example creatures:** puffball-like oozes, exploding slimes, bloat-creatures
- **Notes:** Overlaps with plant-domain `spore-pod` and gas-domain `vent` — the "internal sac that bursts" concept recurs across several exotic domains and might consolidate into one shared key (`bladder` or `sac`).

### `false-limb`
- **What it is:** A limb-shaped extrusion mimicking an animal limb (claw, fist) formed temporarily from the mass.
- **Actions:** strike, crush, rake
- **Example creatures:** shapeshifting oozes, mimic slimes
- **Notes:** Functionally identical to `pseudopods` at a more specific "mimics a known limb" framing — likely merges into pseudopods during curation.

## Plant bodies (vines, thorns, roots, lashing branches, seed/spore pods)

### `vines`
- **What it is:** Long, flexible plant-tendrils capable of whipping, wrapping, and dragging.
- **Actions:** lash, snare, strike, shove
- **Example creatures:** carnivorous plants, treants, plant elementals, kudzu monsters
- **Notes:** Same functional slot as `tendrils`; plant-flavored synonym. Curators should decide if `vines` and `tendrils` are one shared key or two flavor-variant keys.

### `thorns` / `spines` (plant variant)
- **What it is:** Sharp woody or fibrous projections along stems, vines, or a bark hide, used defensively or offensively.
- **Actions:** strike, ward, rake
- **Example creatures:** rose-briar monsters, cactus creatures, bramble beasts
- **Notes:** Overlaps with the animal-domain `spines`/`quills` key — likely a shared cross-domain key. Passive ward (brush against it) vs. active rake (creature lashes with thorned vine) are both plausible.

### `roots`
- **What it is:** Subterranean or surface-crawling root structures that can grip, ensnare, or erupt from the ground to strike.
- **Actions:** snare, strike, shove, drain
- **Example creatures:** treants, mandrakes, root-elementals, strangler figs
- **Notes:** Drain plausible if roots are framed as siphoning vitality/moisture from a grabbed target (real strangler-fig ecology supports this framing). Distinct from vines by being ground-anchored rather than free-swinging.

### `branches` / `boughs`
- **What it is:** Rigid woody limbs extending from a trunk-like body, swung like clubs or interlaced defensively.
- **Actions:** strike, crush, shove, ward
- **Example creatures:** treants, ents, walking trees, wooden golems
- **Notes:** The plant-domain analogue of `arms`/`limbs`. Heavier and more rigid than vines — implies blunt force rather than wrap/snare.

### `bark-hide`
- **What it is:** A thick woody or fibrous outer layer functioning as armor.
- **Actions:** ward
- **Example creatures:** treants, bark golems, ironwood beasts
- **Notes:** Pure defense, no offense. Overlaps conceptually with mineral-domain `plating` and animal-domain `hide`/`shell` — a recurring "armor surface" concept across many exotic domains.

### `seed-pod` / `spore-pod`
- **What it is:** A pod or capsule that bursts open to scatter seeds, spores, or irritant powder.
- **Actions:** burst, cloud, spray
- **Example creatures:** puffball fungi, exploding seed pods, spore-casting plant monsters
- **Notes:** Same "internal sac that bursts" concept as amorphous `spore-bladder` and gas-domain venting — strong candidate for a single shared `pod`/`sac` key used across domains with a flavor note.

### `pollen-cloud` (organ + emitted effect)
- **What it is:** A releasable cloud of fine pollen or spores that drifts and lingers, generated by flowers or fungal caps on the creature's body.
- **Actions:** cloud, drain, terrorize
- **Example creatures:** giant flowers, fungal beasts, spore-mother monsters
- **Notes:** The instrument is really the flower/cap that produces it; consider naming the key `spore-cap` (the organ) rather than `pollen-cloud` (the effect), keeping the field to body parts not outcomes.

### `spore-cap`
- **What it is:** A mushroom-like cap or flower head that releases spores or pollen when disturbed or triggered.
- **Actions:** cloud, spray, terrorize
- **Example creatures:** fungal humanoids, giant mushrooms, flower-headed plant beasts
- **Notes:** The organ-framed counterpart to `pollen-cloud` above — prefer this as the actual key.

### `sap-glands` / `resin-ducts`
- **What it is:** Ducts or glands within stems or bark that ooze sticky sap or resin, usable to trap or slow a target.
- **Actions:** snare, ward, drain
- **Example creatures:** amber-oozing trees, sap monsters, resin elementals
- **Notes:** Drain plausible if sap is framed as sticky+corrosive/sedative. Overlaps mechanically with a "sticky secretion" concept that could also appear in amorphous or insect domains.

### `stinger-thorn` / `barbed-vine`
- **What it is:** A vine or stem tipped with a hardened, needle-like barb, functioning like an animal stinger.
- **Actions:** strike, drain
- **Example creatures:** venomous bramble monsters, thorn-vine beasts
- **Notes:** Likely merges into `thorns` or the core-domain `stinger` key rather than standing alone; flagged for curator judgment.

### `root-tendrils` (fine root-hair mass)
- **What it is:** A mass of fine hair-like roots that can spread over a surface or target, sensing and gripping.
- **Actions:** snare, drain
- **Notes:** Dual sense+act role like antennae/whiskers — fine root hairs both sense soil chemistry and grip. Include per the dual-role exception. Overlaps with `roots` at coarser grain; may not survive as a separate key but flagged since the dual-role note matters.
- **Example creatures:** mandrake-like plant monsters, fungal mycelium creatures

### `canopy` / `frond-crown`
- **What it is:** A broad crown of leaves, fronds, or petals atop the plant body, spread wide for display or to shelter/shade.
- **Actions:** ward, terrorize
- **Example creatures:** giant venus-flytrap monsters, treants, flower giants
- **Notes:** Mostly defensive/display; terrorize plausible if the canopy is framed as suddenly unfurling to loom over a target (venus flytrap snapping open).

## Mineral / crystal bodies

### `crystal-growths`
- **What it is:** Sharp, faceted crystalline projections growing from the body, usable to stab or as fixed armor points.
- **Actions:** strike, rake, ward
- **Example creatures:** golems, elementals, crystalline beasts, living gemstones
- **Notes:** The signature mineral-body offense key. Distinct from `spines`/`thorns` by material (rigid mineral vs. organic) but functionally similar; kept separate since flavor matters to players.

### `stone-fists` / `boulder-fists`
- **What it is:** Oversized hardened hand-like masses of rock or mineral at the end of limbs, swung for blunt impact.
- **Actions:** crush, strike, shove
- **Example creatures:** golems, rock elementals, stone giants
- **Notes:** Kept generic per brief instruction ("stone fists in generic terms"). Functionally a heavy variant of `fists`/`limbs`.

### `plating` (mineral variant)
- **What it is:** Segmented plates of stone or crystal covering the body, providing armor.
- **Actions:** ward
- **Example creatures:** golems, armored elementals, crystal-shelled beasts
- **Notes:** Shared concept with metal-domain `plating` and plant-domain `bark-hide` — one of the recurring "armor surface" keys; consider one shared `plating` key with material as flavor text rather than three separate keys.

### `spire` / `shard-spire`
- **What it is:** A single large jutting crystal or rock spike projecting from the body, used to impale or as a fixed hazard.
- **Actions:** strike, ward
- **Example creatures:** crystal golems, geode beasts, stalagmite creatures
- **Notes:** Singular/larger-scale variant of `crystal-growths`; may merge during curation.

### `mineral-mass` / `rock-body`
- **What it is:** The whole rocky or crystalline bulk of the creature used to slam, shove, or fall upon a target.
- **Actions:** crush, shove
- **Example creatures:** boulder monsters, rockslide elementals, animated statues
- **Notes:** Whole-body-as-weapon key, parallel to `engulfing-mass` (amorphous) and `coils` (serpentine) — a recurring pattern where the exotic body's entire mass is itself the instrument.

### `resonant-crystal` / `chiming-shard`
- **What it is:** A crystal formation on the body that vibrates or resonates, projecting a damaging or disorienting vibration outward.
- **Actions:** beam, terrorize
- **Example creatures:** singing crystal beasts, resonance golems, crystalline sirens
- **Notes:** "beam" here framed as a directed vibrational/sonic pulse rather than light — flag for curators on whether beam should be reserved for light/energy only or can generalize to any directed emission.

### `geode-core` / `crystal-heart`
- **What it is:** An exposed crystal core at the creature's center that can pulse out a burst of shattering force or light.
- **Actions:** burst, beam
- **Example creatures:** golems with glowing cores, crystal elementals, living geodes
- **Notes:** Borderline "internal organ" — but since it is exposed and externally acting (not metabolic), it fits the functional-parts definition better than a heart/lung would. Flag for curator judgment on internal-organ exclusion.

### `crystalline-barbs`
- **What it is:** Small, brittle crystal spikes that can be shed or thrown, breaking off to embed in a target.
- **Actions:** hurl, rake
- **Example creatures:** porcupine-like crystal beasts, shedding geode creatures
- **Notes:** Mineral analogue of quills that detach and are thrown (real porcupine-quill mechanic transposed to crystal); overlaps `crystal-growths`.

## Metal / construct bodies (generic, non-jargon)

### `plating` (metal variant)
- **What it is:** Overlapping segmented plates covering the body, deflecting blows.
- **Actions:** ward
- **Example creatures:** golems, armored constructs, iron-shelled beasts
- **Notes:** Shared cross-domain armor key (see mineral `plating`, plant `bark-hide`); kept generic per brief ("keep keys generic and body-like, not machine-part jargon").

### `blade-limb`
- **What it is:** A limb that ends in or is shaped like a bladed edge rather than a hand or paw, used for cutting strikes.
- **Actions:** strike, rake
- **Example creatures:** constructs, golems, armored automatons, bladed guardians
- **Notes:** Deliberately generic ("blades as body parts... keep keys... body-like") rather than naming a specific weapon type. Functions like a rigid claw.

### `hammer-limb` / `mace-limb`
- **What it is:** A limb ending in a heavy blunt mass, swung for crushing blows.
- **Actions:** crush, shove
- **Example creatures:** golems, siege constructs, iron giants
- **Notes:** Blunt counterpart to blade-limb; both are "limb shaped like an impact tool" rather than a held weapon, consistent with the anatomy field excluding equipment.

### `driving-limb` (piston-like limb, generic)
- **What it is:** A limb that extends and retracts forcefully, delivering a sudden thrusting strike or shove.
- **Actions:** strike, shove
- **Example creatures:** constructs, automatons, mechanical guardians
- **Notes:** This is the generic rendering of "pistons" per the brief's instruction to avoid machine-part jargon — described by motion (driving/thrusting) rather than mechanism.

### `frame` / `construct-body`
- **What it is:** The rigid overall chassis-like body of a construct, used to ram or body-block.
- **Actions:** shove, crush, ward
- **Example creatures:** golems, animated armor, iron colossi
- **Notes:** Whole-body-as-instrument key, parallel to `mineral-mass` and `engulfing-mass`.

### `vents` (construct variant, generic steam/heat)
- **What it is:** Openings in the body that release pressurized heat, steam, or gas outward.
- **Actions:** spray, cloud, burst
- **Example creatures:** steam-powered constructs, forge golems, furnace beasts
- **Notes:** Overlaps with the gas-domain `vents` key below — likely one shared key across construct and gas/mist domains.

### `grasping-claw` (construct variant)
- **What it is:** A mechanical-style pincer or claw limb, generic in form, used to grip and crush.
- **Actions:** crush, snare
- **Example creatures:** constructs, automated guardians, clockwork beasts
- **Notes:** Likely folds into the core-domain `pincers`/`claws` key with a construct flavor note rather than standing alone.

### `spike-plating`
- **What it is:** Plating studded with rigid spikes or barbs, both armor and a passive offensive hazard.
- **Actions:** ward, strike
- **Example creatures:** spiked golems, armored war-constructs, iron hedgehogs
- **Notes:** Combination of `plating` and `spines`; flagged as a possible merge rather than distinct key.

## Gas / mist bodies

### `vapor-mass` / `mist-body`
- **What it is:** The creature's diffuse gaseous or misty form, used to envelop, seep into, or smother a target.
- **Actions:** cloud, snare, drain, terrorize
- **Example creatures:** fog monsters, mist wraiths, living smoke, will-o'-the-wisps' cloud kin
- **Notes:** Whole-body-as-instrument key for the gas domain, parallel to `engulfing-mass`/`mineral-mass`. Drain plausible if the mist is framed as sapping breath/warmth from anything it envelops.

### `vents` (gas-body variant)
- **What it is:** Points on the body where gas is expelled outward under pressure, directed at a target.
- **Actions:** spray, cloud, burst
- **Example creatures:** noxious-gas beasts, sulfur elementals, swamp-gas creatures
- **Notes:** Same shared key as construct-domain `vents`; the emission point is the actual anatomy, the gas itself is the effect.

### `toxic-cloud` / `caustic-fume` (emitted effect, flag as non-part)
- **What it is:** A drifting cloud of noxious or corrosive gas the creature generates and releases.
- **Actions:** cloud, drain, terrorize
- **Notes:** This names the effect, not the part — the actual anatomy key should be `vents` or `mist-body`. Included to flag the distinction for curators; likely cut as a standalone key since it violates "not abstract effects."
- **Example creatures:** gas clouds of folklore, miasma creatures

### `choking-tendrils` (mist that coalesces into limbs)
- **What it is:** Wisps of gas or mist that thicken and coalesce into temporary limb-like tendrils to grip a target.
- **Actions:** snare, strike, drain
- **Example creatures:** smoke wraiths, fog-born spirits, mist elementals
- **Notes:** Gas-domain counterpart to amorphous `pseudopods` — same "temporary limb from a formless body" pattern recurring across gas/amorphous/spectral domains; strong merge candidate.

### `condensing-fist` / `mist-fist`
- **What it is:** A localized thickening of the gaseous body into a dense, semi-solid mass capable of a heavy blow.
- **Actions:** strike, shove
- **Example creatures:** storm elementals, smoke djinn, fog giants
- **Notes:** Same pattern as choking-tendrils but framed as a striking fist rather than a gripping tendril; likely consolidates with `choking-tendrils` into a single "coalescing limb" key.

## Energy bodies

### `energy-limb` / `radiant-limb`
- **What it is:** A limb-shaped concentration of the creature's energy field, used to strike or grasp like a normal limb.
- **Actions:** strike, crush, shove
- **Example creatures:** elementals, spirits of pure energy, living lightning, plasma beings
- **Notes:** Energy-domain counterpart to `pseudopods`/`choking-tendrils` — the same "temporary limb from a formless body" pattern; possible shared key across amorphous/gas/energy domains at the schema level, differing only by flavor text.

### `discharge-surface` / `arcing-surface`
- **What it is:** The creature's outer energy field, which arcs or discharges on contact or proximity.
- **Actions:** strike, ward
- **Example creatures:** lightning elementals, electric spirits, storm beings
- **Notes:** Passive ward (touch it, get shocked) and active discharge (strike) both plausible from the same surface.

### `radiant-core` / `energy-core`
- **What it is:** A visible focal point of concentrated energy within the body from which power radiates or can be released outward.
- **Actions:** beam, burst
- **Example creatures:** elementals, star-spirits, plasma beings, living suns
- **Notes:** Same borderline "internal organ" flag as mineral `geode-core` — externally acting and visible rather than metabolic, so arguably fits; curator judgment call.

### `corona` / `aura-field`
- **What it is:** A visible field or halo of energy surrounding the body, radiating heat, light, or force at range.
- **Actions:** ward, terrorize, cloud
- **Example creatures:** solar spirits, radiant elementals, angelic beings of pure light
- **Notes:** Whole-body-as-instrument key for energy bodies, parallel to `vapor-mass`/`mineral-mass`. "Cloud" plausible if framed as an enveloping radiant field rather than a gas.

### `arc-tendrils` / `lightning-tendrils`
- **What it is:** Branching streamers of electrical or plasma energy extending from the body, lashing outward.
- **Actions:** lash, strike, snare
- **Example creatures:** lightning elementals, storm spirits, electric beasts
- **Notes:** Energy-flavored variant of `tendrils`; same cross-domain pattern as vine/tendril/mist-tendril.

### `beam-eye` / `focal-point` (careful: sensory-adjacent)
- **What it is:** A concentrated point on the body (often eye-like) from which a directed ray of energy is projected.
- **Actions:** beam
- **Example creatures:** cyclopean energy beings, beam-eyed golems, death-ray monsters
- **Notes:** Risk of reading as a pure sensory organ (eye) rather than an instrument — brief excludes eyes as senses-only. Justify inclusion only if framed as an emitter, not a sensor; flag for curator scrutiny, possibly rename to avoid "eye."

## Spectral bodies (generic "parts" a ghost-like creature can act with)

### `spectral-limbs`
- **What it is:** Translucent, insubstantial limb-shapes the creature can extend and use to grip or strike despite lacking a solid body.
- **Actions:** strike, crush, snare
- **Example creatures:** ghosts, specters, wraiths, phantoms
- **Notes:** The signature spectral-body key — gives ghost-type creatures a generic limb-equivalent without implying solid anatomy. Should be broadly usable so spectral creatures aren't anatomy-starved (per brief's stated concern).

### `chilling-touch` / `withering-touch` (touch as instrument)
- **What it is:** Direct bodily contact from the specter that saps warmth, vitality, or life-force from whatever it touches.
- **Actions:** drain
- **Example creatures:** wraiths, dementors-adjacent folklore specters, death spirits
- **Notes:** Names the effect via "touch," anchoring it to the body (touch = contact instrument) rather than being purely abstract; still borderline — flag for curators on whether this should be a `spectral-limbs` action note instead of a separate key.

### `trailing-shroud` / `veil`
- **What it is:** Wisps of translucent burial-cloth-like or vaporous material trailing from the body, used to wrap and smother.
- **Actions:** snare, cloud, terrorize
- **Example creatures:** shrouded ghosts, banshees, wailing specters
- **Notes:** Spectral-flavored variant of the "coalescing tendril" pattern seen in mist/energy/amorphous domains.

### `wail` / `dread-presence` (careful: abstract capacity risk)
- **What it is:** The creature's disembodied voice or looming aura, used to frighten and unnerve rather than physically strike.
- **Actions:** terrorize
- **Example creatures:** banshees, wailing ghosts, dread specters
- **Notes:** High risk of being an "abstract capacity" (brief excludes those, e.g. "mind"). Justify only if tied to a concrete emitting part (a voice / visible spectral face); otherwise cut. Flagging for curator call rather than asserting it belongs.

### `possessing-touch` / `phasing-limb`
- **What it is:** A limb-equivalent that can pass through solid matter to reach and afflict a target directly, bypassing physical barriers.
- **Actions:** strike, drain, ambush
- **Example creatures:** poltergeists, wall-phasing ghosts, possessing spirits
- **Notes:** "Ambush" plausible because phasing through walls enables surprise attacks unavailable to solid creatures — a genuinely spectral-specific tactical framing. Overlaps `spectral-limbs`; may be a trait/note on that key rather than separate.

### `ectoplasmic-mass` (generic, no proper-noun risk — "ectoplasm" is broadly generic ghost-lore term)
- **What it is:** Externalized, semi-tangible spectral matter the creature can extrude and shape into a temporary limb or barrier.
- **Actions:** strike, snare, ward
- **Example creatures:** ghosts, poltergeists, materializing specters
- **Notes:** Functionally identical to `spectral-limbs` with a more material framing (touchable versus purely translucent); possible merge. "Ectoplasm" is generic paranormal-folklore vocabulary, not a borrowed brand/game term.

## Floating / levitating bodies

### `levitating-mass` / `hovering-body`
- **What it is:** The creature's buoyant or antigravity body itself, used to ram, hover over, or drop onto a target.
- **Actions:** shove, crush, ambush
- **Example creatures:** floating jellyfish-like beings, hovering spirits, levitating golems, sky jellies
- **Notes:** Whole-body-as-instrument key for floating bodies, same recurring pattern as `mineral-mass`/`vapor-mass`. Ambush plausible since silent hovering enables surprise attacks from above.

### `trailing-tendrils` (floating variant, e.g. jellyfish-like)
- **What it is:** Long trailing filaments hanging beneath a floating body, used to sting or ensnare anything passing below.
- **Actions:** lash, snare, strike, drain
- **Example creatures:** jellyfish, Portuguese man o' war, floating lantern-spirits, sky jellies
- **Notes:** Same cross-domain tendril pattern; flavor is "hangs beneath a buoyant body" rather than growing from a trunk or limb.

### `orbiting-shards` / `satellite-fragments`
- **What it is:** Small solid fragments or orbs that circle the main floating body, moving independently to strike.
- **Actions:** strike, ward, hurl
- **Example creatures:** ring-bearing spirits, rune-orb guardians, floating shard beasts
- **Notes:** Hurl plausible if a fragment can be flung outward at range. Distinct from the mineral domain's `crystalline-barbs` by being freely orbiting rather than attached.

### `gasbag` / `buoyancy-sac`
- **What it is:** An inflated internal or semi-external sac providing lift, which can rupture and vent to drop or propel the creature suddenly.
- **Actions:** burst, shove
- **Example creatures:** gas-filled floating beasts, balloon-creatures, buoyant blimps-of-flesh
- **Notes:** Borderline internal organ (a sac), but functions actively as an instrument when it ruptures for a burst maneuver — flag for curator judgment, similar to `geode-core`/`radiant-core`.

### `undercarriage` (blunt underside used to ram/drop)
- **What it is:** The broad flat or curved underside of a floating body, used to press down on or crush something beneath it.
- **Actions:** crush, shove
- **Example creatures:** floating manta-like beings, hovering slab-creatures, sky rays
- **Notes:** More specific framing of `levitating-mass` for the "drop and crush from above" tactic; likely folds into the whole-body key.

## Swarm bodies

### `swarm-mass`
- **What it is:** The collective body of many small individuals (insects, motes, tiny creatures) acting and moving as one functional whole.
- **Actions:** cloud, drain, terrorize, crush
- **Example creatures:** locust swarms, bee swarms, rat kings, piranha schools, nanite clouds
- **Notes:** The defining whole-body key for swarm-type creatures — the "body" itself is the aggregate. Crush plausible only at large scale (e.g., a swarm engulfing and smothering); drain plausible for biting/feeding swarms (locusts, biting insects, leeching motes).

### `stinging-cloud` (swarm variant, many small stingers acting together)
- **What it is:** The many individual stinging or biting units within a swarm, striking collectively rather than as one limb.
- **Actions:** strike, cloud, drain
- **Example creatures:** wasp swarms, hornet clouds, biting-insect swarms
- **Notes:** More specific than `swarm-mass`; may be redundant with it, or may be the better key if `swarm-mass` is reserved for the passive enveloping action and this one for active stinging.

### `converging-mass` (tactical framing, swarm splits and reforms)
- **What it is:** The swarm's capacity to disperse into many small units and reconverge elsewhere, striking from an unexpected angle.
- **Actions:** ambush, snare
- **Example creatures:** locust clouds, murmurating starling-like swarms, swarming nanite beings
- **Notes:** This names a tactical capability more than a body part; likely a note/trait on `swarm-mass` rather than its own key — flagged for curator consolidation.

## Cross-domain patterns observed (for central curation)

- **Whole-body-as-instrument** recurs across every exotic domain: `coils` (serpentine), `engulfing-mass` (amorphous), `mineral-mass`/`frame` (mineral/construct), `vapor-mass` (gas), `corona` (energy), `levitating-mass` (floating), `swarm-mass` (swarm). Curators may want one umbrella pattern (a body that IS the weapon) with domain-flavored key names, or may keep them fully separate for flavor-text purposes.
- **Coalescing/temporary limb from a formless body** recurs across amorphous (`pseudopods`), gas (`choking-tendrils`), energy (`energy-limb`), spectral (`spectral-limbs`, `ectoplasmic-mass`) — strongly suggests a shared generic key (e.g., `formless-limb` or reuse of `pseudopods`) usable by all non-solid body types, with material/flavor as descriptive text rather than a new key per domain.
- **Armor-surface** recurs as `plating` (metal/mineral), `bark-hide` (plant), `hide`/`shell` (animal, core domain) — likely one shared `plating`/`hide` family with a material flavor note.
- **Internal sac that bursts outward** recurs as `spore-bladder` (amorphous), `seed-pod`/`spore-pod` (plant), `gasbag` (floating), `geode-core`/`radiant-core` (mineral/energy, beam variant) — candidate for one shared `pod`/`sac`/`core` key with a burst-vs-beam flavor split.
- **Tendril/vine/coil family**: `tendrils` (core), `vines` (plant), `coils` (serpentine), `arc-tendrils` (energy), `trailing-tendrils` (floating), `choking-tendrils` (gas) — all a "long flexible reaching part" concept; curators should decide the split between one shared key with flavor text vs. several distinct keys for player-facing variety.
- **Borderline internal-organ calls flagged throughout**: `geode-core`, `radiant-core`, `gasbag`/`buoyancy-sac` — each is externally visible and actively used (not purely metabolic), but resembles an "organ." Recommend explicit ruling from curators since this same tension will recur in future domains (e.g., any creature with a visible glowing/exposed core).
- **Abstract-capacity risk flagged**: `wail`/`dread-presence` (spectral) is the one candidate in this harvest that most risks reading as an abstract capacity rather than a body part; kept in the log per the "prefer over-inclusive" instruction but explicitly flagged as likely-cut.
