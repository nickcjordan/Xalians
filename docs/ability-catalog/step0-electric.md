# Step 0 — Productive-Word List — ELECTRIC

Source: harvest-electric.md, Sections 1-3 (Adjectives / Nouns / Verbs-gerunds). Per COMPOSITION-BRIEF v5 Section 4 Step 0: every noun/adjective/verb concept from the harvest is classified PRODUCTIVE or NON-PRODUCTIVE. The only valid non-productive reasons are grammatical/formal (multi-word phrase over the 2-word cap, bare combining form, cannot grammatically front/follow a partner). Register, tone, theme, and abstractness are never exclusion reasons. Same-root duplicates are collapsed with a note. Boundary/dual-element words are flagged in the Reason column but kept PRODUCTIVE unless a standing ruling (none exists yet for electric) forces otherwise — flagging, not excluding, per brief Section 8. This is a shared verbatim row set feeding four downstream action-cell composers; no name-form or cell judgment happens here.

Element canon note: ELECTRIC owns lightning, voltage, current-as-electricity, storm-as-lightning, and plasma. Water owns current-as-flow (both elements keep their own "current" compounds; flagged below as a live cross-element tension for the auditor). Air owns wind/storm-as-wind. Fire owns spark-as-ember; electric owns spark-as-electrical-discharge (both keep "spark," flagged).

## Flags summary (raised, not resolved, per brief Section 8)

- **Shared "current" tension:** electric owns current-as-electricity, water owns current-as-flow; both elements will independently compose their own current compounds.
- **Shared "spark" tension:** electric owns spark-as-electrical-discharge, fire owns spark-as-ember.
- **Fire/electric burn-boundary:** Searing, Scorching, Sear, Scorch kept for electric's flash-burn sense (lightning-strike burn), distinct from fire's slow-burn sense.
- **Light-domain dual candidates:** Radiant, Incandescent, Blinding, Flash (harvest explicitly routes these to light elsewhere) — kept productive here per brief (definition-honesty adjudication is a cell-placement/hard-rule-6.1 judgment, not a Step 0 exclusion), flagged for the auditor and for cell composers to weigh at placement time.
- **Wind/air-domain dual candidate:** Squall (harvest excludes standalone use as too wind-primary) — kept productive here, flagged.
- **Water-domain dual candidates:** Cascade, Ripple — electric's claim is the chain-effect/current-spreading sense, distinct from water's falling-water/surface-wave sense.
- **Machinery/borrowed-name boundary:** Wire flagged as leaning machinery/tech-idiom in standalone use; Generator, Dynamo, Transformer excluded outright as bare machinery references with no independent electrical-phenomenon sense.
- **Borrowed-name suspicions carried from harvest §5:** Thunderbolt, Static, Discharge, Spark carry existing-creature-game-title suspicion on their bare standalone use; kept productive as lexicon words/compound components per brief ("unsure = flag, keep").
- **Polysemous-verb boundaries:** Crash and Break are kept only in their thunder/storm-onset senses; their other common senses (collision, breakage) are not electric's claim.
