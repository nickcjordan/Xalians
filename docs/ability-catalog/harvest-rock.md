**METAL domain (exclude — refined/forged, not raw stone):** ore (as a smelting-bound word when paired with forge/smelt), forged, alloy, alloyed, smelted, tempered (metallurgical sense), ingot, girder, rebar, steel, iron, bronze, brass, wrought, riveted (metal fastening sense), ferrous, metallic sheen. Note: raw unrefined stone/mineral words like quartz, feldspar, granite, obsidian, flint were kept per brief ("unrefined stone fine").

**FIRE/MAGMA domain (exclude — molten rock belongs to fire):** magma, lava, molten rock, pyroclastic, volcanic ash, cinder (as ejecta), obsidian's molten origin phrasing, basalt's volcanic-formation framing (kept basalt itself as a rock-type noun, but excluded any lava-flow verb phrasing), pumice (volcanic froth, borderline excluded as too fire-adjacent), scoria (volcanic rock, excluded), magma chamber, eruption, volcanic vent.

**Excluded as borrowed (existing game move names, verbatim or near-verbatim):** Rock Slide, Rock Throw, Rock Tomb, Rock Blast, Stone Edge, Sand Tomb (also sand domain), Earthquake, Stealth Rock, Rock Polish, Head Smash, Bulldoze, Mud Slap (also excludes as other-domain: mud/water). These concepts are folded into the field but must not surface as literal name matches in Stage 2 curation.

**Excluded as other-domain (misc):** seismograph/tremor readings kept as nouns but "Richter" and "magnitude scale" excluded as too technical/branded; "petroleum," "coal," "fossil fuel" excluded (chemical/organic domain, not structural rock); "clay," "loam," "soil," "mud," "earth (as dirt)" excluded — those lean a dirt/plant-adjacent domain distinct from hard rock; "cave" kept as noun but "cave dweller," "troglodyte," "spelunk" excluded as creature/activity words rather than medium words.
