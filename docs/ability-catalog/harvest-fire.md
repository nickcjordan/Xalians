# Fire Element Harvest

Semantic field: heat and relentless aggression — burn, escalate, overwhelm. Volcanic register included (magma, molten, cinder — home planet is a volcanic world, Magmuth).

Boundaries excluded per brief: light/radiance words (radiant, prismatic, solar, luminous), electric/lightning words (spark as electricity, bolt, voltage), explosion-only words carrying no heat (concussive, shrapnel, blast if purely kinetic).

## 5. Excluded as other-domain / borrowed

- Radiant, Prismatic, Solar, Luminous, Incandescent (light-emission sense) — excluded as light/radiance domain per boundary instructions (Incandescent kept above only in heat-of-metal adjective sense; flag for downstream review if that reads too light-leaning)
- Spark, Bolt, Voltage, Static — excluded as electric/lightning domain per boundary instructions
- Shrapnel, Concussive, Blast (pure kinetic sense) — excluded as explosion-without-heat per boundary instructions
- Flash, Flash Fire — excluded, "flash" is primarily a light-domain word
- Fireball — flagged, not confidently included; extremely common across many fantasy/game properties (D&D and countless derivatives) rather than tied to one specific title, but risk of reading as borrowed is high enough to recommend exclusion or downstream legal/uniqueness review
- Tar Pit — excluded as other-domain (petrochemical/earth substance, not properly a fire/heat noun)
- Hydro Pump, Shadow Ball, Earthquake style names — none generated; no borrowed candidates of this type surfaced in this field
- Brimstone (kept in Nouns as volcanic/sulfurous material) — flagged that it carries a strong religious/damnation connotation; brief permits abstract religious register, so retained

## Notes on richness

Adjectives, nouns, and verbs are very rich (English has deep vocabulary for combustion, volcanism, and cooking-heat registers). Ability names are strong for strike, lash, crush, rake, drain, burst, cloud, and hurl. Beam is weak — heat does not naturally collimate; only compound-label solutions available, flagged above. Snare is weak — fire does not naturally hold/bind; only viscous-molten-material logic supports it, and that logic is itself borderline other-domain. Mend is weakest of all sixteen — fire is a destructive/consuming element, so restoration names must lean on a forging/tempering metaphor rather than literal healing, and even those read as a stretch. Terrorize is moderate, working through scale and dread of consuming fire rather than an intimate fear-based mechanic.
