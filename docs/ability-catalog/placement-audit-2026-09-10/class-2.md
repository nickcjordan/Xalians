## Judgment calls

- Acid Arc, Acid Fan, Toxic Arc, Toxic Fan: arc/fan can describe a limb's whipping sweep (lash) or a stream's spray pattern (spray); both readings are live.
- Acid Jet, Venom Jet: a jet is a focused line, which fits beam, but spray's own definition explicitly names "a line," so both hold.
- Acid Vapor, Effluvium: vapor/effluvium can be read as an actively projected mist (spray) or as a lingering occupying gas (cloud).
- Acid Vent, Toxic Blast: a vent or blast plausibly reads as either a directed stream (spray) or a body-wide outward release (burst).
- Corrosive Grip: grip clamps (crush) and also holds/pins (snare) with near-equal force.
- The Aura/Gloom family (Baleful/Dread/Dreadful/Sinister/Spectral Aura; Grave/Spectral Gloom) in ghost/terrorize: whether an ambient atmosphere word itself "acts on courage" or merely sets a scene is a close call, resolved SOUND since the modifiers (dread, sinister, baleful) name a fear function.
- The shroud/veil/screen/curtain/mantle family in ghost and chemical/cloud vs. /ward: these words name both a volume of matter and a protective covering with near-equal weight.
- Tide Slam in water/shove: "slam" as a ramming impact versus a landed blow is genuinely dual.
- Toxic Repel in chemical/ward: reading repel as a deflecting field rather than only a pushing force is a stretch worth flagging.
- The Presence family's split from the Aura family (presence excluded from cloud, aura kept in both cloud and terrorize) rests on treating "presence" as non-substantive and "aura" as substantive; a stricter reader could go either way on both.
- Reagent Volley: whether "volley" names discrete solid units (hurl) strongly enough to exclude a shower reading (spray) is a closer call than the table's binary verdict suggests.
