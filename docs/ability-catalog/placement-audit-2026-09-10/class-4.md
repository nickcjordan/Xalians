# Placement audit, class 4 (2026-09-10)

Each placement is tested against its own action's registry definition in `docs/species-templates/REGISTRY-DEFINITIONS.md`: does the word's core sense actually describe what that action does. Duplication across neutral actions is not itself a reason for anything; a word may sit in every action its definition genuinely supports. A row is UNSOUND only where a specific phrase of that action's own definition is cited as failed; ties and defensible overlaps are marked SOUND as judgment calls, per standing instruction not to cut on taste.

Scope: the 59 named entries in the ledger's "Class 4: a neutral name in more than one neutral action" section (60 rows minus the `see below)` bundler artifact, which is not a name and is excluded from this audit). 125 placements judged.

## Judgment calls

- Arc in spray: curved shape overlap with a projected stream, both defensible.
- Barrage, Fusillade, Volley in spray: many launched objects read as a shower.
- Collision in shove: force transfer displaces along with impacting.
- Crack in strike: a sharp single blow reading alongside its whip-crack sense.
- Slash in lash: a slashing motion also reads as a sweeping blow.
- Full Discharge, Expulsion in burst/spray: outward release can read as either directed stream or area release.
- Hold Fast in snare: transitive reading of holding the target fast.
- Impact in shove: generic force word, displacement plausible without implying a wound.
