# SAND — Composed Names v5-a

Actions: STRIKE, LASH, RAKE, SHOVE. Per Composition Brief v5. Element canon: SAND = Endessa — deserts and dunes over a drowned ocean world, abrasion, scouring, engulfment, vitrified glass (planet glassed from orbit), buried ruins. Boundary rulings (binding, never reversed here): rock owns Boulder/Rubble/Rock/Stone; wind-medium sand words (Sandstorm, Haboob, Sirocco, Simoom, Windrowed, Wind-scoured, Whirl) are sand-primary with an air dual-candidate flag; water/ocean words (Undertow, Riptide, Wash-as-water, Mire) flag or exclude to water; glass/vitrification vocabulary is sand's own turf; Dark owns Vortex/Stasis/Supernova/Tidal; fog/mist are water's.
