# Harvest: RAKE (rapid repeated cuts)

Semantic field: slashing flurries, shredding, rending, scratching, and other multi-strike cutting/tearing actions delivered in quick succession. Distinguished from STRIKE (single blow), LASH (sweeping arc), and CRUSH (grips/compresses) by repetition and cutting/tearing mechanism.

## 6. Honest richness assessment

The field is very rich for the "repeated cutting/tearing" core: English has a deep, layered vocabulary for gradations of cutting violence (scratch → nick → gash → rend → shred → mangle) and for the specific sensation of repetition (flurry, barrage, volley, frenzy, onslaught, tattoo). This gives strong coverage for both mild/precise rake attacks (Scratch Storm, Snipping Frenzy) and severe/brutal ones (Mangling Flurry, Savage Flurry).

The weakest sub-concept is genuinely novel *element-neutral* naming beyond the "[verb] + [flurry noun]" template — most strong candidates recombine a fairly small set of core nouns (rake, slash, claw, shred, rend, flurry, frenzy, barrage) with a fairly small set of modifiers (rapid, furious, ragged, frenzied, savage). This is expected and fine for the field (rake is inherently a "many small hits" action, so the vocabulary naturally clusters around repetition-intensifiers), but it means downstream curation should watch for near-duplicate names (e.g., "Slash Flurry" vs "Flurry of Cuts" vs "Furious Slashing" are close paraphrases) rather than assume each is a meaningfully distinct ability.

Anatomy-implying words are moderately rich but skew toward claw/talon-based mechanisms; abrasive (rasp, grate, scour) and thorn/spine-based (bramble, barb, quill, thorn) mechanisms are the two other strong clusters, giving decent instrument-tag variety beyond "generic claws" for downstream element/anatomy pairing.

## Excluded as other-domain / borrowed

- Fury Swipes, Slash (as standalone), X-Scissor, Cross Poison — excluded as borrowed (recognizable existing-game move names)
- Dice, Julienne, Mince — excluded as other-domain (culinary/kitchen prep domain, not combat)
- Carve (as in sculpture) — kept as adjacent but not listed as a headline noun/verb since its primary sense leans craft/art domain; "Carving Strikes" was considered and excluded as other-domain (woodworking/sculpture)
- Sever — excluded as leaning STRIKE (implies one decisive cut, not repetition)
- Filet / Fillet — excluded as other-domain (culinary)
- Pluck — excluded as other-domain (feather removal / stringed instrument, not cutting)
- Grind (alone) — excluded as leaning CRUSH domain (compression) rather than RAKE (cutting)
