## 5. Excluded as other-domain / borrowed

**Excluded as other-domain (liquid-water domain — per boundary instructions):**
- Tide, Current, Wave, Undertow, Surge, Whirlpool, Ripple, Flow, Stream — all liquid-water motion words, excluded even though ice originates from water.
- Meltwater, Runoff — describe liquid water resulting from melting; excluded as liquid-domain, not ice-state.
- Thaw, Thawing — describes the transition OUT of the ice state into liquid; excluded as boundary-adjacent (names the opposite process, not ice itself). Listed above only as a reference marker, not a usable word.
- Slush — mixed solid/liquid state; excluded as liquid-adjacent, borderline domain violation.
- Sluice, Sluicing — liquid channel-flow term; excluded.

**Excluded as other-domain (wind/blizzard-as-wind — per boundary instructions):**
- Blizzard — primarily a wind-driven storm event; excluded as a wind/weather-system word despite snow content.
- Gust, Gale, Squall — pure wind words; excluded.
- Whiteout — meteorological visibility term driven by wind-blown snow; excluded as wind-domain.
- Windchill — explicitly a wind-cold compound; excluded (names wind's effect, not ice's).
- Note: sleet and hail were KEPT per brief instruction ("frozen precipitation fine: sleet, hail; wind excluded").

**Excluded as other-domain (rock/earth domain):**
- Moraine — technically rock/sediment debris carried by a glacier, not ice itself; flagged above in Nouns as borderline but not used in any candidate ability name.

**Excluded as other-domain (fear/psychological domain, non-cold-derived):**
- Terror, Dread, Horror used bare (without a cold-qualifying word) — excluded as belonging to a general fear vocabulary rather than ice's field; only cold-qualified compounds (Winter's Dread, Bone Chill) were kept as candidates.

**Excluded as other-domain (mortuary/medical, flagged not fully excluded):**
- Embalm/Embalming — kept in Nouns/Verbs sections with a flag since it is a legitimate cold-preservation-adjacent word, but excluded from candidate ability names as too strongly mortuary in connotation for a creature move name.

**Excluded as borrowed (recognized existing game move names):**
- Ice Beam — recognized as an existing creature-battler move name; excluded from final candidates despite being a natural fit (noted in section 4 rationale but not counted as a clean original candidate — flagging rather than silently keeping).
- Blizzard (as a move name) — doubly excluded, both wind-domain and a recognized existing game move.
- Aurora Veil — recognized as an existing game move name; excluded.
- Hail (as a bare move name, e.g. summoning weather) — the word "hail" itself is kept as frozen-precipitation vocabulary per brief instruction, but a bare "Hail" ability name matching known weather-summon move naming was avoided in favor of compounds like "Hail Spray."
- Ice Shard (as a bare move name) — recognized as an existing game move; used only inside compound suggestions with a flag, not offered as a clean standalone candidate.
- Frost Breath — recognized as an existing game move name; excluded.

**Flagged, not excluded (shared vocabulary boundary per brief):**
- Stasis, Suspension, Suspend, Suspending — the brief explicitly calls out that "stasis" itself is shared vocabulary with time-based fields rather than being ice-exclusive. These are KEPT in the nouns/verbs lists because they are cold-preservation-legitimate (cryostasis, suspended animation via cold), but flagged here as words a TIME or LOCK/DENY-type field may also legitimately claim. Recommend curation stage resolve ownership rather than this harvest excluding them outright.
- Lock, Locking, Deny, Denying, Delay, Delaying, Halt, Arrest, Bind — these "stasis-by-restraint" verbs are core to the brief's own definition of ICE ("freeze, lock, delay, deny") so they are kept as primary vocabulary, not excluded — flagged here only to note they may overlap with a hypothetical dedicated "stasis/time" field if one exists elsewhere in the catalog.

**Excluded as not natural English (weak/invalid compounds encountered during harvest):**
- Chillfield, Frostray, Sluggishen, Vising — coined-sounding or non-standard; excluded outright, not offered as candidates anywhere above.
