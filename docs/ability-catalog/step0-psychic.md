# PSYCHIC — Section 0: Productive-Word List

Per COMPOSITION-BRIEF v5 Section 4, Step 0. Every noun/adjective/verb concept from harvest-psychic.md sections 1-3 is classified PRODUCTIVE or NON-PRODUCTIVE. The only valid non-productive reasons are grammatical/formal: a multi-word phrase that cannot compose to 2 words, a bare combining form (rule 6.7), or a word that cannot grammatically front/follow a partner word. Register, tone, theme, and "abstractness" are never valid exclusion reasons at this step.

**Canon-flag column:** per assignment, PUPPETING (mind control of another's body), true invisibility, and time reversal are canon-banned themes — but per brief rule 8/Section 4 they are never excluded at Step 0 on canon grounds. Words that gesture at these themes are marked PRODUCTIVE and carry a `⚑ CANON` flag noting the concern, to be resolved by cell placement/naming choice downstream (e.g. used only in the sense of influence/suggestion, never literal body-control). Hypnotic and dream words are explicitly canon-clear (hypnotic trait exists; dream words are Telypso-owned) and carry no flag.

## Summary

- **Table 1 (Adjectives):** 121 entries — 108 PRODUCTIVE / 13 NON-PRODUCTIVE (all non-productive reasons are pre-hyphenated or multi-word compounds exceeding the 2-word cap; no register/tone exclusions).
- **Table 2 (Nouns):** 149 entries — 145 PRODUCTIVE / 4 NON-PRODUCTIVE (possessive phrase, two over-cap multi-word phrases, one duplicate compound).
- **Table 3 (Verbs/gerunds):** 137 entries — 136 PRODUCTIVE / 1 NON-PRODUCTIVE (one pre-hyphenated compound).
- **Grand total:** 407 source concepts — 389 PRODUCTIVE, 18 NON-PRODUCTIVE.
- **Canon flags raised (not excluded):** 24 PRODUCTIVE entries carry a ⚑ CANON note — clustered around the puppeting-adjacent register (possess/commandeer/puppeteer/puppet/marionette/strings/thrall/servitude/compel/coerce/dominate/subjugate/override/manipulate/enthrall) and the invisibility/reality-alteration-adjacent register (obscure/veil/masked/warp/bend/rewrite). None were cut here — per brief Section 4 Step 0 and rule 11, canon compliance is judged per-name at cell placement, not at the productive-word gate. Hypnotic-register and dream-register words (Hypnotic, Hypnosis, Hypnotize, Mesmerize, Oneiric, Reverie, Daydream, Dream, Trance family, etc.) are explicitly canon-clear per assignment and carry no flag.
