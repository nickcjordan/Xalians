# Composed Names — CHEMICAL — Actions: STRIKE, LASH, RAKE, SHOVE (v5-a)

Per Composition Brief v5. Element: CHEMICAL (Drainov — toxins, acids, corrosion, volatile reactions, industrial meltdown residue, living chemical weapons). Row set: verbatim Section 0 productive list from `step0-chemical.md`. Boundary rulings respected: miasma/vapor-disease words flag GHOST; liquid-medium words flag WATER (kept chemical-primary per Step 0 scoping); venom/toxin chemical-primary at plant border; sap is plant's (excluded here as non-primary); real chemical-weapon proper names are hard cuts (code W, reason "real weapon") if a composed name coincides with one.

Standing rulings: 1-2 words, no possessives/hyphens, American English. Bare combining forms die (no bare "Chemo-"/"Toxi-"). No bare action synonyms alone in cells. One action cell per name — impact verbs (Slam/Smash/Ram/Bash/Wallop) home in STRIKE; edged verbs (Slash/Chop/Shred/Rend/Gash/Gouge) home in RAKE; displacement (Buffet/Barge/Push/Topple/Stagger/Heave) homes in SHOVE; LASH owns the sweeping-arc/whip vocabulary. Grip/restraint → snare (not composed here). Material-defined words → their element (n/a for chemical rows here). Franchise/Pokemon poison vocabulary cut or flagged. Anatomy-evoking names keep and tag from the registry.

**"When in doubt, keep and flag" items:**
- Ichor Strike, Ichor Blow, Ichor Jab — "Ichor" carries a mythic/poetic register per the harvest note; flagged as slightly elevated register relative to the plain catalog tier but not signature-register (still reads as an ordinary 1-2 word ability name), kept.

- Etchant Strike — flagged as a slightly unusual/technical noun-as-modifier pairing; judged to pass the say-it-out-loud test (a corrosive etchant delivered as a strike) and kept.

## Counts and summary

**STRIKE:** 79 kept names. **LASH:** 92 kept names. **RAKE:** 89 kept names. **SHOVE:** 87 kept names. **Total kept across the four assigned action cells: 347 names** (before accounting for the one intentional cross-cell duplication of Acid Slash / Venom Slash between LASH and RAKE, which is flagged rather than deduped per brief guidance).

All four cells land comfortably inside the brief's expected healthy range (roughly 30-150 kept names per cell) with no cell in the many-hundreds/thousands failure zone. STRIKE was the numerically tightest fit for chemical vocabulary (chemical is a substance/state-heavy element with almost no native impact vocabulary of its own — its STRIKE cell is carried almost entirely by anatomy-delivery words like Fang/Sting/Stinger/Barb crossed against the strike noun pool, plus the "quick sharp application of a caustic/toxic substance" reading of Jab/Blow/Punch/Strike/Snap/Rap/Gore/Knock). LASH was the richest cell: chemical's tendril/coil anatomy hook (venom glands, coiling stingers) and its liquid-trail imagery (sludge, ooze trailing through a sweep) gave LASH the widest genuinely-distinct set of compositions. RAKE benefited from the genuine double-fit between corrosion-as-repeated-abrasion and RAKE's cutting/scraping register (Etch, Erode, Abrade, Score, Rasp, Scour all composed cleanly as claw-adjacent RAKE names on their own chemical-process merits, not just via the Claws/Talons anatomy bridge). SHOVE was the hardest conceptual fit — chemical substances do not intrinsically displace a target — and the cell survives mainly through two honest bridges: (1) charge/ram/barge compounds where a creature's body (not the chemical itself) delivers the displacement while trailing/coated-in the substance, and (2) a genuine physics reading of reaction-driven force (a gas eruption, an effervescent blast, a reaction's shockwave) as a legitimate expulsive push. Every rejection across all four matrices was coded per-name against a cited Section 6 hard rule (chiefly rule 1/2 definition-honesty and rule 8 say-it-out-loud); no class-level rejection sentence was used as a substitute for individual judgment, and uniform-column notes each cite three individually-checked example names per the brief's rule-79 requirement.
