## FLAGS

**Held pending wind-vs-water ruling (not placed in any cell):**
- Squall — wind or water storm-cell; excluded from CLOUD and BURST cells pending ruling
- Monsoon — seasonal wind-driven rain system; excluded from all cells pending ruling
- Waterspout — a tornado-over-water; genuinely dual-natured, excluded from BEAM/BURST/HURL pending ruling

**Dual-element candidates (other than the already-adjudicated Vortex/Tidal Lash):**
- Fog Bank / Standing Fog — flagged water/ice boundary in harvest-action-cloud.md section 5; placed in water's CLOUD cell here since fog is fundamentally condensed water vapor, but ice-column composition may want it too — flag for cross-check.
- Frost-adjacent forms were NOT drawn from (Excluded-as-other-domain in harvest-water.md section 4 already routes frost/freeze/glacial to Ice) — no leakage occurred, noted for completeness.
- Steam/scald/boil/geothermal — already excluded to Fire in harvest-water.md section 4; confirmed no leakage.
- Humid/humidity — already excluded to Air-adjacent in harvest-water.md section 4; confirmed no leakage.
- "Chilling Current" (TERRORIZE) — risks reading Ice-adjacent tonally; flagged for curator review, kept in cell as the dread-sense (not thermal) is the intended reading.

**Borrowed suspicions:**
- Hydro Pump — confirmed Pokemon verbatim, excluded (standing ruling, reconfirmed).
- Hydro Beam — new finding this pass: "Hydro" as a bare combining form violates the hard audit rule regardless of borrowing; excluded on that independent ground (see BEAM section).
- Water Globe Hurl — no known borrow match found, but "globe" as a projectile shape is unusual enough to flag for a second borrowing check at final audit.

**Tone concerns:**
- Drowning Fear — cut from TERRORIZE for tone (see section 13 note); drowning is a real, visceral human-death association heavier than the game's register elsewhere.
- Tide Spew (SPRAY) — harsh/unpleasant register; kept as technically valid but flagged for a tone pass.
- Current Squirt / Wave Spurt (SPRAY) — flagged as sounding informal/awkward bordering on unintentionally comic when said aloud; likely audit cuts.

**Signature-register names:** none identified this pass — the water lexicon's names stayed within ordinary two-word combat-ability register throughout; no name read as a tier above (e.g., nothing like "Point of No Return" surfaced).

**Cross-cell tensions:**
- Fluke Slam (STRIKE) vs. Fin Whip (LASH) — both use tail/fin anatomy tags; kept distinct since one is a discrete blow and the other a sweeping arc, per each action's own definition.
- Geyser Toss (HURL) vs. Geyser Burst (BURST) vs. Geyser Beam (BEAM) vs. Geyser Spray (SPRAY) — geyser is productive across four cells; each reading is definition-honest to its own action (geyser as thrown mass is the weakest of the four and is flagged in HURL as low-confidence).
- Wave Hurl / Wave Toss (HURL) vs. Wave Burst / Wave Crash (STRIKE/BURST) — kept distinct per harvest-action-hurl.md's explicit note that "Wave Hurl" names a discrete launched mass, not the spray/impact senses used elsewhere.
- Tide Grasp / Tide Clutch appear in both DRAIN and SNARE — DRAIN's usage is the parasitic vitality-taking touch (Draining Touch register from harvest-action-drain.md), SNARE's usage is the restraining hold; both are definition-honest to their own action and are NOT the same name (kept distinguishable by full context: "Tide Grasp" reads ambiguously enough between the two that a curator should pick one home if true duplication is a concern — flagged).

MEND was the richest cell (30 names) because the water lexicon's own adjective cluster (healing, soothing, cleansing, purifying, restorative, curative, regenerative) converges almost entirely with the MEND action's native vocabulary. The lexicon disposition table accounts for every adjective, noun, and verb from the water harvest's three sections, and the action-synonym disposition table accounts for which synonyms from each of the 14 action files were successfully crossed with water and which were rejected, with reasons, action by action.
