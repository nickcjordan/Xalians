## 2. Flags

**Definition-tension flags (word placed but fit is imperfect):**
- Riptide Strike, Undertow Strike — riptide/undertow are defined by DRAGGING, not striking. Kept in strike per raw-harvest precedent but the honest cell for riptide/undertow-rooted names is snare. Recommend cutting the strike-cell versions and letting Riptide/Undertow's snare-cell entries carry the concept.
- Riptide Rake, Undertow Rake — same drag-not-cut tension; weak fits in rake.
- Riptide Shove, Undertow Crush — same tension in shove/crush; weak fits.
- Undertow Pull — appears twice in my raw sweep (shove and snare); definitionally it belongs in snare (a pull that drags you under is restraint, not displacement). Resolved above by placing it in snare only; flagging here so the auditor sees the reasoning.
- Osmotic Drain vs Osmosis — near-duplicates; Osmosis alone is the cleaner science-register single word, Osmotic Drain is a redundant compound of the same concept. Recommend cutting Osmotic Drain, keeping Osmosis.
- Undercurrent vs Undertow Ambush — both frame "hidden current," near-duplicate concepts in the ambush cell. Recommend keeping Undercurrent (cleaner single word), cutting Undertow Ambush.
- Current Shield vs Tide Shield (ward) — near-duplicate framing. Recommend keeping one.
- Mist Spray vs Spume/Spindrift (spray) — Mist Spray reads as a weaker, more generic duplicate of the sharper Spindrift/Spume. Recommend cutting Mist Spray.
- Downpour Burst vs Cloudburst (burst) — near-duplicate; Cloudburst is the established real term, Downpour Burst is redundant. Recommend cutting Downpour Burst.
- Splash Spray — tautological (splash and spray are near-synonyms); weak.
- Vortex Snare — near-duplicate of the dark-owned Vortex entry itself; likely redundant once Vortex's dual tag is in the catalog. Recommend cutting.

**Signature-register flags (reads a tier above plain 1-2 word catalog names):**
- Font of Renewal (mend) — three words with "of," reads as a signature/ultimate move, not catalog-plain.
- Confluence Renewal, Headwater Renewal (mend) — evocative but grander/more literary than the plain-tier mend entries around them; borderline.

**Anatomy/tone flag:**
- Abyssal Maw (terrorize) — "Maw" is an anatomy-evoking word (mouth/jaws) not in the mandatory instrument-tag registry's exact list but adjacent to "jaws." Recommend cutting rather than tagging, since water has no natural jaws-bearing framing; reads borrowed from other creature vocabularies.

**Borderline/weak fits kept for auditor judgment (thin but not dishonest):**
- Spray Rake, Whitecap Rake (rake) — thin but defensible (spray/whitecap have a shearing, wind-torn edge quality).
- Basin Crush, Confluence Drain, Confluence Snare, Confluence Strike, Spring Jet, Column Jet, Cascade Beam, Fjord Ward, Basin Ward, Reservoir Ward, Trench Dread, Depths Dread — geographically-rooted but low-energy words stretched into combat cells; likely thin cuts but included per "wide sweep" instruction rather than pre-cut by me.

**Pending wind-vs-water ruling (NOT placed in any cell, per assignment instructions):**
- Squall — sudden violent WIND (often with rain). Wind-primary per definition audit.
- Monsoon — seasonal WIND system that brings rain. Wind-primary but water-adjacent; my prior consolidated read leaned dual-tag defensible for Monsoon specifically.
- Waterspout — tornado over water, literally made of water; passes the water-dual audit most cleanly of the three. My prior read: strongest water case of the three.
- These three cannot be scored into cells until the wind-vs-water ruling lands. If ruled water-dual, likely homes: Squall → cloud or ambush; Monsoon → mend (rain-giving, life-bringing register) or burst; Waterspout → hurl (as in consolidated-water.md) or ambush.

**Dual-element cross-refs (not owned by this file):**
- Vortex — water+dark, snare cell, owned by dark file.
- Tidal Lash — water+dark, lash cell, owned by dark file.

**No new borrowed-name or real-weapon suspicions found beyond the already-cut Hydro Pump and Depth Charge.** No new Earth-fauna/flora leaks found beyond the already-cut Kelp/Coral/Reef/Sargasso/Hammerhead.
