# Air — Section 0: Productive-Word List

Composition Brief v5 Section 4, Step 0. Every noun/adjective/verb concept from harvest-air.md sections 1-3, classified PRODUCTIVE or NON-PRODUCTIVE. Only grammatical/formal reasons justify non-productive status (multi-word phrase that cannot compose to 2 words, a bare combining form, or a word that cannot grammatically front/follow a partner). Register, tone, theme, and abstractness are never valid exclusion reasons. Same-root duplicates are collapsed into one row with a note. Other-element boundary words are flagged in the Reason column but NOT excluded on that basis alone — they are marked productive/non-productive purely on grammatical grounds, with the boundary noted for downstream composers' cross-referencing.

Standing rulings noted per assignment (not acted on here, only flagged where relevant):
- Squall is AIR-owned with a water dual tag (ratified).
- Waterspout and Monsoon are WATER-owned water+air duals (air cross-references, never owns).
- "Windmill" was flagged to air from dark's audit.
- Rain/precipitation words are WATER's (flag, don't exclude).
- Lightning/thunder words are ELECTRIC's (flag, don't exclude).
