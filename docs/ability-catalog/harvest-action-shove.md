# Harvest: SHOVE (action field)

Definition scope: forces the target out of position — pushing, ramming, repelling, knocking back, displacing. Distinguished from HURL (launched mass — the *creature's own body/matter* thrown at range), from CRUSH (grips and compresses in place), from SNARE (holds/drags/slows — restricting motion, not expelling it), and from STRIKE/LASH (a blow whose point is damage, not relocation). SHOVE's defining feature is an outcome: the target ends up somewhere else, against its will, still standing (or knocked down) rather than grabbed.

## 6. Honest richness assessment

SHOVE is a very rich field in raw vocabulary — English has an unusually large stock of words for forceful, displacing contact, spanning physical impact (ram, bash, slam, charge), outcome-framing (topple, dislodge, unseat, displace), and staggering/off-balance results (stagger, reel, buckle, sprawl). The neutral candidate list is comfortably the largest of the three sub-buckets and needed active curation to avoid redundant near-synonyms (bash/wallop/clout/bump are all barely distinguishable — kept as separate entries because ability-name catalogs benefit from surface variety even where meaning overlaps tightly).

Two genuine weak spots. First, pure "repulsion" vocabulary (Repel, Repulse, Expel) is thinner than impact vocabulary and skews slightly abstract/formal rather than punchy — it reads well as force-field or psychic-adjacent naming, which is why Psychic got a natural elemental lean in section 5, but it also means these words feel a notch more clinical than the ramming/slamming cluster. Second, mechanism words (section 4) lean heavily toward large-bodied, blunt-instrument anatomy (horns, tusks, bulk, hooves) — there is comparatively little vocabulary for a *small or agile* creature's shove (a light, quick creature pushing something bigger is underserved; "elbow aside" and "nudge" were the best fits and nudge was nearly excluded as too mild). Anyone downstream assigning shove moves to small/fast species may need to lean on "wrench," "jostle," or the pulling-type words rather than true push vocabulary.

Excluded/flagged during harvest: "kickback" was flagged as borderline modern-slang-adjacent and left out of the clean neutral list (included only as a discussion note in section 1, not carried into section 3); "bulldoze" and "piston drive" lean mechanical/modern and were kept but flagged borderline rather than treated as safe defaults; "nudge" was judged too gentle for a combat-shove field and excluded from section 3 despite appearing in the verb list for completeness; "undertow" and "riptide" were noted as better fits for SNARE (pulling/restricting) than SHOVE (expelling) and are flagged accordingly rather than double-counted as clean neutral hits.
