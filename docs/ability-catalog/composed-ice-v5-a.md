Element: ICE — cold, frost, frozen water in all forms, glacial mass. Homeworld Krystos: frozen former resort world, now prison world. Ice strikes with mass and edge — bergs, floes, icicles, hail; Rake is strong for ice (icicle points, shard edges). Liquid-water words (tide, wave, brine, current) are WATER's; fog/fogbank are WATER's. Assignment scope: four action cells only (strike, lash, rake, shove) of the 16-action framework.

Standing rulings applied silently throughout (law, never reversed):
1. No prior consolidated ice file; step0-ice.md candidate names are a starting point only.

5. Anatomy-evoking names kept with instrument tags; never cut for being anatomical.

## Section A — Standalone words per action

A productive word is a standalone name only if, alone, it evokes the specific action without a partner word. Most ice words need a partner; the following read as strike/lash/rake/shove names alone.

**Strike:** Icicle (a hard piercing point — strike-evoking alone), Hailstone (a single hard falling impactor), Growler (a floating chunk that collides), Berg (mass that collides).
**Lash:** none stand alone — ice has no word that alone evokes a whipping arc without a partner (the whip-motion vocabulary belongs to the LASH action harvest, not to ice's own lexicon).
**Rake:** none stand alone cleanly beyond what strike already claims (Icicle is claimed for strike per rule 5, one-name-one-cell); ice supplies edges (Icicle, Serac, Crust) but no bare noun that alone evokes repeated raking motion.
**Shove:** Avalanche (dual: rock — pending audit) (alone evokes overwhelming forward mass displacing everything in its path), Iceberg, Floe (a floe drifting into something displaces it; borderline, kept), Glacier (a glacier's advance displaces terrain; strong shove-alone reading).

Rule 5 (one name, one cell) is applied throughout: Berg is claimed for STRIKE standalone above (a berg colliding with a hull is the more central, definition-honest reading); Iceberg (the fuller word) is placed standalone under SHOVE only, since the two surface forms read distinctly enough not to collide as "the same name" under rule 6.

Ice's canon assignment ("Rake is strong for ice") is borne out mechanically: RAKE is the second-widest cell after STRIKE, driven by the element's genuinely rich edge/point vocabulary (icicles, serac, crust, hail, hailstone) crossing cleanly against the cutting and repeated-strike synonym pool, plus the honest "many small hard pieces" reading that licenses the flurry/barrage/volley/riddle sub-cluster (hail riddling a surface, a barrage of hailstones). STRIKE is the widest cell because ice supplies the largest stock of mass/hardness adjectives and nouns (glacial, frozen, arctic, berg, glacier, permafrost) that compose across the largest and most tolerant synonym pool (35 columns, many near-synonymous impact words). SHOVE is a mid-sized field, honestly thinner than strike/rake because true "mass displacing something" is a narrower reading than "mass hitting something" for most of ice's nouns — several strong strike/rake words (Icicle, Serac) never developed an honest shove reading and were left unclaimed there rather than forced. LASH is by far the thinnest cell and this is an honest field-level finding, not underwork: ice's lexicon is fundamentally a rigid-mass and thermal-sensation vocabulary, and LASH requires flexibility (a whip must bend) which ice does not natively supply — the LASH cell's 44 kept names lean almost entirely on the cutting-arc overlap (Slash, Scythe, Cleave, Sickle, Reap, Mow) rather than true whip-motion, and several individually-tested compositions (Icicle Uncoil) were rejected as impossible collocations specifically because rigid ice cannot coil. Two large blocks of ice's productive vocabulary — the appearance/state-only adjectives (Milky, Translucent, Static, Motionless, etc.) and the grip/bind/restraint words (Locked, Grip, Vise, Suspend, Anchor, etc.) — failed all four actions uniformly and by design, since those words' honest domains are WARD, CRUSH, SNARE, and MEND, none of which are in this assignment's scope; every one of those failures is recorded per-name in the matrices above with named justifying examples per the brief's Section 9.5 allowance, and the words themselves are preserved for a downstream composer working those other cells rather than being lost. No Stasis-family name was placed anywhere in this file. Avalanche was placed throughout with its dual: rock (pending audit) flag wherever it composed honestly.
