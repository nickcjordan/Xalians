### CRUSH

Air's honest crush register is PRESSURE: atmospheric weight, vacuum collapse, pressure differential, and downdraft/gale weight bearing down. Air has no limbs, so grip/vise/clamp-type synonyms are used only when an air noun supplies the mechanism (a vacuum genuinely grips by pulling inward; a gale genuinely presses by mass-of-moving-air weight). Geological/mechanical-tool crush synonyms (millstone, quern, anvil, tongs, nutcracker) are definitionally dishonest for air across the board — air has no solid grinding surface — and are accounted for in the matrix's closing per-name proofs rather than silently dropped.
