## 5. Excluded

### Excluded as other-domain
- Ore, Slag, Pig Iron listed above are borderline: kept as nouns because they name refined/processing states of metal, not unworked stone. Raw unworked stone/mineral words (Granite, Basalt, Quartz, Boulder, Flint) excluded — belong to ROCK element.
- Current, Charge, Voltage, Spark, Circuit, Conduit, Amp — excluded, belong to ELECTRIC element.
- Magnet, Magnetic Field — kept adjective "Magnetized" (surface treatment of metal objects, mechanical sense) but excluded "Electromagnet" and "Induction" as ELECTRIC-domain.
- Reboot, Software, Digital, Upload, Firmware, Byte, Code — excluded on voice per brief; modern-computing register forbidden even though "servo," "piston," and "gear" (pre-digital machinery) are kept.
- Upgrade (as slang/tech-product usage) — excluded per hard constraint; the underlying idea of reinforcement is covered by Reinforcing, Tempering, Case-hardened instead.
- Smith, Blacksmith, Smithy — excluded as occupational/other-domain (person/place, not a describable trait, noun-object, or action) rather than a material or mechanism word.

### Excluded as borrowed
- Steel Wing, Iron Head, Gear Grind, Metal Claw, Metal Sound, Metal Burst, Flash Cannon, Shift Gear, Iron Defense, Iron Tail — recognized as existing creature-game move names, excluded.

## Richness assessment

Adjectives, nouns, and verbs are extremely rich — metallurgy, blacksmithing, and machinery are deep, well-documented English vocabularies with strong technical and archaic registers (forge, temper, anneal, quench, braze). Action-type coverage is strong for strike, crush, snare, ward, mend, hurl, spray, burst, and cloud — metal naturally implies constructed, thrown, or fragmenting matter. Rake and shove are moderate; ambush is thin but serviceable via the "coiled spring" framing. Drain and beam are honestly thin to nonexistent for this element — metal has no natural vitality-leeching connotation and no natural coherent-light-beam connotation (that belongs to Light or Electric); no forced candidates were included for those two actions.
