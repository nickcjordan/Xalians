# ACTION: SPRAY

Definition: matter expelled from the body in a spreading, fan/cone-shaped pattern (not a full radial burst, not a focused beam, not a lingering cloud, not a single launched mass).

## Step A — Standalone words (evocative alone, spray-honest)

- **Gout** (a sudden thick spurt) — air sense: a thick gust-driven expulsion. Kept.
- **Geyser** — borderline; a geyser is fundamentally a water/heat phenomenon (harvest itself notes leans hydrological). Not air-defined. NOT placed bare in air's spray cell (rule 6.1, definition honesty) — flagged as a cross-element word, not air's to use standalone.
- **Deluge**, **Cascade**, **Torrent**, **Fountain**, **Flume**, **Sluice**, **Wash** — all fundamentally liquid-motion words (harvest's own richness note: these lean toward fluid dynamics). Not air-defined standalone. Excluded bare from air; only usable air already qualifies them below where "Gust" or "Gale" fronts them, making the compound air-honest rather than the bare word.
- **Vent** — air-honest alone (an opening through which air escapes, or the act). Kept.
- **Breath** — air-honest alone (an exhaled output). Kept.
- **Fan** — air-honest alone (the spreading pattern; also a device that produces wind). Kept, flagged as bordering modern-tech (electric fan) — the natural-language sense (a fan of feathers/air spreading outward) predates the device and is retained per rule 6 (register/theme is not grounds to cut; the word's core meaning is a spreading shape, sanctioned).
- **Sputter** — air-honest alone (an irregular series of small expulsions, natural for air's gusty register). Kept.
- **Effusion** — air-honest alone (a pouring forth, gradual; can describe air/vapor escaping). Kept, flagged as slightly formal/poetic register (permitted; not a rejection ground).
