# Air Element Harvest

Field: wind and freedom — flight, push/pull positioning, evasion, weather-as-wind. Registers covered: winds (named and generic), sky and altitude, flight and soaring, weather-as-wind (breeze, squall, front, draft — non-electrical, non-thermal, non-frozen).

Boundaries enforced per brief: excluded lightning/thunder/storm-as-electrical (Electric element's), excluded ice/cold/frost words (Ice element's), excluded sand/dust-storm words (Sand element's dust ownership) even though wind carries them. Sirocco flagged as borderline-sand per assignment and excluded with rationale below.

## 5. Excluded as other-domain / borrowed

**Sirocco** — excluded per brief flag: sirocco is specifically a hot, dust-and-sand-laden wind originating from the Sahara; its defining character is the sand/dust it carries, which the brief assigns to the Sand element. Borderline-sand as instructed.
**Sandstorm, Duststorm, Dust Devil, Haboob** — excluded as other-domain (Sand): all are defined by the particulate matter (sand/dust) they carry, not by the air itself.
**Thunder, Thunderclap, Lightning, Storm (as electrical storm), Bolt, Squall Line (electrically defined supercell usage), Tempest (when used in its lightning-storm sense)** — excluded as other-domain (Electric): thunder and lightning are electrical discharge phenomena; excluded even though they occur inside windstorms.
**Frost Wind, Chill Wind, Ice Gale, Blizzard, Flurry, Sleet, Rime, Hoarfrost, Wind Chill** — excluded as other-domain (Ice): all are defined by frozen precipitation or sub-freezing temperature, not by air movement itself.
**Fog, Fogbank, Frost Mist** — excluded as other-domain (borderline Water/Ice): fog is technically a ground-level cloud of condensed water droplets; its defining trait is moisture/condensation, not wind. Listed above once as a borderline "kept" (Cloudbank) but Fog/Fogbank itself is excluded here for consistency — the condensation-defined term goes to Water, only wind-driven cloud/haze terms were retained in section 4.
**Monsoon** — excluded as other-domain (Water): monsoon is defined by its rainfall, not by the wind itself, in common usage.
**Hurricane, Typhoon, Tornado** — excluded as borrowed/other-domain: these name specific real-world storm systems that combine wind with heavy rain/electrical activity and are commonly used as literal proper-noun storm names in other creature games (Tornado is a very common existing move name); excluded as borrowed.
**Air Slash, Aerial Ace, Gust (as standalone move), Whirlwind (as standalone move), Twister, Hurricane, Sky Attack, Wing Attack, Feather Dance, Tailwind (as standalone move)** — excluded as borrowed: these are recognized as existing creature-game move names (Pokemon-type flying moves) and are excluded outright rather than reused, per the no-borrowed-names rule. Component words (wind, gust, wing, feather, sky, tailwind) remain usable within this harvest's original two-word combinations where the combination itself is not a direct lift.
**Vise-grip, Choke** — excluded as other-domain: these imply direct anatomical/physical grappling mechanism (hands, coils) rather than air pressure; Air Vise was kept in section 4 because "vise" there is qualified by "Air" and reads as a pressure-vise metaphor, but the bare mechanical terms are excluded here.
**Lance, Spear (as in Air Lance)** — excluded as other-domain: implies a solid bladed/pointed weapon, not an air phenomenon; the compound "Air Lance" does not read as natural English wind vocabulary so much as a weapon skinned with air, differing from accepted patterns like "Air Cannon" (a mechanism metaphor common across many domains) — flagged as weak/excluded from the beam list.

## Richness assessment notes (informal, included for curator context)

- **Beam** is the weakest action type for Air: wind is not naturally a focused-line medium (that is more native to Electric, Light, Psychic). All four beam candidates above are weak stretches; recommend treating Air as thin/absent for beam during curation.
- **Rake** and **drain** required borderline anatomy-adjacent terms (talon, breath) to fill reasonably; still natural English, flagged for curator review rather than force-excluded.
- **Snare**, **shove**, **burst**, **ward**, and **ambush** are the richest categories — wind's push/pull and evasive character maps very naturally onto these.
- **Terrorize** is well-supported by wind's aural register (howling, wailing, keening, moaning) — a strong sub-field worth keeping in the shared corpus.
- **Cloud** required careful boundary-walking against Water (fog/mist ownership) and Ice (frost mist); only wind-driven haze/veil terms were kept.
- The weather-as-wind register (front, air mass, thermal, updraft, downdraft) is narrower than expected once storm-electrical and precipitation terms are stripped out; it mostly reduces to pressure/current vocabulary, which is captured in nouns/verbs above.
