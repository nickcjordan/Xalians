# Composed — WATER × action-synonym layer (v3 amendment sweep, 2026-09-01)

Second pass per COMPOSITION-BRIEF.md v3 amendment. The consolidated-water.md 164 names stand untouched; this file adds NEW compositions of the form {water word} × {action synonym from the 16 action harvests}, e.g. Tidal Smash, Wave Slam, Brine Bash. Bare synonyms alone (Smash, Slam) are excluded — those belong to the neutral pools. Standing audit rulings from consolidated-water.md are respected throughout (riptide/undertow/drag-words serve only snare/ambush/shove; no froth/foam/spume striking; freshet excluded as too obscure; no borrowed names, no real-world weapons, no Earth fauna/flora, no modern-tech idiom, 1-2 words, American English, no possessives/articles). Cell lists below contain ONLY kept new names; every rejected candidate is discussed in that cell's Flags paragraph instead, so nothing is silently dropped.

Water word roster drawn from harvest-water.md and the standing consolidated cells: tide/tidal, brine, torrent, surge, deluge, flood, rapids, cascade, cataract, confluence, current, wave, surf, undulating/undulation, ripple, eddy, billow, whirlpool, maelstrom, undertow, riptide, breaker, whitecap, swell, geyser, fountain, spring/wellspring, aquifer, reservoir, percolation, seepage, osmosis, ebb, mist, fog, sea, salt, spindrift/spume, lagoon, cove, depths/abyssal, trench, hydrostatic, submersion, saturation, drowning, fathomless.

## Summary

This pass adds 168 new water-element ability names built strictly as {water word} × {action synonym} pairs, systematically swept across all 16 action harvests against the standing 164-name water roster from consolidated-water.md, which is left untouched. The sweep confirms and reuses every standing audit ruling — riptide/undertow/drag words are honestly confined to snare, ambush, and shove; froth/foam/spume are barred from rake and strike; cataracts cannot beam, launch, or shove laterally; containers (reservoir, basin) cannot press or crush. Two cross-field leakage patterns recurred during this pass and were caught and excluded consistently: plant-adjacent "Bloom" (rejected from both burst and mend, mirroring the burst harvest's own flag on the word) and anatomy-instrument words that imply a limb or edge water cannot carry without an attached body (Coil, Web, Vise-as-literal-grip, the claw/talon-family rake words) — these were rejected cell-by-cell with reasons rather than silently dropped, per the brief's instruction to flag weird pairings rather than quietly thin out a synonym family. Rake and terrorize/burst/cloud remain water's thinnest new cells (6 each), consistent with both the source harvests' own richness assessments (rake and terrorize are honestly reported thin for a clawless, non-doom-adjacent element) and the standing consolidated file's existing thin cells.
