# Action Harvest: BURST

**Definition:** violent radial release centered on self — novas, shockwaves, eruptions radiating outward from the creature. Distinct from beam (focused line), spray (spreading arc, not full radial, and matter-based), hurl (a launched mass moving away from self), cloud (lingering area, not a violent instant release), lash (a limb-swept arc). Burst is: sudden, radial (all directions or near-omnidirectional), centered on the creature's own body, and a release of something that was building or stored.

## 6. Honest Richness Assessment

The BURST field is rich and well-supplied across all sections. The core vocabulary (burst, blast, eruption, explosion, detonation, discharge, rupture, shockwave, nova, corona, pulse) gives a strong, genuinely element-neutral spine — this is one of the easiest action types to keep neutral because the words describe the *shape and suddenness* of release rather than any material.

Sub-concepts and their depth:
- **Violent/destructive release** (burst, blast, explosion, detonation, rupture, blowout): very rich, many strong neutral candidates.
- **Radiating wave/shape** (shockwave, ripple, ring, corona, aureole, halo): rich, and these give useful visual variety (ring-shaped vs. omnidirectional-cloud-shaped release).
- **Pressure-building-then-releasing** (vent, overflow, discharge, paroxysm, convulsion, spasm): moderately rich; good for "charge-up" flavored moves.
- **Soft/organic radial release** (bloom, unfurl, efflorescence): thinner — only a handful of words, and they carry a plant-adjacent flavor that risks tipping into element-leaning even when used abstractly (flagged in section 3).
- **Astronomical register** (nova, corona, aureole, flare): a strong small cluster, reads well for light, psychic, ghost, and electric equally; less natural for something like rock or plant.
- **Sound-adjacent burst words** (report, peal, concussion): thin and mostly unusable directly as names (they read as noun-only or awkward), included mainly as flavor/definition support rather than name candidates.

Weakest area: words for a *contained, self-centered* release as distinct from an *outward-traveling* one are scarce — English doesn't have many words that emphasize "centered on self" over "moving away from self," so several candidates (torrent, gush, cascade, volley) drift toward spray/hurl territory and were pushed to Excluded or flagged borderline rather than forced into this list. This is the field's one genuine thinness; the exhaustive search did not turn up more without violating the other-domain or borrowed-name constraints.

## 8. Excluded as Borrowed

- Nova Burst — checked against existing catalog names in genre; "Nova" alone and generic combinations are common enough across many properties to be a risk if used as an exact, isolated single-word move name without qualification; kept here only as a compound ("Nova Burst," "Nova Pulse") which reads as original combinatorial naming rather than a lifted title. No exact matches identified to a specific known move name (e.g., no direct collision with "Explosion," "Self-Destruct," or similar franchise moves), but flagging the bare word "Nova" and "Self-Destruct"-style single-word bursts as high-risk if used unqualified.
- Self-Destruct — excluded outright; this is a well-known move name from an existing creature-collection franchise's move list. Do not use "Self-Destruct" or close variants ("Self Destruction") as a name.
- Explosion (as a bare single-word name) — flagged: "Explosion" alone is a known move name in an existing franchise; safe to use only within a longer, more specific compound (e.g., "Violent Explosion," "Inner Explosion") rather than the bare word as a standalone move title.
