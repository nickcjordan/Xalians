## STRIKE

Definition test applied: a plant word may strike only if it names something hard, pointed, or a mass capable of a single discrete blow — Root, Thorn, Spine, Barb, Bough, Branch, Bramble (as thorned mass), Burr, Seedpod, Bud, Bulb, Stalk, Trellis, Bramblewood, Thornwall. Diffuse/soft/scattering things (Pollen, Spore, Foliage, Leaf as single leaf, Moss, Mulch, Sap, Loam, Humus, Compost) are definition-dishonest for a discrete hard blow and are excluded (D) — those belong to spray/cloud/drain.

## LASH

Plant's signature action per assignment: vines and tendrils ARE whips. Definition test: a plant word may lash only if it names a long, flexible, whip-like or scythe-able growth (Vine, Tendril, Bramble, Bough, Branch, Runner, Sucker, Bramblewood, Bloomburst-as-release) or a mowing/reaping-honest mass (Bramble, Thicket, Bracken, Fern, Reed-flagged, Stalk, Stem, Frond). Rigid point-only words (Thorn, Spine, Barb alone) cannot arc/sweep on their own without a flexible carrier — they are excluded here on definition grounds (a thorn doesn't sweep, the vine bearing it does) unless paired as the trailing edge of a whipping vine, which the harvest's own "Vine Lash" precedent supports.

### Step A — standalone

- **Whiplash** is an action-pool word (belongs to the neutral synonym set, not plant's), not composed here.
- **Scourge** — the plant harvest doesn't own this bare; excluded as a bare action-synonym, not placed standalone in the plant cell (rule: bare action synonyms belong to neutral pools).
- No plant word alone reads as a complete LASH ability name without an action partner (Vine, Tendril, etc. are the instrument, not the motion) — Step A yields no standalone entries, consistent with LASH's whip-motion being the verb half of every valid name here.

### Step B — element-internal compositions

## SHOVE

Definition test: SHOVE displaces the target — ends up elsewhere, still standing or knocked down, not grabbed/held (that's SNARE/CRUSH). Plant's honest carriers: mass-driving growth (Root, Bramble, Bramblewood, Branch, Bough as a swept obstacle), a rising/thrusting root/sprout (root erupting underfoot displaces), and growth-triggered ground upheaval (Root Surge, harvest-ratified). Note the brief's own hard rule 2: "Currents push, they do not strike" — inverted usefully here, since SHOVE is exactly the "push" register, so mass/rigid plant words that failed STRIKE's blunt-force test can still succeed here if their defining feature is displacement rather than impact-damage.
