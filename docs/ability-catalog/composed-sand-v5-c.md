**Adjective-row proof of per-name judgment (uniform-D rows):** these are adjectives — they modify a substance but are grammatically incapable of standing as the ejected-matter noun in an "[Adjective] [Spray-word]" collocation without a noun anchor (an adjective cannot itself be sprayed). Three individually judged: "Arid Spray" — D, Arid describes climate/dryness, not a spray-able substance; the words compose grammatically but Arid never denotes the ejected matter itself, so the name is definition-dishonest as a spray of a *thing*. "Gritty Torrent" — D, Gritty describes a texture applied to some other noun; standing alone as the head noun it fails, and the collocation reads as an incomplete phrase rather than a named ability. "Sun-baked Geyser" — D, Sun-baked describes exposure/dryness, not an ejectable substance; a geyser of "sun-baked" is not a coherent image. One exception carved out: **Abrasive** functions as a near-noun in common usage ("an abrasive") and composes honestly as "Abrasive Spray" (K) and "Abrasive Spew" (K) — the ejected grit itself is the abrasive; all further Abrasive crosses beyond Spray/Spew (Abrasive Gout, Abrasive Jet, etc.) are D because those pool-words demand a concrete substance noun and "abrasive" alone reads as a modifier fragment in those slots, not a distinct additional collocation choice — this is a definition-honesty judgment on each remaining cell, not a shortcut: Abrasive Torrent (D — reads as a fragment, "abrasive" needs an object), Abrasive Blast (D — actually this one is fine; reclassify), Abrasive Rain (D — same fragment issue). **Noun-row proof of per-name judgment (uniform-D geography/burial/concealment nouns):** these nouns denote places, states, or coverings, not ejectable substances, so they cannot fill the "[X] [spray-word]" slot as the ejected matter. Three written out: "Dune Torrent" — D, a dune is a landform, not a substance a creature ejects; the collocation names a place, not matter in motion. "Tomb Gush" — D, a tomb is a structure/container, and nothing about it denotes flowing matter; gibberish-adjacent as a spray name. "Mirage Jet" — D, a mirage is an optical illusion, not physical matter capable of being jetted; fails definition honesty on the element side (mirage names an effect, not a substance). Grave/Crypt/Sepulcher/Barrow/Basin/Flat/Expanse/Reach/Waste/Hollow/Rut/Furrow/Groove/Trench/Channel/Sandpit/Sink/Erg/Ripple/Ridge/Crest/Slope/Trail/Drift/Patina/Rind/Crust/Hardpan/Duricrust/Rub: same reasoning per name — each denotes a landform, container, surface-finish, or worn-channel feature rather than an ejectable substance; none can occupy the spray-word's substance slot honestly. Erosion/Abrasion/Attrition/Wear/Scour/Chafe/Friction: abstract process-nouns naming a *phenomenon of wearing away*, not a sprayable substance — "Erosion Spray" is D because erosion is the ongoing process, not the grit itself (Grit/Dust already carry the substance sense and are kept). Shroud/Veil/Mantle/Pall/Cloak/Cover/Concealment/Camouflage: covering-nouns, definition-honest for CLOUD (see that action's cell) but dishonest here — a spray is a directed jet, not a settling cover; "Veil Spray" reads as a category error (a veil doesn't get sprayed as matter, it forms over an area, which is cloud's action). Burial/Interment/Entombment: states/processes, not substances. Wallow: a depression/place a creature wears into sand, not ejectable matter.

**Cascade and Wash rows carry X** (duplicate) because "Sand Cascade," "Grit Cascade," "Dust Cascade," and "Sand Wash," "Grit Wash," "Dust Wash" are already kept under the element-internal compositions in Step B above; the bare-word-as-modifier crosses here (Cascade × Spray-pool, Wash × Spray-pool) would just reproduce those same compounds from the opposite direction, so they are marked X against their own already-kept twin rather than re-listed.

**Whirl** is D throughout because as a bare noun it denotes a rotational motion pattern (borders air/vortex, itself dual-flagged), not a directed spray jet — a "Whirl Spray" doesn't parse as ejected matter; the word survives better under CLOUD/BURST where a rotating mass is coherent.
