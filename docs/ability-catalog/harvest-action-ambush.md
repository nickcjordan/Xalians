# Harvest: AMBUSH — a burst of closing speed ending in a hit

Semantic field: pouncing, diving, charging, rushing, springing from concealment. Covers pounce/lunge vocabulary, charge/rush words, dive/swoop words, and surprise-attack concepts.

## 6. Honest richness assessment

The AMBUSH field is very rich overall, but richness is uneven across its sub-concepts:

- **Charge/rush vocabulary** (charge, rush, onrush, onslaught, blitz, storm, barrel, careen, gallop) is extremely deep — English has abundant words for forceful forward momentum, both formal and informal register, plus strong military vocabulary (sortie, sally, foray, raid, incursion, skirmish).
- **Pounce/lunge/spring vocabulary** (pounce, lunge, spring, leap, bound, vault) is deep and well-differentiated by implied body mechanics (feline pounce vs. fencing lunge vs. frog-like bound vs. arm-driven vault), which is useful for anatomy tagging.
- **Dive/swoop vocabulary** (dive, swoop, stoop, plunge) is moderately rich but narrower — mostly aerial/raptor-specific, with "stoop" being a genuinely rare, high-value word (falconry term for a hunting dive) worth flagging as a strong differentiator name.
- **Stealth/surprise-attack vocabulary** (ambush, waylay, bushwhack, sneak, stalk, prowl, skulk, lurk, slink, creep, blindside) is very rich and offers excellent register range from formal (ambuscade, waylay) to frontier/folk (bushwhack) to sinister (skulk, lurk).
- **Interception/pursuit vocabulary** (intercept, cutoff, tackle, takedown, home in) is thinner — fewer natural one/two-word ability names here since most collocations skew toward sports or military jargon rather than creature-combat register. "Intercepting Strike" and "Tackling Strike" are serviceable but not as vivid as the stealth or charge sub-fields.
- **Feint/deception vocabulary** (feint, gambit) is genuinely thin for AMBUSH specifically — these words belong more naturally to strategy/psychology than to a physical closing-speed hit, so only two candidates surfaced and both are borderline; this sub-concept is better served by other action types (e.g., a future "deceive" or psychic-domain action) than by AMBUSH.
- Overall: the field comfortably supports 40+ strong element-neutral candidate names, which is well above what most actions in this catalog will yield, because AMBUSH sits at the intersection of two very productive English domains (predator behavior and military surprise-attack idiom).

## Excluded as borrowed / other-domain

- **Excluded as borrowed:** none identified as recognizable existing-game move names within this field (words like "Quick Attack," "Sucker Punch," and "Extreme Speed" were considered and excluded for being direct or near-direct existing-game move names).
- **Excluded as other-domain:**
  - "Predation" / "Predator" — retained cautiously above as a noun only; the adjective/agent-noun forms lean too heavily into biology/ecology taxonomy language and were excluded as candidate ability names.
  - "Interception" in its sports sense (American football) — excluded any name that reads as sports jargon (e.g., "Pick Six," "Sack") rather than natural creature-combat English.
  - "Bushwhack" in its modern hiking-trail sense (to hack through undergrowth) was excluded from that specific meaning; only the older ambush-attack sense is retained.
  - "Blitz" retained for its general "sudden intensive attack" sense, not its American-football or WWII-specific "Blitzkrieg" historical sense.
