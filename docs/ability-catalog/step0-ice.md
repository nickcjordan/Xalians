# Composed Catalog — ICE, Section 0 (Composition Brief v5 compliance)

Element: ICE — cold, frost, frozen water in all forms, glacial mass. Homeworld Krystos is a frozen former resort/prison world. This is Section 0 only (the productive-word list), sourced from harvest-ice.md sections 1-3, feeding four downstream action-cell composers as a shared verbatim row set.

Standing rulings noted here, not acted on: the Stasis family (Stasis, Stasis Snare, Stasis Ward, Stasis Heal, Stasis Mend, Stasis Restore) is DARK-owned with ice dual tags — ice may cross-reference, never own. Fog/Fogbank are WATER-owned (condensed-vapor ruling). Liquid-water words (tide, wave, brine, and similar) are WATER's — flagged, not excluded, where they appear at all in this harvest. Avalanche is a snow/ice mass word and per the 2026-09-01 material-word ruling may be claimed by ice — flagged prominently; rock may contest, the auditor decides.

Every noun/adjective/verb concept from the harvest's lexicon sections is classified PRODUCTIVE or NON-PRODUCTIVE below. The only valid non-productive reasons are grammatical/formal: a multi-word phrase that cannot compose to 2 words, a bare combining form, or a word that cannot grammatically front/follow a partner word. Register, tone, theme, and abstractness are never exclusion reasons. Same-root duplicates are collapsed into one row with a note; other-element boundary words are flagged, not excluded.
