# Composed Catalog — WATER (v4, Composition Brief v5 compliance run, 2026-09-01)

Element: WATER — the liquid medium itself in all its forms and behaviors (tides, currents, waves, pressure, depths, mist, springs). Never spooky, never wind-defined, never ice-defined. Fog/mist are water-owned (condensed water vapor). The neutral-territory healing register (Algael lore) makes water the richest MEND element.

This run follows COMPOSITION-BRIEF v5 Section 4's sweep algorithm mechanically: Step 0 (productive-word list), then per action, Step A (standalone words), Step B (element-internal compositions), Step C (full synonym cross, every element word × every action synonym, individually judged). Standing rulings from the assignment are treated as law and never reversed in either direction.
