## Honest richness assessment

Strike, lash, beam, burst, cloud, and drain are rich sub-fields for electric — natural, plentiful English vocabulary exists without straining definitions. Shove, hurl, spray, ambush, snare, mend, and terrorize are thin: electricity's core imagery (sudden strike, arc, current, storm) does not map naturally onto grabbing, throwing mass, spraying matter, stealth, holding/dragging, healing, or dread. Those sections above report the weak or absent candidates honestly rather than forcing bad fits. Crush is the thinnest of all — no genuine candidate found.
