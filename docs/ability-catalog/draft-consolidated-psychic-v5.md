Redundancy extraction: redundancy-rejections-psychic.txt (60 lines, all from composer A's STRIKE/LASH/RAKE/SHOVE cells and composer B's AMBUSH/HURL cells).

**Restoration policy applied (six clauses + psychic's seventh absolute bar):** redundancy is NOT a cut rule — near-duplicates are welcome by design. Every redundancy-coded rejection is restored (marked °) UNLESS it hits: (1) same-root adjective/noun duplication already present as the identical string in the same cell; (2) a light-tap synonym on a broad-mass word (Knock-class); (3) the composer's own reason also cites a genuine definitional ground (literal keyword: flat, undersells, comic, no-mass, container, drag, target-state, scale, tautology); (4) an identical name-string already owned in another cell; (5) neutral-pool collision; (6) a ratified standing cut; and, absolute for psychic, (7) never restore anything coded X/X-CANON for the puppeting sense — canon rejections are not redundancy rejections and none appeared in the extraction file (all 60 lines are ordinary D/G redundancy codes from STRIKE/LASH/RAKE/SHOVE/AMBUSH/HURL; no canon-coded line was in scope for restoration).
