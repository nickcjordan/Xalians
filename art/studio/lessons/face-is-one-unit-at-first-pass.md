# face-is-one-unit-at-first-pass

- Governs: part order and part scope for heads
- Applies when: the first part pass on a creature whose face is defined by large eyes
- Status: current
- Creature: hypnopet
- Date: 2026-10-13
- Evidence: Hypnopet head previews B-zzjs, B-qci2, B-p3yd (set aside, not shown to Nick)

Three head previews that changed only the skull, with the eyes, ears and muzzle held, came out nearly identical and none read as the cute face of the sheet: the eyeballs sitting on the skull as bulging spheres dominated every view. A head read is made by skull, eyes in their sockets and muzzle together, so the first face pass treats them as one unit (skull, eye sockets, eyes and muzzle) and the separate eyes and muzzle parts refine details afterwards. Preview directions held to one sub-part come out too similar to be worth a choice.
