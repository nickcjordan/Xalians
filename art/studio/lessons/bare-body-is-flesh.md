# bare-body-is-flesh

- Governs: bare-body views, blockout target
- Applies when: Any creature with fur, feathers or other covering that adds volume
- Status: current
- Creature: hypnopet
- Date: 2026-10-13
- Evidence: Hypnopet bare views: B-yx5f (furred), B-ysmr (clay of furred outline), B-olm3 (gaunt), B-xmps/B-xf4u/B-ru2m (approved); Nick 2026-10-13 tail question

The bare-body views show the flesh under the covering, because the blockout models the surface the fur grows from and the rig deforms. Shapes made of hair (a cotton-ball tail, cheek puffs, a chest ruff, an ear fringe) are a stub or slim form in the bare views and get their volume from fur length later; muscle and bone (haunches, long feet) stay full size. Drawing the furred silhouette in gray clay is wrong (Hypnopet's clay tail was a solid sphere). Asking for 'flesh under the fur' overshot into a gaunt, realistic hairless animal; ask for soft, rounded cartoon flesh with no visible ribs or bones, keep the face and eye size from the furred views, and record the fur depth per region in the sheet's consistency note.
