# Luceras head, pass 3: method change after the hold audit (shared addendum to BRIEF.md)

The orchestrator audited the head after two builds in each stream fell short of the sheet (defect: the face reads as balloon masses; long or blunt muzzle, bulging ball eyes, no cheekbone or brow structure). The limiting assumption was the method rule "no image-to-3D in this part". That rule is lifted for the head: the earlier Akinza work and the Hypnopet trial both showed that hand-placed masses stall on faces while reconstruction reads like the sheet.

Pass 3, same deliverables as BRIEF.md, cap 60 minutes:

1. Reconstruct the head from the approved clay views, cropped to the head and neck: front bc153b786d965458e78dab459cc8a9e6027a66b67dc3a38d58e61bccba570981, side d265b6fcbff2e1f3fdd994590fd8b884eca9397680c871d8ccfc3d9d6aec8ec9, three-quarter 352833b55a8c0f986f76f81e9c5d9389bfc2849a99ff4161507a78c920a40b99 (crop each to the head plus upper neck, mask the gray background, mirror the front crop about the face midline if it is turned). Use the multi-view model with front plus side where both are clean; otherwise the single-view model on the three-quarter crop. The tool and rules are those of stage 4 (Hunyuan3D-2 under C:\dev\art-data\tools, GPU under 6.5 GB with offload, one GPU job at a time, run records with the license line). The Claude stream has a runner at C:\dev\src\xalians-creature-pipeline\art\pipeline\recon\reconstruct.py and masks.py (read-only for the Codex stream; Codex writes its own in its folder).
2. Treat the reconstruction as the skull, face, muzzle and neck volume only. Remove its horns, ears and eyes if it grew any. Scale it so the skull width matches your pass-2 head within 5 percent, align it to the blockout's head position, and graft it onto the body at the same neck ring as before with a bridged seam and a declared neck blend.
3. Keep the pass-2 horns (thick, ridged, half-turn, tips inward, skin collar), eyeballs (34 mm, seated in carved sockets with lids) and ears (cupped, rounded, behind the horn bases) as authored rigid or merged parts, re-seated on the new skull. Carve sockets into the reconstruction; do not edit the reconstruction's surface beyond socket carving, the ear and horn seats and the neck blend.
4. Everything outside the head and neck blend unchanged, measured. Same renders, composites, progress strip, measurements, report and five checks as BRIEF.md.

GPU sharing: check nvidia-smi before the reconstruction; if more than 2 GB is in use, another stream is on the GPU: wait, polling every 60 seconds for up to 20 minutes. One Blender process at a time, same wait rule.
