## Method (the same constraint for both streams)

Work in Blender 5.2.2 (WSL: `wsl -d Ubuntu -- /home/njord/.local/opt/blender-5.2.2-linux-x64/blender -b --factory-startup --python-exit-code 1 --python <script> -- <args>`; put --python-exit-code before --python; LF line endings in scripts). Start from the approved blockout mesh. Build the head as a dense sculpt-quality surface: you may carve and add volume with any Blender means (sculpt brushes through bpy, booleans, metaballs, skin modifier chains, displacement from the sheet images, voxel remesh), but the result must be one closed manifold for the skull plus muzzle plus ears (horns and eyeballs may be separate rigid objects, which is normal production practice for rigid attachments). No image-to-3D reconstruction in this part: the comparison is of Blender steering. Keep the head and horns together under 250K triangles. Horns: ridged (ridges may be modeled or a displacement), slim, the curl and tip direction as ruled. Eyeballs: two spheres seated in carved sockets with lids suggested by the surrounding surface. Ears: small rounded shells behind the horn bases. Whiskers: placeholder cards or curves, six to eight per side.

Iterate against the head close-up and the front and side turnaround views at the head camera; keep two or three intermediate renders so the progression is visible. Stop when you can name nothing left that reads wrong against the sheet at a 192 px sprite size and at the close camera, or after 90 minutes of wall time, whichever first, and report what remains.
