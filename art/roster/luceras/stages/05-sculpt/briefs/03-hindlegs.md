Luceras is "a small bounding quadruped, built around its hind legs" (species record): the hind legs are the creature's signature mass, so they must read strongly.

This part is, on both sides (model the left, mirror to the right exactly about X=0): the thigh from the hip joint down (the big muscle mass that reaches up into the rump and forward toward the belly), the stifle (knee), the gaskin, the hock with its point, the long metatarsus, and the hind paw with four toes, toe pads and dark claws. Move the thigh-front seam so the tuck rises as the clay shows, inside a declared band.

Not this part: head and neck (part 1, approved), the trunk (part 2, approved), the forelegs (part 4), the tail (part 5).

## Method

1. Soft forms (thigh, stifle, gaskin, hock, metatarsus) start from the base surface, which is the body reconstruction. Refine by brush work in numpy against the clay views: carve the landmarks the clay shows (the front thigh muscle and its border, the rear thigh edge, the stifle angle, the tendon line down the gaskin, the hock point, the tendons on the metatarsus). Do not build the leg from primitives or metaballs. You may sample the raw reconstruction e6693c32b646bd0337b34f52e4b7ce0d182c42b4b612a39bfda0e6fadebbc936 where it helps; part 2 found it within 1 mm of the base trunk, so expect little.
2. The paw is precise and is authored: four toes with knuckles and pads, shaped as the clay shows, built as clean geometry and unioned into the body (voxel remesh locally around the paw only, then reproject the rest), so the body stays one closed manifold. Claws are separate rigid objects (dark later; matte gray now), seated on each toe tip, bone-parented to leg_hl_03 / leg_hr_03. Transfer skin weights to any new or remeshed vertices from the base by nearest surface, then normalize to at most 4 influences.
3. Belly tuck: raise the underline in front of the stifle toward 0.39 m, blending into the approved trunk inside a band you declare and measure.
4. Mirror: build the left leg, then mirror the leg region to the right exactly, so the two match vertex for vertex where topology allows, or by remesh symmetry otherwise. Report the left-right asymmetry (max distance from a mirrored point to the surface).

## Self-review is part of the job (read this twice)

After every iteration, render the leg cameras and compare them with the clay crops side by side, then write down in iter_notes.json, per iteration, what still reads wrong and the change you will make, with a number where one exists (for example "stifle angle 168 degrees vs about 140 on the clay side"). Strength matters: a landmark that is only visible when you know where to look does not count; at the leg camera each landmark you carve must be visible as a shading change in the render at 1024 px, and the thigh and hock silhouette must match the clay in the side view. The previous part's other build carved landmarks too faint to see and still recommended acceptance; that build was not chosen. Stop only when you cannot name anything in the leg that reads wrong against the clay at the leg cameras and at 192 px, or at the cap. In report.json, "recommended" in each check names what the reviewer should look at and your honest read, never a bare "accept".
