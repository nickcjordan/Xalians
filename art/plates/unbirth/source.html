The Genesis Prototype on Floria

01The Age of Unbirth
The seed pods, the rain, the Generator's light and the storm are live. Reduced motion holds the still frame.

The Vallerii built the first Xalian Generator on Floria, a bare world of rock and shallow sea that its star boiled into a flood every year, so that any mistake it made would simply be washed away.

What it is

The first flood after the Genesis Prototype was switched on. The Generator stands alone on scoured rock under clouds so dense they black out the sun, running at full capacity through the storm. Its vents throw glowing seed pods into the wind, and the flood that was meant to wash its mistakes away carries them across the world instead. Downwind the first green catches in the cracks, a small plant-like Xalian stands in the water, and far off a colossal young World Tree already climbs into the cloud. Floria's history says some Xalians there were born from the Genesis Prototype's first seeds.

How it moves

The Generator's core breathes and each vent flares as it throws a burst of pods, which ride the wind downwind and settle. Rain falls on a slant with the wind and rings the floodwater, lightning moves inside the cloud and once lights the World Tree from behind, and the fronds in the foreground bend in the gusts. Every animation starts mid-cycle, and the page seeks the plate to its first frame before it pauses, so the reduced-motion still shows pods in flight.
