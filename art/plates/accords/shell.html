The APEX Accords placed the Xalian Generators under a single regulating intelligence, and its reach depended on Zolton's Quantum Entanglement Devices, forged in the planet's lightning-wracked bloodstorms.

What it is

The machine that made the link. A QED works stands on the summit of one of Zolton's metal spires, high above a sea of storm cloud lit red from inside. Chips are forged at opposite ends of a crimson sprite: a pod hung on a tether above, and the cradle on the mast below. When the sprite blooms between them, a discharge runs down the mast and both ends pulse together: two chips, entangled. One cold lamp on the mast never flickers.

How it moves

Sprites bloom fast, flicker and fade slowly on eight separate clocks, staggered so the sky is never quiet for long. Bloodstorm lightning falls from the storm wall and from sprites into the spire tips, leaves a glint on the tip and an afterglow in the cloud, and flickers deep inside the cloud sea. The storm's red light swells and settles. Every animation starts mid-cycle, and the page seeks the plate to its first frame before it pauses, so the reduced-motion still shows the forging in progress.
