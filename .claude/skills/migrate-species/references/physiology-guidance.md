# Physiology and presentation guidance retained through the redesign

Use the executable catalog for keys and the current creature contract for capabilities. Removing traits and corporeality does not discard the following source-reading distinctions.

## Attributes and movement

Strength is physical force; vitality is bodily vitality; endurance is sustained output; agility is maneuvering; reflex is reaction; intelligence is reasoning; willpower is mental fortitude; instinct is perception and instinctive judgment; charisma is presence, not eloquence; resilience is toughness. Vitality/resilience and intelligence/instinct intentionally differ.

Flight, swim, burrow, climb, sprint, leap and manipulation describe outcomes, not the mechanism. Zero means unable. Explain unusually strong performance through sourced anatomy or a supported process. Do not resurrect telekinetic or other trait flags as prerequisites. A source-supported mind mechanism can justify manipulation, but psychic identity or sensing alone does not.

## The resting body

- Classify the resting body, not a temporary ability state. Smoke dispersal does not change a flesh body's permanent composition. An emitted substance is not a composition secondary.
- A composition secondary describes a genuinely distinct structural substance, including an internal skeleton or core. It differs from primary. An outer casing is covering, not a duplicate composition secondary. A gas-filled animal is not necessarily made of gas.
- Body plan describes how the body is borne and moves, not the pose in one image. A rearing quadruped does not become a biped. A winged creature primarily walking on legs remains biped/quadruped; avian means flight is its primary movement.
- Swarm describes many units acting as one creature, not ordinary social groups. Floating describes wingless/finless support by gas, a field or an evidenced spectral process. Amorphous describes an unfixed body outline; a rigid casing over flowing contents does not make the contents quadrupedal.
- If still ambiguous, the existing body-plan selection order is swarm, floating, piscine, avian, amorphous, serpentine, multiped, quadruped, biped. Ground dwelling alone is not a new rooted body plan.
- Covering describes the trunk's outer surface. Chitin is grown exoskeleton; plating is another rigid casing; crystal is surface crystal growth; hide is thick rugged skin; bare is the default smooth/uncovered surface. Horns or isolated crystal structures do not make the whole covering crystal. A smooth silhouette alone is not evidence of fur or scales.
- Shell and hide are alternative resting-surface anatomy classifications. Material, covering and element do not grant automatic protection; author supported protection explicitly.
- Respiration and environmental tolerance differ. Breathing phases must be among supported ambient phases. Empty breathes means non-breathing. Chemistry and pressure are not implicitly encoded by gas/liquid/vacuum.
- Environmental temperature is sustained normal activity, not a planet's absolute record extremes. Compare with the planet's habitable band and explain any source-supported extension. The generic compiler does not load planetary lore.
- Communication is outward signaling, not speech, control of one's own projections or an attack's effect. Special senses require evidence. Lowlight means dim-light adaptation only.
- Lifespan remains a species fact about wear-out, not a newly rolled score. Preserve established source-supported lifespans when removing corporeality; do not infer that every gas body is ageless or assign new powers from lifespan. Chirality rolled means levo/dextro 50/50; achiral needs appropriate source chemistry.
- Preserve Phantiri's source-supported spectral architecture when retiring the corporeality label. Do not replace its creatures with flesh interpretations or infer their protections from the ghost element alone.

## Lore fields

Preserve the user's teaser verbatim. Appearance has three to eight defining physical qualities, one readable concept per entry. Overall form first, then covering and defining structures. Use relative size words; measurements already have fields. Do not copy an illustrator's incidental pose, count minor silhouette notches, list absent default anatomy, introduce superlatives that constrain future species, or put gameplay capabilities in appearance.

Origin, habitat, feeding, behavior and company remain required plain-language fields. Each claim needs species, art or planetary source support. Do not silently default company to solitary. These fields may describe a defining fact from another angle but should not copy whole teaser sentences. Keep world exposition in the planet record and avoid dramatic closing lines.
