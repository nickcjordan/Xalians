# Xalians Voice Analysis

Every quoted line below is verbatim from `lambda/src/json/planets.json`, `species.json`, or `glossary.json`. Read the exemplars, not just the rules — the rules are a summary of what the exemplars do.

## Sentence-level fingerprint

- **Long, subordinated sentences** that stack causes into consequences, usually with a participial tail: *"…causing extreme friction in its mantle that stimulates extraordinary levels of volcanic activity."* The tail is where the mechanism lands.
- **Periodic paragraph shape.** The topic sentence states a condition, the middle explains the physics or the economics, and the final sentence delivers the human cost or the turn.
- **Short declarative sentences are reserved for impact** and are rare enough to hit. *"But once it had been built, there was no going back."* *"They would of course, be wrong."* *"But the trenches of Endessa's deepest oceans carried much more than green gold."* *"That is, until the Syndicate stole a Xalian Generator."*
- **Colon and question asides** used for reveals: *"their port of departure? Shadharam IV, a planet in the very same system."* (The source also uses em-dash asides, but per Nick's standing rule new lore never uses em-dashes; achieve the same reveal cadence with colons, semicolons, or a short declarative sentence.)
- **Rhetorical questions** in the openers of the mythic entries: *"Was there intelligent life beyond Vallerii space, or were the Vallerii truly the first and only to walk among the stars…?"* Used only in the grandest registers (Phantiri, Telypso). Do not overuse.
- **Ellipsis endings** — `…` — close nearly every long-form entry. Always attached to a forward-looking, unresolved clause.
- **Scare quotes** mark coined or euphemistic terms on first use: *"company wars," "death tide," "green gold," "milking," "black lightning," "freezing time," "the Endless Sea," "astronauts," "philanthropy" auction*.
- Serial commas are inconsistent in the source; do not treat comma style as a rule. Some typos exist in canon (*"replate," "whos'," "Poesidas," "eek out"*) — do not imitate them.

## Diction registers, and how they interlock

**Planetary science, used precisely.** orbital resonance, plate tectonics, elliptical orbit, tidally locked, supercritical fluid, insulating gases, carbon sinks, magnetosphere, brown dwarf, upwelling of nutrients, planktonic soup, telomere shortening, sulfated polysaccharides, heliobacteria, low-yield nuclear fusion, neutron burst, quantum entanglement, photosynthetic potential, astronomical units.

**Corporate finance, used without irony.** capital discipline, master limited partnership, mineral leases, drilling permits, feedstock system, restructuring plan, liquidate, bankruptcy filings, vested interest, investor funding, monopoly, aggressive cost cutting, revenue stream, troughed, wildcatters, carte blanche, executive's ransom.

**Apocalyptic and gothic.** infernal, hellscape, ghoulish, macabre, tombworld, mausoleum, crypt, necromancer, spectral, deathless underworld, planetary tombstone, museum of the dead, avalanches of corpses, miasma.

The voice's whole engine is the **collision of the first two with the third**. A chemical meltdown gets described in feedstock and containment terms, and then the paragraph ends with two million dead in an hour. Keep all three registers live in any long entry.

## Annotated exemplars

### The economic-motive rule

> "Some of the corporations who already had a vested interest on Zolton before the discovery of black lightning proposed withdrawing their Vallerii workforces and establishing a Xalian Generator on the planet. Since black lightning did no damage to inorganic matter, the Generator and the infrastructure it was meant to service would remain safe and it could simply continue replenishing any loss of labor from such natural disasters. The brutality of such logic stirred enormous debate among the Vallerii, who despite their greed were taken aback by the cold calculus of sentencing their creations to death over something so commonplace as electricity — a commodity which was already in relatively ample supply across the galaxy."

The atrocity is presented as a proposal with a business rationale. The moral weight arrives as a *reported reaction*, not as authorial judgment. Then the sardonic turn: *"All it took was a little push."*

### Flat cruelty

> "The Xalians would be the ones getting their hands dirty anyway."

> "It was merely a fitting economic decision to establish a Xalian Generator on the planet."

> "When Admiral Genik, then chief of Endessa's orbital fleet, asked what the target was, he was told simply, 'the entire damned planet, you fool.'"

Deadpan. No adjective tells you it is monstrous.

### Science before body count

> "Vallerii xenobiologists found that twice a year, a vicious storm season in Poseidas's distant polar regions would cause an upwelling of nutrients from the sea floor. When the current from Poseidas's poles met with the planktonic soup of its more temperate and tropical oceans, it led to a supermassive toxic algae bloom. The subsequent death of these blooms and their decomposition at the hands of various colonies of aquatic microbes coated the planet's oceans in an invisible layer of toxic microorganisms."

Three sentences of mechanism, *then* the settlers die.

### The Generator with agency, hedged

> "With its world fully populated and its safety secured, the Xalian Generator died down to a more regulated level of activity, as if its sensors could detect the stability it had created."

> "As if sensing their unstable auras, the Generator began churning out new forms of life…"

> "Instead, the Generator began to treat the prisoners as patients, hoping to cure them and merge them into the mind of the rest of Telypso's psychosphere."

> "…but the code of the Generator, or perhaps the consciousness of the planet, seemed intent on assimilating them into its fold."

Note *as if*, *seemed*, *or perhaps*. The hedge is mandatory.

### Hedged deep past

> "Rumor has it that many of these facilities were in fact black sites for covert research funded by ECHELON's most classified R&D divisions. In fact, dossiers have been circulated on the black market which indicate that…"

> "To this day, no one quite knows what happened at the Deepwater Black gathering system. All known records of the event have been sealed and classified."

> "Sealed records deep in the libraries of the Old Houses hint that despite its destructive power, what they find on Phantiri's moon may be little more than the tip of an iceberg…"

### Sardonic pivots

> "But all that glitters is not gold."
> "All good things must come to an end, however."
> "It could not have come at a worse time."
> "That is, until the invention of the Xalian Generators."
> "But even this dream was quickly unplugged."
> "They would of course, be wrong."
> "But the Vallerii had left behind a secret Xalian Generator in the bowels of Phantiri…"
> "As it would happen, the Vallerii had been gravedigging the whole time after all."

### Token-and-ellipsis closers

> "…only the accumulation of Scrambler Tokens will keep the denizens of Poseidas secure in their newfound ways of peace and autonomy…"

> "For now, the Scrambler Tokens are simply a means of fueling the machine with a continuing supply of brutalized workers, but to those who dare to dream, the same item which enslaves the Xalians of Stonera today could become the key to a free future…"

> "Only the accumulation of Scrambler Tokens by Zolton's most radical and daring will give the voices of the galaxy's rebels any hope of ever being heard…"

> "…there are rumors that forces are gathering on the dark side of Luminax, away from the prying eyes of King Kozrak's secret police, where they can study the mutations caused by ION-9 and foment the beginnings of revolution…"

Note the shape: *present constraint → the Token as the only lever → an ambition that is not yet achieved → `…`*

### Species descriptions — the model entries

> "Hulking, white-furred apes with the heads of mammoths and tusks made of pure ice, the Yetimoths formed the rank and file of Krystos' prisonguards in ancient times. If their enormous size and strength was not enough to keep prisoners in line, they could also form thick sheets of ice from thin air, covering themselves in a near-impenetrable armor, blocking off escape routes in walls of frost, or encapsulating their opponents until they could lumber over close enough to pummel them into submission with their meaty, ice-gauntleted fists."

*Appositive body description → original engineered function, tied to a named planetary institution → what the abilities do in a fight.*

> "Used in ancient times to quickly harvest the vast crop yields of Luminax's fields, the large, albino feline known as the Chromocat possesses two sickle-shaped blades infused with pure ionized radiation that extend backwards from its front paws. These 'energy swords' allowed them to cut through grains with minimal effort, and their ability to enter a photonic-state and leap through fields at the speed of light meant they could comb through and harvest entire fields in record time. Today, their wicked speed and ionic blades allow them to seemingly 'teleport' around the battlefield, harassing their opponents by barraging them with rapid cuts in a slice-and-dice pattern so swift that reaction becomes nearly impossible."

The **"Today, …"** hinge is the species-entry signature: an agricultural tool repurposed as a weapon by a dying galaxy. Use it.

> "Imprits are demonic looking creatures, resembling flaming, horned monkeys backed by long tails with scythe-like tips. Imprits possess fire-retardant fur that protects their bodies from the flammable oils they secrete, allowing them to survive despite being in a state of constant immolation. They are remarkably intelligent among other Xalians, having been designed in ancient times as natural tinkerers and let loose into the various lightless mining shafts of Magmuth, where they were left to their own devices to fix and maintain the often makeshift mining equipment sent deep into the earth by the Vallerii. Today, they are known for being entirely erratic, likely driven mad by the isolation of the deep tunnels in which they tinkered and toiled."

Note the biological plausibility (fire-retardant fur *because* they secrete flammable oils) and the psychological consequence of the labor they were built for.

> "Hypnopets resemble glowing, golden-furred bunny rabbits with a single color-changing unicorn horn atop their heads. They were created by the Telypso Generator as service animals and therapists for the insane Vallerii imprisoned on the world… With the Nemesis Plague reducing the patient population of Telypso, many Hypnopets have taken to the stars as healers, though some have been forced into service by King Kozrak, who uses their hypnotic abilities as crowd control on rebellious worlds."

Even a cute creature carries the political present. Kozrak reaches into species entries too.

### Glossary entries

> **Age of Unbirth** — "The era in the middling years of the Vallerii Empire where the Cherenkov radiation given off by their tachyon drive cores had rendered most of spacefaring civilization sterile, leading to the development of Xalians."

> **Terracannon** — "A now defunct superweapon built by APEX which used a reverse-tractor beam to propel supermassive asteroids from the Jorian Belt at its targets."

> **Windsailor** — "Rogueish and rugged pilots who were once paid high sums to scour Saiphus for Benthane squalls."

Category noun first, relative clause carrying the causal chain, cross-references named. No ellipsis, no flourish.

## Anti-patterns

These read as out of voice. Do not write them.

- *"Little did they know, the horror was just beginning!"* — melodrama and exclamation. The canon underplays.
- *"This absolute unit of a creature hits like a truck."* — modern idiom, mechanics-speak.
- *"Its Attack stat is high and it resists Water-type moves."* — game mechanics in prose.
- *"Mint your own Xalian on-chain."* — abandoned crypto framing.
- *"Thankfully, the Xalians of Poseidas have finally found peace and rebuilt their world."* — resolved hope. The galaxy is dying; nothing is finished.
- *"The Xalian Generator, which is truly alive and conscious, decided to…"* — confirms what the canon deliberately hedges.
- *"Scientists finally discovered that the moon-weapon was built by the Old Ones to contain a devouring god."* — resolves the cosmic-horror thread.
- *"The planet was mysteriously ruined by an ancient curse."* — vague. The canon always names the mechanism and the corporation responsible.
