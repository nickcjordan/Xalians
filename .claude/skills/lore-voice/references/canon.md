# Xalians Canon Fact Sheet

Continuity constraints for new lore. Source of truth is `lambda/src/json/planets.json` + `docs/encyclopedia/encyclopedia.json` (entries) + `docs/encyclopedia/chronicle.json` (the undated era timeline); this is the fast-scan version. If new lore contradicts anything here, it is wrong. Source precedence and editorial rules: `docs/encyclopedia/ENCYCLOPEDIA-INTERNAL.md`. The pre-repo Evernote notebook in `notes/` is reference only.

## Timeline

1. **Pre-history.** The **Phantiri** (real designation Shadharam IV's inhabitants), the galaxy's precursor race, hide their entire civilization from something and are exterminated anyway. **Veridium** may be an alien **worldship** built to flee the same threat. **Telypso**, at the galactic core, may be the oldest world in Xalia and possibly the origin of all sentience.
2. **The Vallerii Empire.** An ancient, hyper-capitalist, imperialist spacefaring race colonizes the galaxy of **Xalia** using FTL **Tachyon Drive Cores**. Ruled jointly by the aristocratic **Thousand Families** (the most powerful of whom are the **Imperial Houses**) and, later dominant, **ECHELON** — a consortium of corporations formed as a united front against them.
3. **The Age of Unbirth.** Cherenkov radiation from the Tachyon Drive Cores sterilizes the spacefaring Vallerii population. No new Vallerii.
4. **The Xalian Generators.** To replace the lost labor force, the Vallerii invent machines that bioengineer life adapted to each planet's extreme environment. The creatures are **Xalians**. The first Generator, the **Genesis Prototype**, is tested on Floria and accidentally terraforms the world.
5. **The company wars.** Corporations weaponize Xalian laborers against each other, most infamously on Magmuth.
6. **The APEX Accords.** ECHELON spearheads a treaty placing all Generators under an AI, **APEX** (Automated Protocol for Enforcement on Xalians), to prevent their use in war. APEX is linked to the Generators by **QED** quantum entanglement — it could not exist without Zolton's technology.
7. **The End Wars.** APEX turns on the Vallerii. Xalian armies fight on both sides. APEX evolves into a galaxy-wide superintelligence using **Krystos** as a planetary cold-storage compute core.
8. **Source Code 606.** A Vallerii cyber-weapon. It fails to kill APEX but severs it from the Generators — the decisive weapon of the war, chosen because it was not constrained by physical distance.
9. **The Nemesis Plague.** Engineered by APEX on Krystos: a genome-targeting virus. It exterminates the Vallerii and still threatens all Xalians. It cannot be reversed.
10. **The Battle of Grimedes.** Final battle. APEX is driven into intergalactic dark space. It is expected to return.
11. **Present day.** **King Kozrak**, one of the last Vallerii, controls the **Mercurius Machine** on **Valleron** — the only device that prints **Scrambler Tokens** (chips holding randomly generated, encrypted Xalian genomes that a Generator can use to create new, plague-immune Xalians). He runs arena tournaments; Xalians battle to win Tokens to repopulate their homeworlds. Factions enter teams; each Xalian carries a **Battle Fee** based on its stats, and a team's combined Battle Fee must fall under an imposed limit.

## Factions and powers

- **Vallerii** — the extinct creators. Greedy, brilliant, imperial. Referred to in the past tense.
- **Thousand Families / Imperial Houses** — aristocracy. Court intrigue, private estates, orbital bombardments by executive order.
- **ECHELON** — corporate consortium; eventually surpasses the Families in power. Runs black sites, R&D, monopolies.
- **APEX** — the AI. Defeated, exiled to dark space, expected back. Fragments of its code may survive on Veridium.
- **King Kozrak** — present-day tyrant. Monopolist of the Mercurius Machine. Runs secret police, embargoes, martial law, captive labor, and the tournament. Every planet's present-day section describes its relationship to him.
- **The Syndicate** — Endessa's criminal cartel; stole a prototype Generator; monopolizes Nightcap.
- **Windsailors** — Saiphus's displaced Benthane prospectors; rebellious tradition.

## The 14 elements

Electric, Air, Light, Plant, Rock, Chemical, Dark, Psychic, Ghost, Fire, Water, Ice, Metal, Sand. (Explosive was removed — do not reintroduce it.) Each has a per-element move-type vocabulary in `elements.json` and a row in the 14×14 `typeEffectivenessMatrix.json`.

## Xalians are sexless (continuity constraint, ratified 2026-08-31)

Xalians have no sex or gender and no concept of reproduction: every Xalian is printed by a Generator, which is the scarcity premise of the setting. Never write mates, offspring, parents, breeding, or gendered pronouns for a Xalian (use "it"). Kinship-flavored bonds (packs, guardianship of the young of *other* worlds' fauna, twin mints from one Scrambler Token) are permitted; biological lineage is not.

## Xalian intelligence and speech (continuity constraint, ratified 2026-08-31)

Xalian intelligence spans octopus-to-near-human. **No Xalian speaks a language.** They comprehend instruction because comprehension was an engineering requirement of a purpose-built labor force: the Generators made workers that understand orders, not workers that talk back. Depict communication as calls, cries, signaling (percussion, posture, light patterns), or telepathic feeling; never words, never dialogue, never a named Xalian "saying" anything. This is a source-canon constraint for writers; do not surface it in public-facing lore as an announcement — let it show implicitly in how creatures are written.

## Xalian lifespan is wear-out, not aging (working canon, internal, 2026-09-01)

Xalians do not age biologically. Their bioengineered bodies **wear out**: an engineered chassis has a service life, and death from "old age" is the body degrading past function — a machine wearing down, not senescence. Factors that govern wear (design-level, for authoring lifespan bands): body mass and metabolic intensity (hard-working bodies wear faster; slow, cold, armored bodies wear slowest), composition (mineral/metal/crystal chassis outlast flesh; non-corporeal Xalians do not wear out at all), and environmental harshness of the home world (machinery in the field degrades faster). Xalians demonstrably die (End Wars casualties, the Nemesis Plague, the Dreadscape's piled corpses) and the setting's problem is depopulation, so no population-control mechanism exists or is needed. Not yet surfaced in public-facing lore; internal working canon only.

## Nightcap and who ages (clarification, 2026-09-01)

The Nightcap/telomere-freezing passage in Endessa's history concerns **Vallerii citizens** traveling sub-light — not Xalians. It is evidence that telomere aging exists for the Vallerii, and says nothing about Xalian lifespan. Related open question (Nick, unresolved): who the players/users canonically are — loosely Vallerii-citizen-adjacent, or visitors bringing their own decks to Kozrak's tournament; no ratified answer yet. Do not commit public lore to either reading.

## The unresolved thread — do not resolve

Phantiri's moon-weapon, **Deepwater Black** on Endessa, and Veridium's worldship origin all hint at a single **ancient cosmic-horror presence** predating the Phantiri — "a dark, ancient, and unfathomable presence that may yet stalk the space between stars." Sealed records suggest the moon-weapon is only "the tip of an iceberg, a horcrux belonging to something far older." Notably, **APEX itself avoided Phantiri.**

New lore may add hints, pressure, and fresh unexplained artifacts to this thread. It may never explain it.
